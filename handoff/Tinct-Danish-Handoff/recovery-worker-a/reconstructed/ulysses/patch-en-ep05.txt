5 82 Weak joy opened his lips. Changed since the first letter. Wonder if she wrote it herself. Playing the indignant lady: a girl of good family like me, respectable character. Could meet one Sunday after the rosary. Thank you: not interested. Usual love games. Then running round corners. Bad as a fight with Molly. Cigar has a cooling effect. Narcotic. Go further next time. Naughty boy: punish: afraid of words, of course. Rough, why not? Try it anyway. A bit at a time.
5 101 Meet one Sunday after the rosary. Do not deny my request. Turn up with a veil and black bag. Dusk and the light behind her. She might be here with a ribbon round her neck still doing the other thing, on the sly. Their character. That fellow who turned informer against the Invincibles — the political assassins — he used to receive communion every morning. Carey was his name. This very church. Peter Carey, yes. No, Peter Claver I'm thinking of. Denis Carey. And just imagine that. Wife and six children at home. And plotting that murder the whole time. Those church crawlers, now that's a good name for them, there's always something shifty-looking about them. They're not straight businessmen either. Oh, no — she's not here: the flower: no, no. By the way, did I tear up that envelope? Yes: under the bridge.
5 113 He stood up. Hello. Were those two buttons of my waistcoat open the whole time? Women enjoy noticing that. They'll never tell you. But we do it too. Excuse me, miss, there's a — just a — bit of fluff. Or the back of their skirt, fastening unhooked. Glimpses of what's underneath. They get annoyed if you don't point it out. Why didn't you tell me before? Still, I like you better a bit untidy. Good thing it wasn't something worse. He passed out, discreetly buttoning, down the aisle and out through the main door into the light. He stood a moment, unseeing, by the cold black marble font while before him and behind two worshippers dipped furtive hands in the low tide of holy water. Trams: a Prescott's dye works delivery van: a widow in her mourning clothes. I notice her because I'm in mourning myself. He put on his hat. What time is it? Quarter past. Time enough yet. Better get that skin lotion made up. Where is the place? Ah yes, the last time. Sweny's chemist in Lincoln Place. Chemists rarely move. Their green and gold display jars too heavy to shift. Hamilton Long's, founded in the year of the Flood. Huguenot graveyard near there. Visit some day.
