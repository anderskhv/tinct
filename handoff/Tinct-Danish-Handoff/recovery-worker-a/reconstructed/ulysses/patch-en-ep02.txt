2 102 And now his strongroom for the gold. Stephen's embarrassed hand moved over the shells heaped in the cold stone mortar: whelks and money cowries and leopard shells: and this one, spiraled like a emir's turban, and this, the scallop of Saint James. An old pilgrim's hoard, dead treasure, hollow shells.

2 144 Where Cranly led me to get rich quick, hunting his winners among the mud-splashed horse-drawn carriages, amid the shouts of bookmakers on their pitches and the reek of the food stalls, over the muddy slush. Even money Fair Rebel. Ten to one the field. Dice players and shell-game tricksters, we hurried past after the hooves, the competing caps and jackets, and past the meat-faced woman, a butcher's wife, thirstily nuzzling a segment of orange.
