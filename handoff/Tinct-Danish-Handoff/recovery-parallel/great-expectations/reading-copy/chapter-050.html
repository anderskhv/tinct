§ 1 index 0

Mine hænder var blevet forbundet to eller tre gange i løbet af natten og igen om morgenen. Min venstre arm var slemt forbrændt til albuen og mindre slemt op til skulderen. Det gjorde meget ondt, men flammerne var gået i den retning, og jeg var taknemmelig for, at det ikke var værre. Min højre hånd var ikke så slemt brændt, at jeg ikke kunne bevæge fingrene. Den var naturligvis forbundet, men langt mindre klodset end min venstre hånd og arm, som jeg bar i bind. Jeg kunne kun have frakken på som en kappe, løst over skuldrene og knappet i halsen. Mit hår havde taget ild, men ikke mit hoved eller mit ansigt.

English source and modern English

1867 text My hands had been dressed twice or thrice in the night, and again in the morning. My left arm was a good deal burned to the elbow, and, less severely, as high as the shoulder; it was very painful, but the flames had set in that direction, and I felt thankful it was no worse. My right hand was not so badly burnt but that I could move the fingers. It was bandaged, of course, but much less inconveniently than my left hand and arm; those I carried in a sling; and I could only wear my coat like a cloak, loose over my shoulders and fastened at the neck. My hair had been caught by the fire, but not my head or face.
