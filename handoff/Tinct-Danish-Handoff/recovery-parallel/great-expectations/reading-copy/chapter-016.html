Hun iagttog hans ansigt, som om hun var desperat efter at forvisse sig om, at han tog godt imod modtagelsen. Hun viste ethvert tænkeligt ønske om at vinde ham. Der var noget ydmygt, formildende i alt, hun gjorde — den slags, jeg har set i et barns væsen over for en hård herre. Efter den dag var det sjældent, at der gik en dag uden, at hun tegnede hammeren på sin tavle, og uden, at Orlick slentrede ind og stillede sig stædigt foran hende, lige så rådvild som jeg over, hvad man skulle gøre ved det.

English source and modern English

1867 text I confess that I expected to see my sister denounce him, and that I was disappointed by the different result. She manifested the greatest anxiety to be on good terms with him, was evidently much pleased by his being at length produced, and motioned that she would have him given something to drink. She watched his countenance as if she were particularly wishful to be assured that he took kindly to his reception, she showed every possible desire to conciliate him, and there was an air of humble propitiation in all she did, such as I have seen pervade the bearing of a child towards a hard master. After that day, a day rarely passed without her drawing the hammer on her slate, and without Orlick’s slouching in and standing doggedly before her, as if he knew no more than I did what to make of it.

Tinct modern English I confess I expected to see my sister denounce him, and I was disappointed by the actual result. She showed the greatest anxiety to be on good terms with him. She was clearly delighted that he had been produced at last, and gestured that he should be given something to drink. She watched his face as if desperate to confirm that he was taking kindly to his reception. She showed every possible desire to win him over. There was a humble, appeasing quality to everything she did — the kind I've seen in a child's manner toward a harsh master. After that day, it was rare for a day to pass without her drawing the hammer on her slate, and without Orlick slouching in to stand stubbornly before her, as baffled as I was about what to make of it.
