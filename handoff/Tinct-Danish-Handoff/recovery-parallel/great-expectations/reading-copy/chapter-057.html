If you can like me only half as well once more, if you can take me with all my faults and disappointments, if you can receive me like a forgiven child (and indeed I am as sorry, Biddy, and have as much need of a soothing voice and a gentle hand), I hope I am a little worthier of you than I was—not much, but a little. And, Biddy, it shall rest with you to say whether I shall work at the forge with Joe, or whether I shall try for some different occupation in this part of the country, or whether we shall go away to a distant place where an opportunity awaits me that I set aside when it was offered, until I knew your answer. And now, dear Biddy, if you can tell me that you will go through the world with me, you will surely make it a better world for me, and me a better man for it, and I will try hard to make it a better world for you."
