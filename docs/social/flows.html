The library's look in an email: the book's painting on top, a serif title, one dark button. The other emails would follow this.
No capitals-only labels. Section names are the library's bold sentence case; small labels are plain sentence case.
No pill switches. Choices are tick lists with one line of explanation, like the reader's settings. Tabs are underlined words.
Nothing decorative. No picture in top bars except the library's (it's the way in). On You, the picture is the button to change it.
