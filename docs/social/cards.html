Account, as cards

Third pass. Built from Tinct's design system: its paper and surface colours, the reader's sheet, teal only for switches, progress and focus. Tiles instead of lines, icons and chevrons instead of underlined links, real buttons. Playfair Display for titles and figures, EB Garamond for the rest. People and numbers are examples.

What changed in this pass

Design system coloursPaper, surface and a darker surface for tiles and groups. Teal only for switches, progress bars and your own row. Dark theme over the library.
Tiles, not linesYour reading and Leaderboard are tiles with a figure and a tiny chart; AI credits a wide tile with the Top up button inside.
No underlined linksRows have an icon and a chevron; actions are real buttons, dark for the expected one, outlined for the rest.
SettingsGrouped rows like the reader's settings, segmented controls, a teal switch, an (i) with a small dark box.
BooksOne line per book on a soft list, a thin progress line for books in progress, the month for finished ones.
Two fontsPlayfair Display for card titles, names and figures; EB Garamond for everything else.
