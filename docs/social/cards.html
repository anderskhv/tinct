What is different this time

Real proportionsFrames are 390 points wide, like an iPhone, so 17-point text and 48-point rows sit the way they will on a phone. Nothing is truncated.
Settings grammarGrey ground, white groups with 12-point corners, inset separators only inside a group, 32 points of air between groups. One idea per group.
GreyscaleBlack for the switch, the chart's current bar and the main button; mid-grey for everything secondary. Colour comes only from the paintings.
Fewer wordsShort labels, values on the right, "Settings" instead of "Account settings", one footnote where a choice needs explaining.
