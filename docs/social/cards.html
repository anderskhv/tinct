What changed in this pass

GreyscaleWhite, greys and black. No warm tints, no yellow, no teal. Only the paintings carry colour.
No boxesGroups are rows on the card itself, separated by hairlines, with a small grey heading. The segmented controls and buttons are the only shapes.
Dark modeNear-black card, white text and white as the emphasis; the reader's night page shows behind it.
Two familiesPlayfair Display for titles, names and big figures; the system font for rows and controls.
