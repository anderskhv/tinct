Account, as cards

Everything opens as a card over the page you are on, in the reader or the library. A row opens the next card on top; the arrow goes back one; the cross closes all of them and you are where you were, with no page load. Two fonts only, Playfair Display and EB Garamond. People and numbers are examples.

What these cards follow

Cards, not pagesFive cards in all: You, Your reading, Leaderboard, Find friends, Account settings, plus a reader's profile. Each opens on top of the last.
Instant backThe page stays underneath. The cross closes every card at once; nothing reloads.
Two fontsPlayfair Display for names, numbers and headings; EB Garamond for everything else. No monospace, no capitals-only labels, no pills.
One set of numbersLast 7 days, total, finished and streak are the same on every card.
Email confirmed at sign-upNothing here asks for confirmation; sign-up does that once.
PrivacyPrivate: only friends see your reading and you are not in search. The leaderboard switch follows what you share.
