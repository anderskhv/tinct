Reader feel: handoff

  • A marker, not a boxHighlights cover the full height of the letters like a real marker: gently uneven edges, slanted ends and ink pooling at the edges, in Tinct's five colours. The letters stay crisp.
  • Page edges show where you areOn desktop, read pages stack on the left and the rest on the right, inside a cloth cover. On a phone, only a thin hint on the right. Both run out at the last page, and the exact percentage of the chapter stays below.
  • The page rolls awayAs in Apple Books on iPhone, the page rolls back from the right edge into a narrow strip with faint mirrored print, then slides off. The same turn on one page and on two.

Handoff spec

Everything below the controls is a working reference. This section gives the values and rules to build from. Decided by Anders on 18–19 Nov 2026 (DECISIONS.md). Scope: the book reader in app/src/lab, desktop Two-page layout and the phone reader. The Margin layout and the add-a-book reader are out of scope.

0. Fidelity: follow this exactly

  • Every value on this page is binding. Colours, sizes, opacities, durations, thresholds and formulas are copied as written. Nothing is rounded, "simplified", replaced by an existing token that looks close, or tuned by eye.
  • Port, do not reinvent. The stroke paths, the edge shadows and the roll geometry are taken from this file's own script (INK_PATHS, inkURI, stackShadow, roll.set, roll.follow, roll.tween) and adapted only as far as the reader's code structure requires. The maths stays identical.
  • No additions. No extra animation, colour, control, setting, label or behaviour that this page does not describe.
  • Any deviation is Anders's call. If the codebase makes an exact match impossible, or this page is unclear or silent on something, stop that part, explain why, propose the smallest deviation, and wait for Anders's yes. Do not guess and ship.
  • Proof is a side-by-side. Each item in the checklist at the end is shown as a screenshot of the reader next to the same state in this file, at the same viewport.

1. Highlights: a hand-drawn marker

  • Each highlighted line fragment gets one SVG stroke as its background, stretched to the fragment: background-size:100% 100%; background-repeat:no-repeat; box-decoration-break:clone; padding:.04em 3px; margin:0 -3px. Letters are never filtered or distorted.
  • Stroke: viewBox="0 0 200 20" preserveAspectRatio="none", one of three paths (in INK_PATHS in the script), chosen by a stable hash of the highlight's id, so a highlight never changes shape between renders.
  • Fill: a vertical gradient of the highlight colour. Opacity a from 20% to 80% of the height, 1.35a at the top edge and 1.3a at the bottom, so ink pools at the edges.
  • Colours (Light and Book papers), as rgb and a: gold 232,186,60 at .40 · rose 226,128,128 at .36 · sage 130,182,100 at .36 · sky 110,158,216 at .34 · lavender 165,135,218 at .34. Map them to the five existing highlight colours.
  • A new highlight sweeps in left to right: clip-path: inset(0 100% 0 0) to inset(0) over 380ms ease-out. No animation under reduced motion.
  • Not designed yet: the Night paper (start from the same hues at about 0.6 of the alpha, without multiply) and e-ink (keep today's e-ink treatment). Show Anders a screenshot of both before shipping.

2. Where you are: page edges plus "% of chapter"

  • Position is the book position the reader already estimates (the same source as "% of book"), not the chapter. read = (page − 1) / (pages − 1), left = 1 − read.
  • Desktop two pages: a block of page edges behind the spread, drawn as stacked box-shadows. Left sheets round(read × 24), right sheets round(left × 24), sheet i (1 to n) offset ±i px sideways (left side negative) and min(i,4) × 0.5 px down, with no blur, odd sheets #f1ebdf and even sheets #d6ccb8, closed by one shadow at ±(n+1) px, min(n,4) × 0.5 + 1 px down, 2px blur, rgba(0,0,0,.35). A side with 0 sheets draws nothing: at page 1 there is no left block, at the last page no right block.
  • Desktop two pages: a cloth cover behind the book, gradient #24404d to #1a313b, radius 5px, 9px above and 11px below the pages, and 10px beyond the outermost sheet on each side, so it grows and shrinks with the edges.
  • Both pages keep a 46px gutter shade toward the spine: linear-gradient from transparent at the outer side, rgba(60,45,25,.06) at 55%, to rgba(40,30,15,.2) at the spine.
  • Phone, one page: no cover and no left edge. A right-hand hint, round(left × 6) px wide and hidden at 0: repeating-linear-gradient(90deg, #d6ccb8 0 1px, #f1ebdf 1px 2px) with box-shadow: -2px 0 3px rgba(60,45,25,.14), full screen height.
  • Keep the existing "% of chapter" line: under the book on desktop, at the bottom of the phone screen in place of the page number.

3. Turning the page: the roll

  • The same turn on one page and on two. The turning unit (one page, or the whole spread) is clipped to the left of a slightly tilted fold line; a narrow strip at the fold shows the back of the page; the next unit is already rendered underneath.
  • Fold at x (0 to unit width Wt), tilt t = 0.035 × page width (fold runs from x − t at the top to x + t at the bottom). Strip width f = clamp((Wt − x) × 0.42, 0, 56).
  • Strip: solid paper #f7f2e8 holding a mirrored copy of the turning unit (translateX(2x + f) scaleX(−1), text at 26% opacity, blur 0.25px), with a gloss gradient across it and a 42px shadow cast onto the next page (exact gradients in roll.set).
  • Forward goes from x = Wt to x = −70; back is the reverse with the previous unit on top. A click or key turn takes 480ms on one page and 640ms on two, ease-out cubic.
  • Dragging follows the finger (x = Wt + dx × 1.15); past 50px it finishes the turn, otherwise it settles back in 220ms. A two-finger sideways trackpad swipe drives the same drag (wheel deltaX, finished after 130ms without events, then ignored for 650ms so momentum cannot turn twice). A vertical wheel turns one page. Arrow keys and the existing chevrons play the roll.
  • Animate only clip-path, transform and backgrounds; no layout per frame. Under reduced motion and on e-ink, turn instantly.
  • Turning must not change how or when the reading position is saved (CLAUDE.md invariants and the live reader's position code, src/lab/labPosition*). Text selection for highlights must keep working; the roll starts from the page edges, a click on the side, keys or a swipe, not from a drag across the text.

4. Acceptance checklist

  • A highlight in each of the five colours on Light and on Book paper, one spanning two lines: one stroke per line, letters crisp, the same stroke shape on every reload.
  • Making a highlight: the 380ms sweep; none with reduced motion on.
  • Desktop Two-page at 1440 × 900 at page 1, the middle of the book and the last page: no left block at page 1, no right block at the last page, cover tracking the edges, "% of chapter" under the book.
  • Phone at 390 × 844 at the same three positions: hint 6px, about 3px, gone; "% of chapter" at the bottom; no cover.
  • Roll mid-turn on one page and on two, by click, drag from the edge and trackpad swipe; a short drag settles back; one swipe never turns two pages.
  • Reading position after turning, reloading and switching layout is exactly where the reader left off. npm test passes.
  • Night and e-ink: screenshots sent to Anders and approved before they ship.
