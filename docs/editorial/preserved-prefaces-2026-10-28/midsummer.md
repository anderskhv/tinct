Several people enter a forest with firm ideas about whom they love. By morning, firmness will look less dependable.

In Athens, marriage is a matter of parental authority and social arrangement. In the woods, desire becomes subject to a different kind of interference. Shakespeare lets these worlds collide with a group of working people earnestly rehearsing a play. Their confidence in the power of theatre is both ridiculous and touching.

The comedy makes falling in love resemble a revelation and a mistake at once. A person can feel completely certain while being unable to explain why yesterday's certainty has vanished. Imagination supplies beauty; it also supplies evidence for what it wants.

Enjoy the transformations, then notice who has the power to impose them. How do we tell the discovery of a true feeling from an enchantment that happens to be persuasive?
