Macbeth hears that he will become king. The words do not give him a plan. They give his imagination somewhere to go.

The frightening part is how quickly possibility becomes pressure. A future he had not openly claimed begins to feel like something he could lose. His wife understands the desires and doubts involved, and their conversations make a great public crime emerge from private persuasion.

Shakespeare follows what happens when a person tries to secure a life that can no longer feel secure. Every obstacle can begin to look like permission for another wrong. Sleep, ordinary company and the simple experience of being at home become difficult.

Read for the small verbal steps by which an imagined action becomes necessary. When has wanting something made you start treating the fact that you could have it as a reason why you should?
