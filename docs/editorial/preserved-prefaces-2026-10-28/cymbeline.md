Imogen has married against her father's wishes. Separation should test the couple's patience, but a wager about her fidelity creates a more dangerous test: whether a husband will trust a stranger's performance over the woman he knows.

The play spreads from a royal household into wilderness, war and hidden histories. That abundance is part of its appeal. People survive by changing their appearance, accepting help and learning that the position assigned to someone may disclose very little about their worth.

At its centre is the cost of believing too quickly. A persuasive story can seem complete precisely because it gives fear the answer it expected. The person accused may have no opportunity to participate in the judgment.

Read for the practical kindnesses that keep life possible while important people make confident mistakes. When has the wish for certainty made you impatient with the work of trusting?
