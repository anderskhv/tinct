Equality can open a life that inherited rank would have closed. It can also make people unusually attentive to the judgment of everyone around them. Tocqueville sees both possibilities in democratic society.

His inquiry ranges across local government, associations, religion, race, family life and public opinion. Freedom depends on habits and institutions as well as formal rights. People may learn to govern themselves by working together, or retreat into private concerns while a distant authority manages more of their world.

The account contains admiration, anxiety and the limitations of its observer. Its discussions of slavery and Indigenous dispossession make it impossible to treat American liberty as equally available to everyone described.

Read for the small social practices that make political ideals possible. How much independence of mind could you keep in a community that assured you everyone was free to think, while making disagreement feel like exile?
