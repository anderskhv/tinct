Othello has won respect through military service and married Desdemona against her father's wishes. Their marriage begins with a public assertion of trust. Someone nearby knows how to make trust feel foolish.

Iago rarely needs to offer a complete accusation. A pause, a question or an apparent reluctance can invite another person to supply the missing conclusion. Shakespeare lets us see the construction while watching characters experience it as discovery.

The play draws power from the difference between knowing that jealousy is being cultivated and understanding how it takes hold. Othello's standing does not free him from insecurity about belonging. Desdemona's confidence in their relationship cannot guarantee that her words will be heard as she intends.

Listen for the moment when a question stops seeking an answer and starts arranging a verdict. What kinds of evidence become convincing once fear has decided what must be true?
