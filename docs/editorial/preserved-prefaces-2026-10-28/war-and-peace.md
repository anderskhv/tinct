Pierre has inherited possibilities he does not know how to use. Andrei longs for a life larger than polite society. Natasha meets the world with an appetite that makes almost everything feel possible. Around them, families arrange marriages, spend money, argue and wait for news of war.

Tolstoy lets these private lives expand until they meet the events in a history book. The meeting is unsettling. A battle looks different to the man announcing a plan and to the frightened soldier trying to understand what is happening nearby. Nobody can see the whole, though many people later claim they directed it.

The same uncertainty enters love and ambition. We make choices inside circumstances we did not choose, often without understanding our own motives. The novel's immense scale gives room for people to change, contradict themselves and discover that what they wanted was not what they needed.

Read for the families before worrying about the famous generals. Where do you look for a meaningful life when history, other people and your own mistakes refuse to follow your plan?
