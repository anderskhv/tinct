A visitor enters a city where strangers appear to know him. They make claims on his money, his promises and his private life. His servant finds the same thing happening to him. Reasonable explanations become increasingly hard to maintain.

The machinery of the comedy is simple: two sets of twins have been separated. Its effects reach into relationships people thought were secure. A wife interprets a stranger as a husband; an ordinary demand begins to sound like evidence of madness or betrayal.

Shakespeare gives the confusion both speed and a background of family loss. The wish to discover who someone is can mean protecting a reputation, collecting a debt or finding the person one has spent years missing.

Enjoy knowing more than the characters, but notice how fragile their confidence becomes. How much of being yourself depends on other people recognising you correctly?
