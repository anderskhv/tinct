Viola survives a shipwreck and enters a new household in disguise. Her work soon requires her to carry another person's declarations of love while concealing feelings of her own.

The comedy turns on the mismatch between a private self and a public role. People fall for eloquence, melancholy, rank or an image they have helped create. A message may reveal more about the messenger than the person who sent it.

Alongside the romance is a joke played on a man who wants to rise in the world. It can be delightful to watch vanity exposed, until amusement begins depending on someone's humiliation. Shakespeare lets celebration and cruelty share the same house.

Listen for the loneliness inside even the most entertaining performance. How can someone recognise your love when the circumstances that let you speak also require you to hide?
