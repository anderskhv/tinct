Beatrice and Benedick know how to make a conversation into a contest. Neither intends to surrender the last word. Around them, other people have begun making plans for who should love whom.

The comedy delights in the possibility that a little deception can help someone recognise a feeling. Then it places that possibility beside a much darker use of the same technique. What we think we have witnessed may already have been staged for us.

Shakespeare connects wit with trust. A person who can answer every insult may still struggle to say something vulnerable. A person eager to believe an accusation may call that eagerness honour. The costs fall unevenly, especially when a woman's reputation is treated as public property.

Enjoy the verbal fencing, but listen for the point at which being clever is no longer enough. What would you be willing to risk to stand beside someone everyone else had decided to condemn?
