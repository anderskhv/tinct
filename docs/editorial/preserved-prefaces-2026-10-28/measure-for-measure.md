A deputy is left to enforce laws that have long been neglected. His severity quickly puts a young man's life at risk. The man's sister comes to ask for mercy and discovers that moral authority may conceal a private appetite.

The play brings law, desire and power into a deeply unequal conversation. A person can demand purity from others while finding reasons why his own conduct deserves an exception. Those subject to authority have fewer ways to make their account believed.

Shakespeare also questions the comforts of a ruler who watches from a distance and arranges events behind the scenes. Solving a problem can become another way of possessing the people inside it. The comic elements do not make the pressures less serious.

Read for who gets a choice and who is handed an apparent solution. How much would you trust a just outcome if someone else had decided what you must accept?
