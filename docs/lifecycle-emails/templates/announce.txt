What changes in our privacy notice

We now keep your reading time for each day on our servers, so we can show it to you. If you make a profile, you control it, and a photo is optional. If you choose Listen, the text being read is sent to xAI to make the audio, which is stored privately in your account and deleted with the book.

Your highlights, notes and chats are never shown to anyone.
