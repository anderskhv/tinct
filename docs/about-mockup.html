The library

Public and private.

The public library

The texts Tinct provides to every reader. We only add a text after checking that it's in the public domain in the United States and in the European Union, including Denmark. For a translation, the translation itself must also be in the public domain.

Original texts come from Project Gutenberg, the Internet Archive and other public-domain archives. Tinct's modern editions, introductions and narration are our own work.

Your private library

Anything you upload or import yourself. It's visible only to you, it stays yours, and you're responsible for having the right to use it. See section 4 of the Terms.

Found a text that's still under copyright? Copyright rules differ between countries and we can't rule out every exception. If you believe a text in the public library is still protected anywhere, or that you hold rights in it, write to contact@tinct.app with the title and the reason. We'll restrict the text while we look into it.
