The library

Public library. The books Tinct provides to every reader. We only add a text after checking that it's in the public domain in the United States and in the European Union, including Denmark. For a translation, the translation itself must also be in the public domain. Tinct's modern editions, introductions and narration are our own work.

Private library. Anything you upload or import yourself. It's visible only to you, it stays yours, and you're responsible for having the right to use it (see Terms, section 4).

Copyright exceptions. Copyright rules differ between countries and we can't rule out every exception. If you believe a text in the public library is still protected anywhere, or that you hold rights in it, write to contact@tinct.app with the title and the reason. We'll restrict the text while we look into it.
