### Book 9 — the Cyclops

The famous trick: Odysseus gives his name as **Nobody** (_Outis_), so when Polyphemus calls for help the other Cyclopes hear that _nobody_ is hurting him.

- Hospitality (_xenia_) inverted: the guest is eaten
- Odysseus cannot resist shouting his real name from the ship
  - this is the mistake that brings Poseidon's anger
  - compare Achilles and naming in the Iliad
- Note the blinding with the olive stake — very physical
