## Book 9 — the Cyclops

The famous trick: Odysseus gives his name as **Nobody** (_Outis_), so when Polyphemus calls for help the other Cyclopes hear that *nobody* is hurting him.

![[cyclops-krater.jpg|300]]

- Hospitality (*xenia*) inverted: the guest is eaten
- Odysseus cannot resist shouting his real name from the ship
    - this is the mistake that brings Poseidon's anger
    - compare [[Achilles]] and naming in the [[Iliad]]
- Note the ==blinding with the olive stake== — very physical

> [!quote] Book 9 (Butler)
> "Noman is my name."
