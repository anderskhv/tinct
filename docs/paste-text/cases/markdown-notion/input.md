<aside>
💡 **Short version:** pick a book under 300 pages, meet every three weeks, and always end with one question nobody could answer.

</aside>

## How we choose books

1. Anyone can nominate up to two books in the [Nominations](https://www.notion.so/Nominations-8f3a2c19b0e44d7fa6a1c2d3e4f5a6b7?pvs=21) database.
2. We vote with dots — each member gets three.
3. Ties go to the shorter book.

<details>
<summary>Why under 300 pages?</summary>

Because the group lost half its members during *The Brothers Karamazov* in 2024. Long books are welcome as two-part reads, announced a month ahead.

</details>

## Meeting format

| Time | What | Who |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 | First impressions, one sentence each | Everyone |
| 0:15 | Close reading of one passage | Host |
| 0:45 | Open discussion | Everyone |
| 1:15 | The unanswered question | Host |
