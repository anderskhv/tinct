## Book club handbook

> 💡 **Short version:** pick a book under 300 pages, meet every three weeks, and always end with one question nobody could answer.

### How we choose books

1. Anyone can nominate up to two books in the Nominations database.
2. We vote with dots — each member gets three.
3. Ties go to the shorter book.

**Why under 300 pages?**

Because the group lost half its members during _The Brothers Karamazov_ in 2024. Long books are welcome as two-part reads, announced a month ahead.

### Meeting format

| Time | What | Who |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | First impressions, one sentence each | Everyone |
| 0:15 | Close reading of one passage | Host |
| 0:45 | Open discussion | Everyone |
| 1:15 | The unanswered question | Host |
