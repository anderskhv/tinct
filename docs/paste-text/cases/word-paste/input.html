Minutes of the Village Library Committee

Meeting held on 7 October 2026, Parish Hall

Present: J. Hollis (chair), A. Oduya, P. Kemp, R. Szabo

1. Book donations

The committee thanked Mrs Kemp for the donation of 214 books from her late husband’s collection. About a third are duplicates and will go to the spring sale.[1] The remainder need:

·        cataloguing (two volunteers, Tuesday mornings);

·        new shelving in the reading room;

·        a decision on the three incunabula-era facsimiles (see item 2).

2. The facsimiles

Mr Szabo reported that the facsimiles are worth more than the committee assumed. He proposed, and the committee agreed:

1.      to have them valued before the spring sale;

2.      to keep one on display in a locked case.

 

Next meeting: 4 November 2026


[1] A list of the duplicates is kept in the library office.
