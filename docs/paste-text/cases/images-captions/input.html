Field notes: the bookbinders of Leiden

Text and photographs by Sanne de Vries

The workshop is down three steps from the canal, and the first thing you notice is the smell: glue, leather, and old paper warming under a lamp.

Marieke has bound books here for thirty-one years. She showed me how a text block is sewn on tapes, then rounded with a hammer until the spine curves like the back of a hand.

1 / 3

She does not use a computer for anything in the shop except the invoices.

Above the bench hangs a hand-lettered sign. It reads, in Dutch, Langzaam is ook een snelheid — slow is also a speed.
