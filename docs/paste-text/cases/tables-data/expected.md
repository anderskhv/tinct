## How long are the classics?

Word counts for the novels our reading group has finished, from the editions we used. Reading time assumes 250 words a minute.

Table 1. Length of selected novels

| Novel | Author | Length: Words | Length: Chapters | Reading time |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| _Persuasion_ | Jane Austen | 83,000 | 24 | 5 h 32 min |
| _The Great Gatsby_ | F. Scott Fitzgerald | 47,000 | 9 | 3 h 08 min |
| _Middlemarch_ | George Eliot | 316,000 | 86 | 21 h 04 min |
| _Heart of Darkness_ | Joseph Conrad | 38,000 | 3 | 2 h 32 min |
| Total |  | 484,000 | 122 | 32 h 16 min |

The spread is wider than most of us guessed. _Middlemarch_ is almost nine times as long as _Heart of Darkness_.

**Shortest:** Heart of Darkness

**Longest:** Middlemarch
