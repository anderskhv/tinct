## Grandma Elsa’s Cardamom Buns (Kardemummabullar)

There is something about the first cold morning of October that makes me want to bake. When I was little, my grandmother Elsa would wake me up at six on Saturdays, and the whole house would already smell of cardamom. I can still see her flour-dusted apron hanging by the stove.

These buns are not quick, but they are not hard either. The secret is **freshly crushed cardamom**, never the pre-ground kind, and a long, cool rise.

### What is cardamom?

Cardamom is a spice made from the seeds of plants in the ginger family. It is used widely in Nordic baking as well as in Indian and Middle Eastern cooking.

![Fresh out of the oven!](https://www.saltandcrumb.example/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/cardamom-buns-tray.jpg)

### Ingredients

- 500 g plain flour
- 250 ml whole milk, warm
- 75 g butter, soft
- 2 tsp cardamom seeds, crushed

### Instructions

1. Mix the flour, cardamom and yeast. Add the warm milk and butter and knead for 10 minutes.
2. Cover and leave to rise in a cool place for 2 hours.
3. Roll out, spread with cardamom butter, fold, cut into strips and twist into knots. Bake at 220°C for 12 minutes.
