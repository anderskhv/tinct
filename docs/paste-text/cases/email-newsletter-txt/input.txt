** THE LONG SHELF
------------------------------------------------------------
Issue 41 · Sunday 19 October 2026

Hi friends,

This week I finally finished *Middlemarch*, eleven months after
starting it. I want to say something about why it took so long,
because I think the slowness was the point.

------------------------------------------------------------
** 1. WHAT I READ
------------------------------------------------------------

Eliot's narrator interrupts constantly. On first reading I found
this maddening; on the second half of the book I started to wait
for it. The interruptions are where the novel thinks out loud.

Three passages I copied out:

* Chapter 20, the "roar which lies on the other side of silence".
* Chapter 42, Casaubon's will.
* The Finale, which is about how most lives are "unhistoric".

------------------------------------------------------------
** 2. WHAT YOU SENT ME
------------------------------------------------------------

Last week I asked what book took you longest. Some replies:

> "Proust. Four years. I don't regret a page."
> -- Hannah, Leeds

> "Ulysses, and I'm still not sure I finished it."
> -- Tom
