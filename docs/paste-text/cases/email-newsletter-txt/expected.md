## The Long Shelf — Issue 41

Hi friends,

This week I finally finished _Middlemarch_, eleven months after starting it. I want to say something about why it took so long, because I think the slowness was the point.

### 1. What I read

Eliot's narrator interrupts constantly. On first reading I found this maddening; on the second half of the book I started to wait for it. The interruptions are where the novel thinks out loud.

Three passages I copied out:

- Chapter 20, the "roar which lies on the other side of silence".
- Chapter 42, Casaubon's will.
- The Finale, which is about how most lives are "unhistoric".
