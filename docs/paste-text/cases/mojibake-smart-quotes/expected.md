## A Note on the Text

This edition follows the 1818 text. The editor’s changes are listed at the end — “silently corrected” misprints are not.

Readers in São Paulo and in São Paulo again, in Málaga and Zürich, sent corrections by post.

Some words were split by the old typesetter; others had invisible breaks that no one could see.

Straight "quotes" and the writer's apostrophes were left alone -- as were the double hyphens in the letters.

It’s a puzzle, the printer wrote… and then nothing more.

Ellipses... and dashes—unspaced—are the author's.
