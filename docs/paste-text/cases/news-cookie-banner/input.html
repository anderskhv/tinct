Ferry timetable cut to two crossings a day as engine repairs drag on

Islanders say the reduced service leaves commuters and hospital patients stranded on the mainland

By Mara Lindqvist, Transport reporter 14 October 2026, 07:12 · Updated 09:40
Share on FacebookShare on XEmail

The island ferry will run only two return crossings a day until at least the end of November, the operator said on Tuesday, after a second engine on the Solveig failed during sea trials.

Normally the route carries eight crossings each way on weekdays. The reduced timetable leaves at 06:40 and 17:15 from the island and returns at 08:00 and 18:30.

Advertisement

“We are sorry for the disruption,” said Hanne Brekke, operations director at Fjordlink. “Safety comes first, and we will not put the vessel back into service until both engines have passed their tests.”

Patients and pupils hit hardest

Islanders told the Gazette that the gap between the morning and evening crossings makes it impossible to attend hospital appointments in town and get home the same day.

I had to book a hotel for a twenty-minute blood test. That is the reality now.

Secondary pupils, who usually take the 07:30 crossing, are being taken on the 06:40 boat and supervised in the school library until lessons begin. The county council said it was “looking at all options”, including a temporary water taxi.

Fjordlink has chartered a smaller passenger boat, the Terna, which will add two foot-passenger crossings from Monday. It can carry 48 people and no vehicles.

A full timetable is on the operator's website.
