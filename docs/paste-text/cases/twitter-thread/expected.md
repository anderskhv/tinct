## A thread on why I reread the same novel every January

A thread on why I reread the same novel every January (it's Persuasion, and no, I'm not sorry) 🧵

The first time, at 17, I read it as a love story with a happy ending. Anne waits, Wentworth comes back, done.

At 30 I read it as a book about persuasion — about how the people who love you can talk you out of your own life, kindly, and for good reasons.

Now I read it as a book about time. Eight years pass before page one. Everyone has aged except, somehow, the regret.

"She had been forced into prudence in her youth, she learned romance as she grew older."

Rereading isn't nostalgia. The book stays the same so you can measure how much you moved. Free copy here if you want to join me: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/105

That's it. Tell me your January book 👇
