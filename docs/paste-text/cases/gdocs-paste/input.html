Seminar handout: Reading Kafka’s “Before the Law”

Week 4 · Dr. Lena Hartmann

The parable was published in 1915 and later placed inside The Trial, where a priest tells it to Josef K. Read it twice before the seminar: once quickly, once slowly, with a pencil.1


Questions

  1. Who is the door for?

  2. Why does the man never simply walk in?

  3. Is he forbidden, or only told “not yet”?


Two translations

German (1915)

Vor dem Gesetz steht ein Türhüter.

Our class version

Before the law stands a doorkeeper.


Background reading: a short introduction.


“He is still waiting,” one student wrote last year, “because waiting is the only thing that is his.”
