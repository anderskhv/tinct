## Seminar handout: Reading Kafka’s “Before the Law”

The parable was published in 1915 and later placed inside _The Trial_, where a priest tells it to Josef K. Read it twice before the seminar: once quickly, once **slowly, with a pencil**.

### Questions

1. Who is the door for?
2. Why does the man never simply walk in?
  a. Is he forbidden, or only told “not yet”?

### Two translations

| **German (1915)** | _Vor dem Gesetz steht ein Türhüter._ |
|---|---|
| **Our class version** | Before the law stands a doorkeeper. |

Background reading: [a short introduction](https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/kafka/).

“He is still waiting,” one student wrote last year, “because waiting is the only thing that is his.”
