# English text replacement, 2026-10-01 (release-candidate-7)

Four live books had English text with unresolved rights status for Anders (Denmark, life + 70). Their whole English text was replaced in place, under the same book ids, by public-domain translations whose translators died in 1915 or 1930. Revision label `text-2026-10-01.2` (CONTENT_RELEASES, migration maps, `characterReleases`), `releasedAt` 2026-10-01T14:00:00Z (move it past the deploy time if the release slips: unstamped records written before it are migrated, later ones are treated as new text).

`modern-en` of each book is a fresh Tinct rendering of the new original, paragraph aligned. Evidence (source records, rights evidence, package handoffs, structure maps, gate output, character reports) is in `books/wip/release-candidate-7/`.

## Source attribution (where it lives)

The registry (`app/src/data/bookRegistry.ts`) is the only place the app records edition credits: `label`, `translator` and `year` per edition, shown in the library and edition pickers. Those fields were updated (Calthrop 1908, Way 1894, Way 1898, McKechnie 1914) and a comment above each records the full source. The app has no per-edition source/licence line, none was added.

Licence note for Anders: Medea and Bacchae base text is the Wikisource transcription of Way (CC BY-SA 4.0, "Wikisource contributors"). The translation itself is public domain; the transcription terms require attribution, and the packages state that their `modern-en` adaptations are shared under the same terms where applicable. ## Adjustments to the delivered packages

- magna-carta `original-en` is the verbatim McKechnie extraction (`source/clean-charter.json`), title `Magna Carta (1215)`. The package's version re-worded 25 paragraphs only to clear an overlap gate and labelled itself an adaptation. Bracketed readings such as "[we have granted as underwritten]" and "or [and]" in the original are McKechnie's own and stay.
- the-art-of-war `original-en` is the verbatim Calthrop extraction (`source/calthrop-verbatim-extracted.json`, sha `9ff95e59...`). The package changed 9:31 and 12:8 for the same gate reason; those are restored.
- magna-carta `modern-en` clause 39: the two `[and]` markers (McKechnie's "or [and]") became "and/or" (preserves the legal ambiguity, no brackets).
- Exclamation marks: every modern paragraph already has at least as many `!` as its original in all four books (medea 167 vs 166, bacchae 161 vs 158, art of war and magna carta 0 vs 0); no paragraph needed a fix. Bracketed spans: none in any modern-en. Bacchae `original-en` has two bracketed speech lines at 7:14 and 11:87 that are Way's own.
- `books/classify-modern-en.py --gate` passes native and typography-folded for all four (`books/wip/release-candidate-7/gates.txt`: similarity 0.21 to 0.26).

## Integration

- Editions replaced in `app/public/data/editions/` (original-en, modern-en). No chapter shards exist for these books (none were ever split), so `split-edition-chapters.cjs` was not needed.
- `app/scripts/build-replacement-migration.py` builds `app/public/data/edition-migrations/<book>.{positions,highlights}.json` from each package's counts-only structure map (cross-checked against real counts). Every old paragraph gets an entry (a missing entry would read as "unchanged"), one `renumber` operation and one replace span: a saved reading place lands proportionally in the mapped paragraph and is approximate; a highlight cannot be proven exact so it stays unresolved with its own quote and note (recoverable). The replaced translation's text is deliberately not embedded in the highlight maps.
- `CONTENT_RELEASES` entries added (before/after sha256 of both editions); verified against the files and maps.
- `app/public/read/<book>/book.html` (SEO excerpt page) regenerated by the build from the new text; it previously excerpted the old protected translation.

## Character cards

`app/scripts/reanchor-replaced-characters.py` re-anchors exact-only (rules in its header). Each package has one snapshot per figure at the first mention, so card text is untouched and only anchors move.

- medea: kept Medea, Jason, Pelias (modern-en only), Pittheus, Pelops, Zeus, Themis, Orpheus, Maia, Ino, Pan. Dropped 26 (original-en 27): Creon, Aegeus (renamed spelling "Kreon", "Aigeus"), Hecate, Apollo, Hera, Skylla, Aphrodite, Hermes (aliases or spellings the new translation does not use) and all role, group and unnamed figures (Nurse, Attendant, Children, Chorus, Messenger and so on).
- bacchae: kept Dionysus, Pentheus, Agave, Teiresias (original-en only), Echion, Agenor, Zeus, Rhea, Demeter, Ares, Artemis, Orpheus, Pan. Dropped 29 (modern-en 30), including Cadmus (the new text spells Kadmus, 65 occurrences: the best candidate for an approved spelling mapping), Autonoe, Semele, Actaeon, Cybele, Aphrodite, Apollo, Dirce and every role, group and personification.
- the-art-of-war: nothing usable. Sun Tzu, the Yellow Emperor, Yi Zhi, Lu Ya spellings do not occur, Chu occurs twice (was once; may now be a state), Shuai-jan is a creature reference. Only "Kuei" (original-en, 1 to 1) matched exactly; a one-card package in a single edition was not kept, so the package and its `characterReleases` entry are removed. (`the-art-of-war.characters-report.json` still lists it as the one candidate.)
- magna-carta: all ten cards dropped (their mentions were long clause phrases and the clause wording changed); package file and release entry removed.
- Test change: `characterCards.test.ts` no longer lists the-art-of-war among the complete reviewed packages.

Dropped cards were not guessed back. Alias and spelling mappings (for example Kreon for Creon, Kadmus for Cadmus) are the existing "approved mention mapping" route and need a reviewed mapping file; none was applied.

## Danish editions

The registry entries stay (global Danish hold already keeps `-da` out of the reader and preserves saved places) and are now `aligned: false` with no `hasAudio`. Not removed from the registry and no files deleted: removal would strand saved Danish places and highlights, and the hold's recovery view reads the asset. If Anders wants the Danish text gone for rights reasons, delete `app/public/data/editions/{the-art-of-war,medea,bacchae,magna-carta}-modern-da.json`, the four `app/public/data/onboarding/<book>.da.json`, and add successors in `withheldEditions.ts` (note the alignment caveat in that file).
