# Library 2: the background shelf

Approved direction, September 28, 2026.

The returning reader enters a quiet room that changes with morning, afternoon,
evening and night. Their currently reading books stand together at the centre
of the table. The table is the main reading surface and resumes the real saved
position directly.

A future shelf belongs in the background of that room. It can begin empty,
perhaps with a few flowers. Clicking or tapping it opens the reader's inventory:
books they have finished, and books they mean to read. It should be a discovered
part of the room, not competing navigation on arrival.

Preserve the interactive shelf study and its refined real-data implementation
for this later feature. The refined shelf is available in the private preview
with `?preview=1&view=shelf`; append `&demo=reading` for sample books. The original
standalone study remains separate, clearly labelled as sample data.

For the present release, restore the time-of-day reading table. Do not add the
background shelf interaction yet. Keep the Back, direct resume, immediate save,
cover, and loading improvements. Public activation remains a separate decision.
