# Margin reader: fixes after the Gloss test (15 October 2026)

The source is the test report in `docs/margin/gloss-test/report.html`. The live comparison is on the same PDF, "Attention Is All You Need". The goal is to get answers up to Gloss's level while keeping what Tinct does better: the real PDF, Quiz me, synced cards and leader lines. This applies to the PDF reader (PDFs and EPUBs) only; the book reader does not change.

Anders decided on 15 October: **no Fact-check cap for now.** Remove the 10-per-day item from the brief; keep the short-window rate limit as it is.

Ship in three PRs, in this order, each tested on the same paper as the report.

## PR A: whole-document context, speed and models

**1. Send the whole document with every margin request, cached.** This is the change that matters most. Gloss sends the full paper (about 10.6k tokens, 99% read from cache) with each action. That is why it can answer about the whole paper and cite anywhere in it.
- **Prompt order:**
  - The stable prefix comes first: the shared rules, then the whole document text with `<page n="…">` markers (EPUB: `<chapter n="…">`).
  - Mark the end of that prefix with `cache_control: { type: "ephemeral" }`.
  - Everything that changes per request comes after the breakpoint: the selection, the question, the action text and the page the reader is on.
  - Nothing volatile (dates, ids, reading position) may sit before the breakpoint, or the cache never hits.
- **Applies to:** every margin intent (ask, explain, simplify, define, define_sense, factcheck, counterpoint, quiz, quiz_check, primer) and the column-foot question about the whole file.
- **Size limit:** up to about 150k tokens of text, send it all. Above that, send the current unit plus a table of contents, and say so in the prompt.
- **Verify:** log `usage.cache_read_input_tokens`; on the second action on the same file it must be well above zero. Caches are per model, so each model warms its own copy.

**2. Route PDF Explain through the `explain` intent, not `ask`.** It is the main cause of the 7.8 s Explain. Keep the passage-image path when images are on.

**3. Model upgrades (approved by Anders, 15 October).**
- **Lookup model:** for explain, define, simplify, define_sense and quiz_check, change from `claude-haiku-4-5` to `claude-haiku-5-5`.
  - Set `output_config: { effort: "low" }` explicitly. Haiku 5.5 thinks by default at `medium`, which would add latency.
  - Remove any `budget_tokens` or thinking settings left over from Haiku 4.5.
- **Main model:** for ask, factcheck, counterpoint, quiz, primer and the whole-PDF question, change from `claude-sonnet-5` to `claude-sonnet-5-5`, at the same price.
  - Sonnet 5.5 rejects `thinking: { type: "disabled" }` with a 400. Use effort instead: `low` for counterpoint and quiz, `medium` for factcheck, primer and ask.
  - Effort levels were recalibrated, so re-check them against the latency targets below.
  - Handle `stop_reason: "refusal"` before reading content.
- **Book reader:** the book reader keeps its current models in this PR. Change only the PDF reader's intents.

**4. Passage pictures on (approved by Anders, 15 October).** Set `PAGE_IMAGES` to true, so every passage action on a PDF sends the image of the selection. This covers Explain, Simplify, Define on a phrase, Fact-check, Counterpoint, Related, Quiz me and Ask from the bar. Send one image cropped to the selection plus a small margin, at most 1568 px on the long side, as `tinct-api.js` already redraws it. EPUBs send no image.

**5. Related is back (approved by Anders, 15 October).** Add Related to More, between Fact-check and Counterpoint, with the description "Where else the text takes this up". It is a new intent, `related`; the prompt and card are in PROMPTS.md.

**6. Stream the first sentence** for every margin action, as Ask already does.

**Latency targets on the test paper, measured from click to last word:**
- Explain, Simplify and Define: under 2.5 s.
- Ask, Counterpoint and Quiz: under 5 s.
- Fact-check: first words under 4 s, done under 10 s.
- Primer: under 15 s.

## PR B: the three breakages readers hit

1. **Primer reliability.**
   - Abort after 45 s with "Before you start is taking too long. Try again."
   - Retry once automatically if the reply does not parse.
   - On the server, log the parse failure type and reply length (not the reply text).
   - Store the primer even when the reader has closed it or opened another card while it was being made.
   - Goal: 10 out of 10 successes on the test paper.
2. **Primer scope and content.**
   - A PDF without an outline, or one under 40 pages, is one unit: the whole file.
   - Remove the "excerpt" framing from what the model sees, so "Keep in mind" is about the document, not about our extraction.
   - Map rows show the document's own heading once ("1 Introduction", not "1 · 1 Introduction").
3. **Notes on crowded pages.** Opening a note, or any new card, always brings that card out of the "N more" row, opens it and gives it focus. Never create a card the reader cannot see.
4. **Crowding.**
   - Cards may move up, not only down, when there is room above their passage.
   - Before gathering cards into "N more", fold the older ones to one-line rows (label and first words), as Gloss does.
   - Gather only when the one-line rows no longer fit.
   - Add these cases to the placement tests and the layout check: passages in the lower half of the screen, and 8 cards on one page.
5. **The whole-PDF question.** With item A1 in place it sees the whole file. Never answer from "this page" alone, and end without follow-up offers.

## PR C: answer quality, cost holes and small defects

**Answers:**
- **Plain prose.** Add to the shared margin rules: "Plain prose only: no Markdown, no asterisks, no bullet characters, no em dashes." Also render any stray `*word*` as italics on the client.
- **Explain length.** Hold Explain to its prompt: an opening sentence of at most 25 words, then at most three short paragraphs. It came back over 200 words.
- **Define.** When `define_sense` returns 0, or the word is a technical term the dictionary does not cover, ask `define` for the meaning in this text. Show that meaning first, labelled "In this text", with the dictionary senses below it. Offline, show the dictionary alone.
- **Quiz me.** Question 1 must be about the selected passage itself. Add "Question 1 must be answerable from the selected passage alone" to the quiz prompt.
- **Fact-check.**
  - Replace the opening instruction with: "Check whether the passage's factual claims still hold today and what has changed since it was written. Never treat the document itself as confirmation of its own claims."
  - Prefer primary, academic, official and well-known reference sources over personal blogs.
  - Start writing from the document while the search runs, and add sources as they arrive.
- **Simplify.** Rewrite only the selected text. A fragment gets a plain version of that fragment, never the surrounding sentences.

**Cost:**
- Do not call `charge()` for features in `NEVER_CHARGED_AI_FEATURES`.
- With item A1, `define_sense` and `quiz_check` get the cached document plus at most 2,000 characters of their own input.
- Reject `speculative: true` on `simplify`.

**EPUBs:** give EPUBs in this reader the margin. Gate it on the PDF reader, not on `kind === 'pdf'`. Passage images stay off for EPUBs, and EPUB answers cite chapters and quote a few words.

**Small defects:**
- **Phone title:** it collapses to "A."; give it priority and truncate with an ellipsis instead.
- **Primer lookup:** a primer that does not exist yet returns 200 with `{ primer: null }`, not 404.
- **Tap targets:** at least 28 px, including copy, mics, swatches, "Show answer", "Next question" and the sheet handle.
- **Citations:** `{p. 12-13}` with a plain hyphen is a page range.
- **Copy quote:** use the printed page label.
- **Failed quiz marking:** show the error and keep the reader's typed answer.
- **Define while switching PDFs:** a Define started before a switch is never saved into the new PDF.
- **Offline Define:** a capitalised word that is in the dictionary shows its entry.
- **Long cards:** cap quiz answers at 2,000 characters. The server trims or drops an oversized card instead of refusing the whole set, and the reader sees an error when sync fails for any reason other than a conflict.
