### iliad

Use case: illustration-story. Create a finished literary book cover for Tinct, portrait 1024x1536. Reference image is style/finish only, not an edit target: match its fine painterly realism and atmospheric light, but do not copy the ship, moon, border or composition. Book: The Iliad by Homer. A weathered bronze shield and a fallen crimson cloth in a dusty foreground, distant ancient walls of Troy under a muted copper dawn. Restrained umber, bronze, smoky rose and charcoal palette, rich but legible material details, dignified and spare; no battle gore or modern objects. Compose a completely flat full-bleed cover, not a mockup of a physical book. Integrate excellent large cream serif typography in quiet dark upper space: exact title 'THE ILIAD', exact author 'HOMER', both clearly readable at thumbnail size, no other text. Title about one-sixth cover height, author comfortably readable. No logo, subtitle, watermark, gold border, lighthouse or decorative frame. This is a replacement cover meant for actual small library thumbnails.

### the-republic

The reference is only the expected fine painterly quality, not a layout to copy. Different composition: looking from cool shadow within an ancient Greek stone colonnade toward the sunlit empty civic square of Athens, olive foliage and luminous pale stone in the middle distance. One modest bronze oil lamp in shadow at the lower edge, no people, no modern architecture. Literary oil painting with visible restrained brushwork, contemplative, dusty sage green and stone ivory, deep olive shadows. Flat full-bleed cover art, not a photograph or physical book mockup. Place THE REPUBLIC in large elegant cream serif type in a quiet deep-olive upper third; PLATO in readable smaller serif capitals near the bottom. Keep title and author clearly legible in a 120px-wide thumbnail. No extra text, no invented subtitle, no ornamental border, no gold frame, no lighthouse, no repeated ship motif. Fine atmospheric realism but unmistakably painted.

### confessions

Use case: illustration-story. Create a finished flat portrait book cover for Tinct, 1024x1536, exact title 'Confessions', author 'AUGUSTINE'. The reference supplies fine painterly craftsmanship only. A quiet late-Roman garden in North Africa, a fig tree leaning over an old stone bench, a small open codex on the bench, evening light touching pale plaster beyond. No person or church facade. Rich oil painting, fine visible brushwork, intimate rather than epic: aubergine shadows, muted fig-leaf green, warm cream and a little russet evening light. Compose the bench and codex in lower half, tree diagonal at the left. Set 'Confessions' in large elegant ivory serif lettering in quiet deep plum upper space and 'AUGUSTINE' below it in comfortably readable smaller capitals. Both must stay unmistakably legible at a small library thumbnail. No extra wording, no subtitle, no logo/watermark, no decorative border, no photographic book mockup, no medieval cathedral, no lighthouse. Preserve exact title/author spelling.

### nicomachean-ethics

Exact title 'Nicomachean Ethics', author 'ARISTOTLE'. A sunlit olive branch and a small unadorned terracotta drinking cup beside a rolled ancient Greek papyrus on a weathered limestone ledge, below a quiet pale-warm-plaster wall. Calm, humane, tactile, not a fantasy scene or photograph. The long word must be spelled exactly N-i-c-o-m-a-c-h-e-a-n and stay comfortably inside the cover edges. Clear at thumbnail size, varied lighter palette than the reference.
