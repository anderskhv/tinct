Share a highlight

The image is the reader's own page: same header, type and layout, in the edition and look the reader is already using. The quote always sits near the top of the page, with one sentence before it; all other text is greyed out. On desktop, a long highlight shows both pages. If a long highlight doesn't fit, the text gets smaller until the whole quote fits, but never below 75% of the reader's size; past that it's cut at a sentence with “…”. The quote is bold. Two options: surrounding text and the tinct.app line.
