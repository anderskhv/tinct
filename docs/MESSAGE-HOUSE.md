# Tinct message house

Approved by Anders, 5 November 2026. This is the one place for how Tinct describes itself. Product copy, link previews, store listings and posts start here.

## Brand promise (the roof)

**No text is beyond you.**

Use it as written: sentence case, with a full stop when it stands alone. It is not a feature claim. It is what a reader feels after Tinct: the book that looked too old, too long, too dense or too foreign is now theirs.

## Supporting line

Read, listen and talk with the world's great books, or your own, in a language and style that speaks to you.

Shorter, for tight spaces: *The world's great books, or your own. Read, listen, ask.*

## Pillars (what makes the promise true)

| Pillar | What it means | Proof in the product |
|---|---|---|
| Understand any passage | Nothing stays opaque | Explain on any selection; a modern edition beside the original; the primer and cast before you start |
| Hear it and talk it through | Reading is not only silent work | Narration that follows the text; Talk, a companion you can ask anything about where you are |
| Any text, not only ours | The promise covers what you bring | Your own PDFs, EPUBs and pasted articles (tinct.app/byob), private to your account |
| A library worth entering | Great books, presented with care | 100+ classics, Tinct's own modern translations, the cinematic library |

## Proof points (facts we can stand behind)

- Every classic has the original and a Tinct Modern edition, paragraph by paragraph, and many have Danish.
- Translations Tinct makes itself (for example The Master and Margarita, from the Russian) are independently reviewed against the source.
- Your place, highlights and notes follow you to every device.
- Reading the library is free; Premium adds more (state current pricing from the pricing page, never from memory).

## Tone

Calm, literate, confident. Short sentences. No hype words ("revolutionary", "unlock", "supercharge"), no filler, no exclamation marks. Talk about the reader and the book, not about AI.

## Where the promise appears

- Link previews for tinct.app, /library and /reader (title, description, image: `app/public/brand/20261105/share-tinct-1200x630.jpg`), and the shared preview image for other pages (`app/public/og-image-v3.jpg`, generated from `app/scripts/seo/og-image-template.html` by `app/scripts/seo/generate-og-image.cjs`).
- Browser tab titles: home, library, landing, /story.
- Book pages (`/read/{book}`) keep their own book-specific titles and descriptions, with the shared image.
- Share cards keep the call to action "Read free at tinct.app".

When the promise or supporting line changes, change the preview image under a new file name (link previews are cached by URL) and update this file.
