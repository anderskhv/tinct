# Rejected repeated motifs

- Henry IV, Part 2 and The Taming of the Shrew: feather/quill repeated Paradise Lost.
- A Little Princess and Essays: bowl repeated The Imitation of Christ.
- Twelfth Night: lantern repeated The Brothers Karamazov.
- Notes from Underground: glowing ring repeated Cymbeline.
- A Vindication of the Rights of Woman: compass repeated Groundwork.
- The Bacchae: wreath repeated Julius Caesar.
