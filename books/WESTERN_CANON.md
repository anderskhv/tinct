# Western Canon — Reading List

A composite canon drawn primarily from **St. John's College Great Books Program** (the most influential Western canon curriculum in the US, used at both Annapolis and Santa Fe campuses), cross-referenced with **Columbia University's Core Curriculum** (Literature Humanities + Contemporary Civilization) and **Mortimer Adler's Great Books of the Western World** (Britannica, 1952).

Organized chronologically by period. "In Tinct?" column shows current library status.

## Summary

**In library:** 33 books out of ~140 canonical entries above (~24%).

**Top gaps by impact** (short, canonical, PD):
1. **Oedipus Rex** (Sophocles) — archetypal tragedy, ~17K words
2. **Tao Te Ching** (Lao Tzu) — not Western but non-Western reference for contrast, ~6K words
3. **Candide** (Voltaire) — Enlightenment satire, ~35K words
4. **Notes from Underground** (Dostoevsky) — short philosophical novella, ~38K words
5. **Heart of Darkness** (Conrad) — modernist novella, ~38K words
6. **Common Sense** (Paine) — founding political document, ~24K words
7. **Communist Manifesto** (Marx/Engels) — ~12K words
8. **The Consolation of Philosophy** (Boethius) — medieval keystone, ~50K words
9. **Crito + Euthyphro** (Plato) — completes Socratic trilogy, ~12K combined
10. **Meditations on First Philosophy** (Descartes) — modern philosophy entry, ~30K words

**Legend:**
- ✅ Complete (3 editions + audio)
- 🟡 Partial (original text only, needs modern-en / modern-da)
- ❌ Not in library
