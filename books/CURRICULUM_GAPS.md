## Thematic gaps (what's missing tells us what's missing)

**Political philosophy:** None of Hobbes/Locke/Rousseau/Tocqueville/Mill/Marx. This is the spine of the Western political tradition and the largest structural gap.

**Greek tragedy:** None of Sophocles/Aeschylus/Euripides. Three of the four pillars of Greek drama are entirely absent.

**Aristotle:** Completely missing. Ethics and Politics together are arguably the single most-cited body of work in Western moral and political thought.

**German idealism / Enlightenment:** None of Kant/Hegel/Descartes/Hume. Modern philosophy's founding texts absent.

**19th-century existentialism and critique:** Kierkegaard, Nietzsche, Marx — all missing.

**Iliad:** The other Homer. Appears on every curriculum; absence is conspicuous.

## Proposed prioritization for adding

Given that Tinct already has the Odyssey, Republic, Confessions, Divine Comedy, Paradise Lost, and Aeneid — the foundation is there. Filling the highest-signal gaps first:

1. **Iliad** (Homer) — pairs with Odyssey, universally taught
2. **Nicomachean Ethics** (Aristotle) — single biggest philosophy gap
3. **The Prince** (Machiavelli) — short, foundational, high demand
4. **Leviathan** (Hobbes) — foundational political text
5. **Second Treatise** (Locke) — short, foundational
6. **Antigone** or **Oedipus Rex** (Sophocles) — Greek tragedy entry point
7. **Democracy in America** (Tocqueville) — large but foundational
8. **Meditations on First Philosophy** (Descartes) — short, foundational for modern philosophy
9. **Groundwork for the Metaphysics of Morals** (Kant) — short, landmark
10. **On Liberty** (Mill) — short, enormously influential

This list of ten alone would close the most visible thematic gaps and raise Tinct's coverage of the Yale DS + Chicago Core corpus from ~35% to ~55%.
