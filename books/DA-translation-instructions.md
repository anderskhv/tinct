# Danish Translation Instructions — Fabric of Reality

These rules were derived from Anders's corrections to the AI-translated Preface and Chapter 1. Apply all of them when translating English → Danish.

## Rules

1. **Translate function, not word.** Ask "what is this word *doing* in this sentence?" and find the Danish word that performs that function. Don't look up the dictionary equivalent and move on. Example: "namely" (introducing a definition) → "det vil sige" or "altså", NOT "nemlig" (which confirms).

2. **Danish prefers verbs over noun constructions.** When English uses "[have/show/display] + abstract noun", try a Danish verb first. Examples: "have currency" → "cirkulerer". "Growth of knowledge" → "viden vokser". "Is a cure for" → "kurere".

3. **Don't calque English gerunds.** "Det at spise" is grammatically valid but stilted. Use the infinitive directly: "at spise". No Dane would say "det at [verb]" in natural speech.

4. **Don't use English-calqued phrases.** Common traps: "i termer af" (in terms of), "involveret i" (involved in), "har udbredelse" (have currency). Find actual Danish: "handler om", "deltager i", "cirkulerer".

5. **Danish needs explicit nouns where English can leave them implicit.** English: "there is nothing more to X". Danish needs: more *what*? → "mere indhold" (more content). Free-floating adjectives/adverbs feel incomplete in Danish.

6. **Check adjective vs. adverb agreement.** "Delvis" (adjective, modifies nouns) vs. "delvist" (adverb, modifies verbs). English uses "partly" for both — it won't flag the distinction.

7. **Don't over-punctuate.** Danish comma rules are more flexible than the AI assumes. Don't put commas before every "at"-clause. Before restructuring a sentence, check if simply removing commas solves the problem.

8. **English tolerates long appositional chains; Danish doesn't.** Multiple comma-separated insertions between subject and verb collapse in Danish. Restructure: lead with the definition, then the reference — or break into two sentences.

9. **Prefer everyday Danish over formal/academic register.** "Nødvendigvis" not "uundgåeligt". "Forudindtagelser" not "forforståelser". "God" not "bekvem". Deutsch sounds like a thinker talking to you, not a bureaucrat filing a report.

10. **When fixing one problem, scan the rest of the sentence.** Don't tunnel-vision on the obvious error — check every word in the sentence while you're there.

## Key Terminology (use consistently)

- "fabric of reality" → "virkelighedens struktur" (not "væv" — editorial decision)
- "world-view" → "verdensbillede"
- "common sense" → "sund fornuft"
- "explanation" → "forklaring"
- "understanding" → "forståelse"
- "theory of everything" → "teorien om alt"
- "reductionism" → "reduktionisme"
- "instrumentalism" → "instrumentalisme"
- "positivism" → "positivisme"
- "emergence" / "emergent" → "emergens" / "emergent"
- "holism" → "holisme"
- "epistemology" → "erkendelsesteori"
- "the theory of evolution" → "evolutionsteorien"
- "the theory of computation" → "beregningsteorien"
- "quantum theory/physics" → "kvanteteori/kvantefysik"
