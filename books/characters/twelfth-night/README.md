# Twelfth Night recognition cards

All eighteen scenes, 1,120 paragraphs and 65 entries per English edition; 1,367 original and 1,376 modern exact mentions. Source bytes unchanged. Authoring-agent source review, not independent editorial approval; runtime registration pending.

Viola is Central. Sebastian, Orsino, Olivia, Sir Toby, Sir Andrew, Maria, Malvolio, Feste and Antonio are Major. Other participants are Supporting; family memories, reported figures, songs and named allusions are Reference.

- Ordinary identities are available from first encounter. Viola is the main character; Sebastian her twin brother; Olivia the countess and Toby’s niece; Malvolio her steward; Feste her jester. Viola is named in the local stage directions and speaker labels from her first scene: the card need not hide her name until someone speaks it aloud near the end. Sebastian’s first card identifies the family connection without announcing that he survived the shipwreck.
- Viola’s reminder shortens after 4:1 to the main character serving Orsino as Cesario. It drops the opening shipwreck frame. Feste’s Sir Topas role is an overt disguise shown to the reader, not an additional real curate. His reminder adds that role after 16:12.
- Cesario in scene 15 addresses Sebastian, as does Sir Andrew’s report of his assailant at 18:75. Most other Cesario references address Viola. Orsino’s question about his own servant at 18:76 still means Viola. Antonio’s Sebastian at 14:173 is a mistaken address to Viola; her subsequent discussion of her brother’s name remains a reference to Sebastian.
- Sebastian of Messaline is the twins’ father, not the surviving son. At 18:99 the first Sebastian names the father and the second names the brother. Roderigo is Sebastian’s earlier assumed name, introduced together with his real name at 6:4. Olivia’s father and deceased brother are separate from the twins’ family. Viola’s account of a daughter in love at 9:43 refers to herself.
- Olivia and Sebastian’s marriage updates after the priest confirms the ceremony at 18:66. Toby and Maria’s marriage updates only after Fabian reports it at 18:147. Orsino and Viola are intended spouses after 18:153; the cards do not invent a completed wedding. Malvolio’s imagined Count Malvolio does not change his actual rank from steward.
- Viola’s captain is distinct from Antonio. The priest is distinct from Feste’s impersonation; the two arresting officers are retained. Marian at 8:5 is treated as Toby’s familiar address to Maria, consistent with the household context, not a new invented servant. Mistress Accost is Andrew’s mistaken form of address to Maria. Mistress Mall is a separate reference, with no speculative expansion into a particular historical woman.
- Quinapalus, Pigrogromitus, the Vapians and Feste’s hermit anecdote are treated as inventions, not factual biographies. The lady of the Strachy and her husband retain the source’s uncertain identification without invented history. Sowter is a stock hound-name in a metaphor, not a real dog in Olivia’s household. Capilet is Sir Andrew’s actual horse. The modern hound and Shah of Persia renderings retain those references.
- The Egyptian thief comparison refers to Thyamis in Heliodorus’s romance; the card does not reproduce the entire violent anecdote. The mythical, literary and biblical figures remain references. Queubus, Elysium, Lethe, ships and astrological Taurus are not extra people.
