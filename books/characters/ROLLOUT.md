# Library-wide character-card rollout

Requested by Anders after approving the War and Peace Chapter 1 sample and categories, 2026-09-09.

## Editorial standard

Follow [EDITORIAL-POLICY.md](EDITORIAL-POLICY.md). Provide short recognition help, ordinary identity from first encounter, and Central / Major / Supporting / Reference categories. Do not write a new plot recap every chapter. Preserve concealed identities and later developments behind source-based gates. Historical figures may have substantial narrative roles; nonfiction needs people and references, not an invented fictional cast.

## Files and regeneration

- `library-inventory.json` / `library-coverage.md`: current registry plus explicit per-book status, generated by `inventory.py`. Status is not inferred from a draft file's existence.
- `<book>/status.json`: editorial and app progress; only advance after the corresponding work is verified.
- `<book>/recognition-draft.json`: manually authored copy and proposed categories; not runtime data.
- `source-candidates/<book>.json`: first candidate regex matches from existing Threads aliases, generated by `prepare_source_review.py`. These contain future source passages and are editorial-only.
- `source-candidate-audit.json`: shared aliases and candidate/edition pairs needing review.
