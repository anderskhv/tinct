# Dr. Jekyll and Mr. Hyde: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Gabriel John Utterson · central

The London lawyer whose friends include Dr. Jekyll and Dr. Lanyon.

## Dr. Henry Jekyll · central

A London doctor and longtime friend of Utterson and Lanyon.

After source chapter 9, paragraph 33: Utterson’s doctor friend, who transforms into Edward Hyde.

## Edward Hyde · major

The man Enfield identifies as having trampled a young girl in the street.

After source chapter 2, paragraph 1: The man named as Jekyll’s beneficiary in his will, whose background troubles Utterson.

After source chapter 9, paragraph 33: The identity and altered physical form assumed by Henry Jekyll.

## Richard Enfield · supporting

Utterson’s distant relative and companion on his Sunday walks.

## Dr. Hastie Lanyon · major

A London doctor and old friend of Utterson and Jekyll.

## Poole · major

Dr. Jekyll’s butler.

## Sir Danvers Carew · supporting

An elderly Member of Parliament and a client of Utterson.

## Mr. Guest · supporting

Utterson’s head clerk, skilled at examining handwriting.

## Inspector Newcomen · supporting

The Scotland Yard inspector working with Utterson on the Carew case.

## Bradshaw · supporting

A footman in Dr. Jekyll’s household.

## Dr. Denman · reference

The surgeon who previously owned the premises now used by Jekyll.

## The girl in Enfield’s account · supporting

The young girl in Enfield’s account of his late-night walk.

## The doctor in Enfield’s account · supporting

The doctor with an Edinburgh accent who attends the injured girl.

## The girl’s father · reference

The father of the injured girl in Enfield’s account.

## Lanyon’s butler · reference

The servant who admits Utterson to Dr. Lanyon’s house.

## The maid at the window · supporting

The young maid watching the street from an upstairs window.

## Hyde’s housekeeper · supporting

The woman who keeps Hyde’s rooms in Soho.

## Jekyll’s housemaid · supporting

The maid among the frightened servants in Dr. Jekyll’s house.

## Jekyll’s cook · supporting

The cook in Dr. Jekyll’s household.

## The knife-boy · supporting

The young servant in Jekyll’s household whose duties include cleaning cutlery.

## The locksmith · reference

The tradesman called to open the locked door to Jekyll’s cabinet.

## The carpenter · reference

The tradesman assisting with the locked door to Jekyll’s cabinet.

## Cain · reference

The biblical brother of Abel, invoked in Utterson’s remark about leaving others to their own choices.

## Satan · reference

The devil of Christian tradition, used in comparisons with Hyde.

## Damon · reference

One of a legendary pair of devoted friends, alongside Pythias.

## Pythias · reference

One of a legendary pair of devoted friends, alongside Damon.

The figure in a familiar rhyme about disliking someone without knowing why.
