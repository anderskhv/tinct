# Crito: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Socrates · central

The Athenian philosopher, imprisoned after his trial and awaiting execution.

## Crito · major

Socrates’ close friend, visiting him in prison.

## Simmias · reference

A friend of Socrates from Thebes.

## Cebes · reference

A friend of Socrates, mentioned alongside Simmias.

## Homer · reference

The Greek poet traditionally credited with the Iliad and Odyssey. The dream quotes a line from the Iliad.

## The prison keeper · supporting

The prison attendant who admits Crito to see Socrates.

## The woman in the dream · reference

The unnamed woman who addresses Socrates in his dream.

## The Laws of Athens · major

Athens’ laws imagined as speakers in Socrates’ argument, rather than people physically present.

## Socrates’ children · reference

Socrates’ children, whose upbringing concerns Crito. They are not named in this dialogue.
