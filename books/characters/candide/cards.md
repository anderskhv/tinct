# Candide: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Candide · central

The young Westphalian man whose travels the novel follows, raised in the baron’s household.

## Cunegonde · major

The baron’s daughter and Candide’s beloved.

After source chapter 30, paragraph 2: Candide’s wife, the baron’s daughter he has long loved.

## Pangloss · major

Candide’s tutor, who teaches that this is the best of all possible worlds.

## Cacambo · major

Candide’s resourceful valet and traveling companion.

## Martin · major

The pessimistic scholar whom Candide chooses as a traveling companion.

## The old woman · major

The elderly servant who helps Candide in Lisbon.

After source chapter 7, paragraph 12: Cunegonde’s elderly servant and traveling companion.

After source chapter 11, paragraph 1: Cunegonde’s elderly companion, the daughter of Pope Urban X and the Princess of Palestrina.

## Cunegonde’s brother · major

The baron’s son and Cunegonde’s brother, raised in the same household as Candide.

After source chapter 14, paragraph 29: Cunegonde’s brother, the young baron who became a Jesuit.

## The Baron of Thunder-ten-Tronckh · supporting

The Westphalian nobleman in whose household Candide grows up, father of Cunegonde and her brother.

## The baroness · supporting

The baron’s wife and mother of Cunegonde and her brother.

## James the Anabaptist · supporting

The kindly Anabaptist businessman who helps Candide in Holland.

## Paquette · supporting

The baroness’s chambermaid, known to Candide from his childhood household.

After source chapter 24, paragraph 14: The baroness’s former chambermaid, now earning her living through sex work.

## Friar Giroflee · supporting

The Theatine friar whom Candide meets in Venice.

After source chapter 30, paragraph 6: The former Theatine friar whom Candide met in Venice.

## Don Issachar · supporting

The merchant in Lisbon who keeps Cunegonde in his household.

## The Grand Inquisitor · supporting

The senior Inquisition official in Lisbon who shares access to Cunegonde with Don Issachar.

## Don Fernando · supporting
