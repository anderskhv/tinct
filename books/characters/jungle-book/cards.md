# The Jungle Book: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Mowgli · central

The human raised by wolves, at the center of the first three jungle stories.

## Kotick · central

The white fur seal, son of Sea Catch and Matkah.

## Rikki-tikki-tavi · central

The mongoose living with Teddy’s family, the main character of the bungalow story.

## Little Toomai · central

The young son of the elephant driver Big Toomai.

After source chapter 6, paragraph 82: Big Toomai’s young son, honored as Toomai of the Elephants.

## Father Wolf · major

The wolf who raises Mowgli with Mother Wolf.

## Mother Wolf · major

Mowgli’s wolf mother, also called Raksha.

## Bagheera · major

The black panther, Mowgli’s friend and protector.

## Baloo · major

The bear who teaches Mowgli the Law of the Jungle.

## Shere Khan · major

The lame tiger, the chief enemy of Mowgli and his wolf family.

## Kaa · major

The great rock python of the jungle.

## Gray Brother · major

Mother Wolf’s eldest cub and Mowgli’s wolf brother.

## Sea Catch · major

Kotick’s father, a large fighting fur seal.

## Matkah · major

Kotick’s mother and Sea Catch’s mate.

## Nag · major

The cobra in Teddy’s garden, Nagaina’s mate.

## Nagaina · major

The female cobra, Nag’s mate.

## Darzee · major

The tailorbird living in the garden with his mate.

## Darzee’s wife · major

The female tailorbird, Darzee’s mate.

## Kala Nag · major

The experienced elephant cared for by the Toomai family.

## Billy · major

The experienced mule carrying the breech-piece for a mountain gun.

## The troop horse · major

Dick Cunliffe’s Australian cavalry horse, Number Fifteen of the Ninth Lancers.

## Two Tails · major

The elephant working with the siege-gun teams.

## The baggage camel · major

The camel from the 39th Native Infantry talking with the other camp animals.

## Akela · major

The lone wolf who leads the Seeonee pack.

After source chapter 1, paragraph 113: The former leader of the Seeonee wolf pack, an ally of Mowgli.

## The Bandar-log · major

The monkey people of the jungle.

## The gun bullocks · major

The pair of oxen from Hapur who pull the heavy siege guns.
