# Measure for Measure: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## The Duke of Vienna · central

The ruler of Vienna, who puts his authority into Angelo’s hands as the play opens.

After source chapter 3, paragraph 8: Vienna’s Duke, who means to stay in the city disguised as a friar rather than travel abroad.

After source chapter 17, paragraph 43: Vienna’s Duke, who has moved through the city as a friar under the name Lodowick.

## Angelo · central

A magistrate of strict reputation, left as the Duke’s deputy to govern Vienna.

After source chapter 17, paragraph 146: The Duke’s deputy, now married to Mariana.

## Isabella · central

Claudio’s sister, a novice entering the sisterhood of Saint Clare; she has not yet taken her vows.

## Claudio · major

A young gentleman of Vienna, Isabella’s brother, arrested for getting Juliet with child.

## Lucio · major

A gentleman of Vienna and a friend of Claudio’s, at home in the city’s brothels.

## Escalus · major

An experienced counsellor of Vienna, placed second to Angelo in the Duke’s absence.

## Mariana · major

The sister of Frederick, a soldier lost at sea.

After source chapter 9, paragraph 70: Angelo’s former betrothed, left by him when her dowry was lost at sea with her brother.

After source chapter 17, paragraph 146: Angelo’s wife, married to him at the Duke’s order.

## The Provost · major

The officer in charge of the Vienna prison.

## Pompey Bum · major

Tapster to Mistress Overdone and a bawd in her house.

After source chapter 12, paragraph 5: Mistress Overdone’s tapster, now taken on to assist the prison executioner.

## Juliet · supporting

The woman Claudio has got with child.

After source chapter 2, paragraph 80: Claudio’s partner, bound to him by a private contract but lacking a formal wedding.

After source chapter 4, paragraph 21: Claudio’s partner; Isabella calls her cousin, though the tie is a schoolgirl adoption rather than blood.

## Mistress Overdone · supporting

The keeper of a house of prostitution in the Vienna suburbs.

## Elbow · supporting

A constable of Vienna, given to mangling the words he reaches for.

## Master Froth · supporting

A gentleman of eighty pounds a year, brought in as a customer of Mistress Overdone’s house.

## Abhorson · supporting

The executioner of the Vienna prison.

## Barnardine · supporting

A prisoner in the Vienna jail, set to die on the same morning as Claudio.

After source chapter 12, paragraph 63: A Bohemian-born prisoner of nine years’ standing, condemned for a murder he does not deny.

## Francisca · supporting

A nun of the sisterhood Isabella is entering.

## Friar Thomas · supporting

The friar from whom the Duke asks a friar’s habit and instruction.

## Friar Peter · supporting

A friar of the Duke’s acquaintance, sent for to carry out his instructions.

## Varrius · supporting

A friend who meets the Duke outside the city on his return.

## The First Gentleman · supporting

One of two gentlemen trading jokes with Lucio in the street.

## The Second Gentleman · supporting

The other of the two gentlemen with Lucio in the street.

## The Justice · supporting

A magistrate who sits with Escalus at the hearing.

## Angelo’s servant · supporting

A servant in Angelo’s house who carries word of callers to him.

## Angelo’s messenger · supporting

Angelo’s man, sent to the prison with his sealed order.

## The boy · supporting

The boy who sings for Mariana.

## The officers · supporting

The officers who make the arrests and guard the prisoners.

## The attendants · supporting

The attendants who wait on the Duke and are sent on his errands.

## The lords · supporting

The lords of the Duke’s court.

## The citizens · supporting

The citizens who gather at the gate for the Duke’s return.

## Frederick · reference

Mariana’s brother, a soldier lost at sea with his sister’s dowry aboard.

## Ragozine · reference

A pirate who died of a fever in the Vienna prison.

## Mistress Kate Keepdown · reference

A woman Lucio promised to marry after getting her with child.

## Bridget · reference

A woman of Mistress Overdone’s house, named in Lucio’s teasing of Pompey.

## Mistress Elbow · reference

Elbow’s wife, who is with child; the hearing turns on what happened to her, but she never appears.

## Flavius · reference

A man in Vienna whom the Duke calls on first as he prepares to re-enter the city.

## Valentius · reference

One of the men in Vienna the Duke sends word to before entering.

## Rowland · reference

One of the men in Vienna the Duke sends word to before entering.

## Crassus · reference

One of the men in Vienna the Duke sends word to before entering; not the Roman of that name.

## Master Rash · reference

A young man imprisoned over a debt run up on brown paper and old ginger.

## Master Caper · reference

A young man imprisoned at the suit of Three-pile the mercer.

## Master Three-pile · reference

A mercer, and the creditor who has had Caper imprisoned.

## Dizy · reference

A young man among the prisoners Pompey recognises from the house he worked in.

## Master Deep-vow · reference

One of the prisoners Pompey recognises as an old customer.

## Master Copper-spur · reference

One of the prisoners Pompey recognises as an old customer.

## Master Starve-lackey · reference

A prisoner Pompey names as a rapier and dagger man.

## Drop-heir · reference

A young prisoner who killed Pudding.

## Pudding · reference

The man Drop-heir killed.

## Master Forthlight · reference

A prisoner Pompey names as a tilter.

## Master Shooty · reference

A prisoner Pompey names as a great traveller.

## Half-can · reference

A prisoner who stabbed Pots.

## Pots · reference

The man Half-can stabbed.

## Julius Caesar · reference

The Roman general and ruler whose triumphs Escalus and Lucio invoke when teasing Pompey about his name.

## Hannibal · reference

The Carthaginian general who marched on Rome; Elbow reaches for “cannibal” and lands on this name instead.

## Pygmalion · reference

The sculptor of Greek myth whose statue was brought to life as a woman.

## Jove · reference

The Roman king of the gods, also called Jupiter.

## Iniquity · reference

The Vice of the old morality plays, a stock figure of mischief set against Justice.

## Saint Clare · reference

Clare of Assisi, founder of the order of nuns Isabella is entering.

## Saints Philip and Jacob · reference

The apostles Philip and James, whose shared feast day fixes how old the child is.

## The Mother · reference

The superior of the convent Isabella is entering.

## The King of Hungary · reference

The king whose war with the dukes the gentlemen joke about; he is never named.

## The Emperor of Russia · reference

The Russian emperor, named only by title in a rumour about where the Duke has gone.

## The Pope · reference

The Pope, named only as “his Holiness” in the errand the disguised Duke claims to be on.

## Lucio’s child · reference

Lucio’s child by Kate Keepdown, a year and a quarter old and kept by Mistress Overdone.

## The father of Claudio and Isabella · reference

Their late father, remembered by Escalus as a noble man and by Isabella at his grave.

## Froth’s father · reference

Froth’s father, who died at Hallowmas and left him his eighty pounds a year.
