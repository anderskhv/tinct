# Romeo and Juliet recognition cards

All 24 scenes plus the opening prologue, arranged as 25 local units with 1,062 paragraphs per edition. 100 authored entities: 99 original / 96 modern entries and 1,605 / 1,626 exact mentions. Source files unchanged; authoring-agent review, not independent editorial approval. Runtime registration pending.

Romeo and Juliet are Central. The Nurse, Friar Lawrence, Mercutio, Benvolio, Tybalt, Juliet’s parents and Paris are Major. Other local participants are Supporting; the guest-list figures, recalled relatives, deities and literary allusions are Reference. Romeo and Juliet alone receive a later recognition update naming their marriage, at the end of 14:22, where Juliet explicitly calls herself his wife. No death or plot-summary snapshots are added.

- Parents and household identities are available immediately. The Nurse is Juliet’s nurse and companion, also addressed as Angelica. Rosaline is Capulet’s niece and Romeo’s initial romantic interest. Paris is a noble suitor, not Juliet’s husband in the early card.
- Capulet and Montague can identify fathers, families or young people. Montague addressed by Juliet or Paris resolves to Romeo; good Capulet at 13:26 addresses Tybalt. Family usages remain collective. Lady-prefixed names resolve to the mothers. Names inside the Capulet tomb and Saint Peter’s Church remain place references, not incorrect parent/servant bindings.
- The older cousin at the feast is separate from the uncle on the guest list. Their possible kinship overlap is not assumed. Valentine is Mercutio’s brother; Valentio is Tybalt’s cousin. Helena is a guest; Helen is the mythical beauty. Susan, the Nurse’s daughter, is separate from Susan Grindstone.
- The guest list retains named people and separately described wives, daughters, sisters and nieces. Utruvio’s widow and her deceased husband remain distinct. The unnamed son of Tiberio and Lucentio’s son are retained without invented first names.
- Generic servant labels receive scene-specific role records. The invitation bearer is not arbitrarily assigned Peter’s later identity; named Antony and Potpan are not guessed to be the first or second feast servant. These role records do not assert that the source proves every one is a different individual. Musicians, citizens and the watch retain collective entries alongside their named/speaking members.
- Simon Catling, Hugh Rebeck and James Soundpost resolve to the first, second and third musicians. Jack at the end of the musician exchange is left as a familiar form of address, not a newly invented musician. Poor John in 2:18 is fish; Young Abraham Cupid is an allusion to Cupid, not the Montague servant Abram or a biblical patriarch. Prince/King of Cats resolves to Tybalt, not Prince Escalus.
- Modern omits the named Aurora, Jove, Titan/Phoebus and Jesu references. Its generic gods in the lovers’ promise passage has a separate collective card; no claim is made that the translation literally names Jove. Its beggar girl remains Cophetua’s beloved. Modern Diana remains present, although Cynthia later becomes simply the moon. These differences are recorded rather than silently repaired.
