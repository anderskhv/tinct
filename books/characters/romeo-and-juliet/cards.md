# Romeo and Juliet: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Romeo · central

Lord and Lady Montague’s son, one of the play’s two central lovers.

After source chapter 14, paragraph 23: The young Montague, now Juliet’s husband.

## Juliet · central

Lord and Lady Capulet’s daughter, the play’s other central lover.

After source chapter 14, paragraph 23: The young Capulet, now Romeo’s wife.

## The Nurse · major

Juliet’s nurse and longtime companion, also called Angelica.

## Friar Lawrence · major

The Franciscan friar to whom Romeo turns for advice.

## Mercutio · major

Romeo’s friend and a relative of Prince Escalus.

## Benvolio · major

Romeo’s cousin and friend, a member of the Montague family.

## Tybalt · major

Juliet’s cousin, Lady Capulet’s nephew.

## Lord Capulet · major

Juliet’s father and head of the Capulet household.

## Lady Capulet · major

Juliet’s mother and Lord Capulet’s wife.

## Lord Montague · supporting

Romeo’s father and head of the Montague household.

## Lady Montague · supporting

Romeo’s mother and Lord Montague’s wife.

## Paris · major

The young nobleman seeking to marry Juliet, a relative of the prince.

## Prince Escalus · supporting

## Rosaline · supporting
