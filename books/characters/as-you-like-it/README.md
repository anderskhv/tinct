# As You Like It recognition package

All surviving play text and the epilogue in the seventeen source reading units, both English editions: 64 cards each, 1,230 / 1,227 exact mentions. Rosalind and Orlando are Central; Celia, both dukes, Touchstone, Jaques, Oliver, Silvius and Phebe Major; other participants Supporting; cited writers, mythological and religious references Reference. Hymen is Supporting because the figure participates in the finale. Every category appears alongside copy in cards.md.

Rosalind and Celia are identified through their fathers and cousinship immediately. Duke Senior first binds at banished father, Frederick at Celia's father, and Oliver at Orlando's brother. The two Jaqueses are separate: Duke Senior's melancholy courtier and Sir Rowland's second son. Biblical Adam is not the household servant. Sir Oliver Martext is not Orlando's brother; the quoted song's Oliver has a separate reference card. The forest and court lords remain separate, as do Rosalind's claimed religious uncle and the religious man Frederick meets. Rose in Rose at an instant is a verb, not Rosalind.

Disguise reminders release after Rosalind chooses Ganymede at 2:47 and Celia chooses Aliena at 2:48. Mythological Ganymede binds only at own page, not the disguise name. The pretend ceremony in source unit 12 does not release a marriage card. Final pairings release after Hymen's union at 17:47, with Phebe's agreement after 17:50; bride/bridegroom wording avoids pretending the mock ceremony earlier was a real wedding. The dukedom's restoration and Frederick's religious retirement release at the end of 17:52. No rescue, conversion or restoration is placed in a first-encounter card.

Source review is required before enabling: Act 1 scene 1 is missing; several scenes are combined into one unit (2:55 starts Act 2; 8:43 starts Act 3; 12:31 starts Act 4; 14:65 starts Act 5); most later displayed titles incorrectly retain Act 1. Publishing boilerplate interrupts the text at 2:51–54, 12:27–30, 14:61–64 and 17:71–75. The binder excludes those paragraphs and THE END. In particular, the footer's William Shakespeare does not become Audrey's suitor William. No coverage is claimed for absent scene 1 or Dennis. Restoration/redivision requires a new source review and rebuilt hashes/offsets. Existing source files are unchanged here.

Checks use actual repository editions. [Folger's complete play](https://www.folger.edu/explore/shakespeares-works/as-you-like-it/read/) supports the scene-gap review and ordinary cast relationships. [Bullen's edition of Marlowe's works](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/21262/21262-h/21262-h.htm) identifies the quoted line from Hero and Leander; Phebe's Dead shepherd is bound to Marlowe. The queen of night reference is Diana, invoked through her moon and huntress aspects.
