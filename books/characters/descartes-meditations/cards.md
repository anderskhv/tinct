# Meditations on First Philosophy: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## God · reference

The divine creator whose existence and nature Descartes examines.

## Pope Leo X · reference

The pope under whom the Lateran Council made the ruling on the soul cited in the dedication.

## Archimedes · reference

The ancient Greek mathematician, invoked for his geometrical proofs and the image of a fixed point.

## Apollonius of Perga · reference

The ancient Greek geometer whose demonstrations Descartes mentions.

## Pappus of Alexandria · reference

The ancient Greek mathematician named among the masters of geometrical proof.

## The malignant demon · reference

The powerful deceiver Descartes imagines as a thought experiment in radical doubt.
