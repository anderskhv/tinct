# Apology character package

Validated content for both English editions, revision 2026-09-10.1; awaiting integration. Authoring-agent review, not independent editorial approval. 57 original / 51 modern entries, 142 / 124 exact mentions, 92 paragraphs in three reading units.

Socrates is Central; Meletus and Anytus Major; Lycon, Chaerephon and the locally important friends at court Supporting. Cited poets, teachers, relatives and mythical figures are Reference. Plato's authorship is ordinary identity, but he is a supporting attendee in this work. Cards contain one short recognition identity; no verdict or sentence is revealed in the initial Socrates card.

Full proper-name scan and source context review include the easily confused relatives at 1:72–73. Chaerephon's brother is bound only at 1:9. Socrates' sons are separated from hypothetical children. Agamemnon is bound to the singular descriptive reference to the expedition leader in the original; the modern plural “leaders” is not assigned to him.

The modern source does not retain Euripides (an editorial reference), Prytanes, Eleven, Theosdotides, Telamon, or the singular Agamemnon reference; all six omissions are recorded explicitly. Source editions are unchanged. Generic jurors/crowds, places, ordinary God invocations, hypothetical people and adjectival Herculean are excluded. No inference that a cited myth actually occurs in the courtroom.

All categories/copy: cards.md. First contexts: source-review.md. Fingerprints: validation-report.json. Six focused tests join the existing suite (52 passing). Verify with `python3 books/characters/build_apology.py --check`.

Supporting classical text check: [Plato, Apology 41 at Perseus](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Plat.+Apol.+41&lang=original). Bindings use the actual repository editions; external prose is not copied.
