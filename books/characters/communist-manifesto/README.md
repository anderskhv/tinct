# Communist Manifesto: historical references

Opening and all four parts, both English editions, ten named-individual entries each. All are Reference. Ordinary historical identity is immediate: Pope is Pius IX, Czar is Nicholas I, and Metternich and Guizot receive recognizable occupations. The cards do not recount subsequent revolutions or summarize each argument against a school of socialism.

The opening identities are corroborated by the notes in [the Progress edition](https://michaelharrison.org.uk/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/Manifesto-of-the-Communist-Party-marx-and-Engels-Progress-Moscow-1977.pdf). The publishers’ political descriptions are not adopted as neutral card identities. No unprinted preface names or editorial-note figures are added to the local text.

Owenites and Fourierists name movements rather than individual appearances of Owen and Fourier. Generic classes, political institutions, and the metaphorical spectre of Communism are not turned into personal characters. Marx and Engels are not inserted into paragraphs that do not name them. The registered text does not include later named-author prefaces.
