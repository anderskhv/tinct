# The Communist Manifesto: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Pope Pius IX · reference

The pope at the time of the Manifesto’s publication in 1848.

## Nicholas I · reference

The Russian emperor referred to as the Czar in the opening.

## Klemens von Metternich · reference

The Austrian statesman associated with the conservative European order.

## François Guizot · reference

The French statesman and historian prominent under the July Monarchy.

## Jean Charles Léonard de Sismondi · reference

The economist and critic of industrial capitalism discussed under petty-bourgeois socialism.

## Pierre-Joseph Proudhon · reference

The French socialist thinker whose Philosophy of Poverty is cited.

## Gracchus Babeuf · reference

The French revolutionary associated with radical economic equality.

## Henri de Saint-Simon · reference

The French social thinker grouped here with Fourier and Owen.

## Charles Fourier · reference

The French socialist thinker known for planned cooperative communities.

## Robert Owen · reference

The social reformer associated with cooperative communities and improved working conditions.
