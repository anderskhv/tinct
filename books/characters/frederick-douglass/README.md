# Frederick Douglass character package

Full eleven-chapter narrative and appendix in original-en and modern-en: 134 entries each, with 539 / 535 exact mentions. Categories are printed alongside all copy in cards.md. Douglass is Central; important family members, enslavers and allies are Major; other participants Supporting; quoted writers, biblical figures and the parody's illustrative people Reference.

Read both actual source editions, their named contexts and the framing of the appendix. Ordinary family and employment relationships identify people directly. Douglass's father remains uncertain; Captain Anthony is not asserted to be his father. Sandy is not labeled a betrayer. No escape outcome appears in Douglass's first card. Later recognition copy shortens for Douglass, Harriet, Betsey and Sophia. Thomas's remarriage and Anna's marriage release at paragraph end. The narrative's retrospective reference to Anna's cousin can name Anna without suggesting a childhood marriage.

Namesakes are explicitly scoped: grandfather/crew member Isaac, overseer/minister Hopkins, child/adult Thomas Auld/Thomas Hamilton/Thomas Lowe, Lloyd/Weeden Daniels, Harris/Bailey Henrys, Graham constable/sheriff, Betsey Bailey/Betsy Freeland, and the shipyard Bailey/Douglass aliases. Master William at 10:37 means Freeland; Mr. Hamilton there means William Hamilton. Frederick Johnson is not Nathan Johnson. The appendix's Ned and Tom are separate literary examples. Smith's Wharf, Sally Lloyd, the Amanda sloop and John W. Richmond steamboat do not acquire people cards. Ambiguous collective Barneys and two Henrys are not forced onto one man.

Sources are the two repository editions.
