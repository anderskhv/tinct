# The Death of Ivan Ilyich: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Ivan Ilyich Golovin · central

The judge whose life and death the novella follows; Praskovya Fedorovna’s husband.

## Praskovya Fedorovna · major

Ivan Ilyich’s wife and the mother of Lisa and Vasya.

## Gerasim · major

The young assistant to the Golovins’ butler, who also nurses Ivan Ilyich.

## Peter Ivanovich · major

Ivan Ilyich’s colleague and old friend from law school.

## Lisa · supporting

Ivan Ilyich and Praskovya Fedorovna’s daughter.

## Vasya · supporting

Ivan Ilyich and Praskovya Fedorovna’s schoolboy son.

## Fedor Petrovich Petrishchev · supporting

The young examining magistrate engaged to Lisa; Dmitri Ivanovich Petrishchev’s son.

## Dmitri Ivanovich Petrishchev · reference

The father of Fedor Petrovich, Lisa’s suitor.

## Ivan Egorovich Shebek · supporting

A colleague of Ivan Ilyich at the law courts.

## Fedor Vasilievich · supporting

A colleague of Ivan Ilyich and one of his closer acquaintances.

## Schwartz · supporting

A colleague and card-playing acquaintance of Ivan Ilyich and Peter Ivanovich.

## Sokolov · supporting

The Golovins’ butler, senior to Gerasim.

## Peter · supporting

A footman in Ivan Ilyich’s household.

## Dmitri · reference

A servant in Ivan Ilyich’s household.

## Ilya Epimovich Golovin · reference

Ivan Ilyich’s father, a senior government official.

## Baron Greff · reference

The government official married to Ivan Ilyich’s sister.

## Ivan Ilyich’s sister · reference

Ivan Ilyich’s sister, married to Baron Greff.

## Praskovya’s brother · supporting

Praskovya Fedorovna’s brother, Ivan Ilyich’s brother-in-law.

## Alexeev · reference

A court official considered for Ivan Ilyich’s position.

## Vinnikov · reference

A court official mentioned in the discussion of possible promotions.

## Shtabel · reference

A court official mentioned in the discussion of possible promotions.

The official being replaced in the ministry; distinct from Ivan Ilyich’s friend of the same name.

The official expected to replace Peter Ivanovich at the ministry.

An official whose advancement helps Ivan Ilyich’s prospects.

## Zachar Ivanovich · reference
