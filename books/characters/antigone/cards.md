# Antigone: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Antigone · central

Oedipus’s daughter, sister of Ismene, Eteocles and Polyneices.

## Creon · central

King of Thebes, Antigone and Ismene’s uncle, and Haemon’s father.

## Ismene · major

Antigone’s sister, another daughter of Oedipus.

## Haemon · major

Creon and Eurydice’s son, betrothed to Antigone.

## Teiresias · supporting

The blind prophet of Thebes.

## Eurydice · supporting

Creon’s wife and Haemon’s mother.

## The Theban elders · major

The elders of Thebes who speak and sing as the chorus.

## The guard · supporting

The sentry posted to watch Polyneices’ body.

## The messenger · supporting

The attendant who reports events outside the palace.

## The palace messenger · supporting

The second messenger, reporting from inside Creon’s palace.

## Eteocles · reference

Oedipus’s son and brother of Polyneices, Antigone and Ismene.

## Polyneices · major

Oedipus’s son and brother of Eteocles, Antigone and Ismene.

## Oedipus · reference

The former king of Thebes and father of Antigone, Ismene, Eteocles and Polyneices.

## Laius · reference

An earlier king of Thebes, Oedipus’s father.

## Menoeceus · reference

Creon’s father.

## Megareus · reference

A son of Creon and Eurydice, and Haemon’s brother.

## Labdacus · reference

Laius’s father, an ancestor of the Theban royal family.

## Cadmus · reference

The legendary founder of Thebes.

## Amphion · reference

The legendary king and musician associated with building Thebes’ walls.

## Zeus · reference

The chief god of the Greek pantheon.

## Ares · reference

The Greek god of war.

## Bacchus / Dionysus · reference

The god of wine and ecstatic worship, closely associated with Thebes.

## Athena · reference

The goddess of wisdom and protector of cities, here called Pallas.

## Persephone · reference

The queen of the underworld, here called Persephassa.

## Pluto · reference

The ruler of the underworld, also known as Hades.

## Tantalus · reference

Niobe’s father in Greek legend.

## Niobe · reference

The legendary daughter of Tantalus, associated with a weeping rock on Mount Sipylus.

## Danae · reference

The mother of Perseus in Greek legend, confined by her father in a bronze chamber.

## Dryas · reference

The father of Lycurgus, the Thracian king who opposed Bacchus.

## Lycurgus · reference

The Thracian king, son of Dryas, who opposed the worship of Bacchus.

## The Muses · reference

The goddesses who inspire poetry, music and the arts.

## Boreas · reference

The god of the north wind, father of Cleopatra in the legend recalled by the chorus.

## Erechtheus · reference

A legendary king of Athens, an ancestor in the family of Boreas’s daughter.

## Demeter · reference

The goddess of agriculture, associated with the rites at Eleusis.

## Hecate · reference

The goddess associated with crossroads and the underworld.

## The Maenads · reference

The ecstatic female followers of Bacchus, also called Bacchanals or Thyiads.

## Cleopatra · reference

The daughter of Boreas and wife of Phineus in the legend recalled by the chorus.

## The sons of Phineus · reference

The two sons of Phineus and Cleopatra in the chorus’s legend.

## The stepmother in the legend · reference

Phineus’s second wife, stepmother of his sons by Cleopatra.

## Semele · reference

The Theban princess who is Dionysus’s mother.
