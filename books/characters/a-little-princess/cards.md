# A Little Princess: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Sara Crewe · central

The story’s main character, Captain Crewe’s daughter.

## Captain Crewe · major

Sara’s father, a British army officer serving in India.

## Miss Minchin · major

The headmistress of Sara’s London boarding school.

## Miss Amelia · supporting

Miss Minchin’s younger sister, who helps her run the school.

## Ermengarde St. John · major

The good-natured pupil at Miss Minchin’s school who struggles with her lessons.

After source chapter 3, paragraph 68: Sara’s loyal school friend, who struggles with her lessons.

## Becky · major

The young scullery maid at Miss Minchin’s school.

After source chapter 6, paragraph 59: Sara’s friend, the young scullery maid at Miss Minchin’s school.

After source chapter 19, paragraph 24: Sara’s maid and friend, formerly the scullery maid at Miss Minchin’s school.

## Lottie Legh · supporting

One of the youngest pupils at Miss Minchin’s school, whose mother has died.

After source chapter 4, paragraph 56: The little schoolgirl who treats Sara as an adoptive mother.

The older pupil at Miss Minchin’s school who resents Sara’s special treatment.
