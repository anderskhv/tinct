# United States Founding Documents: reference cards

All four sections and both English editions reviewed. Three entries per edition: George III, God as invoked in the Declaration, and Jesus Christ in the Constitution’s Christian dating formula. All are Reference. This is source coverage, not a cast of the founders.

The local Declaration and Constitution omit their signatory lists. Do not add Jefferson, Washington, Franklin or other signers as selectable people when their names do not occur. Similarly, President, Vice President, Chief Justice and other constitutional offices are general offices, not aliases for specific historical people. Institutions, political communities and legal categories belong outside person cards.

“King of Great Britain” and the specific “Prince” in the Declaration refer to George III; the Constitution’s generic prince is not bound. This identification is supported by [the National Archives history of the Declaration](https://www.archives.gov/founding-docs/declaration-history). “Divine Providence” is a religious invocation; “Providence Plantations” is a place name and is not bound to God. No modern legal interpretation or present-day officeholder information is added.
