# Oedipus Rex character package

Validated, awaiting integration. Revision 2026-09-10.1. Authoring-agent review, not independent editorial approval. 33 entries in each English edition, 619 original / 624 modern exact mentions; 474 paragraphs in 11 reading units. No entity omissions.

Oedipus is Central; Jocasta, Creon, Teiresias, Laius and the Chorus Major. Laius's weight in the inquiry justifies Major despite being absent. Messengers, herdsman, priest and final daughters Supporting; other named ancestors/divine figures Reference. Categories and copy in cards.md.

Initial relationships reflect the identity known in the play: Oedipus is Jocasta's husband, and Polybus and Merope his parents in Corinth. The cards do not announce adoptive parenthood or the concealed genealogy. Polybus's identity updates after 9:46. Laius, Jocasta, Merope and Oedipus update after the completed testimony and recognition at 9:145. Teiresias's earlier allegations are not treated as established fact or added as recaps.

The Corinthian messenger (8–9) and palace messenger (11) are distinct. Antigone and Ismene are included from their final uppercase stage direction. Named cast/speakers and full-source proper-name references were scanned, including aliases Polydore/Polydorus, Athene/Athena and the divine epithets. Ismenus in the opening oracle reference is associated with Apollo; Cyllene's lord identifies Hermes. No claim that the Chorus's speculative divine parentage is true.

Supplementary checks: [Jebb's public-domain commentary on Ismenian Apollo](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.04.0016%3Asection%3D1-150); [Apollodorus on Hermes at Cyllene](https://myths.uvic.ca/APOL1-L3.html). External prose not copied. Source editions unchanged.
