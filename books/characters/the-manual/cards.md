# The Manual: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Socrates · reference

The Athenian philosopher, used as an example of living by reason.

## Diogenes · reference

Diogenes of Sinope, the Cynic philosopher known for rejecting conventional comforts.

## Heraclitus · reference

The Greek philosopher from Ephesus, cited alongside Diogenes.

## Euphrates · reference

A philosopher from Syria admired for his eloquent teaching.

A title for the Roman emperor; this example does not identify a particular ruler.
