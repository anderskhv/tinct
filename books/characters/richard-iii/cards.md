# Richard III: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Richard, Duke of Gloucester · central

The youngest of the three York brothers, and the King’s brother.

After source chapter 15, paragraph 53: The youngest York brother, now proclaimed King Richard the Third.

## Queen Elizabeth · major

King Edward the Fourth’s queen, mother of the two young princes.

After source chapter 6, paragraph 18: Widow of King Edward the Fourth, and mother of the two young princes.

## The Duke of Buckingham · major

A nobleman who becomes Richard’s closest ally.

After source chapter 17, paragraph 77: Richard’s ally at court, now leaving him and making for Brecknock.

## Lady Anne · major

Widow of Edward, Prince of Wales, the son of Henry the Sixth.

After source chapter 2, paragraph 92: Widow of Edward, Prince of Wales, and now wearing Richard of Gloucester’s ring.

After source chapter 16, paragraph 20: Richard’s wife, going to be crowned his queen.

## The Duchess of York · major

Mother of King Edward, Clarence and Richard.

## Lord Hastings · major

The Lord Chamberlain, lately released from the Tower.

## Lord Stanley, Earl of Derby · major

A nobleman of the court, and Richmond’s stepfather.

After source chapter 23, paragraph 33: Richmond’s stepfather, holding his men back with his son in Richard’s hands.

## George, Duke of Clarence · major

The middle York brother, sent to the Tower as the play opens.
