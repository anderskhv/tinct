MEPHISTOPHELES. Since, Lord, you approach once again, and inquire how things are going ou with us, and on other occasions were generally not displeased to see me—there- fore is it that’ you see me also among your suite. Excuse me, I cannot talk fine, not though the whole circle should cry scorn on me. My pathos would certainly make you laugh, had you not left off laughing. I have nothing to say about suns and worlds; I only mark how men are plaguing themselves. The little god of the world continues ever of the same stamp, and is as odd as on the first day. He seems to me, with your Grace’s leave, like one of the long-legged grasshoppers, which is ever flying, and bounding as it flies, and then sings its old song in the grass ;—and would that he did but lie always in the grass! He thrusts his nose into every puddle.

The devil who bets the Lord that he can have Faust, and then finds that ruining him is harder than it looked. He calls himself the spirit that always denies, and he is the funniest thing in the play.
