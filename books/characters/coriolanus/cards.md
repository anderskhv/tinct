# Coriolanus: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Caius Martius · central

A Roman patrician and the city’s foremost soldier, hated by the plebeians for his contempt of them.

After source chapter 9, paragraph 12: Caius Martius, given the name Coriolanus by the army for taking the town of Corioles single-handed.

After source chapter 16, paragraph 61: Caius Martius Coriolanus, banished from Rome by the voices of the people.

## Menenius Agrippa · major

An old patrician, a friend to Caius Martius and well liked by the people.

## Sicinius Velutus · major

One of the two tribunes newly given to the people.

## Junius Brutus · major

The other of the two tribunes newly given to the people.

Two common names Coriolanus throws out for ordinary citizens at large, not two men of the play.

The last king of Rome, driven out before the play begins.

The Greek physician, whose authority Menenius mocks.

The Greek hero of Ithaca, whose wife Penelope is named beside him.

Ulysses’ wife, who unwove her web to put off her suitors.

## Hector · reference

The great defender of Troy.
