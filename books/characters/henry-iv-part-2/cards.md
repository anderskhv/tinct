# Henry IV Part 2: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Sir John Falstaff · central

An old knight of the Prince’s company, fat, penniless and quick.

After source chapter 19, paragraph 53: The old knight of the Prince’s company, banished from the new king’s presence.

## Prince Henry · central

King Henry’s eldest son and heir, called Harry Monmouth.

After source chapter 14, paragraph 56: King Henry the Fourth’s heir, now king in his father’s place.

## King Henry the Fourth · major

The King, worn down by rebellion and sickness.

## Justice Shallow · major

A country justice of Gloucestershire, and Falstaff’s old acquaintance.

The chief justice of the King’s Bench, who once committed the Prince.
