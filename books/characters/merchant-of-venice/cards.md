# The Merchant of Venice: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Antonio · central

The Venetian merchant of the title and Bassanio’s close friend.

## Portia · central

The wealthy heiress of Belmont whom Bassanio hopes to marry.

After source chapter 16, paragraph 2: The heiress of Belmont and Bassanio’s wife.

After source chapter 18, paragraph 43: Bassanio’s wife and the heiress of Belmont; Balthazar is her courtroom disguise.

## Shylock · central

The Jewish moneylender in Venice, Jessica’s father.

## Bassanio · major

Antonio’s friend, the Venetian gentleman courting Portia.

After source chapter 16, paragraph 2: Antonio’s friend and Portia’s husband.

## Nerissa · major

Portia’s waiting-woman and confidante.

After source chapter 16, paragraph 13: Portia’s waiting-woman and Gratiano’s wife.

After source chapter 18, paragraph 27: Portia’s waiting-woman and Gratiano’s wife; the lawyer’s clerk is her courtroom disguise.

## Gratiano · major

The outspoken friend of Antonio and Bassanio.

After source chapter 16, paragraph 13: Nerissa’s husband and the outspoken friend of Antonio and Bassanio.

## Jessica · major

Shylock’s daughter and Lorenzo’s beloved.

After source chapter 17, paragraph 7: Shylock’s daughter and Lorenzo’s wife.

## Lorenzo · major

Bassanio’s friend and Jessica’s lover.

After source chapter 17, paragraph 7: Bassanio’s friend and Jessica’s husband.
