# The Art of War reference package

Validated, awaiting integration. Revision 2026-09-10.1. Authoring-agent review, not independent editorial approval. Seven original / five modern entries, 21 / 17 exact mentions, 445 paragraphs in 13 chapters.

All entries are Reference, including Sun Tzu as the named authorial authority. Generic generals, rulers, armies, geographic regions and dynasties are not assigned personal identities. The named shuai-jan snake is retained as an illustrative creature. Every named person/reference was checked against a full-source scan, including diacritics that an ASCII-only name scan would miss.

Chu and Kuei are historical exemplars of daring; their names are omitted from the modern “courage of legends” phrase, so there are no modern cards for them. I Chih / Yi Zhi and Lü Ya / Lu Ya use edition-specific aliases. Source text unchanged. Cards remain concise, with no recaps of the surrounding military advice.

[The source translator's notes on Chu and Kuei](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3ASun_Tzu_on_The_art_of_war.djvu/184) were checked for identity. No external prose copied.

See cards.md, source-review.md and validation-report.json for all categories, first contexts and fingerprints. Verify with `python3 books/characters/build_art_of_war.py --check`; six shared treatise regression checks, total 58 content tests passing.
