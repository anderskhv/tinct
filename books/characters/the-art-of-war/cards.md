# The Art of War: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Sun Tzu · reference

The military thinker traditionally credited with The Art of War.

## The Yellow Emperor · reference

Huangdi, a legendary ruler of ancient China.

## Chu · reference

Chuan Chu, also called Zhuan Zhu: a figure from ancient Wu renowned for daring.

## Kuei · reference

Ts’ao Kuei, also called Cao Gui: an ancient warrior cited as an example of courage.

## I Chih / Yi Zhi · reference

An adviser associated with the rise of the Yin dynasty, also known as Yi Yin.

## Lü Ya / Lu Ya · reference

The adviser also known as Jiang Ziya, associated with the rise of the Zhou dynasty.

## The shuai-jan · reference

The snake used to illustrate coordinated defense: striking one part brings the others to its aid.
