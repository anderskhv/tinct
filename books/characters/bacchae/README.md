# The Bacchae recognition cards

All 11 local sections, 336 paragraphs in each English edition. Forty-two entries in each edition. Authoring-agent source review, not independent editorial approval. Source editions unchanged; runtime integration pending.

Dionysus and Pentheus are Central. Cadmus, Agave, the Asian chorus and the Theban Bacchants are Major. Teiresias, the chorus leader, the two other sisters, the two messengers and the officer are Supporting. Named genealogical and mythological references are Reference.

The opening speech openly identifies Dionysus, his parents and his human appearance. Accordingly THE VOICE, the Stranger, Dionyse, Bromios/Bromius, Bacchios/Bacchus and Iacchos/Iacchus resolve to one known god. His identity is not withheld merely because Pentheus fails to recognize him. Agave is immediately Pentheus's mother. None of the cards retells Pentheus's fate, Agave's recognition scene or the final prophecy.

## Binding review

- The herdsman messenger in section 7 is distinct from the servant who accompanies Pentheus to the mountain, entering at the end of section 10 and speaking in section 11. Their shared MESSENGER labels are scoped by section.
- The Asian chorus and its spokeswoman have separate selectable identities. The Theban women are a different group, identified by explicit contextual phrases. Generic Bacchant words are not indiscriminately assigned to either group.
- The officer is separate from the collective guards. The man who proposes capturing Agave is distinct from the herdsman narrator. Actaeon's father is a separate reference; the complete father phrase takes precedence over the embedded son's name.
- Dionysus's lower-case stranger alias is restricted to reviewed passages. The original's “stranger part” in 7:20 is an adjective, not a person.
- Dirce is bound as a divine reference only where the ode addresses her as Achelous's daughter (section 6). The prologue's geographical waters, Ismenus, Asopus, Axios and Lydias are not treated as people. The original adjective Dian does not create an invented reference to Diana.
- Named personifications are limited to explicit invocations of Purity, Peace, Justice and the earthquake spirit. Generic God, Lord, mother and king words do not receive blanket identity assignments.
- The inherited stage-direction overlap between sections and the explicit missing-manuscript notice near the ending are preserved. No absent passage has been reconstructed.

The local Murray translation and modernization govern all exact spans.
