# Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Christian Wolff · reference

The German philosopher whose general practical philosophy Kant distinguishes from his own project.

## Socrates · reference

The ancient Athenian philosopher known for drawing out people’s understanding through questioning.

## Jesus Christ · reference

The central figure of Christianity, here called the Holy One of the Gospels.

## God · reference

The divine being considered in Kant’s discussion of moral perfection and the will.

## Johann Georg Sulzer · reference

The Swiss philosopher who corresponded with Kant about moral education.

## Juno · reference

The Roman goddess invoked in the mythical image of mistaking a cloud for a goddess.

## Francis Hutcheson · reference

The philosopher associated with the moral-sense theory Kant discusses.
