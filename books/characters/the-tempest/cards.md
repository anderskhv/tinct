# The Tempest: recognition cards

Editorial review copy; source excerpts and later identities may contain spoilers.

## Prospero · central

Miranda’s father, a magician living with her on the island.

After source chapter 2, paragraph 17: Miranda’s father, a magician who was once Duke of Milan.

After source chapter 9, paragraph 22: Miranda’s father, the magician restored to his dukedom in Milan.

## Miranda · central

Prospero’s daughter, brought up with him on the island.

After source chapter 5, paragraph 25: Prospero’s daughter, now pledged to marry Ferdinand.

## Ariel · major

The spirit who serves Prospero and carries out his magic.

## Caliban · major

Sycorax’s son, an island inhabitant whom Prospero keeps in servitude.

## Ferdinand · major

The prince of Naples, son of King Alonso.

After source chapter 5, paragraph 25: Alonso’s son, the prince of Naples, now pledged to marry Miranda.

## Alonso · major

King of Naples and father of Ferdinand and Claribel.

## Antonio · major

The Duke of Milan, traveling with King Alonso.

After source chapter 2, paragraph 23: Prospero’s brother and Miranda’s uncle, now holding the dukedom of Milan.

After source chapter 9, paragraph 29: Prospero’s brother and Miranda’s uncle, the former Duke of Milan.

## Sebastian · supporting

King Alonso’s brother.

## Gonzalo · supporting

An elderly counselor traveling with King Alonso.

## Adrian · supporting

A lord in King Alonso’s traveling party.

## Francisco · supporting

A lord in King Alonso’s traveling party.

## Trinculo · major

King Alonso’s jester.

## Stephano · major

King Alonso’s butler.

## The boatswain · supporting

The ship’s officer directing the sailors during the storm.

## The ship’s master · supporting

The captain of the ship carrying King Alonso’s party.

## The mariners · supporting

The crew of the ship carrying King Alonso’s party.

## Sycorax · reference

Caliban’s mother, a witch who lived on the island before Prospero.

## Claribel · reference

Alonso’s daughter and Ferdinand’s sister, newly married to the King of Tunis.

## The King of Tunis · reference

Claribel’s husband and ruler of Tunis.

## Antonio’s son · reference

The unnamed son of Antonio, mentioned among the ship’s passengers.

## Miranda’s mother · reference

Prospero’s wife and Miranda’s mother. She is not named in the play.

## Jove · reference

The Roman sky and thunder god, also called Jupiter.

## Neptune · reference

The Roman god of the sea.

## Setebos · reference

The god worshipped by Sycorax and invoked by Caliban.

## Dido · reference

The legendary queen of Carthage, associated with Aeneas in Virgil’s Aeneid.

## Aeneas · reference

The Trojan hero of Virgil’s Aeneid, associated with Dido at Carthage.

## Hymen · reference

The classical god of marriage, whose torch represents the wedding ceremony.

## Phoebus · reference

A name for Apollo as the sun god, imagined driving a chariot across the sky.

## Venus · reference

The Roman goddess of love and mother of Cupid.

## Cupid · reference

The winged god of love, Venus’s son.

## Dis · reference

The Roman ruler of the underworld, also called Pluto.

## Mars · reference

The Roman god of war, associated with Venus as her lover.

## Iris · supporting

The rainbow goddess and messenger of Juno, portrayed in Prospero’s masque.

## Ceres · supporting

The goddess of agriculture, portrayed in Prospero’s masque.

## Juno · supporting

The queen of the gods and goddess of marriage, portrayed in Prospero’s masque.

## The nymphs · reference

The water spirits, or Naiads, represented by dancers in Prospero’s masque.

## The reapers · reference

The harvest workers represented by dancers in Prospero’s masque.

## The banquet figures · supporting

The strange figures who bring in and remove the banquet.

## The spirit hounds · supporting

The spirits appearing as hunting dogs under Prospero and Ariel’s direction.

## Mountain · reference

One of the spirit hounds called by name during the chase.

## Silver · reference

One of the spirit hounds called by name during the chase.

## Fury · reference

One of the spirit hounds called by name during the chase.

## Tyrant · reference

One of the spirit hounds called by name during the chase.

## Mall / Moll · reference

One of the women named in Stephano’s sailors’ song.

## Meg · reference

One of the women named in Stephano’s sailors’ song.

## Marian · reference

One of the women named in Stephano’s sailors’ song.

## Margery · reference

One of the women named in Stephano’s sailors’ song.

## Kate · reference

One of the women named in Stephano’s sailors’ song.

## Temperance · reference

A woman’s name in Antonio’s joke about the word “temperance”; no further identity is given.

## Nobody · reference

The comic figure of “Nobody,” invoked when Trinculo hears music without seeing a musician.

## Fortune · reference

Good or bad luck imagined as a female power.

## Fate · reference

Destiny imagined as a power governing human lives.
