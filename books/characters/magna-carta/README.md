# Magna Carta: historical reference cards

Whole charter in both English editions, 51 entries each. All categories are Reference: the document names rulers, advisers, officials and relatives; it does not narrate a protagonist’s experience. John’s card identifies the issuer directly. Repeated names are distinguished by office or family, not by a vague location in the text.

Authoring-agent source review, not independent editorial approval. Sources remain unchanged. No subsequent annulment, civil war or later reissue is added to the recognition cards. No legal advice is being generated.

Every named opening adviser is retained. Titles separate six Williams, the ecclesiastical Henry from King Henry, and the different Peters. The coordinated clause-50 list has separate Peter, Guy and Andrew de Chanceaux entries, and Geoffrey the nephew is not Geoffrey de Martigny. The text’s family linkage for the nephew is not expanded into an asserted genealogy. The barons’ group is distinct from generic occurrences of barons elsewhere. Unnamed siblings and hostages stay identifiable by their actual relationship, without invented personal names.

Compared the historical list with [The National Archives transcription](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/education/resources/magna-carta/british-library-magna-carta-1215-runnymede/) and [the Magna Carta Project’s clause 50](https://magnacarta.cmp.uea.ac.uk/read/magna_carta_1215/Clause_50?com=%21none). The local clause-50 list has inherited punctuation/order irregularities; cards identify the printed people without interpreting that punctuation as new kinship evidence. The original also truncates Athée/Cigogné to Ath/Cigogn; these aliases retain the same identity.

Run author_content.py then build_magna_carta.py to regenerate; use --check for freshness. The book tests verify exact source spans, repeated names, separate family groups, source fingerprints and reference-only categories. Before enabling, recheck source bytes against the shipping editions, explicitly register both pairs, version the asset URL and verify the opening Williams and clause-50 Peters/Geoffreys on production. No runtime/deploy changes are part of this package.
