EPISODE 14 IS THE FIRST EPISODE WHERE ALMOST NONE OF THE NEW CARDS IS A NEW MAN. Joyce marches the same eight men through the history of English prose and renames them in every pastiche: sir Leopold and young Stephen in the Malory, Leop. Bloom and Stephen D. in the Pepys, Mr Canvasser Bloom and Mr Coadjutor Deacon Dedalus in the parliamentary report, Mr L. Bloom (Pubb. Canv.) and Mr S. Dedalus (Div. Scep.) in the scientific one, and Mr Cautious Calmer and Young Boasthard in the Bunyan. THE WORK WAS NOT FINDING PEOPLE, IT WAS DECIDING WHICH DISGUISE IS A CARD AND WHICH IS A COSTUME. The rule applied: a name Joyce writes as a NAME gets a card (Le Fecondateur, Glory Allelujurum, Young Boasthard); a pastiche title that is only an office (Mr Canvasser Bloom, Mr Delegate Madden) is bound to the man.
