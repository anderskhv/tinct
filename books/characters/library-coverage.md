THE FINDING THE RELEASE OWNER MOST NEEDS, and it is unlike any other book in this library: THE MODERN EDITION OF ULYSSES IS A REWRITE, NOT A NORMALISATION, AND IT CHANGES THE CAST OF A PARAGRAPH. It prints Swinburne where Joyce wrote Algy (1:35) and William where he wrote Billy (1:268); it names Stephen where Joyce wrote 'poor dogsbody' (1:47) and Mulligan where he wrote 'him' (1:13), adding mentions; it splits Joyce's compounds so that 'pseudomalachi' (9:194) and 'Sonmulligan' (9:331) become bindable names; and it DELETES three allusions outright -- Chrysostomos (1:9), Mercury's hat (1:293) and 'Chuck Loyola' (1:101). Chrysostomos and Mercury are therefore omittedEntities for modern-en with the reason on the card: a reader of that edition is never offered those two cards. Expect more of this in every episode and check both editions before writing any card that turns on a nickname.

THE DISCIPLINE THAT MATTERS MOST HERE, carried from the Montaigne package: LIST EVERY OCCURRENCE OF A NAME ACROSS ALL 18 EPISODES BEFORE CHOOSING BETWEEN AN ALIAS AND A KEY. In the episode-1 pass alone it found five bindings that would have been wrong in episodes nobody had read: Stephen at 17:39 is 16 Stephen's Green, the Dublin square, and at 15:407 is Saint Stephen's day in the wren-boys' song; Mulligan at 10:349 is John Mulligan, manager of the Hibernian bank; Malachi at 12:50 is the ardri Malachi among the Irish heroes and at 12:513 is Saint Malachy walking with Saint Patrick. All five are SUPPRESS entries in build_ulysses.py and each is pinned by a test. Do not undo them.

A SURNAME IN THIS BOOK IS ALMOST NEVER ONE MAN, AND AFTER TWO EPISODES THAT IS A MEASUREMENT AND NOT A CAUTION: of the twelve surnames episode 2 introduces, TWELVE belong to somebody or something else elsewhere in the book (Cochrane is Cantrell and Cochrane's ginger ale at 5:65, Talbot is lord Talbot de Malahide at 10:43, Halliday is Jacob Halliday the vintner at 12:312, Curran is John Philpot Curran at 7:380 and Sara Curran at 12:172, Temple is Temple bar at 10:235, Blake is Phil Blake's Pat and Bull story at 7:42, Price is Henry Price the basket manufacturer at 17:587, and Gerty and Lily are other women in six later episodes). The working rule for this book is therefore the OPPOSITE of the rule in every other package in this library: KEY BY DEFAULT, ALIAS ONLY ON PROOF.  Dedalus is Stephen in episode 1, his father Simon from episode 6 and his sisters in episode 10, so it is keyed with seven episode-1 keys and a None default; Simon must be a second entity, never a default. Bloom, who arrives in episode 4, will be five people: Leopold, Molly, Milly, Rudolph and Rudy. Nothing in that family should be aliased until all five are separated. Every table in the builder has a None default so that an unread episode gets no card rather than the wrong one, and a test enforces it.

NINE PEOPLE IN EPISODE 1 ARE NAMED ONLY BY A DESCRIPTION and are bound on the phrase the text uses, through the PHRASE table: Mulligan's aunt, his mother, his brother, the milkwoman, the young man and the elderly man at the fortyfoot hole, the boatman and the businessman on the cliff, and the man who was drowned. Stephen's mother is a tenth and the one the episode turns on: she is never named in it. A test pins each, and a second test guards the opposite failure -- a key or a phrase pattern that matches nothing, which is how a card goes unreachable without any other check noticing.

ONE MORE MECHANICAL TRAP, and it costs half a book's mentions when missed: the older edition sets O'Connell and O'Rourke with a CURLY apostrophe and the modern edition with a STRAIGHT one, so a table pattern must spell both (O['\u2019']Connell). A test pins both forms. NEXT, AND THIS LANE DOES NOT DO IT: release review and app integration. This lane never sets appStatus, which stays not-integrated. Two decisions belong to the release owner and are written up under 'What is left, and what was learned' in the README. FIRST, the eighteen citation abbreviations: the older edition writes Hor., Claud., Quintil., Lucret., Propert., Liv., Cic., Mart. and Aug. where the modern edition expands the author's name, so those mentions bind in the modern edition alone. Closing them means adding each abbreviation as an alias on nine authors; it was not done because an abbreviation is not a name, and the release owner may decide the other way. SECOND, Fortune: she is deliberately not cast, with the Christian God, Christ, Satan and Nature, although chapter 33 personifies her throughout and chapter 69 again. That is the one scope decision in the package a reader might disagree with.

THIRTEEN SOURCE DEFECTS are recorded for the release owner and were not repaired; no edition byte was touched. They are listed in full in the README under 'Source defects -- recorded, not repaired', and they fall into four kinds: a LACUNA at 90:7 where the older edition's sentence is broken and names nobody; MISPRINTS carried as aliases exactly as printed (Lactantms, Zenocrates, Metellius, Origeti, Titius Livius, EPICUYUS TO HEYMACHUS, Diogeries, Thamus, Euxodus, Critolaiis, Perrete, Aulus Gelliusor, and the corrupt Latin of 100:46 and 100:43 which both editions share); STRUCTURAL defects, where a note has landed in the wrong place (102:1 belongs to 101:13, one chapter earlier) or a sentence runs across a note (69:468/469, 101:7/101:9); and one MISTRANSLATION, at 74:120, where the older edition renders Acts i. 26 as 'The lot fell upon Matthew' and the text has Matthias. Two further divergences are NOT defects and must not be 'fixed': the modern edition drops 'says Seneca' from 70:35 and writes 'Plato's ring' at 73:64 where the older has the adjective 'the Platonic ring'.

THE EIGHT LESSONS THIS BOOK TAUGHT, in the order of how much each one caught, are written up in the README for whoever authors the next book. The first is the one to carry: LIST EVERY OCCURRENCE OF A NAME ACROSS THE WHOLE WORK BEFORE CHOOSING BETWEEN AN ALIAS AND A KEY. Chapter 69, the Apology, is the chapter this package corrected most often after declaring it done. Do not read a clean audit as a finished chapter. |
