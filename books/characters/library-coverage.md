They are listed in full in the README under 'Source defects -- recorded, not repaired', and they fall into four kinds: a LACUNA at 90:7 where the older edition's sentence is broken and names nobody; MISPRINTS carried as aliases exactly as printed (Lactantms, Zenocrates, Metellius, Origeti, Titius Livius, EPICUYUS TO HEYMACHUS, Diogeries, Thamus, Euxodus, Critolaiis, Perrete, Aulus Gelliusor, and the corrupt Latin of 100:46 and 100:43 which both editions share); STRUCTURAL defects, where a note has landed in the wrong place (102:1 belongs to 101:13, one chapter earlier) or a sentence runs across a note (69:468/469, 101:7/101:9); and one MISTRANSLATION, at 74:120, where the older edition renders Acts i. 26 as 'The lot fell upon Matthew' and the text has Matthias.
