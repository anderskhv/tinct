NEXT, AND THIS LANE DOES NOT DO IT: release review and app integration. This lane never sets appStatus, which stays not-integrated. Two decisions belong to the release owner and are written up under 'What is left, and what was learned' in the README. FIRST, the eighteen citation abbreviations: the older edition writes Hor., Claud., Quintil., Lucret., Propert., Liv., Cic., Mart. and Aug. where the modern edition expands the author's name, so those mentions bind in the modern edition alone. Closing them means adding each abbreviation as an alias on nine authors; it was not done because an abbreviation is not a name, and the release owner may decide the other way. SECOND, Fortune: she is deliberately not cast, with the Christian God, Christ, Satan and Nature, although chapter 33 personifies her throughout and chapter 69 again. That is the one scope decision in the package a reader might disagree with.

THIRTEEN SOURCE DEFECTS are recorded for the release owner and were not repaired; no edition byte was touched. They are listed in full in the README under 'Source defects -- recorded, not repaired', and they fall into four kinds: a LACUNA at 90:7 where the older edition's sentence is broken and names nobody; MISPRINTS carried as aliases exactly as printed (Lactantms, Zenocrates, Metellius, Origeti, Titius Livius, EPICUYUS TO HEYMACHUS, Diogeries, Thamus, Euxodus, Critolaiis, Perrete, Aulus Gelliusor, and the corrupt Latin of 100:46 and 100:43 which both editions share); STRUCTURAL defects, where a note has landed in the wrong place (102:1 belongs to 101:13, one chapter earlier) or a sentence runs across a note (69:468/469, 101:7/101:9); and one MISTRANSLATION, at 74:120, where the older edition renders Acts i. 26 as 'The lot fell upon Matthew' and the text has Matthias. Two further divergences are NOT defects and must not be 'fixed': the modern edition drops 'says Seneca' from 70:35 and writes 'Plato's ring' at 73:64 where the older has the adjective 'the Platonic ring'.

THE EIGHT LESSONS THIS BOOK TAUGHT, in the order of how much each one caught, are written up in the README for whoever authors the next book. The first is the one to carry: LIST EVERY OCCURRENCE OF A NAME ACROSS THE WHOLE WORK BEFORE CHOOSING BETWEEN AN ALIAS AND A KEY. Chapter 69, the Apology, is the chapter this package corrected most often after declaring it done. Do not read a clean audit as a finished chapter. |
