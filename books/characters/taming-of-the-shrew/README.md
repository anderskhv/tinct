Disguised addressees and actual people are source-scoped. Within 1:62, 1:71, 4:12 and 11:52 the same name Lucentio changes referent. The 9:11 stage entrance names the actual Lucentio, while the subsequent marriage negotiation calls Tranio Lucentio. At 11:51 both the Pedant and Tranio claim their false names. References to the real Vincentio as a model remain distinct from the impersonator. Plans for an as-yet-unselected false father at 3:168 and 5:48 do not prematurely introduce the Pedant. The insult pedant in scene 4 means Lucentio, not the traveler first spotted at 7:31.

The framing FIRST SERVANT and Petruchio's FIRST SERVANT are separate, as are the two weddings' priests. All eleven named household servants are retained. Ferdinand is Petruchio's cousin; Troilus his spaniel, not the Trojan hero. Pegasus names an inn and Saint Luke's a church; neither is made into a mythical/religious person card. Priami binds Priam across italics; Æacides is Ajax in this explicit lesson. Europa, Helen and Aeacus are identified at daughter/grandfather references. Grissel/Griselda and Dian/Diana share identities across editions.
