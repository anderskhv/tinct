## Notes on the Source Text

- Uses 1808 orthography: `Studirzimmer` (not Studierzimmer), `Spazirgang` (not Spaziergang), `Vor dem Thor` (not Tor), `Straße` already modern
- `_italic_` markup used throughout for stage directions, speaker names, scene headings, and emphasis — stripped during parsing
- Contains `[Lied]` editorial correction: "Mein Leid[Lied] ertönt der unbekannten Menge" (line 4 of Zueignung)
- Typo in source: `Mehpistopheles` (transposed letters) in one scene heading — preserved in parser pattern, normalized in output
- Chapter titles in the edition JSON use modern spelling (Studierzimmer, Spaziergang, Vor dem Thor preserved as historical)
