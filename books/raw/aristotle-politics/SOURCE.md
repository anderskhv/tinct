## Translator

## Notes on source quality
- Books II–VIII use OCR text from the 1905 reprint. The OCR has been cleaned of:
  - Bekker marginal numbers (e.g., "1252 a", "1261 b")
  - Running page headers (e.g., "Growth of the State", "Sovereignty of the People")
  - Footnote markers (^, *) and footnote text
  - Translator's apparatus (Greek text, bibliographic cross-references)
  - H. W. C. Davis's editorial notes
- One page (Book IV, around Bekker 1297a) has a damaged/stained area in the original scan, causing approximately one sentence fragment to be lost.
- 8 paragraph breaks fall mid-sentence due to OCR page-break structure; these are artifacts of the scan, not of Jowett's text.
