**Edition:** English edition of 1888  
**Source:** Project Gutenberg eBook #61

## Notes

- This is the canonical Samuel Moore 1888 English translation, edited and annotated by Friedrich Engels. Fully public domain.
- The Gutenberg file does NOT include Engels' separate 1888 Preface text as a standalone section — it contains the Manifesto's own preamble ("A spectre is haunting Europe...") immediately before Part I.
- The preamble (5 paragraphs) has been parsed as Chapter 1 ("Preamble") following the approved structure, representing the opening statement of the Manifesto before the four main sections.
