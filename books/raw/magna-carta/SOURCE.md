## Version used

The file PG #10000 contains multiple historical versions of the Magna Carta. The parsed edition uses the **first version** in the file, titled "The Text of Magna Carta", which presents the 1215 King John charter in modern English translation.

- Starts with: "JOHN, by the grace of God King of England, Lord of Ireland..."
- Ends with: "Given by our hand in the meadow that is called Runnymede, between Windsor and Staines, on the fifteenth day of June in the seventeenth year of our reign."
- Clauses numbered as (1)–(49) in parenthetical format, followed by unnumbered enforcement sub-clauses

The second version in the file ("Magna Carta 1215", starting ~line 537) was excluded — it is a different translation with a different clause numbering system.

## Structure

- 1 chapter ("Magna Carta")
- 74 paragraphs:
  - Para 0: Preamble (King John's address + witness list)
  - Paras 1–50: Clauses 1–49 (parenthetical numbering) + mid-preamble grant statement
  - Paras 51–60: Unnumbered enforcement/security sub-clauses (25-barons security mechanism, Welsh/Scottish provisions)
  - Paras 61–73: Closing paragraphs (security mechanism, remission of ill-will, royal wish and command, Runnymede date)
