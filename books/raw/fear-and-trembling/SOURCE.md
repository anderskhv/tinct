## OCR Notes

This is a Google Books OCR scan of a physical book. Known artefacts in the raw OCR:
- Page-break hyphenation: fixed in parse (e.g. "Abra- \nham" → "Abraham")
- Running headers ("Frygt og Bæven. 3. Udg.") appearing between paragraphs: stripped
- Page numbers appearing as isolated lines: stripped
- Roman numeral section markers in Stemning chapter garbled by OCR (e.g. "II" → "sk & ke", "IV" → "4 x 4"): filtered as garbage
- Footnote reference lines (Cfr. citations, Greek text OCR garbage): filtered

## Structure

Flat 8-chapter structure:
1. Forord (Preface)
2. Stemning (Exordium / Tuning)
3. Lovtale over Abraham (Eulogy on Abraham)
4. Foreløbig Expectoration (Preliminary Expectoration)
5. Problema I — Gives der en teleologisk Suspension af det Ethiske?
6. Problema II — Gives der en absolut Pligt mod Gud?
7. Problema III — Var det ethisk forsvarligt af Abraham, at han fortiede sit Forehavende for Sara, for Elieser, for Isaak?
8. Epilog
