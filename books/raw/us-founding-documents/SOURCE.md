# Source: US Founding Documents

## Composition
This Tinct edition combines four founding texts into a single book with sections:

1. Declaration of Independence (1776)
2. Constitution of the United States (1787)
3. Bill of Rights (1791) — first ten Amendments
4. Later Amendments (1795–1992) — Amendments XI through XXVII

## Parse Notes
- Project Gutenberg editor preamble paragraphs (about ALL-CAPS conversion etc.) stripped from Constitution chapter
- Signature blocks stripped from Constitution and Declaration (preserve textual content, not signing roster — signing dignitaries are part of the historical record but aren't part of the legal text)
- Each amendment is one paragraph with section labels embedded inline (e.g., "Amendment XIV. Section 1. … Section 2. …")
- Roman numeral amendment names preserved (Amendment I through XXVII)
- 4 chapters, 153 paragraphs total

## Section Structure
- Section "Declaration of Independence (1776)" → ch1 (33 paragraphs)
- Section "Constitution (1787)" → ch2 (93 paragraphs — preamble + 7 articles + signing statement)
- Section "Bill of Rights (1791)" → ch3 (10 amendments as 10 paragraphs)
- Section "Later Amendments (1795–1992)" → ch4 (17 amendments as 17 paragraphs)
