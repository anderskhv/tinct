## Notes

- The editor's Introduction by W. L. Courtney (LL.D.) was stripped — it is not
  Mill's own text.
- The dedication to Harriet Taylor Mill is included as the first paragraph of
  Chapter I (it is a canonical part of the published book, not a preface).
- The Humboldt epigraph ("The grand, leading principle...") is included as the
  second paragraph of Chapter I.
- All footnotes stripped for readability. Footnotes 11–12 were bibliographic
  citations (Humboldt, Sterling); footnote 13 was a substantive aside on
  legal incapacity declarations.
- Gutenberg italic markers `_text_` converted to plain text.
- Chapter structure: 5 chapters as Mill divided them.
