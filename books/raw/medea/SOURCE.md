## Parsing notes
- Parsed into 7 chapters following classical Greek dramatic structure
- Chapter divisions: Prologue, Parodos, First Episode, Second Episode, Third Episode, Fourth Episode, Exodos
- One paragraph per speaker turn
- Stage directions appended to the preceding speaker's paragraph
- Murray's `* * * * *` section separators stripped (they were scene/stanza separators, not structural divisions)
- Translator's Introduction, Notes, and Transcriber's Note stripped
- 7 chapters, 241 total paragraphs
- Original text preserves straight apostrophes from the Gutenberg source
