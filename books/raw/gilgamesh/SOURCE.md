# Source: Epic of Gilgamesh

**URL:** https://www.jasoncolavito.com/epic-of-gilgamesh.html

**Translator/Adapter:** Jason Colavito (modernized adaptation based on multiple public domain translations)

**Base translations used:**
- William Muss-Arnolt (1901) — primary source, from "Assyrian and Babylonian Literature: Selected Translations"
- R. Campbell Thompson (1930) — "The Epic of Gilgamish"
- George Smith (1876) — "The Chaldean Account of Genesis"
- Morris Jastrow (1915) — "The Civilization of Babylonia and Assyria"
- Peter Jensen (1900) — "Assyrisch-babylonische Mythen und Epen"

**Note from adapter:** "My version reflects the latest scholarship, including the 2003 edition of Andrew George. [...] This version is meant primarily as a reading copy and should not be mistaken for scholarly."

**Public domain status:** The underlying translations (Muss-Arnolt, Thompson, Smith, Jastrow, Jensen) are all pre-1928 and in the public domain. Colavito's adaptation is freely published on his website.

**Downloaded:** 2026-03-31

**Structure:** 12 tablets (standard Akkadian version), parsed as 12 chapters with 253 paragraphs total.
