## What was extracted

Only Rousseau's "The Social Contract" was extracted from this file. The file also contains:
- Cole's Introduction (long scholarly preface) — excluded
- A Discourse on the Arts and Sciences — excluded
- A Discourse on the Origin of Inequality — excluded
- A Discourse on Political Economy — excluded

Rousseau's brief FOREWORD ("This little treatise is part of a longer work...") was identified and the text following it (Book I preamble + 4 books) was parsed.

## Public domain status

This work is in the public domain in the United States and most other parts of the world. Rousseau died in 1778; Cole died in 1959. The 1913 Cole translation is public domain in the US (published before 1928). Verify local copyright laws before use in other jurisdictions.
