# Rights audit — English texts in the library (Claude, 2026-10-01)

Anders operates Tinct from Denmark and wants no illegal content. Danish/EU law: life + 70 years (Danish Copyright Act §63; Directive 2006/116/EC), counted from 1 January after death, applying to authors AND translators. A text can be public domain in the US and still protected in Denmark/EU. This is research, not legal advice. Translator death years marked "memory" were not verified against a source.

## Update after verification research (2026-10-01, later)
Resolved from the UNKNOWN list (verified against Gutenberg texts and library/Wikipedia records):
- CLEAR: the-prince (Marriott d.1927), nicomachean-ethics (D. P. Chase d.1902), the-manual (George Long d.1879), imitation-of-christ (Benham d.1910), fear-and-trembling (Danish 1895 Reitzel text, Kierkegaard d.1855, no editor apparatus; registry "1980" is wrong), second-treatise (Locke's own text; low residual risk about Macpherson's light normalisation).
- STILL OPEN: **magna-carta** — the text is G. R. C. Davis's translation (British Museum 1963, rev. British Library 1989, "Copyright 1995, The British Library Board"), not an anonymous one. Treat as PROTECTED/UNCLEARED. Replacement candidate: McKechnie 1905 (d.1930) — not yet checked in full. The Magna Carta modern-en repair is a derivative and must NOT ship.
- STILL OPEN: **candide** — the text is the anonymous Modern Library 1918 translation (PG #19942), translator unidentified (registry attribution "Fleming 1901" is wrong). Replace with a text of known provenance (e.g. Smollett 1761 or the 1759 anonymous translation) or identify the translator.

Replacement translations found (all verified by research agents, to be built as new editions):

- bacchae: Arthur S. Way 1898 (d.1930); fallbacks Coleridge, Buckley
- the-art-of-war: E. F. Calthrop 1908 "The Book of War" (d.1915, PG #44024); no second candidate
- gilgamesh: R. Campbell Thompson 1928 (d.1941) + Muss-Arnolt (d.1927); Heidel (d.1955; free in the EU since 1 Jan 2026) only if Anders accepts a 1955 death year

- discourse-on-inequality: anonymous 1761 Dodsley translation (IA discourseuponor00rous); PG #11136 is the same text, incomplete
