# Rights audit — English texts in the library (Claude, 2026-10-01)

Anders operates Tinct from Denmark and wants no illegal content. Danish/EU law: life + 70 years (Danish Copyright Act §63; Directive 2006/116/EC), counted from 1 January after death, applying to authors AND translators. A text can be public domain in the US and still protected in Denmark/EU. This is research, not legal advice. Translator death years marked "memory" were not verified against a source.
