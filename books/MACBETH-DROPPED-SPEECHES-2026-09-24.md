# Macbeth — dropped speeches (content repair, not started)

Found 2026-09-24 by the edition-alignment pilot (`books/align/`). **Status:
needs Anders' decision on structure before any edit; publication is Codex's.**

## Problem

The served `app/public/data/editions/macbeth-original-en.json` is missing 13
passages of 20+ words (about 1,100 words, ~7% of the play) that are present in
the Gutenberg source `books/raw/macbeth/raw.txt` (#1533). Every one is a speech
that **continues after a stage direction without the speaker's name being
repeated**, so the parser dropped it. They include "Is this a dagger which I
see before me" (239 words), "The raven himself is hoarse" (129), "To be thus is
nothing" (206) and "Glamis thou art, and Cawdor" (134).

`macbeth-modern-en.json` is missing the same passages, except the Glamis
soliloquy, which it appends to the letter in chapter 5 ¶1 (so the two editions
disagree in that paragraph today).

Checked with the same method: Hamlet, A Midsummer Night's Dream, Romeo and
Juliet and The Tempest have no dropped passages. Bacchae and Oresteia differ only
by editorial notes and cast lists, which are meant to be left out.

## Proposed repair

1. **Glamis soliloquy (chapter 5 ¶1):** append to the letter paragraph in
   original-en. modern-en already contains it there, so no new paragraph and no
   index shift.
2. **The other 12:** insert a new paragraph `SPEAKER. <speech>` immediately
   after the stage direction named above, in **both** editions, with a new
   modern-en rendering (about 1,000 words, written in-session, zero API spend,
   `books/CLAUDE.md` translation rules).
3. Re-derive with the parser fix, not by hand, and re-run the source-vs-served
   word check until it reports only headings and apparatus.

## Impact (why this needs a decision)

New paragraphs shift paragraph indices in chapters 4, 5, 8, 10, 12, 13, 18, 21
and 23. Anything keyed by paragraph index there moves: stored reading
positions, highlights and notes, character-card packages, audio word-highlight
sidecars, `macbeth-lines.json` (regenerate with
`scripts/build-play-verse-lines.py`), threads and the alignment file (regenerate;
its model overrides for affected chapters need re-review). Per
`docs/workflow-boundaries.md`, Claude prepares the accepted text with hashes and
changed coordinates; Codex publishes with character-card compatibility.

Alternative with no index shift: attach each continuation to the *next*
paragraph instead of inserting new ones. Rejected: it would put speeches under
the wrong stage direction and speaker.
