# Fear and Trembling (Kierkegaard) — modern-English workflow

Saved here so future tasks find the process without conversation history.
Adapted from the Meditations package's process template
(`books/staged-replacements/meditations/WORKFLOW.md` on branch
`claude/meditations-modern-en-20260911-v2`), which is itself derived from the
Odyssey Book 10 pilot. Decided by Anders on 2026-09-11 (see `DECISIONS.md`
row for 2026-09-11 listing the five-book modern-English scope: Odyssey,
Hamlet, Macbeth, Fear and Trembling, Meditations); relayed to this task by
the coordinator session.

## Scope

- **Content only.** This package owns Fear and Trembling only. Nothing here
  is merged, deployed, or registered. No changes to About, landing pages, app
  code (`app/src/**`), library UI, registry entries, character cards, or
  audio. Never overwrite the served files under `app/public/data/editions/`;
  staged replacements live here, and only here, under
  `books/staged-replacements/fear-and-trembling/`.
- **Other books are other agents' work.** The Odyssey, Hamlet, Macbeth, and
  Meditations have their own agents/threads.
- **Zero Anthropic API spend.** All drafting happens in the agent
  conversation and is written to files. `generate-editions.cjs` is never run.
  This also disqualifies Tinct's own existing served `modern-en` (itself
  generated by an earlier, unreviewed API run) as a drafting source — see
  `PROVENANCE.md` §1.
- **Paragraph alignment is a hard constraint.** The app's editions are
  paragraph-aligned; audio, Cast, and saved reading positions key on
  paragraph index. A staged modern edition keeps the paragraph count and
  order of whichever served file it is aligned to, exactly: one candidate
  paragraph per source paragraph. Here that served file is `original-da`
  (`PROVENANCE.md` §2), and it is paragraph-identical to `original-en`
  (`PROVENANCE.md` §3), so aligning to the Danish carries no split-view
  consequence.
- **This book has an unusual Step 1 finding.** The served `original-en` is
  not a real historical public-domain translation; it is a 2026 AI paraphrase
  written in a "Lowrie/Hannay-adjacent register" per its own commit message,
  with at least one passage showing word-level closeness to the copyrighted
  Hannay translation. Rights verdict: UNCERTAIN. Per instructions, the
  candidate is drafted from the public-domain Danish original instead. See
  `PROVENANCE.md` for full evidence; this is a decision Anders needs to see,
  not one resolved by this package.

## The eight steps (per chapter)

1. **Verify the source** and assess reuse rights. For this book that means:
   identify what the served `original-en` actually is (translator, date,
   provenance), and whether the Danish and English served files are
   paragraph-aligned. Done once, at the book level, in `PROVENANCE.md` — not
   repeated per chapter, since the verdict and the source (Danish) are fixed
   for the whole book.
2. **Draft with full chapter context.** Read the whole chapter before
   drafting any paragraph. Fix stable renderings for recurring concepts first
   (`GLOSSARY.md`) — for this book, several core terms (the knight of faith,
   infinite resignation, the teleological suspension of the ethical, the
   absurd, the single individual) are fixed in advance from general knowledge
   of the work, before the chapters that introduce them are drafted, so nine
   later chapters don't each improvise a rendering; a row's Danish/English
   pairing is confirmed (and extended if needed) at the chapter that actually
   introduces the term.
3. **Freeze the draft.** Write `chNN/candidate-v1.json` and the readable
   copy, record hashes in `chNN/provenance.json`, and do not edit
   `candidate-v1.json` afterwards. Corrections go to `candidate-v2.json`.
4. **Independent review, three paragraphs at a time, with neighbouring
   context.** Prepare `chNN/review-packets/packet-NN.md` in the Meditations
   Book 5 / Odyssey Book 10 format (assigned paragraphs shown source-beside-
   candidate, one paragraph of context before and after marked `CONTEXT
   ONLY`, within the same chapter), plus `chNN/manifest.json` and
   `chNN/review-instructions.md`. Push and stop. A separate reviewer session
   spawned by the coordinator does the review; this task does not review its
   own draft. Findings come back on this branch under `chNN/review/`.
5. **Record a finding or "No material issue found" for every paragraph.**
   The reviewer's output covers every assigned paragraph ID exactly once.
6. **Apply supported corrections and verify changed passages.** Write
   `candidate-v2.json` (and v3 if a second round is needed), list every
   change by paragraph ID with the finding it answers, re-check each changed
   passage against the Danish source, and note any finding not applied and
   why.
7. **Read the complete chapter for flow.** A continuous read of the
   corrected candidate for voice, pacing, repetition, terminology, and
   transitions — de Silentio's irony and essayistic first person in
   particular.
8. **Accept only when no substantive issue remains.** Record acceptance in
   `chNN/ACCEPTANCE.md` with the accepted file's hash, the review rounds
   applied, and what remains open. Then move to the next chapter.

Order of chapters: Chapter 1 (Forord / Preface) first, drafted and frozen by
this task, as the shortest chapter and the one that establishes de Silentio's
voice before the longer argumentative chapters (Problema I–III, chapters
5–7, 149 paragraphs together) are attempted. This task stops after Chapter 1
step 3 (freeze) and hands off for independent review (step 4); it does not
draft Chapters 2–8.

(`bookN`) layout, since Fear and Trembling's eight chapters are prose

IDs are `CNN-Pxxx` (chapter-paragraph) rather than `Bxx-Pxxx`.

## Accessibility standard (the reading standard)

Same standard as the Meditations and Odyssey threads: clear, natural English
for a thoughtful modern adult, preserving the work's complete meaning and
literary character. Every claim, image, example, qualification, and
meaningful repetition survives; old vocabulary and tangled syntax are
simplified without simplifying away the ideas; ambiguity, contradiction, and
voice are kept; essential unfamiliar terms are explained briefly at the point
of need; nothing is added (no interpretations, motives, diagnoses, historical
facts, explanatory transitions); logical distinctions are preserved; no
mechanically short sentences or generic explanatory prose.

## Voice rules for Fear and Trembling (binding)

- Johannes de Silentio is a pseudonymous persona, not Kierkegaard speaking
  directly — ironic, self-deprecating, essayistic, first-person. Do not
  flatten his irony into plain explanatory prose, and do not narrate him as
  a neutral academic voice.
- Kierkegaard's dialectical structure (raising a position, then complicating
  or reversing it) must survive intact — this book argues by staged
  repetition and reversal (see the Exordium's four retellings of Abraham,
  and the three Problema chapters' repeated structure of stating, then
  undermining, the "ethical" reading). Do not smooth these repetitions into
  a single summary statement.
- Foreign-language insertions Kierkegaard himself wrote into the Danish
  (German, Latin, French) are kept in their original language, not
  translated away; a brief English gloss is folded into the sentence only
  where needed for comprehension (`GLOSSARY.md`).
- Establish stable renderings for the book's technical vocabulary (the
  knight of faith, infinite resignation, the teleological suspension of the
  ethical, the absurd, the single individual, the universal, and others,
  `GLOSSARY.md`) before the chapter that first uses each term.
- Replace archaic vocabulary and tangled 1843 syntax while retaining
  Kierkegaard's own rhetorical devices (rhetorical questions, dashes,
  em-dash interruptions, italicized emphasis) where they carry meaning.
