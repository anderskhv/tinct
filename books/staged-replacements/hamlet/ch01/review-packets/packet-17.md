## C01-P052 — CONTEXT ONLY — reviewed in another packet

**Source (served original-en):**

> MARCELLUS. Good now, sit down, and tell me, he that knows, Why this same strict and most observant watch So nightly toils the subject of the land, And why such daily cast of brazen cannon And foreign mart for implements of war; Why such impress of shipwrights, whose sore task Does not divide the Sunday from the week. What might be toward, that this sweaty haste Doth make the night joint-labourer with the day: Who is’t that can inform me?

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> MARCELLUS. Come now, sit down, and tell me — you who know — why this same strict and watchful guard wears out the country's people every night, and why there's this daily casting of bronze cannon, and buying of weapons abroad; why such pressing of shipwrights, whose hard labor doesn't even spare Sunday from the working week. What's coming, that this sweating haste makes the night a fellow laborer with the day? Who is it that can tell me?
