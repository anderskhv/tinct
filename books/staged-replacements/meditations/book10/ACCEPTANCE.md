- **21.1 (minor) — applied, the reviewer's repair taken as proposed.** X.21's fold of Long's "[is wont]" is **confirmed** — the meditation *is* the double sense of one Greek verb, so dropping the gloss would delete the observation rather than remove a note about Long's choices, which is the VII.13 / VIII.57 principle in another form — but v1's execution put the gloss **inside the quotation marks**. Long's square brackets were the universally understood signal "not part of the quoted text"; em dashes are not, so removing the brackets removed the signal and the candidate credited common speech with words common speech does not use. That is the one thing this meditation cannot afford: its point is that the saying is exactly what people say and that the second sense is supplied *from outside* it. v2 reads **`And is not this too said that "this or that loves"—is wont—"to be produced?"`** — every word, Long's order, his quotation marks and his question mark where he puts it, and the gloss now standing where his brackets stood. - **32.1 (minor) — applied; the reviewer rules against the drafter and the ruling is right.** v1 read "**You, only determine** to live no longer unless you are such", borrowing the vocative comma from the repair the Book IX round-1 review made at IX.40. The two passages are not alike. At IX.40 the fronted pronoun does rhetorical work — the passage is an *alternation* ("One man prays thus: … You, pray thus: …") and the comma preserves a contrast that would otherwise vanish. **X.32 has no contrast at all**, so the fronted "You," carries nothing, and a bare vocative "You," before an imperative reads as a summons to a stranger rather than as self-address, which is the one register Anders's brief rules out; worse, "You, only determine…" invites the misparse "only *you* determine…", the reverse of the sense. v2 reads **"Only determine to live no longer unless you are such."** — a true imperative, unambiguous in mood, self-addressed like every other imperative in the edition, with Long's "only" exactly where he has it, and with the second person held firmly by the sentences on either side. "Do only determine", the reviewer's alternative, was declined with the reviewer: it reads as a concession ("do at least"). **The IX.40 repair is localised, not narrowed** — the vocative comma is licensed where a fronted pronoun carries a contrast, not merely where a "do + thou" imperative needs modernising — and that line is now on the record in `continuity.md` so the next book does not repeat the borrowing.
