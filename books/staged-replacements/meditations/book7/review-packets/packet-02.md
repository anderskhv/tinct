## B07-P005 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (Long 1862):**

> 5. Is my understanding sufficient for this or not? If it is sufficient, I use it for the work as an instrument given by the universal nature. But if it is not sufficient, then either I retire from the work and give way to him who is able to do it better, unless there be some reason why I ought not to do so; or I do it as well as I can, taking to help me the man who with the aid of my ruling principle can do what is now fit and useful for the general good. For what-soever either by myself or with another I can do, ought to be directed to this only, to that which is useful and well suited to society.

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> 5. Is my understanding sufficient for this or not? If it is sufficient, I use it for the work as an instrument given by the universal nature. But if it is not sufficient, then either I withdraw from the work and give way to the man who is able to do it better, unless there is some reason why I ought not to do so; or I do it as well as I can, taking to help me the man who, with the aid of my ruling part, can do what is now fit and useful for the common good. For whatever I can do, either by myself or with another, ought to be directed to this only, to what is useful and well suited to society.
