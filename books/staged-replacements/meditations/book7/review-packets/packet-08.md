**Source (Long 1862):**

> 23. ## B07-P024 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (Long 1862):**

> 24. A scowling look is altogether unnatural; when it is often assumed, the result is that all comeliness dies away, and at last is so completely extinguished that it cannot be again lighted up at all. Try to conclude from this very fact that it is contrary to reason. For if even the perception of doing wrong shall depart, what reason is there for living any longer?

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> 24. A scowling look is altogether against nature; when it is often assumed, the result is that all grace dies away, and at last is so completely extinguished that it cannot be lighted up again at all. Try to conclude from this very fact that it is against reason. For if even the perception of doing wrong shall depart, what reason is there for living any longer?
