## B02-P004 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (served original-en):**

> SOLDIER. Doubtful it stood; As two spent swimmers that do cling together And choke their art. The merciless Macdonwald (Worthy to be a rebel, for to that The multiplying villainies of nature Do swarm upon him) from the Western Isles Of kerns and gallowglasses is supplied; And Fortune, on his damned quarrel smiling, Show’d like a rebel’s whore. But all’s too weak; For brave Macbeth (well he deserves that name), Disdaining Fortune, with his brandish’d steel, Which smok’d with bloody execution, Like Valour’s minion, carv’d out his passage, Till he fac’d the slave; Which ne’er shook hands, nor bade farewell to him, Till he unseam’d him from the nave to the chops, And fix’d his head upon our battlements.

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> SOLDIER. It hung in the balance—like two exhausted swimmers clinging together and smothering each other's strokes. The merciless Macdonwald—well fit to be a rebel, since every kind of villainy nature can breed swarms upon him—was reinforced from the Western Isles with light-armed foot soldiers and heavy-armed fighters; and Fortune, smiling on his cursed cause, seemed for a while to play the rebel's whore. But none of it was enough. Brave Macbeth—he well deserves that name—scorning Fortune, sword drawn and smoking with bloody slaughter, cut his way through like Valor's favorite, until he faced the traitor; and, sparing him neither handshake nor farewell, he ripped him open from navel to jaw and set his head upon our battlements.

## B02-P006 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (served original-en):**

> SOLDIER. As whence the sun ’gins his reflection Shipwracking storms and direful thunders break, So from that spring, whence comfort seem’d to come Discomfort swells. Mark, King of Scotland, mark: No sooner justice had, with valour arm’d, Compell’d these skipping kerns to trust their heels, But the Norweyan lord, surveying vantage, With furbish’d arms and new supplies of men, Began a fresh assault.

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> SOLDIER. Just as ship-wrecking storms and fearsome thunder break from the point where the sun begins to shine, so from that very spring where comfort seemed to rise, discomfort now wells up. Mark this, King of Scotland, mark it: no sooner had justice, armed with valor, forced these fleeing foot soldiers to trust their heels, than the Norwegian lord, seeing his chance, renewed the attack with polished weapons and fresh troops.
