# Continuity sheet — Macbeth, Act 1 Scene 2 (candidate v1, frozen)

Written alongside drafting `candidate-v1.json`. Term renderings follow
`../GLOSSARY.md`; this sheet records paragraph-level decisions specific to
this scene and the one known source anomaly from `../PROVENANCE.md`.

## Source

- `source-ch02.json`, chapter `number: 2`, title "Act 1, Scene 2 — A Camp
  near Forres", `section: "Act I"`, 22 paragraphs. Extracted verbatim from
  `app/public/data/editions/macbeth-original-en.json` (sha256 in
  `provenance.json`).
- The whole scene was read in full before any paragraph was drafted: a
  wounded Soldier (the Folger/Globe tradition's "Captain") reports Macbeth
  and Banquo's battlefield conduct to Duncan in two long speeches, is helped
  offstage, and Ross arrives with a second report (the defeat of the King of
  Norway and the Thane of Cawdor's treason), on which Duncan orders Cawdor's
  execution and Macbeth's promotion to his title — the engine of the whole
  play, since Macbeth never learns of this news honestly earned before the
  Witches greet him with it as prophecy in Scene 3.
- Known source anomaly (see `../PROVENANCE.md` §3): the served scene is
  missing the unattributed line "Who comes here?", present in PG #1533/#100
  between "[Enter Ross and Angus.]" and Malcolm's "The worthy Thane of
  Ross." The candidate keeps the served 22-paragraph scene as it stands,
  per this task's scope; the line is not restored here.
