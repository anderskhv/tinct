## Deliberate choices

- No unclosed quotation (D4) is present in this Book: every speech opens and closes within its own paragraph, verified by quote-balance check.
- 'Ivy-wood bowls', 'suppliant', 'gallery' (for Butler's cloister), 'insolent' (for the suitors, held steady across P9/P15/P38), and 'Good heavens' (Antinous's exclamation, matching accepted Book 13's Phaeacian usage) are treated as recurring-word/formula rows for this Book and rendered identically at each occurrence.
- Athena's transformation-scene epithet 'beautiful, tall, and wise' (P19 draft) is kept as the live text already had it -- it matches accepted Book 13 P30's identical rendering of the same formula, so it was left untouched rather than 'improved'.
- No paragraph required a full rewrite from the source: the live baseline's register was already natural modern English throughout; every defect found was a small, local slip (a dropped word, a swapped synonym that broke a recurring word, a wrong narrator-formula, or a softened concrete detail), not period prose needing wholesale replacement.
- The source's own duplicated word in P0 ('and were\nwere getting breakfast ready') is Butler's/PG's own text; it is not reproduced as an error since the live rendering already read it as a single 'were' with no loss of meaning.
- No Roman name, ethnonym flattening (other than the repaired 'Cretan'), duplicated paragraph, or 'Fury'/hawser instance was found elsewhere in this Book after the full re-read.
