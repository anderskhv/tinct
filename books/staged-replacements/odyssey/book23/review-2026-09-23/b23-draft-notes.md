## Deliberate choices worth recording
- Butler's own unclosed-quotation convention (D4) does not arise in this Book — every
  paragraph's dialogue opens and closes within itself; the paragraph-8/paragraph-9 break
  is an ordinary speech-attribution boundary ("...and said:" / new paragraph begins the
  quoted speech), not a continued unclosed quotation.
- No Butler square brackets in this Book except the one class-B instance at ¶8 (PG
  10132, "at right angles [to that by which she had entered]"), already in GLOSSARY's
  table; disposed of per class B (mark dropped, words kept).
- "Achaean(s)" is never rendered "Greek(s)" anywhere in this Book (three corrections:
  ¶5, ¶18, ¶27) — confirmed against the closed "names that change in no Book" list and
  against accepted Books 20/21, which keep "Achaeans" throughout.
- "cloister"/"cloisters" → "the gallery" applied at all three occurrences (¶2, ¶8, ¶24),
  matching Book 1's fixed rendering.
- Philoetius is named directly by Butler at every occurrence in this Book (¶24, ¶28) —
  he is never referred to by the "stockman" epithet here, so the Book 20/21 "stockman"
  convention does not apply to this Book's text; his name is simply kept as Butler
  writes it.
- The gilding/hyacinth-curls simile (¶15) and "Achilles' bed" test are each rendered as
  fixed recurring formulas where a precedent exists (Book 6 for the former); Butler's own
  small wording differences between the two occurrences (Vulcan "or" Minerva vs "and")
  are preserved rather than silently harmonized.
