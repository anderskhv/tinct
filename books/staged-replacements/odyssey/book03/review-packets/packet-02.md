## B03-P005 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (Butler 1900):**

> She then went quickly on, and Telemachus followed in her steps till
they reached the place where the guilds of the Pylian people were

meat on to the spits while other pieces were cooking. When they saw

hand to each of them, and seated them on some soft sheepskins that were

into a golden cup, handing it to Minerva first, and saluting her at the

## B03-P007 — CONTEXT ONLY — reviewed in another packet

**Source (Butler 1900):**

> As he spoke he handed her the cup. Minerva thought it very right and
proper of him to have given it to herself first; she accordingly
began praying heartily to Neptune. “O thou,” she cried, “that
encirclest the earth, vouchsafe to grant the prayers of thy servants
that call upon thee. More especially we pray thee send down thy grace
on Nestor and on his sons; thereafter also make the rest of the Pylian
people some handsome return for the goodly hecatomb they are offering
you. Lastly, grant Telemachus and myself a happy issue, in respect of
the matter that has brought us in our ship to Pylos.”

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> As he spoke he handed her the cup. Athena thought it very right and proper of him to have given it to her first, and she began praying heartily to Poseidon. “You who encircle the earth,” she cried, “grant the prayers of your servants who call upon you. Above all, send down your grace on Nestor and on his sons; and after that make the rest of the Pylian people some handsome return for the fine sacrifice they are offering you. Last of all, grant Telemachus and myself a happy outcome in the matter that has brought us in our ship to Pylos.”
