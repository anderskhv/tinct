## B03-P001 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (Butler 1900):**

> But as the sun was rising from the fair sea into the firmament of
heaven to shed light on mortals and immortals, they reached Pylos the
city of Neleus. Now the people of Pylos were gathered on the sea shore
to offer sacrifice of black bulls to Neptune lord of the Earthquake.
There were nine guilds with five hundred men in each, and there were
nine bulls to each guild. As they were eating the inward meats and
burning the thigh bones [on the embers] in the name of Neptune,
Telemachus and his crew arrived, furled their sails, brought their ship
to anchor, and went ashore.

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> But as the sun was rising out of the fair sea into the vault of heaven, to shed light on mortals and immortals alike, they reached Pylos, the city of Neleus. The people of Pylos were gathered on the sea shore to offer a sacrifice of black bulls to Poseidon, lord of the Earthquake. There were nine companies with five hundred men in each, and nine bulls to each company. As they were eating the inner meats and burning the thigh bones on the embers in Poseidon’s name, Telemachus and his crew arrived, furled their sails, brought their ship to anchor, and went ashore.

## B03-P002 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (Butler 1900):**

> Minerva led the way and Telemachus followed her. Presently she said,
“Telemachus, you must not be in the least shy or nervous; you have
taken this voyage to try and find out where your father is buried and
how he came by his end; so go straight up to Nestor that we may see
what he has got to tell us. Beg of him to speak the truth, and he will
tell no lies, for he is an excellent person.”

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> Athena led the way and Telemachus followed her. Presently she said, “Telemachus, you must not be shy or nervous for a moment. You have made this voyage to find out where your father is buried and how he met his end, so go straight up to Nestor and let us see what he has to tell us. Ask him to speak the truth, and he will tell you no lies, for he is an excellent man.”

## B03-P003 — ASSIGNED FOR REVIEW

**Source (Butler 1900):**

> “But how, Mentor,” replied Telemachus, “dare I go up to Nestor, and how
am I to address him? I have never yet been used to holding long

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> “But how, Mentor,” Telemachus replied, “dare I go up to Nestor? And how am I to address him? I have never been used to holding long conversations with people, and I am ashamed to begin by questioning a man so much older than myself.”
