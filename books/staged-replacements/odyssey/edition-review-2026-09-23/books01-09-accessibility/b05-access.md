## Notes on what reads well

The Zeus/Athena council opening (paragraphs 0–3) and Hermes's flight to
Calypso's island (4–5) are vivid and easy to follow aloud. The storm sequence
(21–27) reads with strong, propulsive momentum — short declarative sentences
carry the danger without feeling choppy. Odysseus's internal monologues (22,
26, 30, 35) are consistently clear in voice and easy to attribute.

## Verdict: BLOCKING FINDINGS

Two items — the tangled simile in paragraph 29 and the invented word
"riverhood" in paragraph 33 — will genuinely trip up a first-time listener
and warrant a fix before this chapter is considered accessible. The
remaining two items are minor vocabulary stumbles that don't block
comprehension.
