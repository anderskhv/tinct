Style references: accepted `book12/candidate-accepted.json` (immediate) and
`book11/candidate-accepted.json`. Glossary/punctuation/workflow files read in
full. `book13` has no accepted candidate (only `candidate-v1.json`), so it
was not used as a style reference per the task's explicit instruction to use

Given the screening finding that the live text stays unusually close to
Butler (avg similarity 0.82) and, on inspection, contains a serious content

each paragraph's own translation was drafted, never as an unverified base.

## Quote-structure verification

Programmatically compared per-paragraph counts of “ and ” between
`source-book14.json` and the draft: **0 mismatches** across all 35
paragraphs. This confirms:
- The single continuous speech opening at ¶10 ("And Odysseus answered...")
  and not closing until the end of ¶17 is preserved unclosed at every
  intermediate paragraph break (¶10–¶16 end with no closing mark; ¶17
  closes), exactly as Butler has it.
- The nested-quotation structure of ¶26–¶31 (Odysseus's own storytelling
  frame, opened with “, containing directly quoted dialogue in ‘ ’) is
  reproduced index for index: ¶27, ¶29, ¶30 open the outer mark afresh and
  close only the inner quote; ¶31 closes the outer mark, ending the whole
  speech that began in ¶26.
- ¶18→¶19 is a shorter instance of the same convention (Eumaeus's speech
  ends unclosed at ¶18 and Butler reopens with a fresh “ at the start of
  ¶19, closing at its end) — corrected in the draft, since live had
  dropped the reopening mark at ¶19.

## Deliberate choices

- Unclosed quotations at paragraph breaks (¶10–¶16, ¶18, ¶26, ¶27–¶30) kept
  exactly as Butler has them, per PUNCTUATION.md D4 — not "fixed."
- "Ilius" kept beside "Troy" per the standing Book 2 ruling; not flattened.
- "the river Aegyptus" kept as Butler's own name; not silently corrected to
  "the Nile."
- No number invented for the Ithaca goat flocks, where Butler gives none.
- The storm/shipwreck formula in ¶15 is matched in vocabulary and phrasing
  to accepted book12 ¶39–40 (the same episode recounted in the "true"
  narrative), without forcing verbatim identity where Butler's own wording
  differs slightly between the two Books.
- American spelling and typographic quotes/apostrophes throughout; no
  ASCII quote or apostrophe remains (asserted by script).
