A flow read is the step that can see what a three-paragraph packet cannot.
For this Book it also has a second job the earlier Books did not give it,
because this round's content was **sentence division**: a division is invisible
to every check in the package except the two this round added, and the only
instrument that can tell a good division from a bad one is a continuous read.

## F-1 — acted on. Dividing both joints of B04-P009 left two sentences opening `But`

Butler's 71-word opener of Menelaus's speech has two semicolons, and the first
build divided at both:

> …everything about him is immortal. **But** among mortal men—well, there may be another who has as much wealth as I have, or there may not. **But** at all events I have traveled much…

Read at speed that is a stammer, and it is a stammer the draft introduced:
Butler's two `but`s are *inside* one period, where the second is a resumption
rather than a fresh start. **The second division is reverted.** The surviving
sentence is 48 words — comfortably under the sixty-word line the substantive
finding is about, and the paragraph still gains a sentence, which is what S-1
asked of it.

This is the general lesson of the round and it belongs in the record: **the
sixty-word count is a floor to clear, not a target to maximize.** A drafter who
divides at every semicolon Butler wrote will produce a text that passes the new
check and reads worse than the one it replaced.

## F-2 — `at that` six times, and it is not a tic

Finding 64.2 collapsed one connective rendered four ways into one rendering
(`whereon` ×5 and the sentential `on which` ×1 → `at that`). Read continuously,
the six are at B04-P003, P036, P038, P043, P048 and P064 — spread across the
whole Book, never two in a paragraph, and never two in adjacent paragraphs.
Two of the six (P036 and P048) are Butler's own twin formula, the dive and the
walk back to the ships, where he repeats himself deliberately and the identical
rendering is what preserves that. Butler's other sentential connective, `On
this` ×4, keeps its accepted `At this` at P008, P019, P024 and P046. Two Butler
forms, two renderings, and B04-P022's `At that moment` — his own temporal
phrase, not the connective — asserted untouched by name.

## F-3 — the three surviving sixty-word sentences are the three that should survive

B04-P038 (105 words, Proteus's shape-changing, ruled to stand by round 1
section B.6 and asserted to stand by the build), B04-P072 (62, the launching of
the suitors' ship, a catalogue of simultaneous actions that reads as one
movement) and B04-P076 (62, Penelope's `I am even more anxious about him than
about my husband`, one breath of anxiety). Each was re-read at length against
Butler and each earns it. Fourteen of Butler's seventeen are gone.

## F-4 — the instructions read as instructions now

This was round 1's sharpest point: the paragraphs carrying things the reader
has to follow were the ones left chained. Re-read as a set —
Idothea's recipe (**P034, P035**), Proteus's conditions (**P040**), the recast
ambush (**P037**) — the sequence is now one step per sentence: count the seals,
sleep among them, seize him, hold on, he changes, hold harder, he speaks, let
go, then ask. Butler's five semicolons in one 94-word period became four
sentences, and not one word of his moved.

## F-5 — `heartbroken` twice is Butler's own repetition, forty words apart in effect

B04-P041 and B04-P045, `broken hearted` in Butler both times, and both times
Menelaus about the same news. The compound rule closes both; the repetition is
his.

## F-6 — the two `for fear` clauses now match, and both are Butler's apprehensive

P071 (Antinous) and P076 (Penelope) both rendered Butler's `lest`. v1 had `in
case` in both, which is consistent but weaker; `for fear` is the same strength
as `lest` and is not archaic. No third rendering of `lest` exists in the Book.

## F-7 — the seven byte-identical paragraphs, re-read as prose and left

P039, P054, P061, P063, P070, P079, P080. Each read again in its place rather
than in a list. `was dissipated into thin air` (P080, optional finding 80.1) is
the only one that invited a change and the flow read agrees with the reviewer's
own reason for not making it: `vanished` is the sentence's first verb four
words earlier, so `vanished into thin air` repeats it, and `melted into thin
air` is a different image. Declined, recorded in `changes-v1-to-v2.md`.

## F-8 — the `morning … in the morning` echo at P017 stands, and why

`Morning will come in its own time, and in the morning I do not care how much
I cry…`. Butler's second word is `forenoon`, which is on the package's dead
list and means the morning. Every plainer rendering either moves the time of
day or adds a word Butler did not write. The echo is the cost of a correct
rendering, not a slip. Recorded rather than repaired.

## F-9 — nothing the corrections touched broke a formula or a speaker

Re-checked at distance, not in packets: the guest-welcome formula (P008/P019),
the beach-camp formula (P036/P048), Penelope's `brave and lion-hearted husband`
(P065/P076), `I will make it all quite clear to you` (P032/P034), `the
heaven-fed stream of Egypt` (P040/P048) and `thought of another matter`
(P020/P074) are each identical where Butler is identical. The twenty-one
unbalanced paragraphs of Proteus's nested narrative (**D4**) still open their
own marks, and the divisions inside them — P035, P037, P040, P041, P044, P045
— fall inside quoted speech and add no mark of any kind. The B04-P040 repair
is untouched and is still the only single-mark difference from the source.
