“Hear me,” he cried, “daughter of aegis-bearing Zeus, unwearying, hear me now, for you paid no heed to my prayers when Poseidon was wrecking me. Now, therefore, have pity on me and grant that I may find friends and be hospitably received by the Phaeacians.”
