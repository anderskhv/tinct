## B06-P024 — CONTEXT ONLY — reviewed in another packet

**Source (Butler 1900):**

> So saying she lashed the mules with her whip and they left the river.
The mules drew well, and their hoofs went up and down upon the road.
She was careful not to go too fast for Ulysses and the maids who were
following on foot along with the waggon, so she plied her whip with
judgement. As the sun was going down they came to the sacred grove of
Minerva, and there Ulysses sat down and prayed to the mighty daughter
of Jove.

**Candidate (modern English v1):**

> So saying, she lashed the mules with her whip and they left the river. The mules drew well, and their hoofs went up and down on the road. She was careful not to go too fast for Odysseus and the maids who were following on foot along with the wagon, so she used her whip with judgment. As the sun was going down they came to the sacred grove of Athena, and there Odysseus sat down and prayed to the mighty daughter of Zeus.
