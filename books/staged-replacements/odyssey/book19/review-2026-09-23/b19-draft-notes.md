# Book 19 draft notes

Screening verdict confirmed on read-through: the live-baseline text is Butler's own
1900 prose with only name-table/spelling substitutions applied (no genuine
modernization). Nearly every paragraph is therefore REWRITTEN from source rather
than kept/repaired. All 39 paragraphs rewritten; none kept verbatim from the live
baseline because none of them cleared the "clear, natural sentences a first-time
listener can follow" bar without full rewriting (archaic syntax throughout:
"inasmuch as," "whereon," "notwithstanding all this," "the means whereby," "I am
obliged to go begging about after the manner of tramps," etc.).
