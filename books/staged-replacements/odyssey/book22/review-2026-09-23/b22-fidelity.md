**Verdict: BLOCKING FINDINGS.** 32 findings: 12 blocking, 20 non-blocking. All are in `b22-fidelity.json`, and every "old" string was checked with Python as exact, unique in its paragraph and not overlapping another.

**Paragraphs read: 52 of 52.** Each was compared against source-book22.json with its neighbours, and then the whole Book was re-read for continuity. The paragraph count and order match the source.

## Coordinator ruling (applied)
- **¶47 "vitals".** Butler has "they drew out his vitals and gave them to the dogs raw". The draft's "tore out his genitals" imports the Greek sense beyond the locked source. **The finding restores "drew out his vitals and gave them raw to the dogs"**, which neither corrects nor softens Butler. It is blocking. The drafter's note keeping "genitals" is overruled.

## Coordinator data point: ¶23 quotation-mark count
- The count is confirmed. ¶23 is the only paragraph whose “ ” ‘ count differs from Butler's (source 2/2/0, candidate 1/1/0). **The structure is wrong.** Butler writes “Ulysses,” said she, “your strength…, and the candidate dropped the speech tag, plus "very angrily" became "angrily". This is the one speech-tag structure the drafter did not restore. It is blocking. There is a separate non-blocking finding in the same paragraph: "lamentably less valiant" became "pathetically weak".
