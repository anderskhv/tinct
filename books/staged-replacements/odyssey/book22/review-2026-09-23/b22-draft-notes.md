## Deliberate choices carried over unchanged
- Melanthius's mutilation (idx47) keeps "genitals" (not Butler's euphemism "vitals") and the hanging of the maids (idx46, idx51's echo, etc.) keeps its full description — both per this round's explicit instruction not to soften the violence.
- No unclosed Butler quotation spans a paragraph break in this Book (checked directly against source-book22.json); every quotation in the draft opens and closes within its own paragraph, so D4 (preserve Butler's unclosed cross-paragraph quotations) does not apply here.
- Philoetius is named directly once (idx14, "Odysseus told Philoetius to stand by this door") — Butler's own choice to use the name rather than "the stockman" at that single spot is kept, since Butler himself alternates between the name and the title through the Book.
- "Domed room" (not "round room") kept for Butler's chamber where the maids are hanged, since "domed" describes the roof (matching "the roof of the domed room" in idx46) and "round" would assert a different, unstated floor plan.
- No colon or semicolon was added where Butler has a comma, and no dash was introduced where Butler's mark was a comma; several places where the live baseline had already added a colon before a quotation, or a dash over a causal "for"/"but", were repaired back to Butler's own comma-and-attribution structure (idx1, idx11, idx22, idx25, idx28, idx41 — see per-paragraph notes above).
