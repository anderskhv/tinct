# Book 13 draft notes — Odysseus leaves Scheria and returns to Ithaca

Base: `live-baseline-book13.json`. Source: `source-book13.json` (Butler 1900, PG #1727).
Style reference read in full: Book 11 accepted (`book11/candidate-accepted.json`, the
immediate precedent) and Book 9 v2 / Book 10 accepted for register. Book 12 not consulted
as a precedent (still under review, per instructions).

Narrator note: Book 13 returns to Homer's own third-person narration (Odysseus's frame
narrative closes at the end of Book 12), so quotation is single-level throughout — “ ” at
top level, no nested ‘ ’ — matching the source exactly (no nested quotation anywhere in
this Book). Checked for Butler's paragraph-break unclosed-quotation convention (D4): none
occurs in this Book — every quoted speech opens and closes within its own paragraph.

Bracket: source ¶32 carries the poem's sixth and last class-C bracket (PG 6016, "near it is
the cave sacred to the Naiads..."), the one Butler's footnote 122 attributes to another
editor, not to himself, and which never closes in PG. Disposition per GLOSSARY D12 class C:
mark dropped, all words kept, no recasting across the boundary. The live baseline had
already applied this correctly; kept as is.

## Deliberate choices

- No unclosed cross-paragraph quotation occurs in this Book (checked against D4); nothing
  to preserve or normalize on that count.
- The class-C bracket at ¶32 (PG 6016, fn 122, not Butler's own bracket) is dropped per
  D12 with no recasting across its boundary; this was already correct in the live
  baseline and is unchanged.
- "Bless my heart" (¶21, Butler) rendered "Good heavens" — a mild oath with no fixed
  package rendering and no recurrence elsewhere in the poem; kept as a natural modern
  equivalent, not treated as a defect.
- "the weary sea" (Butler, ¶8 and ¶24, the same recurring line "the field of battle...
  the weary sea," which also appears once in Book 8, not yet reviewable here) rendered
  "the restless sea" in both of this Book's instances, consistently. Left as is: internally
  consistent, and Book 8's accepted candidate was not available as a reference for this
  round.
- "the pavement" (¶38 source numbering / draft ¶33's "bespatter the pavement") rendered
  "the floor" — read as an acceptable modern equivalent (the paved floor of the hall), not
  a loss of concrete detail worth restoring.
- "bounteous soil" → "fertile soil" (¶33/28) and "loosed Troy's fair diadem from her brow"
  → "stripped Troy of her fair crown" (¶37/32) — modernizations of period vocabulary that
  preserve the image and are not flagged.
- Quotation marks and apostrophes: converted throughout from the live baseline's ASCII
  `"`/`'` to typographic `“ ” ’`, per PUNCTUATION.md §1. Verified no nested single-quote
  level is needed anywhere in this Book (Homer's own narration, no reported speech inside
  a speech), matching the source's own single-level `“ ”` throughout.
- Name mapping (already correct in the live baseline, verified unchanged): Ulysses →
  Odysseus, Minerva → Athena, Jove → Zeus, Neptune → Poseidon. Hazard check: "Arete"
  (queen) occurs twice, untouched; "Ops" does not occur in this Book.
