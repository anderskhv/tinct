## Deliberate choices worth recording
- No unclosed quotation at a paragraph break occurs anywhere in Book 18 (verified by
  quote-mark parity per paragraph against the source before drafting), so D4's
  preservation rule was not triggered anywhere in this Book.
- The one class-B Butler bracket in this Book, `[into the open part of the court]`
  (P9), was already handled correctly in the live baseline (mark dropped, words kept,
  nothing recast across the boundary) and needed no change.
- Followed the accepted Book 15 precedent of rendering Butler's "wooer(s)" as "suitor(s)"
  where he uses it as a synonym in the same breath as "suitors" (P22: "these wooers of
  yours"), since that flattening is already established practice in an accepted Book,
  not a fresh judgment call to make here.
- Did not "correct" Butler's own repeated exclamation style; kept the live draft's
  handful of added exclamation marks on genuinely exclamatory lines (P1, P4, P37),
  matching the same practice already accepted in Book 16 (P17, P34) — these are
  punctuation, not an invented profanity/oath, so they are outside the binding "never
  add an exclamation" lesson, which is about inserted curses/oaths Butler doesn't have.
- Full typographic pass (curly “ ” and ’, no ASCII survivors) applied as the final
  mechanical step over the whole candidate, matching PUNCTUATION.md §1; nested single
  quotes in P24 (Butler's embedded "Wife,...leave this house." speech) converted to
  proper opening/closing curly single quotes rather than the plain apostrophe glyph,
  matching the accepted Book 15 precedent (P34-P37) for nested quoted speech.
