# The Odyssey, Book 5 — step 7, the continuous read

`WORKFLOW.md` step 7, run on `candidate-v2.json` after the round-1 corrections
and before acceptance. A continuous read of all 37 paragraphs for voice,
pacing, repetition, terminology and transitions — the instrument round 1's
section H.3 names as the only one that can see a division that satisfies
**D17** and hurts the prose.

## F-1 — `and on the Bear itself` — **applied**

The S-1 recast of B05-P021 first read:

> Calypso had told him to keep the Bear on his left, so he never closed his
> eyes, but kept them fixed on the Pleiads, on late-setting Bootes, and **on
> the Bear itself**. Men also call it the Wain…

`itself` was doing contrastive work the sentence does not ask for, and it put
`the Bear` twice inside one sentence where Butler names it once and refers back
with `this`. Dropped. The change is made in
`../scripts/build_book05_v2.py`, not in a later version — the same way Book 4's
flow-read finding F-1 fed back into its own build script — and the build
asserts `Bear itself` is absent.

## What the read confirms, and what it declines to change

- **The three reversals read better than the divisions they replace.** B05-P020
  is back to Butler's `and then led the way`, so the run of four sentences
  opening `She … So she … She also … Then she` is three. B05-P021's
  `She gave him a goatskin … ; she also gave him a bag …` is one sentence
  again, and the twin openings `She … She also …` — the Book 4 F-1 shape — are
  gone. B05-P027's deliberation closes on Butler's own pointing, and the two
  consecutive sentences opening `I` inside a speech that already opens six
  clauses with `I` are one sentence.
- **The three recasts are audible.** P009 no longer suspends a relative chain
  across a main clause; P017's `It makes no difference how anxious you may be`
  gives Calypso's concession its own sentence, which is where the Book's
  emotional centre needed a breath; P021's Bear no longer reaches back over a
  thirty-word parenthesis for its reason.
- **P029 is still one 51-word sentence with a mid-sentence semicolon, and it
  should be.** Round 1 named it the best evidence in the Book that the gate was
  not being farmed: dividing `But Athena decided … ; but she roused …` would
  produce two consecutive sentences opening `But`. The read agrees.
- **P030 and P037 stay long.** P030 is 62 words, exactly Butler's, after the
  30.2 trim; P037's fire-seed simile is 59 after finding 37.1 and is the Book's
  last sentence. In both the chain is the figure.
- **One ambiguity is Butler's and is left.** P009's `They sailed home in the
  tenth year, after sacking it` leaves `it` six words from `the tenth year`.
  Butler's own sentence has the same referent at the same distance
  (`sailed home in the tenth year after having sacked it`), and repairing it
  would mean either repeating `the city` or supplying a word. Left, recorded.
- **Terminology holds across the read.** `still undecided` twice, `sorely
  against my will` once, the dawn formula, the guest-welcome formula,
  `Nevertheless` three times for Butler's three, `At this` twice and `At that`
  twice for his two and two, `seashore` twice, no `saying` tag anywhere, and
  `sacrifices or choice victims` not colliding with any `offering`.

No other change. 37 of 37 paragraphs read.
