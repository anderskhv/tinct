## Deliberate choices worth recording
- Butler's unclosed quotation across the paragraph break at P41→P42 (Odysseus's begging
  speech to Antinous, the Egypt story) is preserved exactly: P41 ends without a closing
  quotation mark and P42 opens with a fresh one, per PUNCTUATION.md §2 (D4). Verified by
  a per-paragraph open/close quote-count diff against the source — every paragraph's
  count matches the source's exactly, including this one's asymmetry.
- "Wallet"→"bag" (5 occurrences) is kept from the live baseline throughout: a sound,
  consistent modernization (Butler's "wallet" is a beggar's pouch, and the modern word
  "wallet" now means something else entirely — a false-friend the live drafter already
  avoided correctly).
- "Shamefaced"→"shouldn't be ashamed" (3 occurrences, P31/P32/P57) is kept from the live
  baseline: not a GLOSSARY-fixed formula, but rendered identically all three times, which
  is what matters.
- The "On this"/"whereon" connective family: five "On this" instances become "At this"
  with no comma (P2, P35, P44, P49, P62); the one exception, P19 ("On this, as he
  passed,..."), is rendered without the connective at all rather than forcing "At this,"
  with a comma the general rule forbids — recorded here as a judgment call, not an
  oversight. Three "whereon" connectives (P6, P10, P34 ×2) become "At that" per the
  Book 4 fixed-row convention.
- Standardized Butler's "bearing-post" as "post" throughout (P2, P8, P31) for internal
  consistency; no GLOSSARY row existed for this term before.
- "Court"/"courts" (Butler's word for the space around Odysseus's house, 6 occurrences)
  is kept as "court"/"courts" throughout, matching accepted Book 15's usage; the live
  baseline had introduced "courtyard" and "hall"/"halls" as unmotivated variants.
