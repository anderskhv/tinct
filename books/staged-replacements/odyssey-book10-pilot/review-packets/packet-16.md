## B10-P045 — CONTEXT ONLY — reviewed in another packet

**Source (Butler):**

> “‘When you shall have thus besought the ghosts with your prayers, offer
them a ram and a black ewe, bending their heads towards Erebus; but
yourself turn away from them as though you would make towards the
river. On this, many dead men’s ghosts will come to you, and you must
tell your men to skin the two sheep that you have just killed, and
offer them as a burnt sacrifice with prayers to Hades and to
Proserpine. Then draw your sword and sit there, so as to prevent any
other poor ghost from coming near the spilt blood before Teiresias
shall have answered your questions. The seer will presently come to
you, and will tell you about your voyage—what stages you are to make,
and how you are to sail the sea so as to reach your home.’

**Candidate (Tinct Clear v1):**

> ‘Once you have pleaded with the ghosts in this way, offer them a ram and a black ewe, turning their heads toward Erebus, but turn yourself away, toward the river, as if to go there. Then many ghosts of the dead will come to you, and you must tell your men to skin the two sheep you have just killed and burn them as a sacrifice, with prayers to Hades and Persephone. Then draw your sword and sit there, to keep any other poor ghost from coming near the spilled blood before Teiresias has answered your questions. The seer will soon come to you and tell you about your voyage—what stages lie ahead, and how you are to cross the sea to reach your home.’
