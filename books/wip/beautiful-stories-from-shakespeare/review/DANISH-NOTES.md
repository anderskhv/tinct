# Beautiful Stories from Shakespeare (Nesbit) — Danish edition notes

- Play titles in the chapter list are the usual Danish titles; character names stay in English (Hamlet, Lear, Prospero), while *Romeo og Julie* and *Timon af Athen* use Danish forms.
- Mixed forms remain in places: Athen / Athens, *Edvard Bekenderen* / English names, *herre / lord*.
- Verse quotations are meaning-first and often unrhymed.

## General

- Distances: English miles are “engelske mil”; feet, yards and inches are kept.
- Coins and units with no Danish equivalent (groat, farthing, shilling, pottle) are kept.
- Speeches that run across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end.
