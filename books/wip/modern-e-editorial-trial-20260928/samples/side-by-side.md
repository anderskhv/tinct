# Independent review packet — Tinct Modern E editorial trial

You are an independent literary reviewer. For each of the four cases below you are given: the original text, the currently-served Tinct Modern E rendering, and a proposed alternative rendering. You are NOT told which one (if either) the requesting editor prefers.

Tinct's stated principle for "Modern E": *it removes avoidable barriers; it does not make every book easy. Preserve difficult ideas, ambiguity, distinctive voices, rhythm, wordplay, and meaningful stylistic experiments.*

For EACH case, answer:
1. Does the PROPOSED version improve accessibility compared to the CURRENT version, without losing meaning, allusion, voice, or literary effect present in the ORIGINAL?
2. Does the PROPOSED version introduce any new problem (interpretation added, ambiguity resolved that shouldn't be, voice flattened, content cut or invented)?
3. Verdict: prefer CURRENT, prefer PROPOSED, or neither (explain)?
4. Any residual concern with EITHER version.

Be blunt. Do not defer to the fact that a "proposed" version is presented — it may well be worse than current.

## Case 1 — Woolf, To the Lighthouse, "Time Passes · 4", opening paragraph

ORIGINAL (Woolf, 1927):
So with the house empty and the doors locked and the mattresses rolled round, those stray airs, advance guards of great armies, blustered in, brushed bare boards, nibbled and fanned, met nothing in bedroom or drawing-room that wholly resisted them but only hangings that flapped, wood that creaked, the bare legs of tables, saucepans and china already furred, tarnished, cracked. What people had shed and left—a pair of shoes, a shooting cap, some faded skirts and coats in wardrobes—those alone kept the human shape and in the emptiness indicated how once they were filled and animated; how once hands were busy with hooks and buttons; how once the looking-glass had held a face; had held a world hollowed out in which a figure turned, a hand flashed, the door opened, in came children rushing and tumbling; and went out again. Now, day after day, light turned, like a flower reflected in water, its clear image on the wall opposite. Only the shadows of the trees, flourishing in the wind, made obeisance on the wall, and for a moment darkened the pool in which light reflected itself; or birds, flying, made a soft spot flutter slowly across the bedroom floor.

CURRENT Modern E:
So, with the house empty and the doors locked and the mattresses rolled up, those stray breaths of air, the advance guard of great armies, blustered in, brushed the bare boards, nibbled and fanned, and met nothing in bedroom or drawing room that fully resisted them—only hangings that flapped, wood that creaked, the bare legs of tables, saucepans and china already furred, tarnished, cracked. What people had shed and left behind—a pair of shoes, a shooting cap, some faded skirts and coats in the wardrobes—these alone kept the human shape, and in the emptiness showed how once they had been filled and brought to life; how once hands had been busy with hooks and buttons; how once the looking-glass had held a face, had held a hollowed-out world in which a figure turned, a hand flashed, the door opened, and in came children rushing and tumbling, and went out again. Now, day after day, the light turned its clear image on the opposite wall, like a flower reflected in water. Only the shadows of the trees, tossing in the wind, bowed low on the wall and for a moment darkened the pool in which the light reflected itself; or birds, flying past, made a soft spot flutter slowly across the bedroom floor.

PROPOSED:
So, with the house empty, the doors locked, and the mattresses rolled up, those stray breaths of air moved in — advance guards of great armies. They blustered through, brushed the bare boards, nibbled and fanned. In bedroom or drawing room they met nothing that fully resisted them: only hangings that flapped, wood that creaked, the bare legs of tables, and saucepans and china already furred, tarnished, cracked. What people had shed and left behind — a pair of shoes, a shooting cap, some faded skirts and coats in the wardrobes — these alone still kept the human shape. In the emptiness they showed how once they had been filled and brought to life: how once hands had been busy with hooks and buttons; how once the looking-glass had held a face, had held a hollowed-out world where a figure turned, a hand flashed, the door opened, children came rushing and tumbling in, and went out again. Now, day after day, the light turned its clear image on the opposite wall, like a flower reflected in water. Only the shadows of the trees, tossing in the wind, bowed low on the wall, darkening for a moment the pool where the light reflected itself; or birds, flying past, sent a soft spot fluttering slowly across the bedroom floor.

## Case 2 — Joyce, Ulysses, "Calypso" (Episode 4), paragraph 2

ORIGINAL:
Kidneys were in his mind as he moved about the kitchen softly, righting her breakfast things on the humpy tray. Gelid light and air were in the kitchen but out of doors gentle summer morning everywhere. Made him feel a bit peckish.

CURRENT Modern E:
Kidneys were on his mind as he moved about the kitchen softly, setting up his wife's breakfast things on the bumpy tray. Cold light and air filled the kitchen but out of doors gentle summer morning everywhere. Made him feel a bit peckish.

PROPOSED:
Kidneys were on his mind as he moved about the kitchen softly, straightening her breakfast things on the bumpy tray. Cold light and air filled the kitchen but out of doors gentle summer morning everywhere. Made him feel a bit peckish.

(Note: at this point in the novel Bloom's wife has been referred to only as "her" — Molly is not yet named on the page. "His wife's" makes explicit what Joyce leaves as pronoun.)

## Case 3 — Joyce, Ulysses, "Proteus" (Episode 3), opening paragraph (excerpt)

ORIGINAL:
Ineluctable modality of the visible: at least that if no more, thought through my eyes. Signatures of all things I am here to read, seaspawn and seawrack, the nearing tide, that rusty boot. Snotgreen, bluesilver, rust: coloured signs. Limits of the diaphane. But he adds: in bodies. Then he was aware of them bodies before of them coloured. How? By knocking his sconce against them, sure. Go easy. Bald he was and a millionaire, maestro di color che sanno. Limit of the diaphane in. Why in? Diaphane, adiaphane.

CURRENT Modern E:
The inescapable reality of the visible: at least that much, if nothing more, thought through my eyes. Signatures of all things I am here to read — seaweed and wrack, the nearing tide, that rusty boot. Snot-green, blue-silver, rust: colored signs. Limits of the transparent. But Aristotle adds: in bodies. So he was aware of them as bodies before he saw their colors. How? By banging his head against them, of course. Take it easy. Bald he was and a millionaire, the master of those who know. The limit of transparency. Why through? Transparent, opaque.

PROPOSED:
The inescapable reality of the visible: at least that much, if nothing more, thought through my eyes. Signatures of all things I am here to read — seaweed and wrack, the nearing tide, that rusty boot. Snot-green, blue-silver, rust: colored signs. Limits of the transparent. But he adds: in bodies. So he was aware of them as bodies before he saw their colors. How? By banging his head against them, of course. Take it easy. Bald he was and a millionaire, maestro di color che sanno. The limit of transparency. Why through? Transparent, opaque.

(Note: "he" is Stephen's recollection of Aristotle, never named on the page here — this is Stephen half-quoting Aristotle's De Anima from memory. "maestro di color che sanno" is Dante's Italian phrase for Aristotle, Inferno IV.131 — a quotation embedded verbatim in Joyce's English, not English prose in need of modernizing.)

## Case 4 — Joyce, Ulysses, "Penelope" (Episode 18), opening paragraph (excerpt)

ORIGINAL:
...doing his highness to make himself interesting for that old faggot Mrs Riordan that he thought he had a great leg of and she never left us a farthing all for masses for herself and her soul greatest miser ever was actually afraid to lay out 4d for her methylated spirit...

CURRENT Modern E:
...doing his highness to make himself interesting for that old faggot Mrs Riordan that he thought he had a great connection with and she never left us a penny all for masses for herself and her soul greatest miser ever was actually afraid to spend fourpence for her methylated spirit...

PROPOSED:
...doing his highness to make himself interesting for that old faggot Mrs Riordan that he thought he had a great thing for her and she never left us a penny all for masses for herself and her soul greatest miser ever was actually afraid to spend fourpence for her methylated spirit...

(Note: "a great leg of" is obscure period Dublin slang (roughly "fancied/had a thing for"); "farthing"/"4d" are period currency the current rendering already modernizes reasonably. The disputed phrase is "great connection with" — abstract, office-register English — versus "great thing for her," which keeps Molly's blunt, bodily, un-euphemistic register.)
