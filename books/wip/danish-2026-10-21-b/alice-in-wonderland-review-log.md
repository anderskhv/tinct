## Review method (read this first)

The brief asked for a second, independent review pass per chapter by a subagent that sees only the English and the Danish. **This run had no subagent tool available** (this agent had no Agent/Task tool; I did not start separate cloud sessions as reviewers without explicit authorisation). The second pass was therefore done by the translator, not independently:

1. Automated per-paragraph pass over EN/DA pairs: paragraph count, line-break count (verse lines), emphasis-marker parity, English-word heuristic, and every paragraph of 15+ words whose Danish is under 85% of the English word count or has two or more fewer sentences. Each flagged pair was read side by side.
2. A read of the Danish for stiff phrasing and anglicisms, and of every pun and verse against the English.

Status: **self-reviewed, not independently reviewed.** An independent EN/DA read remains open for the integration step or a follow-up run.
