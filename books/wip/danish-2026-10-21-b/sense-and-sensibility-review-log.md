## Register and address

- Austen's ironic narrator kept dry and balanced; long periodic sentences are allowed where the modern English keeps them.
- De/Dem between adults in society, including Elinor and Edward, Marianne and Edward, everyone and Colonel Brandon, Mrs. Jennings and the sisters, Lucy and Elinor.
- du within the family (mother and daughters, the sisters, John and his sisters, spouses such as John and Fanny or the Palmers), and between Marianne and Willoughby in private once they are intimate (from ch12). In public at the ball (ch28) Willoughby retreats to a cold De while Marianne still says du; this mirrors the scene rather than adding to it.
- Edward and Elinor move to du after their engagement (ch49).
- Mrs. Jennings's "my dear" = "min pige"; "ma'am" = "frue"; "Mama" = "mor".
- Lucy's and Anne Steele's grammatical slips are kept lightly in Danish ("min søster og mig", "Anne og mig", "hun havde forærede os", "Edward han har"). Thomas the servant speaks plainly.
- Units and money are not converted (miles, fod, alen, pund, guineas, shilling).

## Names and recurring terms

- Kept: Norland Park, Barton Park, Barton Cottage (also "huset"/"det lille hus"), Allenham (Court), Combe Magna, Cleveland, Delaford, Whitwell, Stanhill; London street names; all personal names.
- Mr./Mrs./Miss = hr./fru/frøken; the eldest sister is "frøken Dashwood", the others "frøken Marianne", "frøken Margaret". Colonel = oberst. Sir John and Lady Middleton keep their titles; "her ladyship" = "hendes nåde".
- living = præstekald; parsonage = præstegård; curacy = kapellanstilling; annuity = livrente.
- beau(x) (Anne Steele's word) = kavaler(er); "setting one's cap at" = "lægge an på"; "making a conquest" = "gøre en erobring".
- "draw him in" (Fanny about Elinor, ch4) = "tegne ham ind i sine planer", keeping the drawing pun.
- "One shoulder of mutton drives another down" (Mrs. Jennings, ch30) = the Danish proverb »Den ene kile driver den anden ud«.
- The overheard sentence in ch39 is rendered identically in both places (»Jeg er bange for, at det ikke bliver muligt ret snart.«) so the misunderstanding still works.
