# Act 4, Scene 5 — A public road

[0] Enter Petruchio, Katherina, Hortensio and Servants.

[1] PETRUCHIO. Come on, in God's name — once more toward our father's house. Good Lord, how bright and lovely the moon shines!

[2] KATHERINA. The moon! It's the sun — it is not moonlight now.

[3] PETRUCHIO. I say it's the moon that shines so brightly.

[4] KATHERINA. I know it's the sun that shines so brightly.

[5] PETRUCHIO. Now by my mother's son — and that's myself — it shall be moon, or star, or whatever I please, before I travel another step toward your father's house. Go on and fetch our horses back again. Always crossed and crossed — nothing but crossed!

[6] HORTENSIO. Say what he says, or we'll never go.

[7] KATHERINA. Forward, please, since we've come so far. And let it be moon, or sun, or whatever you please. And if you please to call it a rush-candle, from now on I vow it shall be so for me.

[8] PETRUCHIO. I say it is the moon.

[9] KATHERINA. I know it is the moon.

[10] PETRUCHIO. No, then you lie — it's the blessed sun.

[11] KATHERINA. Then, God be blessed, it is the blessed sun. But it is not the sun when you say it is not, and the moon changes just as your mind does. Whatever you wish it to be called, that is what it is — and so it shall be for Katherine.

[12] HORTENSIO. Petruchio, carry on — the battle is won.

[13] PETRUCHIO. Well, forward, forward! That's how the bowl should run, and not unluckily against the bias. But, soft! Company is coming this way.

[14] Enter Vincentio, in travelling dress.

[15] [_To Vincentio._] Good morning, gentle mistress — where are you going?

[16] Tell me, sweet Kate, and tell me truly — have you ever seen a fresher young woman? Such a war of white and red in her cheeks! What stars adorn the heavens with such beauty as those two eyes that grace that heavenly face? Fair lovely maid, once more good day to you. Sweet Kate, embrace her for her beauty's sake.

[17] HORTENSIO. He'll make the man mad, turning him into a woman.

[18] KATHERINA. Young budding virgin, fair and fresh and sweet — where are you going, or where is your home? Happy are the parents of so fair a child. Happier still the man whom favorable stars assign you to be his lovely bedfellow.

[19] PETRUCHIO. Why, how now, Kate! I hope you're not mad: this is a man — old, wrinkled, faded, withered — and not a maiden, as you say he is.

[20] KATHERINA. Pardon me, old father, for my mistaking eyes — they've been so dazzled by the sun that everything I look at seems green. Now I see you're a reverend father. Pardon me, I pray you, for my foolish mistake.

[21] PETRUCHIO. Do, good old grandsire — and also tell us which way you're travelling. If it's along with us, we'll be glad of your company.

[22] VINCENTIO. Fair sir, and you my merry mistress — who much amazed me with your strange encounter — my name is Vincentio. My home is Pisa, and I'm bound for Padua to visit a son of mine whom I haven't seen in a long while.

[23] PETRUCHIO. What is his name?

[24] VINCENTIO. Lucentio, gentle sir.

[25] PETRUCHIO. Well met — and even better met for your son's sake. And now, by law as well as by your venerable age, I may rightfully call you my loving father: this gentlewoman, sister to my wife, your son has by now married. Don't be astonished, and don't be grieved — she is of good standing, her dowry generous, and her birth worthy. Besides, she is so qualified as to befit the wife of any noble gentleman. Let me embrace old Vincentio — and let us go together to see your honest son, who will be overjoyed at your arrival.

[26] VINCENTIO. But is this true? Or is it simply your pleasure, like jolly travellers, to play a joke on the company you've overtaken?

[27] HORTENSIO. I assure you, father, it is so.

[28] PETRUCHIO. Come, come along, and see the truth of it for yourself — our first piece of fun has made you suspicious.

[29] [_Exeunt all but Hortensio._]

[30] HORTENSIO. Well, Petruchio, this has put fresh heart into me. Off to my widow! And if she's contrary, then you've taught Hortensio how to be just as contrary in return.

[31] [_Exit._]
