# Act 5, Scene 1 — Padua. Before Lucentio’s house

[0] Enter on one side Biondello, Lucentio and Bianca; Gremio walking on other side.

[1] BIONDELLO. Softly and swiftly, sir, for the priest is ready.

[2] LUCENTIO. I fly, Biondello; but they may chance to need thee at home, therefore leave us.

[3] BIONDELLO. Nay, faith, I’ll see the church o’ your back; and then come back to my master’s as soon as I can.

[4] [_Exeunt Lucentio, Bianca and Biondello._]

[5] GREMIO. I marvel Cambio comes not all this while.

[6] Enter Petruchio, Katherina, Vincentio and Attendants.

[7] PETRUCHIO. Sir, here’s the door; this is Lucentio’s house: My father’s bears more toward the market-place; Thither must I, and here I leave you, sir.

[8] VINCENTIO. You shall not choose but drink before you go. I think I shall command your welcome here, And by all likelihood some cheer is toward.

[9] [_Knocks._]

[10] GREMIO. They’re busy within; you were best knock louder.

[11] Enter Pedant above, at a window.

[12] PEDANT. What’s he that knocks as he would beat down the gate?

[13] VINCENTIO. Is Signior Lucentio within, sir?

[14] PEDANT. He’s within, sir, but not to be spoken withal.

[15] VINCENTIO. What if a man bring him a hundred pound or two to make merry withal?

[16] PEDANT. Keep your hundred pounds to yourself: he shall need none so long as I live.

[17] PETRUCHIO. Nay, I told you your son was well beloved in Padua. Do you hear, sir? To leave frivolous circumstances, I pray you tell Signior Lucentio that his father is come from Pisa, and is here at the door to speak with him.

[18] PEDANT. Thou liest: his father is come from Padua, and here looking out at the window.

[19] VINCENTIO. Art thou his father?

[20] PEDANT. Ay, sir; so his mother says, if I may believe her.

[21] PETRUCHIO.

[22] [_To Vincentio_]  Why, how now, gentleman! why, this is flat knavery to

[23] take upon you another man’s name.

[24] PEDANT. Lay hands on the villain: I believe a means to cozen somebody in this city under my countenance.

[25] Re-enter Biondello.

[26] BIONDELLO. I have seen them in the church together: God send ’em good shipping! But who is here? Mine old master, Vincentio! Now we are undone and brought to nothing.

[27] VINCENTIO.

[28] [_Seeing Biondello._] Come hither, crack-hemp.

[29] BIONDELLO. I hope I may choose, sir.

[30] VINCENTIO. Come hither, you rogue. What, have you forgot me?

[31] BIONDELLO. Forgot you! No, sir: I could not forget you, for I never saw you before in all my life.

[32] VINCENTIO. What, you notorious villain! didst thou never see thy master’s father, Vincentio?

[33] BIONDELLO. What, my old worshipful old master? Yes, marry, sir; see where he looks out of the window.

[34] VINCENTIO. Is’t so, indeed?

[35] [_He beats Biondello._]

[36] BIONDELLO. Help, help, help! here’s a madman will murder me.

[37] [_Exit._]

[38] PEDANT. Help, son! help, Signior Baptista!

[39] [_Exit from the window._]

[40] PETRUCHIO. Prithee, Kate, let’s stand aside and see the end of this controversy.

[41] [_They retire._]

[42] Re-enter Pedant, below; Baptista, Tranio and Servants.

[43] TRANIO. Sir, what are you that offer to beat my servant?

[44] VINCENTIO. What am I, sir! nay, what are you, sir? O immortal gods! O fine villain! A silken doublet, a velvet hose, a scarlet cloak, and a copatain hat! O, I am undone! I am undone! While I play the good husband at home, my son and my servant spend all at the university.

[45] TRANIO. How now! what’s the matter?

[46] BAPTISTA. What, is the man lunatic?

[47] TRANIO. Sir, you seem a sober ancient gentleman by your habit, but your words show you a madman. Why, sir, what ’cerns it you if I wear pearl and gold? I thank my good father, I am able to maintain it.

[48] VINCENTIO. Thy father! O villain! he is a sailmaker in Bergamo.

[49] BAPTISTA. You mistake, sir; you mistake, sir. Pray, what do you think is his name?

[50] VINCENTIO. His name! As if I knew not his name! I have brought him up ever since he was three years old, and his name is Tranio.

[51] PEDANT. Away, away, mad ass! His name is Lucentio; and he is mine only son, and heir to the lands of me, Signior Vincentio.

[52] VINCENTIO. Lucentio! O, he hath murdered his master! Lay hold on him, I charge you, in the Duke’s name. O, my son, my son! Tell me, thou villain, where is my son, Lucentio?

[53] TRANIO. Call forth an officer.

[54] Enter one with an Officer.

[55] Carry this mad knave to the gaol. Father Baptista, I charge you see that he be forthcoming.

[56] VINCENTIO. Carry me to the gaol!

[57] GREMIO. Stay, officer; he shall not go to prison.

[58] BAPTISTA. Talk not, Signior Gremio; I say he shall go to prison.

[59] GREMIO. Take heed, Signior Baptista, lest you be cony-catched in this business; I dare swear this is the right Vincentio.

[60] PEDANT. Swear if thou darest.

[61] GREMIO. Nay, I dare not swear it.

[62] TRANIO. Then thou wert best say that I am not Lucentio.

[63] GREMIO. Yes, I know thee to be Signior Lucentio.

[64] BAPTISTA. Away with the dotard! to the gaol with him!

[65] VINCENTIO. Thus strangers may be haled and abus’d: O monstrous villain!

[66] Re-enter Biondello, with Lucentio and Bianca.

[67] BIONDELLO. O! we are spoiled; and yonder he is: deny him, forswear him, or else we are all undone.

[68] LUCENTIO.

[69] [_Kneeling._] Pardon, sweet father.

[70] VINCENTIO. Lives my sweetest son?

[71] [_Biondello, Tranio and Pedant run out._]

[72] BIANCA.

[73] [_Kneeling._] Pardon, dear father.

[74] BAPTISTA. How hast thou offended? Where is Lucentio?

[75] LUCENTIO. Here’s Lucentio, Right son to the right Vincentio; That have by marriage made thy daughter mine, While counterfeit supposes blear’d thine eyne.

[76] GREMIO. Here ’s packing, with a witness, to deceive us all!

[77] VINCENTIO. Where is that damned villain, Tranio, That fac’d and brav’d me in this matter so?

[78] BAPTISTA. Why, tell me, is not this my Cambio?

[79] BIANCA. Cambio is chang’d into Lucentio.

[80] LUCENTIO. Love wrought these miracles. Bianca’s love Made me exchange my state with Tranio, While he did bear my countenance in the town; And happily I have arriv’d at the last Unto the wished haven of my bliss. What Tranio did, myself enforc’d him to; Then pardon him, sweet father, for my sake.

[81] VINCENTIO. I’ll slit the villain’s nose that would have sent me to the gaol.

[82] BAPTISTA.

[83] [_To Lucentio._] But do you hear, sir? Have you married my daughter

[84] without asking my good will?

[85] VINCENTIO. Fear not, Baptista; we will content you, go to: but I will in, to be revenged for this villainy.

[86] [_Exit._]

[87] BAPTISTA. And I to sound the depth of this knavery.

[88] [_Exit._]

[89] LUCENTIO. Look not pale, Bianca; thy father will not frown.

[90] [_Exeunt Lucentio and Bianca._]

[91] GREMIO. My cake is dough, but I’ll in among the rest; Out of hope of all but my share of the feast.

[92] [_Exit._]

[93] Petruchio and Katherina advance.

[94] KATHERINA. Husband, let’s follow to see the end of this ado.

[95] PETRUCHIO. First kiss me, Kate, and we will.

[96] KATHERINA. What! in the midst of the street?

[97] PETRUCHIO. What! art thou ashamed of me?

[98] KATHERINA. No, sir; God forbid; but ashamed to kiss.

[99] PETRUCHIO. Why, then, let’s home again. Come, sirrah, let’s away.

[100] KATHERINA. Nay, I will give thee a kiss: now pray thee, love, stay.

[101] PETRUCHIO. Is not this well? Come, my sweet Kate: Better once than never, for never too late.

[102] [_Exeunt._]
