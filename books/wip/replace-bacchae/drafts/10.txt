Chorus. Rise, swift hell-hounds of Madness! Race into the mountain valleys where Kadmus' daughters celebrate. Sting them into fury so they tear apart the man who has come dressed as a woman to spy on the Bacchanals, the madness-stricken fool! His mother will see him first, watching their actions from a rock worn by water or a tree split by storms. Her cry will ring down to the Maenads: "Who has come here along the mountain paths to spy on Kadmus' daughters, the girls who race across the hills, our Bacchanal sisters? What mother gave birth to such a creature? Who was she, who? I think no woman's blood produced him. He must have sprung from a lioness, the forest's scourge, or perhaps from those demons, the Gorgons of Libya." Justice, approach us, come close with your avenging sword. Kill this unrighteous offspring of earth-born Echion. Cut clean through his throat: he fears neither God nor law.

Chorus. See how he comes, impious and lawless, driven mad by spite, to fight your rites and your mother's, Bacchus, trying with his powerless strength to overthrow what cannot be conquered! A person can win a life without sorrow by keeping the spirit sober and obeying heaven promptly, without complaint or attempts to reach beyond a mortal's limits. I desire no such presumptuous cleverness. I seek instead—and the search itself makes me happy—the things it cannot know: the great, glorious wisdom revealed from everlasting days. I would shape my life in purity and holiness, pursue noble aims from dawn until the day's death, honour the Gods and refuse the road of injustice. Justice, approach us, come close with your avenging sword. Kill this unrighteous offspring of earth-born Echion. Cut clean through his throat: he fears neither God nor law.

Chorus. Dionysus, show yourself! Appear as a bull, or take the form of a dragon, a many-headed monster, or a lion whose limbs are wrapped in dazzling flame. Come, Bacchus, and smile in mockery as you surround him with destruction's net. Let the hunter be bound among the crowded Maenads, caught by the quarry he set out to find. (A Messenger enters.)
