Chorus. Will my white feet shine in the dancing through another whole night? Will I drift again through the Bacchanal's ecstatic dream, tossing my head into the dew-filled air? Like a fawn playing happily in green pastures after escaping the terror of pursuit, fleeing past the watchers and over their cunningly woven, deadly nets. The huntsmen shout encouragement to the racing dogs, while she bounds desperately across the broad river plain, straining every muscle in bursts of storm-swift speed. At last the trees close their sheltering arms around her, their leafy hair enfolding her in twilight. Happy creature, rescued and hidden from her enemies in the lonely forest where no human follows! What crowning wisdom, what more glorious prize can the Gods grant mortals than to stretch a victorious hand above an enemy's head? Honour, I know, remains precious forever.

Chorus. Heaven's power advances slowly, yet never misses its aim. It teaches a harsh lesson to those who, in their mad imaginings, exalt their disbelief and enthrone it as king: mortals daring to diminish divine things! The Gods wait in a subtle ambush. Time walks onwards, but they never abandon their purpose; merciless fate hunts like a tracking hound until it finds the godless. Neither in our secret thoughts nor in our actions may we put ourselves above the law of established custom. Faith's precious inheritance costs little: trusting the sovereign authority of what heaven sends and of what human nature has sanctioned through age after age. What crowning wisdom, what more glorious prize can the Gods grant mortals than to stretch a victorious hand above an enemy's head? Honour, I know, remains precious forever.

Chorus. Blessed is the one who escapes the ravenous sea and reaches a peaceful harbour; blessed the one who succeeds after labour and struggle. One person rises higher in wealth or power than another, yet countless hopes still burn in countless hearts. Some ripen into fulfilment; others end in nothing. Happy the person whose joy increases with each passing day. (Dionysus enters.)
