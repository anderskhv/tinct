# Independent editorial review

An independent reviewer read all 324 source/modern paragraph pairs, including the full messenger reports and theological passages. No omitted narrative blocks or names were found. Five suggested corrections were applied: explicit first-person identity in 6:6; unexpected rather than unwished events in 11:115; redundant phrasing at 5:18; removal of an added bloodshed target in 7:67; clearer anatomical phrasing at 9:10. The independent reviewer rechecked all five final revisions against the original and confirmed them; opening stage direction in both editions was also confirmed. No remaining review issues.

Review used only the Way candidate and fresh modern draft. No protected or alternate translation was consulted.
