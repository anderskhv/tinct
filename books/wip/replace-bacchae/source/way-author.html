Arthur Sanders Way
(1847–1930)
→
  • sister projects: Wikipedia article, Commons category, Wikidata item

Australian classical scholar, translator and headmaster of Wesley College

1462606Q4800630Arthur Sanders WayArthur SandersWayArthur Sanders WayAustralian classical scholar, translator and headmaster of Wesley College

Works

[edit]
  • The Odes of Horace, literally translated in Metre (1875)
  • The Odyssey of Homer done into English Verse (by Avia, 1880);
  • Homer's Iliad in English Verse (1885)
  • The Tragedies of Euripides, in English verse, 3 vols. (1894–1898)
    (transcription volumes: 1, 2, 3)
  • The Tale of the Argonauts (1901)
  • Æschylus, in English verse, 3 vols. (1906–1908)
    (external scans (multiple parts): 1, 2, 3)
  • Sophocles, in English verse, 2 vols. (1909, 1914)
  • The Fall of Troy by Quintus Smyrnaeus in Loeb Classical Library (1913)
  • Sappho and the Vigil of Venus (1920)
  • Pindar, in English verse (1922) (transcription project)
  • Lucretius on the Problem of Existence. In English verse (1933)
