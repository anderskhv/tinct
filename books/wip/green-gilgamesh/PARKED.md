### A. Sexual-content softening — STILL OPEN

1. **T1P13 / T1P14 — Shamhat's epithets erased.** Source names her "the
   ensnarer" three times and "the siren" once; the candidate has none of
   them, using "the harlot" or "she". T1P13 also drops "succumbed to her
   charms", "with her abundance", "in love enthralled", and "to the feet
   of the harlot". T1P13 is one of the three paragraphs the fix pass
   edited — the explicit verb was restored while the characterization the
   source builds around her was left erased.
2. **T1P11.** "uncover thy nakedness **that he may receive thy favours**"
   → "Uncover yourself so that he may **see** you." The sexual offer is
   reduced to being looked at. "lay hold upon his soul" → "take hold of
   his attention" (and, in the matching execution line T1P12, "captured
   his attention").
3. **T1P9, T1P10.** "tear off her clothing and **disclose her nakedness**"
   → "remove her clothing and reveal her **body**" (both occurrences).
4. **T1P15.** "forever ready **to take to sheets forever spread on the
   couches of the night**" → "always ready for **the pleasures of the
   night**."
5. **T6P1.** Ishtar's proposition: "give, O give unto me thy **manly**
   strength" → "Give me your strength." The identical source phrase is
   kept as "manly strength" at T6P4, so this is inconsistent softening of
   the one line where Ishtar propositions Gilgamesh directly. The same
   sentence also replaces source's "To win the favor and love of
   Gilgamesh, Ishtar … desired" with the invented "gazed upon the beauty
   of Gilgamesh and desired him."
6. **T6P16.** "her followers, **that ruin men**, the **hierodules** and
   the sacred prostitutes" → "her followers — the **temple women** and
   sacred prostitutes." The characterizing clause is dropped and
   "hierodule" is replaced by the same softening "temple" vocabulary
   round 2 was called for.

### B. Repeated ritual invocation collapsed — same class as T2P7, not swept

The fix pass treated T2P7's collapsed repeated invocation as blocking and
restored it, but did not look for the pattern elsewhere.

- **T8P7.** Source repeats "displayed them to Shamash, praying that the
  gods would welcome Enkidu and walk beside him" after **each of five**
  paired offerings (Ishtar/Namra-sit, Ereshkigal/Dumuzi,
  Namtar/Hushbisha, Quassu-tabat/Ninshuluhha, Bibbu/Dumuzi-abzu). The
  candidate states it once, as a trailing summary. This is the funeral
  liturgy; the repetition *is* the rite.
- **T2P15.** Source's doubled question ("Why do you desire to do this
  thing that is exceedingly difficult? Why dost thou desire to do this
  thing that is very difficult?") is collapsed to one.

### C. New defect classes found in this pass

7. **Meaning inversion — T2P25.** Source: "I will establish my name, for
   **before Gilgamesh has fallen the corpse of Humbaba**, the terrible
   one!" (Gilgamesh kills Humbaba.) Candidate: "**If I fall before
   Humbaba**, I will have established my name. People will say that
   Gilgamesh fell fighting the terrible Humbaba." This reverses who dies
   and invents a whole sentence.
8. **Wording imported from a different translation — T11P23.** Source has
   Utnapishtim say "**Now sleep!**" The candidate substitutes "**Let me
   test you. Try not to sleep.**" — the standard scholarly stay-awake
   reading of the test, which this source does not contain. This is the
   exact batch-1 carried-forward failure lesson.
9. **Invented/altered detail inside a just-fixed paragraph — T6P4.**
   - "a battering ram that destroys **all** walls" → "destroys the walls
     of **allies**" (invented; contradicts the insult's logic).
   - "**seven and seven times** didst thou dig a snaring pit" → "you dug
     **seven** pits."
   - "continually brought unto thee **dainties**" → "brought you
     **fruit** every day."
   - "**its wing** thou didst break and crush" → "broke its **wings**",
     while still quoting the bird's cry "O my wing!"
   - "a shoe that **cuts the feet** of its wearer" → "**blisters the
     foot**."
10. **Whole sentences dropped.**
    - T7P1: "For me is only the land of the dead, where I shall sit
      forever." — absent.
    - T10P7: "And if thou shouldst reach the Waters of Death, what
      wouldst thou do?" — absent.
11. **Proper noun / term losses of the round-1 class — T3P9.** "Ea of the
    **Apsu** sea below" → "Ea of the deep sea" (Apsu is preserved at
    T11P6, so this is an inconsistency, not a policy); "rule the
    **black-headed** people with Irnina" → "rule the people with Irnina."
12. **Unit inconsistency.** "double-hour" is preserved 12 times but
    genericized to "a great distance" at T2P24 and T2P30 (and elsewhere),
    while the acceptance record lists the unit as deliberately preserved.
