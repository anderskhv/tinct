## Index 9

**Findings, four in this paragraph:**
1. Editor ruling 2 — pronoun ambiguity: "he was saved" reads as pointing to "the fifth prisoner," just named, who is in fact about to be shot; the true referent is Pierre. Smallest fix: added "himself" ("he himself was saved").
2. Fidelity MINOR — "gazed" recurs at paragraph 16 ("Pierre gazed now with dazed eyes"); "watched" here broke the pairing. Restored "gazed."
3. Fidelity MINOR — source alternates "coat" (index 2)/"cloak" (here and index 10); candidate had silently harmonized to "coat." Restored "cloak."
4. Fidelity MODERATE — "incredible" softened to "hard to believe" and the normative "should kill him" shifted to the capability "could kill him," dulling the chapter's disbelief motif (echoing index 6, "They could not believe it"). Applied the review's proposed language: "unbelievable that men would kill him."
