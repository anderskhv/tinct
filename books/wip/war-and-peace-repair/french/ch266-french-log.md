# Chapter 266 — French pass log

## Paragraph 0

**Before:** Nine days after the abandonment of Moscow, a messenger from Kutuzov reached Petersburg with the official announcement of that event. This messenger was Michaud, a Frenchman who did not know Russian, but who was, as he said of himself, "though a foreigner, Russian in heart and soul." *

**After:** Nine days after the abandonment of Moscow, a messenger from Kutuzov reached Petersburg with the official announcement of that event. This messenger was Michaud, a Frenchman who did not know Russian, but who was, as he said of himself (in French), "though a foreigner, Russian in heart and soul."

## Paragraph 1

**Before:** * Though a foreigner, Russian in heart and soul.

**After:** * Quoique étranger, russe de cœur et d'âme.

## Paragraph 2

**Before:** The Emperor at once received this messenger in his study at the palace on Stone Island. Michaud, who had never seen Moscow before the campaign and who did not know Russian, was nevertheless deeply moved (as he wrote) when he appeared before "our most gracious sovereign" with the news of the burning of Moscow, "whose flames illumined his route." *(2)

**After:** The Emperor at once received this messenger in his study at the palace on Stone Island.
