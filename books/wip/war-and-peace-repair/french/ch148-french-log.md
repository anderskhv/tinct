# Chapter 148 — French pass log

## Paragraph 3

**Before:** Metivier, who came that morning with his congratulations, considered it proper in his capacity as doctor to force his way past the barrier, as he explained to Princess Mary, and went in to see the prince. It happened that on this name-day morning the prince was in one of his worst moods. He had been going around the house since dawn finding fault with everyone and pretending not to understand what was said to him and not to be understood himself. Princess Mary knew this mood of quiet, absorbed grouchiness well — it generally built to an explosion of rage — and she went about all morning as though walking past a loaded gun, waiting for the inevitable shot. Until the doctor's arrival the morning had passed safely. After admitting the doctor, Princess Mary sat in the drawing room with a book near the door through which she could hear everything from the study.
