## Paragraph 10

**Before:** Berg smiled with a sense of superiority over his weak-natured wife and paused, reflecting that this dear wife of his was after all only a weak woman who could not understand everything that constitutes a man's dignity — what it means to be a man. Vera, at the same time, smiled with a sense of superiority over her good, conscientious husband, who nevertheless understood life incorrectly, as in Vera's view all men did. Berg, judging by his wife, thought all women weak and foolish. Vera, judging by her husband alone and generalizing from that, believed that all men, though they understand nothing and are conceited and selfish, credit only themselves with common sense.
