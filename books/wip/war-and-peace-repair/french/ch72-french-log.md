## Paragraph 24

**Before:** "I'll tell you the whole secret of dueling in two words. If you're going to fight a duel and you sit down to write your will and tender letters to your parents, thinking you might be killed — you're a fool and you're done for. But if you go with the firm intention of killing your opponent as quickly and surely as possible, then everything will be fine. That's what our bear hunter back in Kostroma used to tell me: 'Everyone's afraid of a bear,' he'd say, 'but once you see one, all your fear vanishes — your only thought is not to let him get away!' That's how it is with me. À demain, mon cher."
