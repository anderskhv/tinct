## Defect detail — Chapter 186

**Defect: place-name inconsistency.** In paragraph 14 (0-indexed 13), the modern-en text read:

> "...awarded a Saint George's Cross of the Fourth Class for courage shown in the **Ostrovnoe engagement**..."

Source (Maude): "...awarded a St. George's Cross of the Fourth Class for courage shown in **the Ostróvna affair**..."

The project consistently renders this place as "Ostrovna" elsewhere in the same batch (Ch. 181: "orders to move on to the little town of Ostrovna"; Ch. 182: "After the affair at Ostrovna he was brought into notice..."). "Ostrovnoe" is a different, unrelated place name — this reads as an inserted/incorrect variant that breaks continuity with the surrounding chapters' own usage.

**Fix applied:** changed "the Ostrovnoe engagement" → "the Ostrovna affair" to match the source and the term used consistently elsewhere in this same batch.
