# War and Peace — Batch I Fidelity Check (Chapters 188–209, excl. 195, 200)

Method: every paragraph of the Maude source was read against the corresponding modern-en
paragraph, sentence by sentence, for all 20 chapters. Checked for omitted content, invented
content, meaning inversions, factual/plot distortions, placeholder/broken text, and
character-name normalization (Andrew, Kutuzov, Helene, Nicholas, Mary).

**Result: all 20 chapters were found sound. No genuine fidelity defects were identified.**
`full-batchI-corrected.json` is therefore byte-for-byte identical to
`full-batchI-current-modern-en.json` — no changes were made.
