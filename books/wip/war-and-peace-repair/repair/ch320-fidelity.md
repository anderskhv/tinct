- **The unnamed "him" is a structural device, not a gap.** The source keeps Andrew nameless throughout, and ¶17 states the reason: the two women avoid the name so as not to cheapen what they feel. The narration observes the same silence. The candidate names him at both points, so the device is destroyed twice and ¶17 becomes self-contradictory. The referent is beyond doubt from ¶5; the fault is definiteness, not identification. *Proposal (corrector decides): restore "him"/"of him" at both places.*
