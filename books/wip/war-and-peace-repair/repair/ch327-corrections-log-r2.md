## p5

**Before:** Because they couldn't understand him, all these people assumed it was useless to talk to the old man. He would never grasp the profundity of their plans, they thought; he would answer with his phrases (which they thought were mere phrases) about a "golden bridge" and the impossibility of crossing the frontier with a mob of ragged men. They had heard all that before. And everything he said—that they needed to wait for supplies, that the men had no boots—was so simple, while what they proposed was so complex and clever, that it was evident that he was old and stupid, and that they, though not in command, were military geniuses.
