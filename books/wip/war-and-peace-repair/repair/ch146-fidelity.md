## Directed checks

- **§1 "his benefactor / Joseph Alexeevich".** No invented fact — Joseph Alexeevich Bazdeev is Pierre's benefactor, and the source joins them in the same sentence. But the apposition converts an inference into an assertion and double-names him. Logged MINOR; the drafter's note is accurate as to fact and understates the cost.
- **§11 reordered attribution.** The move itself is clean: the proud judgment, the humble counter-thought, and the attribution "in moments of humility" are all present, with nothing cut. Two side effects: the added "something different" and the curly quotes (both MINOR above).
- **§13 "rival Masonic lodges".** **Cleared.** The sentence already establishes both the Masonic frame ("My brother Masons… they intrigue") and the opposition ("Astraea Lodge against the Manna Seekers"); the gloss adds only the category and no date, biography or motive. Minimal and allowed. No fix.
- **§13 "a priest".** **Cleared.** The baseline had silently dropped the appositive; the candidate restores it exactly as the source has it ("a minister of that same law of love and forgiveness, a priest, gave the soldier a cross to kiss"). Correct restoration.
- **§13 "the fourteenth of June".** **Cleared — no gloss needed.** The source supplies its own point (both sides thank God for beating the other on the same date) and names no battle; any gloss would be a claim the source does not make. Drafter's judgment is right. Nothing else is lost in the sentence.
- **Dropped small words (again/still/only/itself).** That class is intact: "began going to the club **again**" (§1), "**still** lived" (§2), "I am **still** dissatisfied" (§11), "**only** living this way temporarily" (§10), "**Only** after a bottle or two" (§14), "expressed **itself**" (§12). The real drops are elsewhere: "a bit" (§9), "superficially" (§14), "by vehicles" (§2), "of the town" (§12), "the collections for the poor" (§13), "the meaning of which" (§13).
- **Added intensifiers/connectives.** "unbearably" (§1), "real passion" (§1), "very same" (§9), "same state" (§11), "violent attacks" (§12), "too deeply" (§14), "vaguely aware" (§14), "self-satisfied" (§11), "something different" (§11), "ever" (§4).
