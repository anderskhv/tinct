## Whole-chapter notes

- The cattle-herd conceit runs from ¶4 to ¶7 and must stay a single sustained image; it does, and the three classes of historian map onto the three quoted speeches in the right order. The damage is confined to the ¶6 parenthesis, where the candidate keeps the metaphor closed instead of letting Tolstoy break it — the one place in the sequence where the source steps outside.
- Terminology is otherwise consistent: "transfer of the people's collective will" throughout, "historical figures" for the source's alternating "historic persons / historical personages", "Divinity/Divine" and "God" for "the Deity" in ¶17–18 (consistent within the pair).
- The closing move — power defined as a *relation* (¶16), then the two things that must be restored (¶18) — is intact and sets up chapter 7 correctly.
- Register is a good deal brisker than the source ("that's because", "isn't", "ends up") and, within this chapter, uniformly so; nothing there is a fidelity fault, but the drafter should be aware it is a step further from Maude than chapters 357 and 360.
