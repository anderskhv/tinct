## P13

- Before: "and you scattered farmers still kept from your fields by groundless fear—listen!"
  After: "and you scattered farmers still kept out in the fields by groundless fear—listen!"
  Finding: P13 MAJOR — editor ruling: restore the inversion (peasants are out in the open, not kept at home; contradicted the paragraph's own later line about "hiding in the forests").

- Before: "He wishes to end your suffering and return you to your homes and families. Come to us without fear."
  After: "He wishes to end your suffering and return you to your homes and families. Respond, therefore, to his benevolent intentions and come to us without fear."
  Finding: P13 MAJOR (the proclamation's central demand — "respond to his benevolent intentions" — deleted with its connective).

- Before: "And you, peasants—come out of the forests where you hide in terror. Return to your villages without fear, knowing you will find protection!"

  Finding: P13 MODERATE ("villages"→"huts": the proclamation sends peasants home, not to their villages, and "huts" answers "the forests where you are hiding") + P13 MINOR ("lastly," closing the enumeration of addressees, dropped).

- Before: "Craftsmen and industrious workers, return to your trades"
  After: "Craftsmen and industrious artisans, return to your trades"
  Finding: P13 MINOR ("artisans" silently swapped for "workers," used elsewhere for a different group).

- Before: "Markets have been set up in the city where you may bring your surplus produce."
  After: "Markets have been set up in the city where peasants can bring their surplus supplies and the products of the soil."
  Finding: P13 MODERATE (two-item list — supplies and produce of the soil — collapsed to one; addressee shifted from third to second person).

- Before: "The government has ensured free trade through the following measures:"
