## Rulings requested

- **¶9, "his daughter Helene's death"** — MINOR, not MODERATE. The fact is squarely supported by the source two and three paragraphs earlier (¶7 "Countess Hélène Bezúkhova had suddenly died", ¶8 "the death of Hélène"), so the addition states nothing the source does not. It is still an addition, and it costs something: Maude's bare "his daughter's death", set against Prince Vasili's fluent public grief in the same sentence, is colder than the named version.
- **¶6, the untangled "suffered because of the suffering" sentence** — MODERATE. The tangle is doing work: the repetition enacts the relay of feeling from Emperor to courtier. Untangling it would be legitimate; substituting a different cause is not. *(Proposal, corrector decides: "The courtiers suffered because of the suffering that the suspense was causing the Emperor.")*
