## Explicit rulings requested

- **Splitting the nested sentences — one failure, elsewhere clean.** The chapter's three genuinely nested periods are p17, p21 and p27.
  - **p21 passes.** "This unshakable, irrefutable consciousness of freedom, uncontrolled by experiment or argument, recognized by all thinkers and felt by everyone without exception, this consciousness without which no conception of man is possible constitutes the other side of the question" becomes five sentences. Every modifier survives with its scope intact ("uncontrolled by experiment or argument" → "No experiment or argument can touch it"; "by everyone without exception" → "every person without exception"), and no connective is invented — the five sentences are asyndetic, exactly as the source's appositives are. Only "even" is added ("no conception of man is even possible").
  - **p27 fails on the connective.** See the MODERATE finding: "because" is new. The other split in the same paragraph ("They say, and write, and print, that … And they say the soul and free will do not exist because …") is faithful; Maude's semicolon carries the same verb forward and the candidate simply restates it.
  - **p17 fails on the split itself.** See the MODERATE finding, plus the dropped modal "may".
  - **p28 splits cleanly except for the "and" → "But" substitution (MINOR above).**
- **No qualifier dropped in a split except p17's "may".** Checked every split boundary: p17, p21, p27, p28. "however incontestably … may prove" (p17), "however impossible it may be" (p17), "(and can but recognize)" (p10), "quite apart from and independent of" (p8) and "in some given case" constructions all survive with their hedges.
- **Every "however"-type concession survives.** p4 "If in a thousand years even one man in a million"; p11 "whether he observes the necessity of taking food, using his brain, or anything else"; p17 "However often …" (structurally damaged, content present), "however incontestably reason and experiment may prove", "however impossible it may be"; p20 "If the conception of freedom appears to reason to be a senseless contradiction"; p28 "If men descended from the apes at an unknown period of time". None is dropped and none is converted into an assertion.
