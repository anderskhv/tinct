## Rulings requested

- **"the chancellor Rumyantsev"** — MODERATE. States an office, not a category; unsupported by the source.
- **"the dowager empress Maria Fedorovna"** — MODERATE, on two counts (added rank; wrong name form per conventions).
- **"the Tsarevich (heir to the throne)"** — MINOR, at the edge of acceptable. It glosses what the title itself means and adds no claim about the scene, but it does assert a succession status the source never states, and the Tsarevich here (Constantine) was heir presumptive rather than "the heir".
- **"captured from the French by the Russian general Wittgenstein"** — no finding. A bare category, and the source supports it in the same sentence ("the hero of Petropol", banners taken from the French for Russia).
- **"the inconvenience of having married two husbands"** — the source does **not** say it that plainly. Maude's "an inconvenience resulting from" is the indirection, and removing "resulting from" is what breaks it. See ¶4 above. MAJOR.

## Whole-chapter notes

- The four glosses in ¶1 and ¶14 are the chapter's signature. Three of them are defensible in kind; only "the Russian general Wittgenstein" is clean. The pattern to watch is that each gloss adds a *rank or office*, which is exactly the currency Tolstoy is satirising — a narrator who explains who outranks whom undercuts a paragraph about court parties being "drowned by the buzzing of the court drones".
- The chapter's argumentative spine (¶1 "phantoms and reflections of real life" → ¶2 the reading staged for political effect → ¶4 the unmentionable illness → ¶22 the guests wondering what they are to blame for) survives intact; the losses are local.
- Bilibin's face motif (¶13 "having wrinkled up his face he was evidently preparing to smooth it out again", ¶17 "his brow became smooth again") is preserved here, which is what makes ch 236 ¶18's "pursed his lips" visible as an error.
- Verse-like and quoted material (the Bishop's letter ¶20, ¶24; the diplomatic dispatch ¶17) is preserved nearly verbatim; no dropped sentences anywhere.
- Typography and quote style follow the conventions (unspaced em dashes, straight double quotes). Names follow the conventions table except "Maria Fedorovna".
