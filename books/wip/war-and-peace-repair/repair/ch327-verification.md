### The three seams — all PASS

The review identified three splits where the candidate added material at the join. All three are repaired:

**Seam 1 — ¶0.** Source: "**If** so much has been and still is written about the Berëzina, on the French side this is only because…". The candidate had cut the conditional frame into a free-standing assertion ("So much has been and still is written about the Berezina.") and then started a new sentence. Corrected restores the conditional: "If so much has been and still is written about the Berezina, on the French side this is only because…". The corrector keeps a sentence break later, before "On the Russian side", which is legitimate — it divides rather than adds, and the second clause stands on its own without needing the protasis. Also restored here, all source-exact: "**their** army", "**previously** enduring", "at one moment", "**remained in** every memory" (the candidate's "seared itself into" was an added figure), "a plan (**again** one of Pfuel's)" with "again" back on Pfuel where the source has it, "a **strategic** trap", and "Everyone **assured himself**" — self-persuasion, which is Tolstoy's point, not the candidate's plain "was certain".

**Seam 2 — ¶1.** Source: "proved the fallacy of all the plans for cutting off the enemy's retreat **and** the soundness of the only possible line of action". The candidate had split this and manufactured "**It also confirmed**" — a connective *and* a second verb the source does not have, with "proved" downgraded to "confirmed" on the second half. Corrected restores the single "proved … and …" construction. Correct: this was the one split in the chapter that added rather than divided.
Also in ¶1: "the **fallacy**" (not "bankruptcy"), "the **general** mass of the army", "pressed forward into boats and **into** the ice-covered water", and "**did not surrender**" — all source-exact. The into/onto repair matters: going *into* freezing water rather than surrender is the image; "onto the ice" is a different event. "did not surrender" removes the volition the candidate's "refused to" had added.
The Latin "**vis inertiæ**" is restored in place of "sheer momentum". This is the source's term and the restoration is right on fidelity; as a reader I note it is the chapter's one genuinely opaque phrase, but Maude prints it bare and the surrounding clause ("carried forward by…") carries enough sense for a reader to move on. No change recommended.

**Seam 3 — ¶11.** Source: "…was expressed in the highest degree by the behavior of Chichagóv, **who knew** of the accusations that were being directed against Kutúzov." The candidate's "**as he was well aware** of the accusations" had converted an appended fact into the stated cause of his behaviour; the source sets the two side by side and leaves the connection to the reader. Corrected restores "by the behavior of Chichagov, **who knew** of the accusations". Correct.
