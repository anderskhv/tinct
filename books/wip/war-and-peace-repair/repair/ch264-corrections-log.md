## Paragraph index 1 (¶2)
Finding: MINOR ¶2 — restore "His Lordship the Bishop"; restore "political significance" (was "purpose")
Before: At Anna Pavlovna's on the twenty-sixth of August—the very day of the battle of Borodino—there was an evening party, the chief feature of which was to be the reading of a letter from the Bishop sending the Emperor an icon of the Venerable Sergius. It was considered a model of ecclesiastical, patriotic eloquence. Prince Vasili himself, famous for his delivery, was to read it. (He used to read at the Empress's.) The art of his reading was supposed to lie in rolling out the words, quite independently of their meaning, in a loud and singsong voice alternating between a despairing wail and a tender murmur, so that the wail fell randomly on one word and the murmur on another. This reading, as was always the case at Anna Pavlovna's parties, had a political purpose. That evening she expected several important personages who had to be made ashamed of their visits to the French theater and aroused to a patriotic spirit. A good many people had already arrived, but Anna Pavlovna, not yet seeing everyone she wanted in her drawing room, did not let the reading begin but wound up the springs of general conversation.
