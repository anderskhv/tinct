# Chapter 280 — corrections log

## P0

- Before: "In historical events, the first and most primitive explanation was the will of the gods"
  After: "In historical events (where the actions of men are the subject of observation), the first and most primitive approximation was the will of the gods"
  Finding: P0 MODERATE (parenthesis dropped) + P0 MINOR (approximation→explanation).

- Before: "a person seizes on the first explanation that seems intelligible and says"
  After: "a person seizes on the first approximation that seems intelligible and says"
  Finding: P0 MINOR (approximation→explanation, second instance).

- Before: "But we need only look beneath the surface of any historical event—which really lies in the activity of the masses who take part in it—to see"
  After: "But we need only penetrate to the essence of any historical event—which lies in the activity of the masses who take part in it—to see"
  Finding: P0 MINOR (essence→surface, "really" intensifier added).

- Before: "but knew there were laws governing its motion."
  After: "but knew there were laws directing its movement and that of the other planets."
  Finding: P0 MODERATE ("and that of the other planets" dropped).

- Before: "some of these laws we know while others we cannot yet comprehend."
  After: "some of these laws are known to us while we are conscious of others we cannot comprehend."
  Finding: P0 MINOR ("yet" added; "we are conscious of" dropped).

## P3

## P4

- Before: "the prevailing idea among the Russian commanders was the most natural one: a direct retreat along the Nizhni road."
  After: "the prevailing idea among the Russian commanders was the one naturally suggesting itself, namely, a direct retreat along the Nizhni road."
  Finding: P4 MINOR (superlative replaces description of how the idea arose).

- Before: "This was the first indication that they needed to deviate from the most natural course"
  After: "This was the first indication that they needed to deviate from what had previously seemed the most natural course"
  Finding: P4 MODERATE (hedge "what had previously seemed" dropped).

- Before: "concern for the arsenal at Tula, and the advantage of moving closer to provisions"
  After: "concern for the safety of the arsenal at Tula, and especially the advantage of moving closer to provisions"
  Finding: P4 MINOR ("the safety of" dropped; "especially" dropped, losing the ranking of causes).

- Before: "But countless circumstances forced the army to turn even further south: the reappearance of French troops, plans for giving battle"
  After: "But countless circumstances forced our army to turn even further south: the reappearance of French troops who had for a time lost touch with the Russians, plans for giving battle"
  Finding: P4 MINOR ("our army"→"the army" removes narrator's stance) + P4 MINOR (relative clause on "reappearance" dropped).

- Before: "did people begin assuring themselves that they had planned this movement all along and had foreseen its outcome."
  After: "did people begin assuring themselves that they had desired this movement and long ago foreseen its result."
  Finding: P4 MODERATE ("planned" states the very thing the paragraph argues nobody did; source says "desired").
