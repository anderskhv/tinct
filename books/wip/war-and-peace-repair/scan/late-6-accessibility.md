## Chapter 335 — Book Fifteen (1812–13) — Chapter 18

Counts: clear 57 / hard 1 / unclear 1 (59 paragraphs)

Hard/unclear obstacles:
- Para 18 (unclear): "I've seen the princess," she replied. "I heard they were arranging a match for her with young Rostov. It would be a very good thing for the Rostovs—they say the family's utterly ruined." — Pierre has just asked about Natasha Rostova, but the cousin answers about "the princess" (presumably Princess Mary) and then "her" — it genuinely reads two ways: is "her" Natasha or the princess? Rhythm/voice: Natural modern English elsewhere, including Pierre's long internal monologues, which read fluidly rather than as choppy or archaic.
Unexplained references: "that business of hers" (para 20, see above) is the notable gap; also "Anna Semyonovna" (para 16), minor and non-blocking.
Thread: Pierre delays his trip to Petersburg, visits Natasha and Princess Mary daily, and finally asks Mary to plead his case to Natasha — statable in two sentences, apart from the paragraph-18/20 confusion noted above.
Anachronism/over-explanation: None.

**Verdict: REVISE** — one unclear paragraph (18) whose referent genuinely forks two ways on first reading.
