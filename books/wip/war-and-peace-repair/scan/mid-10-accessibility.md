## Ch. 253 — Book Eleven (1812) — Chapter 24

Counts: clear 16 / hard 4 / unclear 0 (20 paragraphs)

- [0] hard — "He had known Moscow would be abandoned not merely since his meeting with Kutuzov on Poklonny Hill the previous day, but ever since the Battle of Borodino — every general who came to Moscow after that battle unanimously declared it impossible to fight again, and since then government property had been removed every night and half the inhabitants had left with Rostopchin's own permission." One long sentence stacks three separate facts across dashes; requires re-reading to keep them apart.
- [4] hard — "It is obvious that there would have been even less cause for unrest if, after the Battle of Borodino, when Moscow's surrender became certain or at least probable, Rostopchin — instead of stirring up the people by distributing weapons and broadsheets — had taken steps to remove the holy relics, the gunpowder, munitions, and money, and had plainly told the population that the city would be given up." Three nested subordinate clauses (if / when / instead-of) suspend the main clause; hard to hold in mind on first pass.
- [5] hard — "Not only did he believe (as all administrators do) that he controlled the external actions of Moscow's residents, but he also thought he shaped their inner attitudes through his broadsheets and posters, written in a coarse style that the common people despise in their own kind and don't understand coming from those in authority." Long "not only...but also" construction plus an embedded relative clause whose logic (despised in peers, not understood from superiors) takes real unpacking.
- [11] hard — "...From the Consistory, from the Senate, from the University, from the Foundling Hospital, the Suffragan has sent..." "Suffragan" (a rank of bishop) is dropped in with no cue at all; a new reader has nothing to anchor it to.

Rhythm/voice: reads as natural modern English overall; the long analytical sentences keep period essayistic density (multiple nested clauses per sentence) rather than being broken up for ease, but they are not choppy or translationese.
