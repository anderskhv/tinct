Argument/story thread: The old prince suffers a stroke, lingers paralyzed for weeks watched by his daughter, and dies after a final lucid exchange with her; the chapter closes on the household gathering at his coffin. Clear and trackable throughout.

Anachronism/over-explanation: None. No modern idiom intrudes; nothing over-explained.
