## Chapter 231 — Book Eleven (1812) — Chapter 2

- Para 2 (hard): "the Russian army retreated immediately after the clash as inevitably as a ball bounces back after striking another with greater momentum, and with equal inevitability the ball of invasion ... rolled on for some distance" — the extended two-balls metaphor is grammatically ambiguous on first pass (which ball has "greater momentum"?) and needs re-reading to assign French vs. Russian correctly.
