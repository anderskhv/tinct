### Chapter 210 (Book Ten, Ch. 20) — DEFECTIVE (1 fix)
**Defect — meaning shift in Pierre's stated intention (omission/softening of plot content).**
- Source: *"Pierre got out and talked to the doctor, explaining his intention of **taking part in a battle**."*
- Candidate (before fix): *"Pierre got out and talked to the doctor, explaining his intention of **being present at the battle**."*
- Why it's a defect: "taking part in" (participate/fight) was softened to "being
  present at" (merely observe). This changes what Pierre tells the doctor he
  intends to do — a real distinction in a novel where Pierre later (Ch. 222,
  same batch) is unexpectedly swept into hand-to-hand combat at the battery.
  The source's stronger claim is what the doctor is reacting to when he says
  "Why should you be God knows where out of sight, during the battle?"
- Fix applied: restored to *"explaining his intention of taking part in the battle."*

Rest of the chapter (Mozhaysk descent, wounded convoy, doctor's casualty
estimate, Górki militia scene) checked clean.

### Chapter 244 (Book Eleven, Ch. 15) — DEFECTIVE (1 fix)
**Defect — dropped clause (omission of content).**
- Source: *"...orderlies and servants sent by wounded officers came to the Rostóvs' and wounded men dragged themselves there **from the Rostóvs' and** from neighboring houses where they were accommodated, entreating the servants to try to get them a lift out of Moscow."*
- Candidate (before fix): *"...orderlies and servants sent by wounded officers came to the Rostovs', and wounded men dragged themselves there from neighboring houses, begging the servants to try to get them a ride out of Moscow."*
- Why it's a defect: the source names two sources of wounded men converging on
  the major-domo — those already billeted within the Rostovs' own house, and
  those from neighboring houses. The candidate drops the first source
  entirely, leaving only "from neighboring houses," which understates how
  many wounded were already inside the household itself and slightly
  distorts the scale of the situation the major-domo is managing.
- Fix applied: restored to *"...wounded men dragged themselves there from the Rostovs' own house and from neighboring houses, begging the servants..."*

Rest of the chapter (crowd at the Three Hills, price inflation, Count Ilya
Rostov's morning, the countess/count argument over the carts) checked clean.

### Chapter 245 (Book Eleven, Ch. 16) — DEFECTIVE (1 fix)
**Defect — meaning inversion (flipped to the opposite claim).**
- Source: *"Pétya was in the porch, engaged in giving out weapons to the servants who **were to leave Moscow**."*
- Candidate (before fix): *"Petya was on the porch, handing out weapons to the servants who **were staying behind in Moscow**."*
- Why it's a defect: this is a direct inversion — "were to leave Moscow"
  (departing, presumably being armed for the road) was rendered as "were
  staying behind in Moscow" (the opposite group). This is exactly the kind of
  flipped claim the spot-check is designed to catch.
- Fix applied: restored to *"...handing out weapons to the servants who were leaving Moscow."*

Rest of the chapter (Berg's visit and bragging, the Natasha/countess/count
confrontation over the carts, the household mobilizing to load the wounded)
checked clean; no character-name inconsistencies found anywhere in the batch
(Andrew, Helene, Kutuzov, Rostov, etc. all correctly normalized per project
spelling convention — no Andrei/Nikolai/Marya slips).
