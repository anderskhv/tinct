# Evidence across all 99 catalogue books

Research date: 26 September 2026. Counts exclude the Bible. Each book received a general recommendation search and a search restricted to recommendation indexes, followed by source checks for promising matches. The full search logs are retained in this folder.

**Status key.** A = title-specific recommendation, positive rating or reading-list selection found in a primary source or authorized compilation. B = affirmative secondary evidence, pending original-source verification. C = related evidence only (reading, ownership, interpretation, author praise or an unverified lead). N = no eligible title-specific evidence found in this pass. Collection selections are shown separately and never upgrade an individual title. A is evidence strength, not permission to imply endorsement of Tinct or its edition.

Evidence IDs link to the observed source. Consult evidence.json for dates, explanations, original URLs and caveats. Empty results do not establish that no recommendation exists.
