# Fidelity review round 1: chapters 18-23 (Pride and Prejudice, modern-en)

## 1. Coverage
I read every paragraph in this batch on its own, comparing the source with the candidate:
- Ch 18: 18.0-18.76 (77 paragraphs)
- Ch 19: 19.0-19.21 (22)
- Ch 20: 20.0-20.34 (35)
- Ch 21: 21.0-21.27 (28)
- Ch 22: 22.0-22.19 (20)
- Ch 23: 23.0-23.24 (25)

Total: 207 paragraphs. I read them in packets with their neighbouring paragraphs for context. Afterwards I re-read each chapter as a whole to catch problems that cross paragraph boundaries. These include the day sequence (Tuesday ball, Wednesday proposal), how Mrs. Bennet addresses her daughters, and how "living" is rendered (the edition uses "parish" everywhere).

## 2. Proposals (14 total, all blocking)
- 18.13 (qualification): "speaking for your own feelings" becomes "considering your own feelings". The source verb is "consulting".
- 18.58 (qualification): "a week ago Saturday" becomes "a week ago yesterday". The source has "yesterday se'nnight", and the candidate had invented a weekday.
- 18.62 (omission): restores the seating detail "within one of each other" and "that one person (Lady Lucas)".
- 18.74 (tone): Lydia's "God, I'm so tired!" becomes "Lord, I'm so tired!".
- 19.21 (omission): restores "affectation" ("affectation and coquetry").
- 20.0 (irony): restores "he trusted". Without it, Collins's self-satisfaction reads as a fact stated by the narrator.
- 20.29 (omission): "a hundred miles away" becomes "in York", which is the place the source names.
- 21.2 (qualification): "privately admitted" becomes "volunteered ... _had_". This keeps "voluntarily" and the source's emphasis.
- 21.25 (addition): "scheming" becomes "wishes". The source does not suggest plotting, and "those wishes" in the next clause needs "wishes" here to refer back to.
- 22.1 (addition): removes the scare quotes around "accidentally", which spelled out the irony.
- 22.2 (irony): restores "pure and disinterested desire for an establishment". The candidate's "practical and clear-headed" turned the irony into sincere praise.
- 22.4 (qualification): "unlikely ... best" becomes "uncertain ... must be their pleasantest".
- 22.4 (qualification): "could hardly be kept" becomes "was not easily kept". In the source Collins kept the secret, though with difficulty.
- 23.3 (qualification): restores the hedge "the happiness that might be expected from the match".

## 3. Considered and deliberately left alone
- 18.2 "blind devotion to Darcy": the source says only "blind partiality". Reading it as partiality toward Darcy is the standard reading, so I left it.
- 18.7 "ten times his social standing": this is a correct reading of the Gutenberg "often times" (= ten times).
- 18.20: the candidate adds italics to "_your_" friendship. The source already says "with emphasis", so this is harmless.
- 18.31 "careful about what _creates_ it": close enough, and Darcy's "I am" still fits.
- 18.47 "Insolent woman!" (source: "girl"): a synonym shift that doesn't change the meaning.
- 18.52 "parish" for "living": the edition uses "parish" consistently from ch 13 on (15.23, 15.25 and others). Changing it only here would be inconsistent.
- 18.65 / 19.4 "Mama" for "madam"/"ma'am": this looks like an edition convention and doesn't change the meaning.
- 19.9: "government bonds" for "the 4 per cents" and "between hands at cards" for "pools at quadrille" are acceptable clarifications.
- 19.18: "inheritance" for "portion" and "the life I can offer" for "establishment" are borderline. Both stay within the sense of the source, so I left them.
- 20.26 "such excitement" (source: "such fun"): borderline tone, but Lydia's glee still comes through.
- 21.12 "warn me off" (source: "put me on my guard"), 21.17 "cunning" (source: "ingenuity"), and 21.24 "may never be mine to make" (source: "will never be required"): minor, and the meaning survives.
- 22.12, 23.9 "in two weeks' time on Monday" (source: "Monday fortnight"), 23.10 "gentle complaints": the irony is preserved.
- 23.6: "consider happiness improbable" correctly resolves the source's "it".
