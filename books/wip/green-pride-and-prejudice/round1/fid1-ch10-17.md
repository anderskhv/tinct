## 2. Proposals (25 in total: 23 blocking, 2 non-blocking)
- 10.14 "absolutely in love with" -> "quite in raptures over". Restores the word Darcy picks up in 10.15. (tone, non-blocking)
- 10.45 "such a man" -> "so great a man". Rank dropped. (qualification)
- 10.52 "considerably helped along" -> "received some assistance". Ironic understatement inflated. (irony)
- 11.6 "the cook" -> "Nicholls". Name dropped. (omission)
- 11.27 "too unforgiving ... world's liking" -> "too unyielding ... world's convenience". (qualification)
- 11.27 "attempt to manipulate them" -> "attempt to move them". (addition)
- 12.0 "though Elizabeth was eager" -> "at least not to Elizabeth's wishes, for she was eager". The cause was reversed. (qualification)
- 12.2 "knew she was right" -> "felt she was right". (qualification)
- 12.5 "would catch cold" -> "would have caught cold". (qualification)
- 13.7 "most unjust ... taken away" -> "hardest ... entailed away". Entail and Mrs. Bennet's own word restored. (qualification)
- 13.11 "family scruples" -> "scruples as a dutiful son". Restores the meaning of "filial". (qualification)
- 13.25 "left with very little" -> "destitute enough". Restores Mrs. Bennet's exaggeration. (tone)
- 14.13 "Lydia's mouth fell open" -> "Lydia yawned". In period usage "gaped" means yawned. (non-blocking)
- 15.0 "not a clever man" -> "not a sensible man". Echoes 13.20. (qualification)

- 16.36 "is proof of being good" -> "might vouch for your good nature". Hedge restored. (qualification)
- 16.39 "his best quality" -> "his best friend". Wickham's barbed idiom restored. (irony)
- 16.55 "widely believed" -> "believed". (addition)
- 16.56 "destined for another" -> "destined himself for another". Restores "self-destined". (qualification)
- 16.58 "considered first-rate" -> "have a first-rate mind". Restores "understanding". (qualification)
- 16.59 "money she had lost and won" -> "counters she had lost and won". The fish were game counters. (qualification)
- 17.0 "suffered such cruelty" -> "endured such unkindness". Restores Jane's gentleness. (tone)

- 17.1-2 "interested" -> "biased": close enough, and Elizabeth's joke still works.
- 17.1 "I dare say" -> "I'm sure": minor.
