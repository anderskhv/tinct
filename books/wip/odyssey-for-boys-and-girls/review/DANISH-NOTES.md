# The Story of the Odyssey (Church) — “Odyssey for Boys and Girls” — Danish edition notes

- Greek names stay in English spelling, as in the Iliad. Exceptions that remain mixed: *Kyklopen* and *Fæakerne* (Danish) beside *Phaeacia*, *Phaeacians* and *Cyclops* in a few places.
- Pronunciation and note markers `[n]` are copied as in the English.

## General

- Distances: English miles are “engelske mil”; feet, yards and inches are kept.
- Coins and units with no Danish equivalent (groat, farthing, shilling, pottle) are kept.
- Speeches that run across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end.
