# Character identity proposal

`identity-proposal.json` contains 19 proposed identities, source coordinates and alias decisions. It is editorial staging, not a runtime character registry. All coordinates are one-based and refer to the pinned original-en, whose decorative scene breaks are documented separately.

The essential separation is the elder Catherine Earnshaw/Linton from her daughter Catherine Linton/Heathcliff. Both may be called Cathy. Likewise, Edgar Linton is not Linton Heathcliff; Hindley is not Hareton; Nelly is not Zillah. Heathcliff is a mononym, not an unrecorded Earnshaw first name. His exact ancestry is deliberately unresolved.

Married titles, “master,” and bare family surnames cannot be resolved globally. An introductory cast is supplied in onboarding, while later facts in this editorial proposal must be revealed only when the narrative reaches them. The opening frame comes later in story time than Nelly’s account, so a simple date sort is insufficient for spoiler control.

Do not create a real household identity from the dream preacher or the 1500 inscription. Do not turn Joseph’s Yorkshire voice into generic modern standard speech. Narrator reliability, reported prejudice and spectral uncertainty should remain visible in any final character copy.

Claude owns acceptance, runtime schema conversion and registration. No audio roles or generated portraits are included.
