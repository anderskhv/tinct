## Passages I was unsure of

1. **The telegram's word order (18:2–5).** The Russian reads «Меня только что зарезало трамваем», and the explanation is that «меня» replaced «Берлиоза», which "took the form" «Берлиоз» and moved to the end. EN: "I have just been run over by tram…" The explanation reads "The words 'I have' had … strayed in … in place of the words 'Berlioz has,' which had turned into plain 'Berlioz'", so the change of form survives through has/have. Danish verbs do not inflect for person, so DA swaps only «Jeg» for «Berlioz», "som var blevet forskubbet". The case-change detail is lost there.
2. **«дочертыхался» (17:55).** EN: "I always, always stopped him when he swore by the devil! And now he's sworn himself to the devil!" DA: »…når han bandede ved fanden! Og nu har han bandet sig selv lige til fanden!« The devil is kept literally in both, per the motif rule.
3. **«Черный, здоровый, как бегемот» (17:60).** EN "Black, huge, like a behemoth" keeps the pun on the cat's name. Danish *Behemot* is not a common noun, so DA uses »som en flodhest«. The pun is lost in Danish. Check the Behemoth introductions in other batches for consistency.
4. **«Черти чтоб взяли» (17:60).** EN "devils take me!" / "Devils take you? Why not, that can be done!" DA »gid djævlene ville tage mig!« / »Djævlene skal tage Dem? Jamen, det kan godt lade sig gøre!« Both keep the plural "devils", because Woland's people take it literally.
5. **«мощная с хрипотцой октава» (17:74).** «Октава» here means a basso profundo voice, not a musical interval. EN: "basso profundo"; DA: »dybe bas«.
6. **«тише воды, ниже травы» (18:62).** EN keeps the folk image: "quieter than water and lower than grass". DA: »stille som en mus, lavere end græsset«, half idiom and half image.
7. **«подумаешь, бином Ньютона!» (18:148).** EN: "As if it were Newton's binomial theorem!" DA: »det er da ikke Newtons binomialformel!«
8. **«пудов десять» (18:46).** Kept as "ten poods" / »ti pud« for period flavour. A pood is about 16 kg. Decide whether to keep the unit.
9. **«второй свежести» (18:104–107).** EN: "second-grade fresh … There is only one freshness—the first, and it is also the last." DA: »anden friskhedsgrad … Friskhed findes kun i én grad – den første, og den er også den sidste.«
10. **«к лешему» (18:165).** The leshy is a forest spirit. It is kept as a folk curse: "go to the goblins" / »gå til trolden«. It is not devil vocabulary, so it is not rendered as "devil".
11. **«Тузбубен»** ("Ace of Diamonds") is left untranslated, like Bezdomny, so the joke is lost. It could go in onboarding notes.
12. **Tolstoy (18:65).** «Все смешалось в доме Облонских» is our own rendering, "Everything was in confusion in the Oblonskys' house" / »Alt var i forvirring i Oblonskijs hus«. It is not taken from any published *Anna Karenina* translation.
13. **«хоть убей» (17:12).** EN: "not for the life of them"; DA: »ikke for alverden«.
14. **«мессир» / «Рыцарь» (18:91).** Hella's terms of address are kept as titles: Messire (EN, capitalised as a form of address) and messire (DA, lower case, like *hegemon*). Align with the other batches.

## Suspected OCR / print errors in the Russian (not edited)

- **18:78:** «экономистаплановика» should read «экономиста-плановика» (hyphen lost).
- **18:208:** «Ноя не могу понять» should read «Но я не могу понять» (space lost).
- **18:122:** «Господи боже мой!» — подумал нервный, как все буфетчики, Андрей Фокич. — Вот квартирка!» The opening « before «Вот» is missing, so the guillemets are unbalanced. This may be in the print, or it may be OCR. The translations treat the whole of it as one thought.
- **17:10:** «Тузабубен» is not an error. It is Bulgakov's oblique case of the compound name (Туза-бубен).
