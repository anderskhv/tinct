# Batch b8: chapters 25–26 (Modern English and Modern Danish)

- Files: `b8-modern-en.json`, `b8-modern-da.json` (chapters 25 and 26, section "Part Two" / "Anden del").
- Paragraphs: ch. 25 = 131, ch. 26 = 243 in RU, EN and DA (1:1, no merges or splits).
- Words (RU / EN / DA): ch. 25 2,938 / 4,014 / 3,740; ch. 26 6,347 / 8,544 / 8,025.
- Both translations were made directly from the Russian. No published translation was consulted.
- Length screen: 2 EN flags (<1.0×) and 2 DA flags (<0.95×). All four are short lines of dialogue and are complete: EN 25:125, 26:117; DA 25:91, 25:125. A fifth DA flag (26:160) was a real thinning («как же» lost) and was fixed («Åh, hvordan kunne jeg dog ikke forudse det!»). A sentence-count screen found one merge (25:19, «Каков ураган? А?»), now restored as two sentences in both languages.
- Address: Pilate and Aphranius, Pilate and Mark, and Aphranius's report all use *De/Dem/Deres* in Danish. Pilate and Levi, Aphranius and Niza, and Judas and Niza use *du*. In Pilate's dream (26:72–73) the Russian moves from «ты» to «вы» within the speech to the philosopher, and the Danish keeps that move (*du* → *De*). Levi's «Все хотите отнять?» is rendered with plural *I* ("you Romans"), and his «Вы мне его верните» with an imperative that has no pronoun.

## Passages I was unsure of

1. **Judas's last words, «Ни... за...» (26:63).** They can be heard as the beginning of "Niza" or of «ни за что» ("for nothing"). Both languages keep "Ni... za..." exactly, so the ambiguity survives and nothing is explained.
2. **«благодарит и не винит за то, что у него отняли жизнь» → «Кого?» (25:65–66).** The Russian has no object, and Pilate's hollow "Whom?" depends on that. EN: "he was grateful, and did not blame them for taking his life" / "Whom?". DA: «takkede og ikke bebrejdede dem, at de havde taget hans liv» / »Hvem?«. "Them" keeps the question natural. A blank verb would have been ungrammatical in both languages.
3. **«Я слушаю приказания прокуратора» / «Но ничего не услышите, пока не сядете» (25:23–24).** This is a hear/listen play. EN: "I am ready to hear the procurator's orders" / "But you will hear none until you sit down…". DA: «Jeg er rede til at høre…» / »Men De får ingen at høre…«.
4. **«Чего стоил один этот мессия» (25:43).** The idiom means "what a business that messiah alone was". EN: "That messiah alone… what that cost us!" DA: «hvad den har kostet!». "Us" is a light interpretation of the impersonal Russian.
5. **«Я не говорю уже о природе!» (25:43).** Rendered "I am not even speaking of the land itself!" / «Jeg taler slet ikke om selve landet!». "Nature" read badly in English. Pilate means the country and its climate.
6. **«одним из самых главных он считает трусость» (25:69).** EN "one of the very chief", DA «en af de allervigtigste». In the dream (26:72) Pilate's correction «это самый страшный порок» is "the most terrible vice" / «den frygteligste last». Levi's scroll (26:215) has «...большего порока... трусость» → "...greater vice... cowardice" / «...større last... fejhed». The three lines are kept as close variants of each other, because the novel's motif runs through them.
7. **«переложился куда-то» (26:128).** This is an odd surveillance verb, roughly "shifted, switched direction". EN "doubled off somewhere", DA «slog et smut et eller andet sted hen».
8. **«черен» (Levi, 26:188).** It is unclear whether this means dark-skinned/sunburnt or black-haired. Rendered "dark" / «mørk», which covers both.
9. **«Это, конечно, немного сделано» (26:239).** Rendered "It is not much that has been done, of course, but still it was I who did it." / «Det er naturligvis ikke meget, der er gjort, men det var dog mig, der gjorde det.»
10. **«срезало его ноги, туловище» (25:131).** The figure going down the stairs is "cut off" from view piece by piece. Kept literal: "then his legs were cut off, then his body…" / «så blev hans ben skåret væk, så hans krop…».

## Suspected OCR errors in the Russian (not edited)

- **25:33 «Опятьтаки»** should be «Опять-таки» (hyphen lost). Translated as "Once again" / «Atter».
- **26:73 «КрысобояВеликана»** should be «Крысобоя-Великана» (hyphen lost at a line break). Translated as "Ratslayer the Giant" / «Kæmpen Rottedræber».
- **26:54 «всё»** has «ё», where the 1990 print normally uses «е». This is probably as printed and is harmless, but worth checking against the page image.
- **25:84 «вас,— продолжал»**: the space before the dash is missing. This is punctuation only.
