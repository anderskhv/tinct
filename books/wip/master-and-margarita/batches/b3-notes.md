## Passages I was unsure of

1. **«Я — мастер»** (13:76). English needs an article, so it reads "I am a master." with the indefinite article, set against Ivan's "You're a writer?". The narration elsewhere keeps "the master" (lower case), as fixed. Danish says «Jeg er mester» with no article, which matches the Russian exactly.
2. **«ихний помощник»** (12:15). This is a substandard, servile form. EN “assistant to their honor”; DA »herskabets assistent«. Both keep the lackey register, but the grammar error is lost.
3. **«Поздравляю вас, гражданин, соврамши!»** (12:41). The verb form is deliberately illiterate. EN “Congratulations, citizen, you been and gone and lied!”; DA »Tillykke, borger, med at ha' løjet!«.
4. **«Зрительская масса» / «Зрительная масса»** (12:128–129). Koroviev turns "the audience mass" into "the visual mass". EN “The viewing masses… / The visual masses…”; DA »Tilskuermassen… / Den visuelle masse…«.
5. **«Урежьте марш!!»** and the narrator's gloss on it (12:146–147). EN "Cut loose with a march!!" and "did not begin to play, nor even burst into music, nor even crash into it, but… cut loose with". DA »Fyr den af med en march!!« and «fyrede af med». In both, the cat's verb is kept as the vulgar one in the list.
6. **«ну его в болото!»** (11:18). EN "the swamp can have him!"; DA «kan han ryge i sumpen!». I kept the swamp image rather than using a stock "to hell with him", so as not to bring devil vocabulary where Bulgakov has none.
7. **«дело нечисто» / «нечистая сила»** (11:20, 13:34). EN "something unclean about this business" and "an unclean force"; DA «noget urent ved den sag» and «en uren magt». The demonic overtone is kept on purpose.
8. **«ветхому, прежнему Ивану»** (11:20). EN "the old, former Ivan" (DA «den gamle, tidligere Ivan»). The biblical "old man" echo of ветхий is muted.
9. **«мантия» vs «плащ»**. Chapter 13 uses мантия for Pilate's garment. EN uses "mantle" there and keeps "cloak" (from ch. 2) in 11:8. DA uses «kåbe» in ch. 13 and «kappe» in 11:8, following the same distinction.
10. **«человек девственный»** (13:58). EN "a virgin soul"; DA «et uberørt sind». The sense is "untouched by reading".

## Wordplay and verse

- **Разоблачение** ("exposure"; literally also "un-robing"). The chapter title and every use say "exposure / expose" in EN, which also carries the sense of undressing, and «afsløring / afsløre» in DA (from «slør», veil). The joke pays off later with the women's clothes.
- **The march (12:149–152)** is rendered as rhymed verse rather than literally, keeping 4 lines and the "!!!":
  - EN: "His Excellency fancied / The birds about the yard, / And pretty girls he chanced on / He took under his guard!!!"
  - DA: "Hans Højvelbårenhed / var glad for høns og ænder, / og søde piger tog han ved / i sine gode hænder!!!"
  - Literal: "His Excellency / loved domestic birds / and took under his protection / pretty girls."
- **«Стукнет калитка, стукнет сердце»** (13:122). The repeated verb is kept: EN "The gate bangs, my heart bangs"; DA «Lågen slår, hjertet slår».
- Koroviev's mock-folksy «таперича» becomes EN "This here deck… / Now then" and DA «Dette her kortspil / Nu, hvor…». The pseudo-foreign phrases are kept as foreign text: "Ein, zwei, drei", "Avec plaisir", "Oui, madame", "Pardon", "Merci".
