# The Master and Margarita: harmonisation pass (5 Nov 2026)

Scope: the assembled `editions/master-and-margarita-modern-en.json` and `-modern-da.json` (batches b1–b10, ten translators) and `-original-ru.json`. Every edit was made in place. No paragraph was merged or split, and no published translation was consulted. The binding term list is now `GLOSSARY.md`.

How the forms were chosen: README and sample (ch. 1–2) first. After that, the form introduced first, if it was also the most frequent. When the counts were close, the form that is clearer to the reader and matches the other language won. Counts below are replacements made (EN = modern-en, DA = modern-da). Locations are `chapter:paragraph`, 1-based.

## 5. Typography and address (checked, nothing to change)

- EN: curly quotes only, unspaced em dashes, `...` ellipses as in the sample. The epigraph's spaced dash (1:1) is the sample's.
- DA: »…« with nested ›…‹, spaced en dashes, no curly quotes or em dashes. The one unspaced dash, «–« her rev Afranius» (26:132), is an interrupted speech.
- Quote balance: all unbalanced paragraphs are intentional multi-paragraph speeches, the same 59 in both languages. They are the master's monologue (54 continuation paragraphs in ch. 13: 96–101, 105–111, 118–124, 138–140, 148, 151–180), the telegram (18:2–3), Margarita's note (20:11–12) and Koroviev's speech (22:16 → 22:17).
- Danish De/du: speeches were screened where the Russian has only *вы* but the Danish only *du*, or the reverse, and where a single speech mixes *De* and *du*. Every hit is deliberate: 18:163 (the Russian switches «вас» → «Ты»), 24:200–201 (the cat's du/De joke), 26:133 (the exclamation «du milde»), 25:43 (generic «на тебя самого» = «en selv»). No slips were found.
