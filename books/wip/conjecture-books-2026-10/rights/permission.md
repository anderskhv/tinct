## Points to note (not blockers)

1. **The individual authors hold the copyright on three titles.** Aaron Stupple owns *The Sovereign Child* and Brett Hall owns *The Farthest Reaches*. *Bold Conjectures* I is © Logan Chipkin. The grant came from Logan, speaking for Conjecture Institute about "our books". If Anders wants belt-and-braces evidence, a one-line confirmation from Stupple and Hall would close this gap. The recorded grant covers the package as it stands.
2. **The *Lords of the Cosmos* copyright page waives copyright outright.** Its OPF metadata still says "All rights reserved". The book's own page is the more permissive statement, and the grant covers the book either way.
3. **Third-party material inside the collections.** *Bold Conjectures* I transcribes interviews from other people's shows (Closer To Truth, Conversations with Tyler, Mindscape and others). Its Acknowledgments say the interviewers "graciously permitted me to include their interviews". *Bold Conjectures* II has essays by twelve contributors under Conjecture Institute's 2026 copyright. Julian Barbour's essay notes that it "will appear, in a possibly extended form, in a book currently being written". *The Farthest Reaches* has a transcribed conversation between Peter Boghossian and Richard Dawkins and a screenshot of a 2018 post by David Deutsch quoting Richard Dawkins (Preface, figure f1). Tinct reproduces each book exactly as the publisher published it.
4. **Supporter credit.** The *Bold Conjectures* I front matter names "Anders Hvelplund" among the volume's financial supporters. That is reading text and is kept. It is not a rights issue.
5. **Attribution.** Keep author/editor, publisher and copyright lines visible with each book. Do not label these books "public domain" anywhere in the app or search metadata.
