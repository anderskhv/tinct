## Third-party material

- **Illustrations.** Maxfield Parrish died in 1966, so his pictures in the Hawthorne edition are protected in the EU until the end of 2036. None are shipped. No shipped edition file of the eight contains `[Illustration`, a caption or his name.
- **Project Gutenberg name and licence.** None of the 22 shipped edition files contains "Gutenberg", licence text or transcriber's notes.
- **Editor's text in the Nesbit volume.** Units 1 and 2, "Preface" (signed "E. T. R.") and "A Brief Life of Shakespeare", are by the editor of the 1907 Hertel, Jenkins & Co. edition, E. T. Roe, not by Nesbit. A catalogue entry gives Roe's birth year as 1847; no death date was found. He would have had to live past 108 for the two pieces to be protected in the EU in 2026. Decision (Anders, 23 Nov 2026): keep both units.
- **Introductions in the two Church books.** Unsigned text from the 1905 Macmillan school edition. Free either way: as Church's own, or as anonymous work published more than 70 years ago.
