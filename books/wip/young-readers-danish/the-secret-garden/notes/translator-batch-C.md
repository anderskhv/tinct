- animal charmer / charmer = dyretæmmer; snake charmer = slangetæmmer; boy animal = drengedyr
- lump (on the back) = knude; hunch = pukkel; backbone lumps = rygradsknuder

- cottage = hytte; lad = knægt; lass = tøs; selfish = egoistisk

## (b) Unsure
- 15:20 "noget Magisk" keeps the capital on an adjective; could be "Magien" in some other turn.
- 15:8 "my nurse" (Colin as a small child) given as sygeplejerske per glossary; barnepige is also possible.
- 18:11, 19:29, 19:54, 19:69, 20:2: comma or full stop set inside « after a quoted word or phrase in narration, following rule 5.
- 18:15, 18:21, 18:24, 19:22, 21:3–5: Mary's and Colin's Yorkshire is only lightly country (rigtignok, gevaldig, såmænd).
- 19:35: three levels of quotation; written » › » … « ‹ and the first inner quote closed, which the English leaves open.
- 19:84: "a chap" read as "one" and "he" as the lark, following the original wording.
- 20:20/22/48: Mary's Indian formula uses De so that Colin can repeat it word for word to Roach.
- 21:91: "lad" here = dreng (Ben moved), elsewhere knægt.
