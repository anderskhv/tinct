# Historien om kong Arthur og hans riddere: decisions after translation (these win)

- A knight of the fellowship is "fælle ved Det Runde Bord" (not "fælle af"); the body is "ridderne af Det Runde Bord"; the full title is »De Gamle og Hæderværdige Fæller ved Det Runde Bord«.
- Bynames take capitals whenever they name the man: Den Sorte Ridder, Den Røde Ridder, Den Hvide Ridder, Den Hvide Kæmpe, Den Sorte Kæmpe, Den Blide Ridder, Den Trofaste Ridder. A plain description ("en sort ridder") stays lower case.
- magic = trolddom (never "magi"); black magic = sort trolddom; conjurer, enchanter, wizard = troldmand; no "heks", "heksemester", "heksekunst" (use troldkvinde, troldmand, trolddomskunst).
- Fay, fairy = fe, feerne; Faëry = feernes land. Morgana "le Fay" keeps her name.
- champion: one who fights for another = forkæmper; a great fighter = kæmpe.
- Seneschal = drost; quest = færd; adventure = eventyr; palfrey = ridehest; war-horse, courser = stridshest; belted knight = ridder med ridderbælte; portcullis = faldgitter; device on a shield = mærke; surcoat and a herald's tabard = våbenkjortel.
- Pellias's enchanted collar = halssmykket; King Arthur's collar and chain = halskæde.
- Launcelot of the Lake = Sir Launcelot af Søen. "Urien" always (never Uriens). North Umber, but Northumberland where the English paragraph itself has Northumberland.
- Address: du and I only. "Sir Knight" = herr ridder; several = herrer riddere; "my lord King" = herre konge; a maiden = jomfru; a married or ruling lady = frue.
- Pyle's own slips (who is whose son, the colour of a tent, the portcullis that is let down before a knight rides out) stay as the English has them.
- Chapter 5 ¶12 and chapter 25 ¶41: the Danish closes a speech the English forgot to close. Keep it closed.
