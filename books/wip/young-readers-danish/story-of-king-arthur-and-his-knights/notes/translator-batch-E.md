# Batch E: ch29–33
## (a) Names and recurring terms decided here (English = Danish)
- necklace / collar (Pellias's jewel) = halssmykke, as in ch24–26; King Arthur's own collar (32:3) = halskæde, as in ch10
- fay / wholly fay / half fay = en fe / helt og holdent fe / halvt fe; land of faëry = feernes land
- the lady (Lady of the Lake, unnamed noblewomen) = fruen; lords and ladies of the Court = herrer og damer
- lord / lady / knight of the castle = borgherren / borgfruen / borgens ridder
- Sir Launcelot of the Lake = Sir Launcelot af Søen; Companion of the Round Table = fælle af Det Runde Bord
- Montenoir, Gansaret, Gaheris, Galahad, Percival = unchanged; Tintagalon = Tintagalon (as in ch10)
- Sir Sorloise / Sir Brian of the Forest = af Skoven; Sir Alardin of the Isles = af Øerne; Sir Ablamor of the Marise = af Marise
- Knight of the Silver Tongue = Ridderen med Sølvtungen
- white hart / stag = hvid hjort; hound = hund; brachet = støver
- palfrey = ridehest; war horse = stridshest; half-armor = halv rustning; feast = gæstebud
- courtesy, courteous = høviskhed, høvisk; belted knight = ridder med ridderbælte
- quest = færd; adventure = eventyr; Messire(s), Sir (alone, as address) = herre(r); Sir Knights = herrer riddere
- forest sanctuary = helligdommen i skoven; cell = celle; fountain = kilde; last sacrament = det sidste sakramente
- loadstone = magnetsten; elixir = eliksir (_elixir vitæ_ kept in italics); black magic = sort magi
- sport (the beheading game) = leg; locket = medaljon; conjurer = heksemester
- Knight-Enchanter, knight-conjurer = troldmandsridderen
- old woman = gammel kone; old hag = gammel kælling; to have her own way = at få sin vilje
- surcoat = våbenkjortel; coat of arms = våbenmærke; device (on a shield) = mærke
## (b) Paragraphs I am unsure about
- 29:62 "leaned on his trowel" = "støttede sig til sin spade": nobody can lean on a planteske.
- 30:24 "the second knight … The other said" = "den anden af ridderne … Den første sagde", to avoid "den anden" twice.
- 30:68 "a prisoner of honor" = "som fange i al ære".
- 31:19 "enjoyed the entertainment they were given" = "nød alt, hvad de blev budt" (read as hospitality).
- 31:26 "it stood up and walked" = "blev den stående og gik" (the original has "it stood, and it walked").
- 31:40 "the papers in my cabinet" = "papirerne i mit skab" (could also be a small private room).
- 32:17 "a household fit for a queen" = "et følge, der var en dronning værdigt".
