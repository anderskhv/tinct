## (b) Paragraphs I am unsure about
- 12:2, 12:40: English says the knights came "from Camelot to Tintagalon" (earlier: from Carleon); kept Camelot as written.
- 12:2: English has "Uriens" and "Morgana la Fay"; written Urien and Morgana le Fay per glossary. "half related by blood" = "halvt af hans blod".
- 10:41 / 11:10: the undergarment is purple linen in one, purple silk in the other; followed the English each time.
- 11:35: the storyteller's "you" to the reader is plural (I, jer), matching "fortalt jer" in 10:0.
- 13:20: "string of large beads" = "snor med store kugler" (the gardener does not see they are pearls; "perler" would hide that).
- 13:22, 13:24: "this very morning" kept (her til morgen, i morges) though the scene is in the afternoon.
- 14:27: "his brains swam like water" = "svømmede det i hans hoved som vand".
## (c) Verse and inscriptions
- 12:38: shield inscription, glossary wording with "med livet som indsats".
