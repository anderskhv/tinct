# A Wonder Book and Tanglewood Tales (Hawthorne) — Danish edition notes

- Greek names are kept in English spelling (Hercules, Perseus, Medusa, Quicksilver, Circe, Ulysses, Proserpina, Ceres, Pluto) and genitives take a bare apostrophe after s. Danish forms used: *gorgon/gorgonerne*, *pygmæ/pygmæerne*, *kyklopen*, *nymferne*, *Minotaur*, *moder Ceres*, *Det Gyldne Skind*, *den talende eg*.
- Mixed spellings remain in places: Athen / Athens, Boeotien / Boeotia, and the Danish sound-effect of the bird’s call (“Pip, pip, pi — ve!”).
- Hawthorne’s long ironic sentences were split where natural; a few phrasings remain bookish.

## General

- Distances: English miles are “engelske mil”; feet, yards and inches are kept.
- Coins and units with no Danish equivalent (groat, farthing, shilling, pottle) are kept.
- Speeches that run across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end.
