# NOTES — Book 11, paragraphs 0-40 (whole book)

Files: `000-005.txt`, `006-010.txt`, `011-016.txt`, `017-022.txt`, `023-027.txt`, `028-030.txt`, `031-035.txt`, `036-040.txt`.

Book 11 was re-rendered afresh, paragraph by paragraph from Pusey with the Latin (O'Donnell) checked throughout. The previously served modern-en was consulted only as a reference point; almost every sentence has been re-expressed.

## Terminology kept consistent across the time analysis

- *distentio* → **distension** everywhere it occurs in the Latin (p29 "time is a kind of distension"; p32 "a distension ... of the mind itself"; p38 "my life is nothing but distension ... not distended but extended"; p40 "without any distension in Your action"). Pusey uses different words for this term: "extension" (p29), "protraction" (p32), "distraction" (p38, p40). Using one term makes the argument's key concept visible.
- *attentio / attendit* → **attention / attends** (p36, p37). Pusey has "consideration / considers".
- *expectatio* → expectation; *memoria* → memory; *contuitus* (p25) → "sight" (Pusey's word, kept).
- *praesens intentio* (p35) → "the present intent of the mind"; *secundum intentionem* (p38) → "intently" (Pusey).
- *spatium* → "extent" (for "the present has no extent", p19, p26, p32, p33) or "span" (span of time). Pusey's "space" was avoided because modern readers take it spatially.
- past/present/future and "exist / no longer exist / does not exist yet" are kept strictly distinct throughout pp16-23 and 32-37. Pusey's tense of each verb was checked against the Latin.

## Latin consulted

- p3 "Behold ... wherein is my desire" (x2): Latin *unde sit desiderium meum* → "where my longing comes from".
- p4 Truth's inner verdict: Pusey "It is truth"; Latin *verum dicit* → "He is telling the truth" (so it answers Augustine's "You are telling the truth").
- p7 "the Word of my Lord": Latin *verbum ... dei mei* → "the Word of my God".
- p8 "not unthankful to assure Truth" (Pusey misprint/obscurity): Latin *certae veritati* → "not ungrateful to sure Truth". "unto the Word coeternal with Thee Thou dost ... say": Latin *verbo tibi coaeterno ... dicis* → "by the Word who is coeternal with You, You say".
- p10 "hear Thee inwardly discoursing out of Thy oracle: I will boldly cry out": Latin attaches *ex oraculo tuo* to *clamabo* → "I will cry out boldly from Your own oracle". The obscure "severing my cloudiness which yet again mantles over me, fainting from it" was checked (*discindens nubilum meum, quod me rursus cooperit deficientem ab ea caligine atque aggere poenarum mearum*) and rendered as a clear sequence.
- p12 "uttereth the times": Latin *dictat*; kept "utters".
- p17 Pusey "often days since ... often days hence" is a misprint for "ten days" (Latin *decem dies*) → "ten days ago / ten days from now".
- p21 "could not relate them": Latin *non ... vera narrarent* → "could not tell of them truly". p22 "when past facts are related": Latin *cum vera narrantur* → "recounted truly".
- p27 "How long time is it since he said this" / "since he did this": Latin *quamdiu dixit / fecit* = how long did he take → "How long did he take to say this? ... to do this?"; the third (*quam longo tempore illud non vidi*) → "How long it is since I saw that!".
- p28 Pusey reverses the syllables ("because those sounded in a shorter time, these in a longer" after "some short, others long"). Latin *illae longiore tempore ... istae breviore* → long syllables sounded longer, short ones shorter. Fixed.
- p29 "a certain extension": Latin *distentionem* → "a kind of distension" (see terminology).
- p32 "the space of a rood": Latin *spatium transtri* (a beam) → "the length of a beam"; "protraction" → "distension".
- p36/p37 "consideration" → "attention" (*attentio*). p37 "divided between my memory ... and expectation": Latin *distenditur vita ... in memoriam ... et in expectationem* → "stretched out two ways: into my memory ... and into my expectation".
- p38 "distraction" (*distentio*) → "distension"; "re-collected from my old conversation" (*a veteribus diebus conligar*) → "gathered back from my old way of life".
- p39 "even if there be any creature before all times": Latin *supra tempora* → "above all times".
- p40 "unchangeably eternal, that is, the eternal Creator of minds": Latin *vere aeterno creatori mentium* → "that is, truly eternal, the Creator of minds". "distraction of Thy action" → "distension in Your action".

## Corrections to prior draft

The previously served Book 11 modern-en was a light pronoun update of Pusey (similarity ~0.75). Substantive errors corrected besides the general re-rendering:

- p4: prior draft added "give me also **the grace** to understand them"; "grace" is not in Pusey or the Latin. Removed. Also "It is truth" → "He is telling the truth" (Latin).
- p7: "the Word of my Lord" → "the Word of my God" (Latin *dei mei*).
- p8: "everyone who is not ungrateful to assured Truth" (meaningless, carried from Pusey's misprint) → "not ungrateful to sure Truth"; "unto the Word ... You do ... say" (archaic "unto", "You do say") → modern syntax.
- p10: "hear You speaking inwardly out of Your oracle. I will boldly cry out" → the oracle belongs to the cry (Latin).
- p15: "This day have I begotten You" (archaic inversion) → "Today I have begotten You".
- p27: "How long is it since he said this? How long since he did this?" (Pusey's reading) → "How long did he take to say this / to do this?" (Latin *quamdiu*).
- p28: prior draft kept Pusey's reversal ("some syllables short and others long, but only because those sounded in a shorter time, these in a longer") → fixed per Latin.
- p29, p32, p38, p40: prior draft had "extension" (p29, p32) and "distraction" (p38, p40) for the single Latin term *distentio*; unified as "distension". p36-37 "consideration" → "attention". p32 "rod" (Pusey "rood") → "beam" (Latin *transtri*).
- p39: "before all times" → "above all times" (Latin *supra*). p40 "the eternal Creator" → "truly eternal, the Creator" (*vere aeterno*).
- p28, p38, p39, p40 (and p27): prior draft added quotation marks around scripture phrases that Pusey does not mark ("Your lovingkindness is better than all lives", "forgetting what is behind", "You raise up those that are bowed down", etc.), against style note section 4. Removed.
- Archaisms removed throughout (prior draft retained "shall" futures, "do I speak", "this do I live", "Behold" outside scripture, "let no man", "self-same", "mock at man", "bowels of my soul", "penal disease", "predicated", "that vanity", and alternated "the heavens and the earth" with "heaven and earth"; now consistently "heaven and earth" as Pusey mostly has it).

## Uncertainties / open issues

- "distension" is a technical, slightly unusual word; a reviewer may prefer Pusey's "distraction" in p38 (the famous line "my life is but a distraction"). I chose consistency with p29/p32/p40, where the same Latin word carries the argument.
- p25 *contuitus* rendered with Pusey's "sight"; "attention" would be closer to how the term works in the argument but would collide with *attentio* in p36-37.
- p35 *praesens intentio* rendered "the present intent of the mind" ("of the mind" makes explicit the subject already addressed as "my mind" in the paragraph).
- p0 "Your Father knows what you need before you ask": Latin adds *ab eo* ("ask Him"); Pusey omits it, followed Pusey.
