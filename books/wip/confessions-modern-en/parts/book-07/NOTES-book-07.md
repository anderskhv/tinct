# Book 7 — notes (paragraphs 0-26)

Files: `000-006.txt`, `007-012.txt`, `013-018.txt`, `019-022.txt`, `023-026.txt`.
Fresh rendering from Pusey, checked against O'Donnell's Latin. The previously served
modern-en draft was consulted as a starting point only; nearly every sentence was
re-expressed.

## Pusey kept on purpose

- 12, 13: John 1:1-14 and Phil. 2:6-11 kept in near-Authorized-Version cadence ("In the beginning was the Word…", "thought it not robbery to be equal with God", "obedient unto death, even the death of the cross") — famous scripture; only archaic verb forms modernized ("shines", "did not comprehend"). Sim 0.73 / 0.66 for this reason.
- 13, 26: "meek and lowly in heart", "labor and are heavy laden", "Learn of Me" — familiar scripture wording.
- 15: "I AM that I AM" — divine name formula (Exod. 3:14), kept as Pusey.
- 15: "the region of unlikeness" — famous phrase (regio dissimilitudinis).
- 22: "THAT WHICH IS" in capitals, as Pusey (id quod est).
- 25: "not skilled, but killed" — Pusey's pun rendering Augustine's peritus / periturus; kept because no better modern pun preserves it.
- 26: "a broken and a contrite heart", "the Bridal City", "the Cup of our Redemption", "the least of Your apostles" (unquoted, as Pusey).

## Latin consulted

- 1: *intentionem* — Pusey "notion of the mind"; rendered "the very act of mind".
- 2: *prima voce abominandum* — "repulsive the moment it was spoken"; *sermo … servienti liber et contaminatae purus et corruptae integer* kept as free/pure/whole relieving the enslaved/stained/corrupted soul.
- 4: *iam iamque animadvertebam* — "on the very point of seeing" (Pusey "all but saw").
- 5: *nulla natura est nisi quia nosti eam* — Pusey "Nor is there any nature in things, but Thou knowest it"; Latin is stronger (things have a nature *because* God knows them). Rendered "Nothing has any nature at all except because You know it."
- 6: *de timore mortis et non inventa veritate* — "the fear of death, and the truth not yet found". The Latin quotation marks around "I said: Look, here is God…" were NOT added (Pusey has none).
- 7: *dealbatiores vias saeculi* — "brighter roads of the world" (Pusey "gilded paths"); *tamquam in regno suo* — "since each was master in his own domain"; *ex paribus domorum intervallis* — messengers met exactly halfway.
- 9: *non dicat, non dicat* — doubled "Let him not say it" restored (Pusey has it once).
- 10: *tu gaudium verum mihi subdito tibi* — Pusey "Thou art my true joy when subjected to Thee" is ambiguous; rendered "You were my true joy as long as I was subject to You". *cogitanti autem imagines corporum ipsae opponebantur redeunti* — "the images of bodies themselves stood in my way as I tried to turn back". *in cervice crassa scuti mei* — "the thick boss of my shield".
- 11: *donec mihi per interiorem aspectum certus esses* — "until You became certain to me by my inward sight" (Pusey "manifested"). *acri collyrio salubrium dolorum* — "stinging salve of wholesome pains".
- 12: *immanissimo typho turgidum* — "swollen with the most monstrous conceit" (Pusey "most unnatural pride"). *quod factum est in eo vita est* — "that which was made in Him is life" (Pusey "by Him"; Latin *in eo*).
- 13: *cothurno tamquam doctrinae sublimioris elati* — "strut on the high boots of what they take to be a loftier learning" (the tragic buskin; Pusey "lofty walk"). Pusey's typo "how" read as "bow".
- 17: *incorruptibilis substantia, magnum utique bonum* — "a great good" (Pusey "a chief good").
- 18: Pusey's garbled sentence ("do yet with the inferior part … harmonising with it") rendered per Latin: the mutually ill-fitting things fit the lower part of the world, earth, with its cloudy and windy sky suited to it.
- 19: *aliena loquebatur* — "talked wildly"; *fovisti caput nescientis* — "cradled my head, without my knowing it"; *cessavi de me paululum* — "I let go of myself a little".
- 20: Pusey's served text has a dropped word ("true so far as they nor is there"); restored from Latin *in quantum sunt* — "true insofar as they exist". *omnitenens manu veritate* — "hold all things in Your hand by Your Truth".
- 21: *nedum vipera et vermiculus* — "much more, then, do the viper and the worm [displease them]" (Pusey "reptiles"; Latin "little worm"). *proicientis intima sua et tumescentis foras* — "throws away its own inmost life and swells outward" (Pusey "casting out its bowels"). *a summa substantia … detortae in infima* — "twisted away from the supreme substance … toward the lowest things".
- 22: *non stabam frui deo meo* — "I did not stand firm in the enjoyment of my God" (Pusey "did not press on"). *quousque possunt bestiae* — "as far as animals can go".
- 23: *humilis humilem* — "I was not humble enough to hold the Humble One". *nec cuius rei magistra esset eius infirmitas* — "what His weakness was meant to teach". *tunicae pelliciae nostrae* — "our garment of skin".
- 24: **Doctrinal:** *non persona veritatis, sed magna quadam naturae humanae excellentia … praeferri ceteris arbitrabar* — Pusey "not only as being a form of Truth, but…" softens the point; the Latin says Augustine then ranked Christ above others NOT as the Person of Truth but for human excellence. Rendered per Latin: "not because He was the very Person of Truth, but because of some great excellence of His human nature". *totum hominem* — "a whole man"; *cum corpore sine mente animum* — "a body together with an animal soul lacking a rational mind" (Pusey "sensitive soul without a rational"). *auctoritatem magisterii* — "authority as a teacher". Photinus and the Apollinarians named exactly as Pusey, no gloss.
- 25: *non flebam, insuper et inflabar scientia* — "I did not weep over it; I was puffed up with knowledge instead" (Pusey's "but rather scorn" is a pun-driven addition not in the Latin). *si eos solos quisque didicisset* — "if he studied nothing else".
- 26: *praeposito mortis* — "the ruler of death" (Pusey "king of death"); *creasti in principio viarum tuarum* — kept Pusey's "formed" (Prov. 8:22; doctrinally deliberate choice by Pusey). *cura caelestis imperatoris munitam* — "guarded by the care of the heavenly Commander" (Pusey "host of the heavenly General"). *de silvestri cacumine* — "wooded mountaintop" (Pusey "shaggy top"). *inviscerabantur* — "sank into my inmost being" (Pusey "bowels").

## Corrections to prior draft

- Quotation marks: the prior draft added quotation marks around scripture and quoted speech in 12, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18, 19, 22, 23, 24, 26 where Pusey has none. Removed; only Pusey's own quotation marks kept (7, 10, 15, 18, 22, 25 etc.).
- 5: "There is no nature in existence which You do not know" → follows Latin causal sense ("except because You know it").
- 9: missing repetition "let him not say it" restored.
- 10: "You are my true joy when I am subjected to You" (tense and sense) → "You were my true joy as long as I was subject to You".
- 11: "until You were manifested to my inward sight" → "until You became certain to me".
- 12: "What was made by Him is life" → "in Him" per Latin; "unnatural pride" → "monstrous conceit".
- 17: "chief good" → "great good" per Latin.
- 20: draft silently repaired Pusey's dropped word; kept repair, now noted.
- 21: **mistranslation** — draft "Your righteousness displeases the wicked, much more than the viper and the reptile" reversed the sense; now "much more, then, do the viper and the worm" (i.e. no wonder these displease them). "Casting out its bowels" modernized.
- 22: "I did not press on to enjoy my God" → "did not stand firm in the enjoyment" (*non stabam*).
- 23: "I, humbled, to the Humble" (unclear) → explicit sense of *humilis humilem*.
- 24: **doctrinal** — "not only as a form of Truth, but also for…" → "not because He was the very Person of Truth, but because of…" (Latin *non persona veritatis*). "perfect man" → "whole man" (*totum hominem*).
- 25: "I did not mourn, but rather scorned" → "I did not weep … puffed up with knowledge instead" (no "scorn" in Latin).
- Throughout: residual archaisms in the draft ("Whereupon", "abode", "prated", "bowels", "shaggy", "hid", "ye"-era syntax, "unto" outside scripture, "did I learn" inversions) removed; syntax genuinely modernized.

This is below the 0.55-0.65 the task mentioned as an aim for this revision but inside the brief's 0.4-0.6 band; no paragraph is under 1.02 word ratio, and fidelity was preferred over artificial closeness to Pusey. Kept Pusey's structure (modernized); a reviewer might prefer the Latin order.

- 12: "books of the Platonists" source-man left unnamed, as in Pusey and Latin.
