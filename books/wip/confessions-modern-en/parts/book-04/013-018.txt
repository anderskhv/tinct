@@ 13
This is what we love in friends, and we love it so much that a person's conscience accuses itself if he does not love the one who loves him, or does not return the love of the one who loves him, seeking nothing from the other's person except signs of goodwill. This is where that mourning comes from when someone dies, the darkness of sorrows, the heart soaked in tears, all sweetness turned into bitterness; and, from the lost life of the dying, the death of the living. Blessed is the one who loves You, and his friend in You, and his enemy for Your sake. For he alone loses no one dear to him, to whom all are dear in the One who cannot be lost. And who is this but our God, the God who made heaven and earth and fills them, because by filling them He made them? No one loses You except the one who leaves You. And the one who leaves You, where does he go, or where does he flee, except from You at peace to You angered? For where does he not find Your law in his own punishment? And Your law is truth, and truth is You.

@@ 14
Turn us, O God of hosts, show us Your face, and we shall be saved. For wherever the soul of man turns, except toward You, it is pinned fast to sorrows, even if it is pinned to beautiful things outside You and outside itself. And yet those things would be nothing at all if they were not from You. They rise and they set. By rising they begin, as it were, to exist; they grow in order to be made complete; and once complete, they grow old and die. Not all of them grow old, but all of them die. So when they rise and strive to exist, the faster they grow toward being, the more they hurry toward not being. That is the measure set for them. This much You have given them, because they are parts of things that do not all exist at once, but by passing away and following one another they together make up the whole of which they are parts. Our speech, too, is completed in just this way, through sounding signs; for a sentence will not be whole unless each word, once it has sounded its part, passes away so that another can follow. Out of all these things let my soul praise You, O God, Creator of all; but let it not be stuck fast to them by the glue of love through the senses of the body. For they go where they were going, toward not being, and they tear the soul apart with deadly longings, because the soul itself wants to exist and loves to rest in the things it loves. But in these things there is no place to rest, because they do not stay; they run away. And who can follow them with the senses of the flesh? Who can grasp them even when they are right at hand? For the sense of the flesh is slow, because it is the sense of the flesh; that is its limit. It is enough for the other purpose it was made for, but it is not enough to hold things fast as they run their course from their appointed beginning to their appointed end. For in Your Word, by whom they are created, they hear their sentence: "From here, and this far."

@@ 15
Do not be foolish, my soul, and do not let the ear of your heart go deaf with the uproar of your foolishness. You too, listen. The Word Himself calls you to come back, and there is the place of undisturbed rest, where love is not abandoned unless it abandons first. See how these things pass away so that others may take their place, and so this lower universe is made complete in all its parts. "But do I go away anywhere?" says the Word of God. Make your dwelling there; entrust to it whatever you have from there, my soul, at least now that you are worn out with deceptions. Entrust to Truth whatever you have from the Truth, and you will lose nothing. What has decayed in you will flower again, all your sicknesses will be healed, and your perishing parts will be reshaped, renewed and bound closely to you. They will not drag you down to where they themselves are sinking, but will stand firm with you and remain forever before God, who stands firm and remains forever.

@@ 16
Why, then, turned the wrong way, do you follow your flesh? Let it turn around and follow you instead. Whatever you perceive through it is only a part, and you do not know the whole of which these are parts; and yet they delight you. But if the sense of your flesh were capable of grasping the whole, and had not itself, as your punishment, been justly limited to a part of the universe, you would want whatever exists in the present moment to pass on, so that the whole might please you more. For you hear what we say through that same sense of the flesh, and you certainly do not want the syllables to stand still; you want them to fly past, so that others may come and you may hear the whole. So it always is when any single thing is made up of many parts, not all of which exist at the same time: all of them together would delight us more than each one alone, if all of them could be perceived together. But far better than these is He who made all things, and He is our God; and He does not pass away, because nothing takes His place.

@@ 17
If bodies please you, praise God for them, and turn your love back toward their Maker, so that in the things that please you, you do not displease Him. If souls please you, let them be loved in God; for they too are changeable, but in Him they are made firm and steady; otherwise they would pass on and pass away. So let them be loved in Him. Carry with you to Him as many souls as you can, and say to them: "Let us love Him, let us love Him. He made these things, and He is not far away. For He did not make them and then leave; they are from Him and in Him. Look, there He is, wherever truth is savored. He is within the very heart, but the heart has wandered away from Him. Return to your heart, you transgressors, and cling to Him who made you. Stand with Him, and you will stand firm. Rest in Him, and you will be at rest. Where are you going, along these rough roads? Where are you going? The good you love comes from Him; but it is good and pleasant only as it relates to Him, and it will rightly become bitter, because whatever comes from Him is wrongly loved if He is abandoned for it. Why do you still keep on walking these hard and wearisome roads? There is no rest where you are looking for it. Seek what you are seeking, but it is not where you are seeking it. You are seeking a blessed life in the land of death; it is not there. For how can there be a blessed life where there is not even life?

@@ 18
"But our Life Himself came down here, and carried our death, and killed it out of the abundance of His own life. And He thundered, calling out to us to return from here to Him, into that hidden place from which He came out to us: first into the Virgin's womb, where the human creation, mortal flesh, was wedded to Him, so that it would not be mortal forever; and from there, like a bridegroom coming out of his chamber, He rejoiced like a giant to run his course. For He did not delay, but ran, calling out by His words, deeds, death, life, descent and ascension, crying out to us to return to Him. And He withdrew from our eyes, so that we might return to our heart and find Him there. For He went away, and look, He is here. He did not want to stay with us long, and yet He did not leave us; for He went back to the place He never left, because the world was made by Him. And He was in this world, and He came into this world to save sinners. To Him my soul confesses, and He heals it, for it has sinned against Him. O sons of men, how long will you be so heavy of heart? Even now, after Life has come down to you, will you not rise up and live? But where can you rise to, when you are already on high and have set your mouth against the heavens? Come down, so that you may rise, and rise to God. For you fell by rising up against Him." Tell them this, so that they may weep in the valley of tears, and so carry them with you to God; for you are saying these things to them from His Spirit, if you say them burning with the fire of love.
