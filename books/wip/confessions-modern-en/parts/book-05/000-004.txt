Accept the sacrifice of my confessions, offered by the hand of my tongue, which You formed and roused to confess Your name. Heal all my bones, and let them say, O Lord, who is like You? For a person who confesses to You does not inform You of what is going on inside him. A closed heart does not shut out Your eye, and human hardness of heart cannot push back Your hand: You melt it whenever You will, either in pity or in punishment, and there is nothing that can hide itself from Your heat. But let my soul praise You so that it may love You, and let it confess Your own mercies to You so that it may praise You. Your whole creation never ceases and is never silent in praising You: every spirit praises You with a voice turned toward You, and living things and material things praise You through the voice of those who contemplate them, so that our souls may rise out of their weariness toward You, leaning on the things You have made and passing beyond them to You, who made them so wonderfully; and there is refreshment and true strength.

Let the restless and the wicked go their way and run from You; You still see them, and You mark off the shadows. And look: the whole universe, with them in it, is beautiful, though they themselves are ugly. What harm have they done You? How have they brought disgrace on Your rule, which is just and whole from the heavens down to the lowest things of earth? Where did they run to when they ran from Your face? Or where is there a place You do not find them? No, they ran away so as not to see You seeing them, so that in their blindness they might stumble against You (for You abandon nothing You have made). They ran so that the unjust might stumble against You, I say, and be justly hurt, pulling away from Your gentleness, colliding with Your uprightness and falling onto their own roughness. The truth is, they do not know that You are everywhere, You whom no place contains, and that You alone are present even to those who go far away from You. So let them turn back and seek You, because You have not deserted Your creation the way they deserted their Creator. Let them turn back and seek You, and look: You are there, in their heart, in the heart of those who confess to You, who throw themselves on You and weep in Your lap after all their hard roads. Then You gently wipe away their tears, and they weep all the more and find joy in their weeping, because it is You, Lord, and not some human being of flesh and blood, but You, Lord, who made them, who now remake and comfort them. But where was I when I was looking for You? You were right in front of me, but I had gone away even from myself. I could not find myself, so how much less could I find You!

@@ 2
I want to set out in the sight of my God that twenty-ninth year of my life. At that time a certain bishop of the Manichees had come to Carthage, Faustus by name, a great snare of the Devil, and many people were entangled in him by the lure of his smooth speech. I did praise that smoothness, but I could still tell it apart from the truth of the things I was eager to learn. I was not so much looking at the dish his words were served in as at the knowledge this Faustus, so famous among them, was setting before me to eat. His reputation had already told me that he was highly expert in every respectable branch of learning and especially accomplished in the liberal arts. And since I had read a good deal of the philosophers and still held it in my memory, I compared some of their teachings with the long fables of the Manichees, and the philosophers' account seemed to me the more probable. Yet they were only able to go so far as to form a judgment about this lower world; its Lord they could by no means find. For You are great, O Lord, and You look upon the humble, but the proud You know from far away. You draw near only to the contrite in heart, and You are not found by the proud, not even if with their curious skill they could count the stars and the grains of sand, measure the regions of the sky and trace the paths of the planets.

For they investigate these things with the understanding and ingenuity You gave them, and they have discovered a great deal. Many years in advance they have predicted eclipses of those great lights, the sun and the moon: on what day, at what hour and to what extent they would happen. Their calculation did not fail them; it happened just as they predicted. They wrote down the rules they had worked out, and these are read to this day. From them others predict in what year, in what month of the year, on what day of the month, at what hour of the day and in what portion of its light the moon or the sun is to be eclipsed, and it will happen just as it is foretold. People who do not know this science marvel at these things and are amazed, and those who do know it exult and are puffed up. Through an ungodly pride they draw away from You and fall short of Your light; they foresee an eclipse of the sun that will come so long afterward, yet they do not see their own eclipse, which is happening now. For they do not ask in a religious spirit where they got the ingenuity with which they investigate these things. And when they find that You made them, they do not give themselves up to You so that You may preserve what You made. They do not sacrifice to You what they have made of themselves. They do not slaughter their own soaring thoughts, like the birds of the air, or their own diving curiosities, with which, like the fish of the sea, they roam the hidden paths of the deep, or their own self-indulgence, like the beasts of the field, so that You, Lord, a consuming fire, might burn up their dead cares and re-create them to live forever.

@@ 4
But they did not know the way, Your Word, through whom You made these things that they count, and the people who do the counting, and the sense by which they perceive what they count, and the mind from which they count; nor did they know that of Your wisdom there is no number. But the Only Begotten Himself has been made for us wisdom and righteousness and sanctification, and He was counted among us and paid tribute to Caesar. They did not know this way, by which they could come down from themselves to Him and through Him go up to Him. They did not know this way, and they thought themselves raised on high among the stars and shining; and look, they fell to the earth, and their foolish heart was darkened. They say many true things about the creature, but they do not seek the Truth, the Maker of the creature, in a spirit of piety, and so they do not find Him. Or if they do find Him, though they know Him to be God, they do not glorify Him as God or give thanks, but become futile in their thinking and declare themselves wise, claiming for themselves what is Yours. And through this, with the most perverse blindness, they go on to attribute to You what is their own, heaping lies on You who are the Truth, changing the glory of the incorruptible God into an image made like corruptible man, and birds, and four-footed beasts, and creeping things. They change Your truth into a lie, and they worship and serve the creature rather than the Creator.
