# NOTES — Book 5 (paragraphs 0-24)

## Pusey kept on purpose

- 15: "by which we all die in Adam" and "original sin" — Pauline formula (1 Cor 15:22), doctrinal term.
- 17: "set a watch before my mouth, and a door of safe keeping around my lips" — Psalm 141:3, kept recognizable (Latin *ostium continentiae*; Pusey's "safe keeping" retained rather than introducing "continence" here).
- 4: "changing the glory of the incorruptible God into an image made like corruptible man, and birds, and four-footed beasts, and creeping things" / "worship and serve the creature rather than the Creator" — Romans 1, kept close.
- 12: "the steps of a man are directed by the Lord, and He shall choose his way" — Psalm 37:23, cadence kept (Pusey "ordered ... dispose").
- 22: "salvation is far from sinners" — Psalm 119:155.

## Latin consulted

- 0: Pusey "the spirit of man with voice directed unto Thee"; Latin *nec spiritus omnis per os conversum ad te* — rendered "every spirit praises You with a voice turned toward You". Also *per os considerantium ea* = "through the voice of those who contemplate them".
- 2: "nor did I so much regard the service of oratory" — Latin *nec quali vasculo sermonis* ("what sort of dish of speech"); rendered with the dish/eating image, which Pusey's "set before me to feed upon" also carries.
- 3: Pusey "re-create themselves immortally"; Latin *recreans eos immortaliter* (You re-creating them) — rendered "re-create them to live forever". Also "see not their own [failure], which is" = *in praesentia suum non vident*, rendered "their own eclipse, which is happening now".
- 7: Pusey "Behold piety and wisdom"; Latin *ecce pietas est sapientia* (Job 28:28, Old Latin) — rendered "Behold, piety is wisdom." Also "skill in which was no element of piety" checked against *sine quorum peritia pietas disci poterat*; kept Pusey's sense ("skill in them was no part of piety").
- 11: parenthesis "(of which indeed however ignorant, he might have held the truths of piety, had he not been a Manichee)" — Latin *sed si manichaeus non esset*; rendered as "could have been ignorant of them and still hold the truth of piety, but only if he had not been a Manichee".
- 12: "to engage with him in the study of that literature" — Latin *coepi cum eo pro studio eius agere vitam*; rendered "spend time with him on account of his own enthusiasm for literature".
- 14: "preserving me ... from the waters of the sea, for the water of Thy Grace" — Latin *usque ad aquam gratiae tuae*; rendered "to bring me to the water of Your grace". "The inheritance of Eve" (*reliquiarium Evae*) kept as Pusey.
- 18: "discourage that host of mine" — Latin *nec dissimulavi ... reprimere* ("I did not hesitate to restrain"); rendered "I did not hesitate to warn my host, freely and openly".
- 21: last sentence (*magis eos pati nolebam malos propter me, quam fieri propter te bonos volebam*) — rendered "I was more unwilling to put up with their wickedness for my own sake than I was wishing them to become good for Yours".
- 22: "sent him at the public expense" = *impertita etiam evectione publica* (public transport warrant); rendered "to send him with travel at public expense". "showed me an Episcopal kindness on my coming" = *peregrinationem meam ... dilexit*; rendered "was kind about my coming there as a stranger". "flour of Thy wheat" (*adipem frumenti*) rendered "the finest flour of Your wheat".
- 23: Pusey "ofttimes 'in a figure,' which when I understood literally, I was slain spiritually. Very many places then..." — Latin punctuation puts *spiritaliter* with the following clause (*occidebar. spiritaliter itaque plerisque ... locis expositis*). Followed the Latin: "passages which had been killing me when I took them literally. So when many passages of those books had been explained in a spiritual sense..." Reviewer may want to confirm this departure from Pusey.
- 24: Pusey "the Catholic Church, to which I had been commended by my parents"; Latin *in catholica ecclesia mihi a parentibus commendata* (the Church commended to me by my parents) — rendered "the Catholic Church, which my parents had commended to me". (Logged in fix pass after review.)

## Uncertainties / open issues

- 23: departure from Pusey's attachment of "spiritually" (see above).
- 7: "Behold, piety is wisdom" follows the Latin rather than Pusey's "Behold piety and wisdom".
- 22: "Your devout worshipper" for Pusey "Thy devout servant" (*pium cultorem tuum*) — either is fine; could revert to "servant".
