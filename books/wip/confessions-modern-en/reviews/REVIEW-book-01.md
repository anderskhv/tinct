## Renderer's Latin departures — verification

- **¶14 "diligens"**: correct. O'Donnell's text reads "ita parvi aestimet, diligens eos qui haec acerbissime formidant, quemadmodum parentes nostri ridebant tormenta…". Pusey's "mocking at" follows the variant *deridens*. The candidate's "while loving those who fear them most bitterly" follows the text the brief says to use. Accepted.
- **¶35 "How hidden You are"**: correct. The Latin reads "quam tu secretus es, habitans in excelsis in silentio, deus solus magnus". Pusey's "How deep are Thy ways" is a loose rendering. Accepted.
- **¶32 "Latium"**: accepted. The served Pusey text's "Latinum" is not a word here and looks like a misprint. Augustine's Latin paraphrase of Virgil has "Italia … avertere regem", so a strictly literal version would be "Italy". "Latium" still fixes the misprint with the right place, and the verse line stays a quotation.
- **¶9 "simple women"** and **"find You without working it out"**: correct against "auctoritatibus etiam muliercularum" and "amet non inveniendo invenire potius quam inveniendo non invenire te".
- **¶10 "cried with my mouth wide open for the breast"**: correct against "uberibus inhiabam plorans". The candidate's "I have never seen anyone… knowingly throw away what is good" follows the Latin "nec vidi quemquam scientem… bona proicere", which is fine.
