# Review: Book 5 (Confessions modern-en), independent fidelity sample

- **Book:** 5
- **Paragraphs reviewed:** 0, 1, 3, 4, 7, 9, 13, 14, 18, 19, 23, 24
- **Approx. words reviewed:** ~3,500 Pusey / ~3,870 candidate
- **Method:** each paragraph compared with Pusey clause by clause, and checked against O'Donnell's Latin (`latin/book05.txt`) wherever the candidate departs from Pusey.

## Renderer's logged departures toward the Latin (verified)

| para | departure | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | "every spirit praises You with a voice turned toward You … through the voice of those who contemplate them" (*nec spiritus omnis per os conversum ad te, nec animalia nec corporalia per os considerantium ea*) | Correct. Pusey's "spirit of man" is loose, and *animalia nec corporalia* = "living things and material things". |
| 3 | "re-create them to live forever" (*recreans eos immortaliter*: God re-creates them, not they themselves) | Correct. Pusey's "re-create themselves" misreads it. "their own eclipse, which is happening now" = *in praesentia suum non vident*, also correct. |
| 7 | "Behold, piety is wisdom" (*ecce pietas est sapientia*, Job 28:28 Old Latin) | Correct. Pusey's "piety and wisdom" is wrong, and the argument that follows ("he might have been ignorant of that") needs the predicate. |
| 23 | *spiritaliter* goes with the following clause: "passages which had been killing me when I took them literally. So when many passages … had been explained in a spiritual sense …" | Correct. O'Donnell punctuates *occidebar. spiritaliter itaque plerisque … locis expositis*. Pusey's "slain spiritually" results from misplaced punctuation. *dumtaxat* is kept as "at least the despair in which…", which is right. |

**Unlogged, but correct:** ¶24, "the Catholic Church, which my parents had commended to me". This reverses Pusey's "to which I had been commended by my parents". The Latin is *in catholica ecclesia mihi a parentibus commendata*, so the candidate is right and Pusey has it backwards. This is not a defect. The renderer should add it to NOTES under "Latin consulted".

No omissions, reversals, doctrinal flattening or unrecognizable scripture found in the sample. Checked in particular: Rom 1:21–25 in ¶4 ("incorruptible God", "creature rather than the Creator"), "the Only Begotten … made for us wisdom and righteousness and sanctification" (¶4), the Holy Spirit/Comforter claim with Pusey's quotation marks kept (¶7), the dualist "two masses" and "stretched out … from the mass of Your most shining substance" (¶19), "born in the flesh … defiled by the flesh" (¶19, "flesh" kept), "catechumen", "saving Name of Christ" (¶24), and the Cyprian shrine and "inheritance of Eve" (¶14). Candidate ¶14 "while she stayed behind, praying and weeping" follows the Latin *illa autem non; mansit orando et flendo*, and is sound.

**Convention skim (whole book):** no Thou/Thee/Thy/Thine, archaic verb forms or "Holy Ghost". There is no lowercase "you" or "your" anywhere in Book 5, and all direct address to God is capitalized. He/Him/His for God and Christ are capitalized (¶4, ¶12, ¶15). Names follow Pusey (Manichaeus, Faustus, Ambrose, Cyprian, Romanianus not present). None found.
