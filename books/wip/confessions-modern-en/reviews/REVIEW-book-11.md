# Review: Confessions modern-en, Book 11

- **Book:** 11 (time and eternity)
- **Paragraphs reviewed (0-based):** 0, 1, 4, 10, 16, 17, 18, 19, 28, 29, 32, 33, 36, 38, 39, 40
- **Approximate words reviewed:** about 4,050 Pusey plus 4,400 candidate. Every sample paragraph was checked clause by clause against Pusey. The Latin (O'Donnell) was checked for ¶4, 10, 28, 29, 32, 38, 39 and 40.

## Focus checks (all passed)

- **¶28, long and short syllables:** correct and follows the Latin (*aliae longae syllabae, aliae breves, nisi quia illae longiore tempore sonuissent, istae breviore*). The candidate reads: "Would some syllables in our words be long and others short, except because the long ones sounded for a longer time and the short ones for a shorter?" Pusey's reversal has been removed. "notions common to things both small and great", the potter's wheel, "equal intervals" (*aequalibus morulis*), "little wooden wheel" (*ligneolae*) and the Gen 1:14 list are all present.
- **distentio → "distension":** used at ¶29 (*quandam esse distentionem*), ¶32 (*distentionem ... ipsius animi*), ¶38 (*distentio est vita mea*) and ¶40 (*sine distentione actionis tuae*). "not distended but extended" (¶38) is correct.
- **attention / expectation / memory (¶36):** "It expects, it attends, it remembers, so that what it expects passes, through what it attends to, into what it remembers" is correct. "attention lasts" matches *perdurat attentio*.
- **Past / present / future and existence tenses (¶16–19, 32–33, 36):** these stay distinct and correct throughout. The ¶16 closing argument ("the very reason it exists is that it is about to stop existing") and the ¶19 conditional chain both match the Latin. The ¶18 point that the year is not present *as a whole* follows *totus*, which Pusey had blurred. ¶17 "ten days" is correct (*decem dies*).
- **Eternity vs time (¶16, ¶39, ¶40):** "if it were always present ... it would ... be ... eternity"; "no times are coeternal with You, nor is any creature, even if there is some creature above all times" (*supra tempora*, correct); "unchangeably eternal, that is, truly eternal, the Creator of minds" (*vere aeterno*, correct).
- **Scripture:** recognizable throughout (Ps 48:1, Mt 6:8, the Beatitudes, Ps 103:3–5, Rom 8:24–25, Ps 104:24, Ps 63:3, Phil 3:13–14, Ps 146:8).
- **No omissions** were found in any sample paragraph. Word ratios are 1.0 to 1.2.

## Convention skim (whole book, ¶0–40)

No problems found. There are no Thou/Thee/Thy forms and no archaic verb forms. The only regex hits are "art" (the craftsman's art, ¶6) and "Behold", which does not appear. Every lowercase *you/your* is addressed to a human (¶18, human soul; ¶26; ¶33 and ¶35, "my mind") or sits inside a scripture quotation spoken to disciples (¶0). Every lowercase *he/him/his* refers to a human (Moses, the craftsman, the joker in ¶13, Joshua, the singer). God's pronouns are capitalized throughout. "Beginning" is capitalized only where it is identified with the Word/Wisdom (¶9, ¶10, ¶40), apart from ¶4 (finding 6).
