- Paragraphs reviewed: 0, 1, 3, 8, 12, 17, 21, 32, 41, 44, 45, 46, 47, 51 (52 = colophon `GRATIAS TIBI DOMINE`, verbatim on purpose, not reviewed)
- Approx. words reviewed: ~4,030 Pusey / ~4,230 candidate
- Checked clause by clause against Pusey and against O'Donnell's Latin for 1, 17, 21, 32, 44, 45, 46, 47.

## Renderer's Latin-driven departures (all verified; accepted)

- **¶17**, added final clause "though it is not yet ours": Latin *sicuti est, domine, videre nostrum, quod nondum est nobis*. Pusey leaves out *quod nondum est nobis*, and the candidate is correct to restore it. "Your mercy, which proclaims in time You who made times" is also correct: *enuntiantem* agrees with *misericordiam*.
- **¶21**, gifts list: Pusey's served text is corrupt. It has "the word of wisdom, as it were the lesser light: to another faith; to another the gift with the light of perspicuous truth", which duplicates "to another faith" and names the wrong light. The Latin reads *sermo sapientiae tamquam luminare maius propter eos qui perspicuae veritatis luce delectantur tamquam in principio diei*, and the candidate's "greater light, for the sake of those who delight in the light of clear truth, as for the rule of the day" matches it exactly. The rest of the list (*sermo scientiae … luminare minus, fides, donatio curationum …*) is complete. "Our light break forth in its season" (*temporanea*) and "in which the day just mentioned rejoices" (*quo gaudet praedictus dies*) are also correct to the Latin.
- **¶45**, "subject to the male sex": Latin *sexu tamen corporis ita masculino sexui subiceretur*. Correct. The closing comparison (*subicitur appetitus actionis ad concipiendam … sollertiam*) is rendered accurately.
- **¶46**, indicative "Your works praise You … and we love You": O'Donnell's Latin has *laudant … amamus*, so the candidate is correct to the Latin and the change is logged in NOTES. No change of doctrine. Accepted, and I see no need to restore Pusey's jussive.

## Convention skim

- There are no Thou/Thee/Thy forms, no archaic morphology, and no British spellings anywhere in Book 13. Every lowercase "you" addresses a human (the soul, the reader, Paul, the Philippians, Scripture addressees), and capitalization for God is consistent.
- **Quotation marks are handled inconsistently within the book.** Slice a (¶3, 7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 14, 15, 19, 22, 23, 24) adds quotation marks around scripture where Pusey has none. Slice b (¶26–51) follows Pusey and adds none. ¶35 also drops Pusey's only quotation marks (around "multitude"). Rows 1–4 give fixes for the sampled paragraphs. Applying the same rule to the other slice-a paragraphs, and restoring the marks in ¶35, would bring the book into line with STYLE-NOTE §4. MINOR.
