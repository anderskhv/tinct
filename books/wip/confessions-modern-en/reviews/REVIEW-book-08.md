### Specific checks requested

- **¶2–4 (Virgil verse inside one sentence).** The sentence reads correctly across the split. ¶2 ends "who had filled the people with love of", ¶3 carries the verse ("Anubis, the barking god, and every kind Of monster-god, who once took arms against Neptune and Venus and Minerva:"), and ¶4 picks up with "gods whom Rome had once conquered and now worshipped, all of which the aged Victorinus had defended ...; he now did not blush to become the child of Your Christ". The subject "that aged man" in ¶2 connects to "he now did not blush" in ¶4. The "gods" added at the start of ¶4 is a light resumptive word that the Latin supports (*deum monstra ... a se victis iam Roma supplicabat*), and it is logged in NOTES. Nothing is lost: Anubis, the barking god, Neptune, Venus, Minerva, the thunderous eloquence, the font, the yoke of humility and the shame of the Cross are all there. The only fix is the stray capital "Of" (finding 6).
- **Garden scene (¶28–29).** It is recognizable and exact. The fig tree, "How long, Lord? Will You be angry forever? Do not remember our former iniquities", "tomorrow, and tomorrow", "Why not now?", the voice "whether a boy's or a girl's I do not know", "Take up and read; take up and read", Antony and Matthew 19:21, the volume of the Apostle left beside Alypius, and "read in silence" are all present. Romans 13:13–14 is kept in Pusey's cadence ("Not in rioting and drunkenness, not in chambering and wantonness, not in strife and envying; but put on the Lord Jesus Christ, and make no provision for the flesh, in concupiscence."). The only changes are "put ye on" to "put on" and "make not" to "make no", which is acceptable. The "light ... infused" and the flight of the darkness of doubt are kept (see finding 2 for one wording addition).
- **¶30.** "Receive him who is weak in the faith" and "above that which we ask or think" ("more than we ask or think") are recognizable. The vision and the "grandchildren from my body" contrast are intact.
- **¶1, ¶12, ¶13, ¶18.** The Latin-based readings logged in NOTES were checked and are correct: *ad tolerandam illam servitutem*; *placebat et vincebat / libebat et vinciebat*; "third term as assessor"; "ten years and more" for Pusey's misprint "often years" (Latin *decennio et amplius*). In ¶12, "flesh lusts against the spirit", "law of sin", "inner man" and "body of this death ... Your grace alone" keep their precise terms.

## Convention skim

Whole book (¶0–30) skimmed. No Thou/Thee/Thy or -est/-eth forms. No lowercase "you" addressed to God and no lowercase he/him for God found. Human addressees are lowercase ("Where are you now, my tongue?"; Simplicianus and Victorinus exchange). "Holy Ghost" and "charity" are absent. The one name-form point is "Trier" (finding 5).
