## Renderer's logged departures toward the Latin (verified)

| para | departure | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 18 | "Life is wretched" for Pusey's "Life is vain" | Correct. The Latin reads *vita misera est, mors incerta est*. Keep it. The related "how little it would take now to obtain some official post" (*ecce iam quantum est ut impetretur aliquis honor*) keeps Pusey's sense and is acceptable. "break off our pursuit of them" (*praecidenda est intentio*) is more exact than Pusey. |
| 23 | "about ten people" for Pusey's "some often persons" | Correct. The Latin reads *decem ferme homines*, and Pusey's text is a typo. |
| 23 | "to open Your hand" added (Pusey omits it) | Correct. The Latin reads *daturus escam in opportunitate et aperturus manum atque impleturus animas nostras benedictione* (Ps 145:15–16). STYLE-NOTE §8 explicitly covers restoring words Pusey dropped. Keep it. |
| 0 | "she did not leap for joy as though she had heard something unexpected" | Correct. The Latin reads *non quasi inopinatum aliquid audierit, exilivit laetitia*. |
| 12 | "But that was long afterward" | Consistent with NOTES (*sed longe postea*). |

**Unlogged, but correct:** ¶6, "the one true and most truthful God". This departs from Pusey's "most true". The Latin is *unius veri et veracissimi dei*, so "most truthful" is right. The renderer should add it to NOTES under "Latin consulted".

**Open issue from NOTES now resolved:** ¶24 has "My concubine" in the current candidate, as STYLE-NOTE §8 requires. The NOTES "Uncertainties" line about "the woman I had been living with" is stale.
