# Review — Confessions modern-en, Book 7

- **Book:** 7
- **Paragraphs reviewed (0-based):** 0, 1, 5, 6, 7, 12, 13, 15, 16, 19, 21, 22, 24, 26
- **Approx. words reviewed:** ~5,550 candidate / ~5,100 Pusey (plus O'Donnell Latin for every sampled paragraph)
- **Method:** clause-by-clause comparison, candidate vs Pusey, with the Latin checked for every sampled paragraph. I was strictest on the doctrinal passages: 5–6 (God's incorruptibility, "where does evil come from"), 12–13 (the Platonist books vs the Word made flesh / kenosis), 15–16 (the ascent, "I AM that I AM", things that neither fully are nor fully are not), 19–22 (evil is not a substance, iniquity as a perverse will, the ascent to THAT WHICH IS), 24 (Christology: Photinus / Apollinarians) and 26 (Paul, grace, what the Platonists lack).

## Doctrinal spot-checks that pass

- ¶5: "Nothing has any nature at all except because You know it" follows the Latin (*nisi quia nosti eam*) correctly. "what He wills for Himself is good" = *quod sibi vult*. Correct.
- ¶12: "that which was made in Him is life" = *in eo*. Correct. The Johannine prologue can still be recognized.
- ¶13: "renewed by sharing in the Wisdom that abides in itself" follows the Latin *manentis in se sapientiae* rather than Pusey's "abiding in them". This is correct and is the better reading, but it is **not logged** under "Latin consulted" in NOTES-book-07.md. It needs a note, not a text change. Kenosis (Phil. 2) is complete: emptied, form of a servant, obedient unto death, exalted, every knee, every tongue.
- ¶16: "neither fully are nor fully are not … what truly is, is what remains unchangeably". Precise. "And You are my Lord" follows the Latin (*dominus meus es*).
- ¶21: Evil as privation is intact: iniquity is "not a substance, but the perversity of a will twisted away from the supreme substance … toward the lowest things". The *nedum vipera et vermiculus* sense is correct (the earlier draft's reversal is fixed).
- ¶22: "the habit of the flesh" keeps *consuetudo carnalis* (it does not collapse to "body"). The ascent from bodies → sensing soul → inner sense ("as far as animals can go") → reasoning power → THAT WHICH IS is complete.
- ¶24: All the key points are correct. The candidate renders "not because He was the very Person of Truth, but because of some great excellence of His human nature" (*non persona veritatis*), which correctly repairs Pusey's softening "not only". "a whole man … nor a body together with an animal soul lacking a rational mind … but a real man" correctly describes the Apollinarian position. Alypius's misunderstanding keeps "soul and mind", following the Latin (*animam mentemque*). Photinus is correctly placed as Augustine's own later-corrected error. 1 Cor 11:19 closes the paragraph intact.
- ¶26: Rom 7, Dan 9 / Ps 106, "body of this death" (*huius*), Prov 8:22 "formed", Col 2:14 handwriting, Ps 62, Matt 11 are all present and recognizable. The list of what the Platonist pages lack is complete (8 items). The imagery of the mountaintop, the road and the deserters is complete.

No BLOCKER or MAJOR findings. I found no omitted clauses, names, examples, questions or scripture phrases in the 14 sampled paragraphs. The largest are ¶6 (every question in the chain about evil matter is present), ¶7 (the Firminus story is complete, including the messengers meeting halfway), ¶24 and ¶26. I also found no reversals, and the ¶21 and ¶24 repairs recorded in NOTES check out against the Latin.

## Convention skim (whole book, ¶0–26)

No Thou/Thee/Thy forms and no archaic verb morphology. The hits for "art" in ¶7–8 are the astrologers' art, "unto" in ¶13 is inside Phil 2:8, and every lowercase "you" is inside a quotation (the phantoms in ¶10, God's voice in ¶15, Matt 11 in ¶13 and ¶26). Names follow Pusey (Vindicianus, Nebridius, Firminus, Alypius, Photinus, Apollinarian). One inconsistency: the divine neuter pronoun for Light/Word is lowercase in ¶15 (finding 4) but capitalized "It" in ¶23. ¶23 also switches from "It" to "Himself" within one sentence ("carry them across to Himself"). That is tolerable because the Word is Christ, but it could be harmonized to "Itself" if the renderer wants consistency. Separately, the ¶13 *manentis in se* Latin choice should be logged in NOTES-book-07.md.

Verdict: PASS
