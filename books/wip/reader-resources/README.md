# Reader resources: proposal (30 September 2026)

A per-book "Resources" tab: links out to the best videos, podcasts, essays, X posts, TikToks and, later, Reddit threads. The first batch covers nine featured books. `resources.json` lists only items whose URL, creator and channel were checked by fetching them. Status: **proposal, awaiting Anders's picks.** Content only; no code.

## How the tab should behave (for integration)
- **Group items by timing:** Before you start / While reading / After you finish. Spoiler badges read *none*, *light* or *full*. Full-spoiler items appear only under "After you finish", or after the reader has passed the relevant chapter.
- **Links open outside the app.** Don't embed third-party players. Show the type, creator, length and a one-line "why".
- **Labels are neutral.** Nothing may imply the people or channels endorse Tinct. Items carrying a `flag` are shown only once Anders approves them.
- Honour each `pre_publish_check` (missing lengths or dates, or links still to be supplied) before items go live.
