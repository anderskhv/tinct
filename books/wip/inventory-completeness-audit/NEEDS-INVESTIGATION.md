Related scope and policy questions from the first-pass reports:

- **Don Quixote Part II front matter.** The Dedication to the Count of Lemos that is served inside "Part 1, Chapter 52" is not Ormsby's text either (V07-N5). Keep it (and add the Béjar dedication) or drop both? One decision covers both.
- **Notes policy across the library.** Authors' and translators' footnotes are handled inconsistently:
  - served as body text: The Prince, Politics, Imitation of Christ, Kant, Hume, Utilitarianism, Karamazov, Douglass, Around the World;
  - dropped entirely: On Liberty, Discourse on Inequality, Democracy in America's appendix, Leviathan's margins;
  - served as fragments: Social Contract.

  A single notes policy is needed before these are repaired. For example: footnotes as separate records, as the Fear and Trembling package does, or omitted with a stated scope.
- **Dramatis personae** are not served in any play. Recommend recording this as scope.
- **Federalist** salutations, "PUBLIUS" and newspaper datelines are not served. This is consistent with the edition's approach; record it as scope (V08-NEW-3).
- **Iliad:** Butler's book summary is served as the first paragraph of each book. The Odyssey carries its summaries in the chapter titles. **Aeneid:** Dryden's arguments are served unlabelled (S4). Keep, label or drop them?

## D. Coverage gaps and method limits

- **modern-en fidelity was not audited in full.** It was checked at every source-edition defect, wherever screening flagged a paragraph (length ratio, duplicates, count mismatches), and in the reviewers' samples. Invented text turned up in Jerusalem, Faust, Moby-Dick and Great Expectations, and unmodernised chapters in Vindication, Jerusalem and Jungle Book. `docs/edition-divergence-audit-2026-09-12.md` lists nine books with high verbatim rates. **Recommendation:** a separate modern-en integrity sweep (omission, fabrication and verbatim-copy detection per paragraph). That is outside a completeness audit.
- **modern-da** was inspected only at defect coordinates and in spot checks. It was not audited for completeness in general.
- **Short-paragraph drops.** The alignment tool's minimum run (12 tokens) cannot see a dropped paragraph shorter than that. So the lead ran a separate short-paragraph survival test over 91 source editions. It found drops in The Awakening and Macbeth, plus heading and caption artefacts, and nothing else. The 15 editions without a cached source rely on their auditor's own run inspection:
  - As You Like It, The Tempest, Politics;
  - Symposium candidate, Gilgamesh, Prince (it);
  - Ivan Ilyich (ru, en), Faust (en), Niels Lyhne (da);
  - Fear and Trembling (da, en), Descartes;
  - Discourse (fr), US founding documents.

  G01 inspected runs down to 1 token; G06 ran its own one-line-paragraph test.
- **Runtime patch overlay.** `/api/edition-patches` (Supabase `edition_patches`) was not enumerated. Sampled books returned `[]`. The overlay can alter paragraph text but cannot add paragraphs.
- **Production lags main.** On 2026-09-25, 11 edition files on tinct.app were older than main (listed in `INVENTORY.md`). The audit applies to main. The production versions of Jane Eyre and Pride and Prejudice are the pre-cleanup texts.
- **Repository records that are wrong or stale:**
  - `books/raw/meditations/` holds Casaubon while Long is served.
  - `books/raw/aristotle-politics/SOURCE.md` cites the wrong archive.org scan (`arisuoft`; the raw is `arisiala`).
  - `books/raw/genealogy-of-morals/SOURCE.md` claims §11 is missing; it is served.
  - `books/raw/fear-and-trembling/SOURCE.md` dates the scan to 1980; it is the 1895 third edition.
  - `books/raw/us-founding-documents/raw-amendments.txt` is a saved Gutenberg 404 page.
  - `books/raw/candide/SOURCE.md` names the wrong translator.
  - `books/characters/henry-iv-part-2/README.md` claims the Induction is present.
- **SEO pages** publish some of these defects:
  - Jerusalem's spurious "Unity, Unity." chapter;
  - Antony and Cleopatra's "Scene 0" titles;
  - As You Like It chapter titles.

  They need regeneration after any repair (Codex).
- **Out of scope.** These books are not served: the staged `treasure-island` and the WIP `to-the-lighthouse`.
