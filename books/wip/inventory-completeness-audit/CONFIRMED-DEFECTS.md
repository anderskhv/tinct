# Confirmed defects, ranked by reader impact

**Audit:** source completeness of every served edition. **Audited commit:** `main` `1a7d89ebd816af8a2ac18239010c34fab4bf48c5`. **Date:** 2026-09-25.
**Confirmation rule:** a finding is listed here only after an independent second reviewer confirmed it. That reviewer received the claim without the first auditor's reasoning, fetched the source independently and inspected the served JSON directly (see `verification/V01–V11`). The three Symposium items were confirmed by that package's own independent reviews. Every other S1–S3 finding was second-reviewed. One claim was rejected (§6).
**Severity:**

- **S1:** a missing opening or ending, a whole unit, a prologue, induction or epilogue, or a famous or plot-critical speech; or boilerplate at the start or end.
- **S2:** a missing authorial passage of 100 words or more; many losses in one book; a major misplacement; wholesale mislabelling.
- **S3:** a missing passage of 20–99 words; a misplaced chorus or prologue; boilerplate mid-text; authorial matter whose scope is unclear.
- **S4:** cosmetic.

Coordinates are `chapter.paragraph`: the chapter `number` (1-based) and the paragraph index (0-based).

## 3. Root causes: the defect classes behind these findings

Fixing these at the import step prevents recurrence. They are described for the coding agent; nothing here changes tooling.

| Class | Mechanism (observed) | Books |
|---|---|---|
| A. Speech continuations dropped | A speech block without its own speaker heading, after an inline stage direction, is discarded | Macbeth only (tested in all 23 plays) |
| B. Front matter and opening units stripped | Import starts at the first chapter or scene heading and drops what precedes it: prologues, inductions, proems, introductions, prefaces, dedications, epigraphs | Henry V, Shrew, Henry IV Pt 2, Herodotus, Wealth of Nations, Montaigne, Don Quixote, Leviathan (dedication, Table of the Sciences), Social Contract (Foreword), Symposium (package), Moby-Dick (package), Frankenstein, Jekyll, Karamazov, Moby-Dick dedication |
| C. Incomplete imported source | The fetched public text itself lacks authorial parts, so every later check passed against it (the Symposium mode) | KJV Psalm titles (PG #10), Paradise Lost Arguments (PG #26), Frankenstein front matter (PG #84), Karamazov epigraph and Moby-Dick dedication (PG) |
| D. Paragraph filters | Rules that drop paragraphs by pattern: under 20 characters; starting "From the"; OCR-cleanup line rules; first footnote line; last 4 characters of each chapter | The Awakening, Federalist, Aristotle's Politics, Social Contract, Second Treatise |
| E. Act and scene headings misparsed | Only bare `SCENE N.` recognised; the act never advances; licence blocks kept | As You Like It; Antony and Cleopatra ("Scene 0"); Cymbeline (split titles) |
| F. Apparatus served as reading text | Footnotes, line numbers, running heads, page markers, transcriber notes, licence text, facing-page German | The Prince (en, it), Politics, Measure for Measure, Merry Wives, Faust (en, de), Beyond Good and Evil, As You Like It, Montaigne, Imitation of Christ, Nicomachean Ethics, Social Contract, Fear and Trembling, Herodotus numerals, Malachi `***` |
| G. Mislabelled or mis-grouped units | Fixed templates or naive splits: Greek drama "Stasimon"/"Episode" labels on the wrong text; Book and Part boundaries off by one; a spurious chapter | Oedipus Rex, Antigone, Oedipus at Colonus, Oresteia, Medea, War and Peace, Anna Karenina, Wealth of Nations, Imitation of Christ, Jerusalem, Herodotus (modern titles; single-reviewer) |
| H. Misattributed text | The enclosing speaker's label is applied to nested songs or bracketed speech | The Tempest (Ariel's songs), Othello |
| I. Wrong edition identity | Label or translator does not match the served text | Faust (Hayward/Buchheim, not Taylor), Candide (1918 Modern Library, not Fleming), Gilgamesh (Colavito adaptation; rights), Nicomachean Ethics (Chase, not Ross), Meditations and Politics raw records |
| J. Translation-only omission, duplication or fabrication | modern-en drops or duplicates source paragraphs or sentences, sometimes inventing text; modern-da copies modern-en | Apology, Crito, Great Expectations, Jerusalem, War and Peace, Moby-Dick (package), The Manual (package), Wealth of Nations 11.207, Faust; unmodernised chapters in Vindication (package), Jerusalem and Jungle Book |
| K. Served-path divergence | The chapter shards loaded by the primary view differ from the whole-book file used by Compare, search and narration | Wealth of Nations modern-en (confirmed); Montaigne modern-en/da `["…"]` wrappers and modern-da titles (S4); Don Quixote modern-da (single-reviewer) |

## 6. How to use this register

- Every row names the edition(s) it affects. "modern-en / modern-da" shows whether the translation inherits the source-edition defect (usually) or has it alone.
- Repairs are content packages for Claude plus integration work for Codex. See `HANDOFF.md` for small, concrete tasks and their dependencies.
- Existing packages that already fix a row are named in the "Existing repair" column and in `EXISTING-REPAIRS.md`. Do not duplicate them.
- Rows for Danish editions are reported only. The current language strategy excludes Danish repairs.
