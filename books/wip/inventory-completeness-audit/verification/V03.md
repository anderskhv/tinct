## G05-frankenstein-01: CONFIRMED (S3)

**Served.** All three editions open at 1.0 `_To Mrs. Saville, England._` / 1.1 `St. Petersburgh, Dec. 11th, 17—.` (da: `_Til Mrs. Saville, England._`). There are 28 chapters (Letters 1–4, Chapters 1–24) and no front-matter chapter. No shards.

**Which text is served.** `original-en` is PG #84: every served 8-gram occurs in `pg84.txt`, and the served text covers 99.93% of PG #84's 8-grams. Only the contents list is unmatched. PG #84 carries the **1831 revised text**:
- 97.7% of served 8-grams occur in PG #42324 (1831, Colburn & Bentley), against 81.7% in PG #41445 (1818).
- In Chapters 1–2, where the 1831 revisions are concentrated, the figures are 0.977/0.964 against 0.277/0.283.
- 1831-only readings are present: "gone by himself to Milan", "Destiny was too potent".
- The 1831 body is 98.4% covered (5-grams). The residue is spelling variants such as "to-day" and "any thing".

So the registry label `Original (1831)` (`bookRegistry.ts:1053`) and `SOURCE.md` ("1831 revised edition") are correct for the body. The 1831 title page reads "REVISED, CORRECTED, AND ILLUSTRATED WITH A NEW INTRODUCTION, BY THE AUTHOR". The label therefore implies both the Introduction and the Preface belong. The 1818-only Godwin dedication and *Paradise Lost* epigraph (PG #41445 ll. 59–78) are not in 1831, so they are out of scope.

**Source.** In `pg42324.txt` (sha256 `34a453ed…3733`):
- INTRODUCTION is at ll. 60–264. It opens "The Publishers of the Standard Novels, in selecting "Frankenstein" for one of their series…" and is signed "M. W. S. / _London, October 15, 1831._".
- PREFACE is at ll. 269–327 and ends "…has been completed. / Marlow, September, 1817."
- Ll. 330–336 are the half-title, and LETTER I starts at l. 341.

The second copy is the scan-backed en.wikisource *Frankenstein, or the Modern Prometheus (Revised Edition, 1831)*. Its contents list reads Introduction (p. i), Preface (p. 1), Letter 1 (p. 3). Its /Introduction and /Preface pages match PG as word sequences: 2,269 and 573 normalised tokens, similarity 0.9996 and 1.000.

**Authorship.** Mary Shelley wrote the Introduction. The 1831 Introduction says the Preface was "entirely written by" Percy Shelley (ll. 243–244). It was nevertheless printed as the author's preface in both 1818 and 1831, so it is part of the edition.

**Extent.** Introduction: 2,248 words with heading and signature, 2,240 text only. Preface: 572 with heading and date line, 568 text only. **Total 2,820.** The claim's 2,825 includes the 5-word half-title. The boundaries are as claimed.

**Phrase search** (original-en, modern-en, modern-da): "hideous progeny", "Standard Novels", "pale student", "Byron", "Polidori", "Darwin", "Marlow", "physiological writers", "German stories", "ghost story", "1831", "1817", "1816" each have **0 hits**. Two incidental hits are body text only: "unhallowed arts" at 12.32 (Ch. 8) and "Shelley" at modern-en 14.3 (a gloss on the "Mutability" quotation).

**Severity.** S3, as claimed: authorial front matter. Because the label names the 1831 edition, this sits at the top of S3.
