## Method

1. I printed the served paragraphs around every coordinate in original-en, modern-en and modern-da. For all five plays, all three editions are paragraph-aligned: every chapter has the same paragraph count in each edition.
2. I ran a word-level diff (`difflib` over normalised tokens) between each source and served original-en. Before the diff, speaker-label lines and ACT/SCENE heading lines were removed from the source, and leading speaker labels were removed from served paragraphs. The diff lists every source passage that is absent from the served text, and every served-only insertion.
3. I ran normalised phrase searches over the whole served editions. The normalisation folds case, quotes, apostrophes and dashes.
4. I checked modern-en presence two ways: by searching paraphrase-proof keywords, and by an outlier scan of paragraph length (modern-en against original-en, and modern-da against modern-en). For modern-da I checked presence and structure only.
5. I counted words two ways. **ws** counts whitespace tokens that contain a letter or digit. **split** also splits on hyphens and dashes. Every claim figure I checked matches the *split* convention.

Scratch scripts and outputs are in `/tmp/claude-0/…/scratchpad/verify/V06/`.
