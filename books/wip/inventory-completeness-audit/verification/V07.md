**Served:** absent in all editions. "Gibraleon", "Bejar", "Béjar", "bribery of the vulgar" and "littleness of so humble a service" all get 0 hits.

**Correction: this is not Ormsby's text.**
- The 1885 Ormsby vol. 1 has no dedication. Its contents (OCR lines 51–57) and body (3896 → Preface → 4294 Verses → Chapter I) go straight from the Part I title to the Preface.

**Scope:** the dedication is authorial (Cervantes, 1605), but it is outside the edition that Tinct labels "Ormsby (1885)". That fits the rubric's "unclear-scope authorial matter" (S3).

**Decision needed:**
- If the policy is to follow Ormsby 1885, the absence is correct, and the Lemos dedication now served at 52.48–52.55 is equally extraneous.
- If the policy is to follow PG #996, both dedications belong, attributed as supplements.
