## G04-medea-01: CONFIRMED-WITH-CORRECTIONS (MISPLACED, S2)

**Test.** For every bracketed direction in pg35451.txt (l.294–2910), I took the source line immediately before the direction and located the served paragraph that contains the direction. In **every** case that preceding line is in the **next** served paragraph. So each direction is served one speech early.

**Examples:**

| Served | Source | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| 7.5 ends "…That were comfort to my soul. [_Exit_ MESSENGER." | l.2483 follows the Messenger's speech ("…but to no man happiness.") | The Messenger exits before 7.6, his speech. |
| 7.49 "JASON. Give me the dead to weep… [_She rises on the chariot and is slowly borne away._" | l.2823 follows Medea's "Never! Myself will lay them…" (7.50) | Medea is borne away before her speech. |
| 3.21 ends "[_Exit_ CREON _with his suite_." | l.950 | Creon exits before his speech 3.22. |
| 7.10 "[A cry is heard within." | l.2562, after the "Others" stanza 7.11 | The cry comes one stanza early. |
| 6.28 carries four directions, ending "She follows the CHILDREN into the house" | l.2188, 2252, 2258, 2275 | All the stage business of the monologue precedes it (6.29). |

**Corrections:**
- **Count:** there are 35 bracketed directions in the play, and **all 35** are displaced. The claim's "32 of Murray's 34" undercounts.
- **Location:** the claim's 32 locations are all correct. Add:
  - **3.23**: l.1020 "[MEDEA _goes into the House_." It should follow Medea's speech 3.24 ("…wondrous in the paths of pain.").
  - **4.76**: l.1713 "[AEGEUS _and his train depart_." It should follow Medea's 4.77 "Go! Go thy ways rejoicing…".
  - **6.29**: l.2320 "[MEDEA _comes out alone from the house_." It should follow the choral anapaests 6.30.
- **Extent:** all 35 directions total **363 words**. The 32 claimed total 343 by my count (the claim says 327); the 3 added total 20.
- **Heading directions.** The two directions embedded in speaker headings, "MEDEA (_suddenly throwing herself down…_)" and "MEDEA (_rising_)", sit in the right place. Their speeches, however, are merged into Creon's paragraphs (NEW-5).

**Second copy.** In the HTML, each `<p class="direct3">` follows the speech's `</pre>` and precedes the next heading, exactly as in the TXT. So this is an import error, not a feature of the edition.

**Text intact.** The token diff shows no verse missing. Every difference is a moved direction, and one child's line at 7.17/7.18 moves with its direction.

**Editions.** modern-en and modern-da carry the same directions at the same paragraph indices (e.g. modern-en 7.5 "…That would be a comfort to my soul. [Exit MESSENGER.]").

**Severity.** S2 stands: the misplacement is major and pervasive, and it reverses the dramatic sense in several places.

## G04-gilgamesh-02: CONFIRMED (MISSING character, S4, original-en only)
