# Jekyll & Hyde modern-en: chapters 1–2 fidelity and comprehension review

## What I read
I read every source/candidate paragraph pair in full: chapter 1 (0–27) and chapter 2 (0–49). That is 78 paragraphs. The paragraph counts match: 28/28 and 50/50. Proposals are in `jek-ch12-proposals.json`. There are 15 proposals, none blocking, and each "old" string was checked with Python to occur exactly once in its paragraph.

Overall, both chapters are faithful and read well. I found no actor swaps, no negation or causality errors, and no dropped sentences.

## Findings by category
- **Meaning (3):** 1:7 "sweat … like running" became "cold sweat" (the simile was dropped and "cold" was added). 2:24 "fronted about" became just "turned" (it should say he turned to face Utterson). 2:49 "my mind misgives me" became the neutral "I have a feeling", which loses the foreboding.
- **Addition (1):** 2:15 "almost superstitious": the "almost" hedge is not in the source.
- **Reference/gloss (10):** Cain's heresy (1:0), apothecary (1:7), Queer Street (1:13), holograph will (2:0), Damon and Pythias (2:8), conveyancing (2:9), "bowels of mercy" (2:12), troglodytic (2:36), Dr. Fell (2:36) and *pede claudo* (2:49). Each gloss is literal or taken from a reference work, with no interpretation added.
- **Aloud (1):** 2:0 "to whom the fanciful was the immodest" is reordered so it can be followed when heard.

## Notable rejections (left as is)
- **Juggernaut** (1:7, 2:12): "juggernaut" is now an ordinary English word, so no gloss is needed.
- **Sawbones:** the candidate already renders it as "doctor", which is acceptable. Some slang colour is lost, but that is not a defect.
- **Black Mail House** (1:9): the line before it, "Blackmail, I suppose", already explains it.
- **Mr. Seek** (2:14): the pun works when heard aloud.
- **Coutts's:** the candidate already glosses it as "Coutts's bank".
- **No gloss needed:** harpies, "make capital", à propos, the degree letters, fender, statute of limitations, "Master Hyde" and "jack-in-the-box".
- **Hyde's description** (1:21, 2:36): the deliberate vagueness about his deformity is preserved, and I proposed no change.
- **2:49 "as clear as transparency" → "as clear as glass":** this is a slight change of image, but it is harmless and I did not propose a fix.
- **2:12:** "even" was dropped from "even at that dead hour". The loss is minor and I did not propose a fix.
- **Italics:** the source's emphasis italics (_dines_, _is_) are dropped throughout, and the candidate has no italic markup anywhere. This seems to be house style, and the emphasis cannot be heard in audio anyway, so I flag it only.
- **Voice:** Hyde's and Enfield's voices are preserved. The candidate's tendency to add contractions is fine.
