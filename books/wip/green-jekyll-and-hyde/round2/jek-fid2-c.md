**Checks the brief asked for:**
- **10.1, the "mere aura" editorial decision.** The candidate reads "recognise my natural body as no more than the aura and radiance given off by certain of the powers that made up my spirit". This is the first-edition sense ("for the mere aura") and it is rendered correctly. Not a finding.
- **10.7, the Philippi gloss.** It says "like the captives at Philippi, freed when the earthquake shook open their prison doors". This matches Acts 16:26: an earthquake, the doors opened, everyone's bands were loosed. The gloss is accurate. It does not claim the prisoners fled, which Acts 16:28 contradicts, so it adds nothing beyond the source. It is fine.
- **Jekyll's voice.** His hedges ("I think", "I suppose", "perhaps") and his self-exculpating moves ("connived ... for even now I can scarce grant that I committed it", "He, I say—I cannot say, I") are all preserved.
