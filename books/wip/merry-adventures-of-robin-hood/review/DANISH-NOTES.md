# The Merry Adventures of Robin Hood (Pyle) — Danish edition notes

- Fixed terms: Lille John, Broder Tuck, jomfru Marian, sheriffen af Nottingham, dronning Eleanor, kong Richard Løvehjerte, Sherwood-skoven, yeoman/yeomen (unchanged), Guy of Gisbourne, Blue Boar (inn), Sir/sir kept for knights.
- *Curtal Friar* is rendered *Curtal-broderen* or *den korte munk* in different places; *warrant* is *arrestordre* in some chapters and *brev* in others.
- Address forms (du / De / I) vary between speeches.
- Verse and songs keep line counts but are only loosely rhymed.

## General

- Distances: English miles are “engelske mil”; feet, yards and inches are kept.
- Coins and units with no Danish equivalent (groat, farthing, shilling, pottle) are kept.
- Speeches that run across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end.
