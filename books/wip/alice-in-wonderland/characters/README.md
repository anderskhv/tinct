# Character proposal

`identity-proposal.json` contains 31 proposed identities, aliases, one-based first-mention coordinates, delayed reveals and separate inset-story figures. These are editorial decisions, not generated runtime cards or mention offsets.

The onboarding cast is a brief introductory cast. It omits the baby’s transformation, Bill’s later trial role and the outcome of the trial. Position-gated reader cards must additionally respect first mention versus first appearance: for example the Duchess is mentioned in chapter 2 and appears in chapter 6; the Hatter is named in chapter 6 and appears in chapter 7.

Before integration, verify mentions in both exact edition files. Prefer narrow, context-aware matches for `Rabbit`, `Cat`, `King`, `Queen`, `cook`, `Two`, `Five` and `Seven`. Do not resolve ordinary species, ranks or numbers as unique characters everywhere. Do not import characters, names or relationships from *Through the Looking-Glass*, films or illustrations.

No cross-book identity migration is needed for this new proposed package. All production character assets and reader spoiler checks remain integration work; none has been edited here.
