## Verdicts on pre-flagged items in my range

- ch3 p7 national +1: OK. "the national government will have the widest field" replaces source "it will have"; referent only. No term drift.
- ch3 p17 Doge "chief magistrate" to "chief executive": acceptable under the table; "chief official" suggested as optional NIT F3.
- ch1 p7 caps (UTILITY, ATTAINMENT, ANALOGY, AFFORD, SPECIES): the whole list is in caps in the source and in the candidate. The words changed (USEFULNESS, ATTAIN, RESEMBLANCE, GIVE, KIND) are ordinary modernization inside an all-caps block; no emphasis lost. Accepted. Candidate adds periods between the list items (source has none); acceptable.
- ch4 p1 JUST: restored via fix F4. ch4 p8 INVITING: replaced by "INVITED" in caps, emphasis kept; OK.
- ch5 p0 ANNE: paragraph-initial "QUEEN ANNE" normalized; OK. Quoted letter verbatim; OK.
- ch7 p4 DENOMINATE: sentence stays in caps with "CALL"; OK.
- ch7 p1 "the Union" gained: OK, referent replaces "that" in "If that were at an end" and "under a continuation of the Union"; no drift.
- ch6 p3 footnote markers: markers (1)-(4) appear in numerical order in source and candidate, in the same places. What is irregular is that the source has footnote paragraphs 1 and 4 only (2 and 3 are missing); candidate follows the source. Open issue O2.
- ch6 p3 "or ... or" restructured to "Perhaps ... Or perhaps": accepted. Same four motives, same order, nothing added or dropped; "Perhaps" renders the source's disjunction. The war attribution is moved before the motives; consequences unchanged.
