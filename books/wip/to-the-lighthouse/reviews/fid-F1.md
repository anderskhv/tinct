# Fidelity review F1: To the Lighthouse, Modern E, chapters 1–8

**Coverage:** I read every source/modern pair in full, then re-read each chapter as a whole for cross-paragraph issues: 1.0–1.27, 2.0–2.1, 3.0–3.8, 4.0–4.18, 5.0–5.11, 6.0–6.27, 7.0–7.8, 8.0–8.6 (114 paragraphs). Paragraph counts match the source in every chapter. A mechanical pass found no mismatches in italics, brackets, verse lines or refrains. The one parenthesis difference, at 4.15, is acceptable ("she addressed him silently" became "she told him silently"). Word-count ratios all fall between 0.80 and 1.3.

**Counts:** blocking 0, recommended 4 (motif 1, register 1, antecedent 1, meaning 1). See `fid-F1.json`.

## Adjudication of the renderers' uncertain passages

- **1.8 "infidel ideas" → "heretical ideas"**: Accept. It keeps the sense of unbelief and the link to "atheist".
- **1.20 "settlements" → "settlement houses"**: Accept. This is the correct term for the Toynbee Hall-style charitable institutions. The separate "chemist" issue in the same paragraph is flagged below.
- **1.25 "glass shades" → "glass domes"**: Accept. These are the bell-jar covers placed over ornaments.
- **3.3, the split sentence and the added line "That left only the monotonous fall of the waves on the beach"**: The bridging line is acceptable as syntactic scaffolding. The problem is the tail of the sentence: "—this sound" now dangles, and it can be read as the men's murmur, which the modern text itself calls "This sound" earlier. Flagged as recommended.
- **4.0 gloss "like Tennyson's Light Brigade"**: Accept. It is short and accurate, and the brief allows it.
- **4.7 placement of "(Lily thought)"**: Accept. It still attaches to the distant-views thought.
- **4.11 "and Andrew brains" → "and Andrew would have brains"**: Accept. This is the correct reading of Woolf's ellipsis.
- **5.9, "Nature has but little clay…" kept verbatim**: Accept. It is a quoted saying, so keeping "moulded" is fine.
- **5.11, source "raised his head"**: Accept "lifted his head". It is faithful to the source (James's head).
- **"jacmanna" → "clematis"**: Accept. Jackmanii is a clematis.
- **"Sweet Alice" → "sweet alyssum"**: Accept. Sweet Alice is the common name for sweet alyssum.
- **"white deal" → "white pine"**: Accept. Deal is pine or fir board.
- **6.2 Tennyson gloss**: Accept. It is accurate and allowed by the brief. It is slightly redundant after the 4.0 gloss, but it is not an error.
- **6.3 Tennyson fragments kept**: Accept. Woolf's own variant, "Stormed at by shot and shell", is preserved.
- **7.6 "tinged her physical fatigue"**: Accept. "her physical tiredness was also tinged with…" is faithful.
- **8.0, aside moved after "her son James" and "his wife" named**: Accept. The antecedents are correct: the wife is the one who makes him ask for money and turns him out.
- **8.1, Grimm prose lightly modernized**: Accept. It is prose, not the rhyme, and it is faithful.
- **8.2 "liftman in the tube" → lift operator in the Underground**: Accept. It is consistent with "the door of the lift" later in the paragraph.
- **8.3 "spared no phantom"**: Accept. It is kept as "sparing no phantom, indulging in no vision".

## Other findings (recommended)

- **1.5**: "chase" became "follow", which breaks the "chased" echo in 1.7 and 1.8.
- **1.20**: "chemist" is left unrendered. A reader will take it to mean a scientist, not a pharmacist who keeps a shop.
- **6.26**: "a little consciously" became "a little self-consciously", which adds embarrassment that Woolf does not state.
