# To the Lighthouse: character source review

Editorial worksheet for `editorial.json` and `to-the-lighthouse-threads.json`. **Contains spoilers; never expose to readers.**
Locations are `chapter.paragraph` with zero-based paragraph indices in `editions/to-the-lighthouse-original-en.json`.
First mentions were computed by exact, case-sensitive, word-bounded alias matching with longest-alias-wins; the same first-mention paragraph was found in the in-progress modern render (`render/B*.json`) for every entity.
Snapshot gates are the END of the cited paragraph. Evidence sentences are quoted from the original edition.
