@@ 4.4
The clematis was a bright violet; the wall was a staring white. @@ 4.6

@@ 4.7
They both smiled, standing there. @@ 4.8

@@ 4.9

@@ 4.10

Mr. Bankes liked her for telling him to “think of his work.” He had thought about it, again and again. Countless times he had said, “Ramsay is one of those men who do their best work before they are forty.” At only twenty-five he had made a definite contribution to philosophy in one small book; everything afterward was more or less enlargement and repetition.
