A

Discourse

upon the

Origin and Foundation

of the

Inequality among Mankind.


By John James Rousseau,

Citizen of GENEVA.



Non in depravatis, sed in his quæ bene secundum
naturam se habent, considerandum est quid sit naturale. Arist. Politic. L. 2.
