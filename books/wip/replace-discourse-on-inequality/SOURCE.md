Only the 1761 translation is approved as a working source. No later English translation is a source for either edition.

Conclusion under the assignment's rule: anonymous pre-1800 translation with no identifiable translator/editor/adapter in the checked records; Rousseau died before 1955. No later commentary or editorial introduction is included. The source notes are Rousseau's own notes in that same anonymous 1761 translation. Publishers are not presented as translators or adapters. The Gutenberg Harvard reprint was not used because it omits required sections.

## Extraction decisions

The .prp-pages-output reading-text region is used; website navigation, styles, page markers and running furniture are excluded. Inline text nodes are joined without inserting spaces inside words (important for long-s spans). Long s is rendered as the transcription's underlying s. HTML line breaks become spaces; typography is consistently straight quotation marks/apostrophes. Original spelling and capitalization remain.

The source's display headings become chapter titles. The salutation joins the first dedication paragraph. The signature's display lines join as one paragraph; its date, outside HTML p elements, is retained. Rousseau's Advertisement Concerning the Notes is retained as authorial text, not mistaken for a publisher advertisement. The Notes chapter retains all nineteen numbered notes; page-reference headings are removed. The two Latin verse passages in Part II and a non-p quotation container in Note 16 are retained. The verse introduced with a colon is attached to its introductory paragraph. A trailing note marker in First Part paragraph 5 is moved before its period. No translation wording is deliberately changed.

This is an extracted candidate, not yet an accepted complete package. Full scan-level completeness review and modern-English rendering remain pending. Original-overlap gate fails on historical text itself; see HANDOFF.md. Do not paraphrase this historical edition merely to make that test pass.
