- Italics are conveyed by wording: 112.6 "You too, put up your gravestone"; 118.1 "where I am now … where I shall be"; 118.2 "it is these that shall guide me"; 115.4 "the skin called the poke".
- The period term "negroes" is kept in 115.4 (brief rule 4).
