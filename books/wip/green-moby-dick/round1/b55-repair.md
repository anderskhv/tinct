## Notable kept-as-is decisions

- 56.1, 58.0, 59.11 and 57.5 are faithful, but the candidate modernizes proper-name spellings: Hackluyt to Hakluyt, Crozetts to Crozets, Pontoppodan to Pontoppidan, Albert Durer to Albrecht Durer. The brief says to keep names as printed, but the edition already uses Crozet, Lacepede and Pontoppidan elsewhere. I did not open items just for spelling (see the open questions below).
- 56.3, 57.4, 57.7, 58.7–58.10, 59.0–59.4 and 59.6–59.9 are full and modern. They differ from the source only in small word choices ("quick intensity" became "fierce intensity" in 59.4, which I accepted).
- 60.0 and 57.11 are fine.

## Glossing decisions (one gloss per term per chapter)

- 55: howdah ("the canopied seat carried on an elephant's back").
- 56: Leuwenhoeck ("the pioneer of the microscope").
- 57: busks ("corset stays") and skrimshander ("scrimshaw"). I kept Melville's term.
- 59: Anak ("the biblical giant").
- 60: worm-pipe of a still ("the coiled tube"), sound ("dive"), loggerhead ("a stout wooden post in the stern"), mahogany ("dinner table") and Mazeppa ("like Byron's hero, lashed to a runaway horse").
- Left unglossed on purpose: Traitors' Gate, Prodromus, Richard III, terra incognita, Circassian, burghers of Calais, hove-to. Each is either clear from context or would need a longer note.

## Rendering choices to check

- 55.9: "authentic abortions" kept literally. Melville's figurative sense is "misbegotten productions", and softening it would break rule 4.
- 55.11: "the noble animal itself" kept, since Melville treats the ship as an animal.
- 60.4: "like critical ice" rendered as "like ice at the critical point".
- 55.3: "stock of a descending anchor" kept as "stock" (the anchor's crossbar), not "shank".
- 55.8: I used "Lacépède" per the brief's name list. The rest of the candidate has "Lacepede", so the lead should pick one.

## Open questions (not blocking)

1. Proper-name spelling policy (Hakluyt/Hackluyt, Crozets/Crozetts, Pontoppidan/Pontoppodan, Albrecht/Albert Durer, Lacépède/Lacepede). The candidate edition is inconsistent with the brief, and the lead should settle it globally.
2. 58.2/58.3: the source footnote marker (`*`) is not carried in modern-en. The edition already drops it, so I left it.
3. 59.2: the lead should confirm the "the negro" restoration against the edition-wide policy. It follows hard rule 4 and the existing practice in the Pip and Daggoo passages.
