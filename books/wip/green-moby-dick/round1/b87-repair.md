# b87 repair — chapters 87–93

Reviewed 155 / repaired 60 / kept 95. Blocking: 7.

Per chapter: 87 (35 paras), 88 (11), 89 (17), 90 (21), 91 (50), 92 (7), 93 (14). All paragraphs read in full, source against candidate.

## Main patterns found
- **Whaling terms replaced.** "drugg/druggs/drugged" became "drag/dragged" throughout ch 87 and in 91.2 and 91.6. "waif/waifed/waifing" became "marker/mark/marking" in 87.33 and ch 89, although ch 89 opens by naming the waif as the fishery's "grand symbol". All are restored.
- **Period language softened** (lead decision 1): "Asiatics" became "Asians" (87.12), "Crappoes/Crappo" became "Frenchmen" (91.6), and "his tribe; a tribe" became "people" (93.2). The ch 88 Ottoman/Bashaw/Grand Turk joke was flattened to "sultan/lord", "concubines" became "consorts" and "Lothario" became "seducer".
- **Allusions dropped:** solemn league and covenant, Porus's "Indian" battle, Dardanelles, Esau and Jacob, Coke-upon-Littleton, doxology, crim. con., widow's mite, "Republican slaves" (made "American"), St. Jago monkey, Brandreth's pills, Lying-in Hospital.
- **Dropped hedges:** "here and there" (87.3), "presumed" (87.13), "as it were" and "as it seemed" (87.28), "often" made "constantly" (89.1), "perhaps" (91.49), "somewhat" (92.5).

- 87.25 "Leviathan amours" as "courtship" is kept. The footnote asterisk is in place in 87.25 and 87.26.
- 88.2 "_en bon point_" as "plumpness" is kept as an accurate rendering. No Latin/French is left in italics.
- 90.0 is kept: the Bracton epigraph is printed as in the source ("Bracton, l. 3, c. 3."). "Plowdon" is kept as printed in 90.17.
- 90.19: Prynne's "Ye tail is ye Queen's" is rendered "The tail is the Queen's". "Ye" there is the old spelling of the article, not a pronoun, so the modernization is a spelling change of an ordinary word. Kept.

- 90.4–90.15: the "It is his." refrain is intact.
- 93.12: the "infinite of his soul" passage is complete and was kept unchanged. 93.1, 93.8 and 93.11 keep "negro" as printed.
