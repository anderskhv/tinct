## Open questions (non-blocking)
- Quote marks: in this chapter the candidate closes every narrated paragraph with a closing " mark. The source uses the continued-quotation style, with no closing mark. The source also gives 54.28, 32, 34 and 85 no opening mark, so the candidate's text ends up with unbalanced quotes there. My repairs follow the candidate's practice. The lead may want to normalize this across the chapter.
- 54.2: I rendered 'never transpired abaft the Pequod's main-mast' as 'never traveled aft of the Pequod's mainmast'. I added no gloss about the officers' quarters.
- 54.3: I kept the modern 'Galapagos' from the candidate, although the source prints 'Gallipagos'. The candidate also modernizes 'Manilla' to 'Manila'.
- 54.5: 'hove out' is left unglossed because I was unsure of the exact nautical sense.
- 54.67: I glossed 'the ship's run' as 'the narrow afterpart of her hold'. Please check.
- 54.103, 107 and 109: I restored 'Evangelists', with a gloss ('the Gospels') the first time it appears. The lead may prefer the candidate's 'Gospels' throughout.
- 54.98: I restored '* * * *'. Where it should sit relative to the closing quote is a matter of typography.
