## Notable kept-as-is decisions

- 94.7–94.11, the terms of the case: Melville's own definitions of white-horse, plum-pudding, slobgollion, gurry and nippers are all present and accurate. Only "Berkshire marble" (94.7) and "tendinous stuff" / "blandishments, as of magic" (94.11) needed restoring. "Bailing" (94.0) is the correct modern form of the source's "baling".
- 96.1–96.2, the try-works construction: every figure and part checks out (ten feet by eight, five high, iron knees, battened hatchway, two pots, soapstone, the cycloid, fire-board, water reservoir, tunnel, no external chimneys).
- 96.9: the famous opening is kept with its wording "in the face of the fire" and its "O man!" apostrophe. Only the grammar was modernized ("Do not look too long ..."), per the brief's rule for elevated rhetoric.
- 99.13, 99.15, 99.17, 99.19, Pip's "I look, you look, he looks; we look, ye look, they look": kept verbatim. It is a quoted grammar-book paradigm (Stubb names Murray's Grammar in 99.14) and "ye" is the paradigm's second-person plural; modernizing it would break the joke and Pip's "we, ye, and they" echo.
- 99.6 and 100.37: Ahab's inverted sentences ("What is best let alone, that accursed thing is not always what least allures") were kept as rhetoric, not archaism. There are no archaic pronouns in them.
- 99.21: "some old darkey's wedding ring" is kept, per lead decision 1. Pip's "Hish!" became "Hush!", which is acceptable.
- 100.1/100.3: the hail is already "Have you seen the White Whale?" (decision 6). 100.42 "Manila men" follows decision 7.
- 101.8 and 101.11, the provisions figures: all ten numbers match the source exactly (400,000 / 60,000 / 150,000 / 550,000 / 72,000 / 2,800 / 20,000 / 144,000 / 550 / 10,800), as do 180 sail, 30 men, 5,400 seamen, two barrels per man, twelve weeks and three months. Only the units were restored.
- 101.0: the dates 1775, 1726, 1778 and 1819 and the history of the Amelia, the Rattler and the Syren are complete. Dropping the parenthetical repeat "(1775)" is acceptable.
- 100.24–100.28, the Boomer/Bunger comedy: the rum toddies, "very severe" diet, "late of the reverend clergy", the dent Bunger was born with and "die in pickle" are all intact. Only the hydrophobia joke (100.26) had been broken.
