## Glosses (brief, one each)
- The dial's "style (the dial's upright pointer)" in 32.23; "Lamatins (that is, manatees)" in 32.15; "Tartarian tiles of the underworld" in 32.28.
- "ex lege naturae jure meritoque (by the law of nature, justly and deservedly)" in 32.11. Melville's anatomical Latin "penem intrantem feminam mammis lactantem" is kept untranslated, because the Latin veil is his own joke. The candidate had replaced it with an invented paraphrase.
- In 33.4, "in terrorem (by way of intimidation)". In 33.5, "hustings, the platforms of public election".
- "salt-junk (salt beef)" in 34.7; "carlines (the beams under the deck overhead)" in 34.8; "tierce or pipe (both big casks)" in 35.5.
- In 35.8, "Phaedon (Plato's dialogue on the immortality of the soul)" and "Bowditch (the standard navigation manual)".
- Left unglossed: Ahaz-dial, Charing Cross, Elephant and Castle, Black Letter, Logan, Cranmer, Descartian vortices and Mephistophelean. The text carries them, and a short gloss risks inaccuracy.
