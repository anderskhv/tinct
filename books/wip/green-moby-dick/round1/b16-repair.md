## Main findings
- **Deleted content (blocking):** 22.19 had lost two of Bildad's parting instructions, "If ye touch at the islands, Mr. Flask, beware of fornication" and the cheese line to Starbuck. 16.42 turned "morbidness" into "darkness" and dropped "thereby chiefly, but with some help from accidental advantages".
- **Names and allusions changed:** Thorkill-Hake, the Ethiopian emperor, the Pottowottamie Sachem, the XXXIX Articles, "young Hittite" (it had become "heathen"), Belial, "the idol Bell" (it had become "Baal"), Booble Alley, Watts, "grinding the face of the poor", "the pitiless jaw" (it had become "sea"), the "silver calabash" (it had become "cup"), and Gayhead.
- **Period terms softened** (lead decision 1): "little black boy" (17.4), "sagacious savage" (17.25), "tattooed savages" (18.4) and "squaw Tistig" (16.80, 19.33) are all restored.
- **Bildad's Quaker speech left in thee/thou:** 16.57, 16.62, 16.64, 18.10, 18.21 and 18.24. It is modernized to "you" per the brief. His cadence, his Scripture and his "Son of darkness" are kept.
- **Peleg's diction:** the brief keeps the characterization in diction and rhythm, so I restored "marchant service" (16.14, 16.28, 22.10), "talk shark" / "sharkish" (16.26, 18.23), "parmacetty", "all-fired", "start my soul-bolts", "canting", "Hard down out of that!", "a straight wake with ye" and the repeated "Spring ...!" at the windlass.
- The lay numbers are intact and unchanged: 275th, 200th, 777th, 300th and 90th.

## Notable keeps
- **16.49–16.50** "Does thee?" / "I does," I said, unconsciously mimicking him. The joke rests on Ishmael echoing Bildad's Quaker grammar, so the candidate's "Does thee?" (a real Quaker plain-speech form) with Ishmael's ungrammatical echo is the right way to carry the italic "_dost_" joke. This is the one deliberate "thee" left in the range.
- **16.57 / 16.60 / 16.55** Bildad's mumbled Scripture ("Lay not up ... where moth and rust do corrupt", "there will your heart be also") stays in KJV wording as quotation. The _lay_ pun works through repetition.
- **Elijah (ch19, ch21):** the candidate keeps a plain but slightly salty voice. Only 19.22 needed restoring: "Step and growl; growl and go", "skrimmage", "silver calabash", "parmacetti". Every hint is present: Cape Horn fit, the Spaniard before the altar in Santa, the calabash, the prophecy, "tell 'em I've concluded not to make one of 'em", the men boarding in the dim light, and "all in the family", "Grand Jury".
- **22.8–22.13** Bildad's psalmody while piloting is kept throughout. The hymn "Sweet fields beyond the swelling flood" is kept verbatim, with the source's line capitals restored (22.13).
- 16.5, 16.15, 16.41, 16.44, 16.75, 16.78, 17.9, 17.20, 18.12 and all of ch21 dialogue are accurate modern renderings. Queequeg's pidgin in 18.15, 21.23 and 21.28 is kept light. 18.14 was brought into line with it.

- Brief glosses added: Sachem "(chief's)" 16.4; "teenth ... a fraction" 16.58; castors "(the stands that hold the table's condiment bottles)" 17.4, which sets up the "condiment stand of her face" pun in 17.5; "dyspeptic ... those with chronic indigestion" 17.25; "Catholic — that is, universal —" 18.11; jury-masts "(makeshift masts)" 18.25; "confluent smallpox, the kind whose sores run together" 19.1; calabash "the gourd-shaped bowl" 19.22; costermongers "(street sellers of fruit and vegetables)" 20.2; "Watts, the hymn-writer" 22.8; "piers" rendered as "wall spaces between the windows" 21.25.
