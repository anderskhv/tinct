## Notable keep-as-is decisions
- The whole of chs 120 (except 120.6) and 122, and 119.4–6 (Stubb's song). The song is already lightly modernized with " / " line breaks, which I kept as the existing layout.
- 119.19 "Himalayas" for "Himmalehs": a spelling modernization of a place name, not an allusion swap, so kept.
- 119.24 "jet-black negro" for "jet negro": kept as period language, not softened.
- 119.36 "forbear", 119.19 "though we be": Starbuck's and Ahab's elevated register is readable as it is, so these are not rewritten for taste.
- 125.9, 125.13, 125.18: Ahab's Man/unmanned wordplay and the Manxman's "skewer ... out of the middle of the world" are complete and clear.
- 126.8–17 and 127.14–18: the stage dialogue is complete and modern.

## Open source questions (not blocking)
- 119.34 "open eyes; see, or not?" is read as Ahab ordering his own eyes open: "open, eyes; do you see, or not?". It could also be a plain imperative to the crew.
- 119.34 "Take the homage of these poor eyes, and shutter-hands. I would not take it." is kept literal. Who "I would not take it" refers to is ambiguous.
- 119.3: the source has no closing quotation mark before "(sings.)". A closing quote is added before the direction.
- 121.4 "lighting on deck" is taken as alighting (Stubb climbing down from the bulwarks), so it is rendered "landing on deck".
- 123.7 "locked Japan" refers to Japan's closed ports. It is rendered "locked-up Japan" with no explanatory gloss.
