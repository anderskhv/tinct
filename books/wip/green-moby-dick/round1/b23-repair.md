## Open source questions
- 24.7 "unrecorded, javelin islands" is rendered "javelin-throwing islands". This is a mild interpretive expansion; "javelin islands" alone would also be defensible.
- 24.21 "Drive down your hat" is rendered as keeping the hat jammed on before the Czar, which is the standard reading.
- 29.5: the source is missing its closing quote after "Avast!". The candidate's punctuation is fine, and the paragraph is kept.
- 24.12: "Other" is Melville's own form of Ohthere. I kept it as printed under decision 7; the lead may prefer to gloss it.
