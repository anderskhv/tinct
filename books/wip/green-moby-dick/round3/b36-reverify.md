- 42.16 (R2-acc, clause-by-clause): DEFECT, non-blocking.

  - "how is mortal man to account" becomes "how can any mortal account". OK.
  - "would seem impossible" becomes "seems impossible". The tentative "would" is dropped, a minor hedge loss.
  - "by the citation of some of those instances wherein" becomes "by citing some of those cases where". OK.

  - "can we thus hope to light upon some chance clue to conduct us" becomes "hope to stumble upon some clue that leads us". OK: "stumble" carries "chance", and dropping the restart is legitimate.
  - The grammar of the restructured question is now sound. A minimal fix is proposed.
