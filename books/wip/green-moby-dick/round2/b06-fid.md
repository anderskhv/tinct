## Answers to the repair editor's open questions

1. **9.15 "steel tags":** Accept. The reading fits the Roman Corso horse races, in which riderless horses carried spiked weights that goaded them as they ran. "The steel tags hung on it ... into its flesh" says no more than the image does. No change.
2. **9.17 unclosed quote after "What people?":** Accept closing it. It is a source typo, and the next sentence is Mapple speaking directly again.
3. **15.1 "asses' ears" gloss:** Drop it. "Their ear-shaped handles known as asses' ears" presents a trade term that I cannot confirm, and the brief says to leave a term unglossed when a gloss is uncertain. The replacement reads "hung by asses' ears". The Tophet gloss ("that biblical place of fire") is accurate and stays.

On the Scripture split, I agree with the editor. Passages Mapple cites as text ("it is particularly written", or 9.23's quoted fragments) keep their KJV wording. Dramatized speech is modernized: the master's "What do you mean, O sleeper? Arise!", the sailors' questions in 9.17, and Jonah's "who has made". This matches lead decision 8. The narrative lines "Then Jonah prayed to the Lord" and "God spoke to the fish" are Mapple's own words and are correctly modernized.
