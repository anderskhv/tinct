# b128 fidelity review: chapters 128–132

Reviewed all 75 paragraphs (128.0–18, 129.0–9, 130.0–14, 131.0–11, 132.0–18) against source.json, clause by clause. I checked the 53 repaired paragraphs most closely.

**Findings: 0 blocking, 2 non-blocking.**

## Per chapter

- **128 The Pequod Meets The Rachel:** Clean. The Rachel's approach keeps "windward", the burst bladders and the "smitten hull". Gardiner's plea (128.9, 128.14) is complete: "forty-eight hours", "pay handsomely", the Golden Rule and "a child of your old age". Ahab's refusal (128.15) is complete: "Avast", "don't touch a rope-yarn", "God bless you, man, and may I forgive myself", the binnacle-watch order and "three minutes". 128.6 keeps every step of the search and all of its hedges ("seemed to have succeeded", "as far as the man at the mast-head could tell"). 128.12 keeps the second son and the rule of picking up the majority first. The Rachel quotation in 128.18 is intact.
- **129 The Cabin:** Clean. The Ahab–Pip exchange is fully modernized with its content intact: "like cures like", the screwed chair, "bigot in the fadeless fidelity of man", "circumference to its center". Pip's monologue (129.9) is complete, keeps light dialect and keeps "a little negro lad" (lead decision 1). The closing quote added in 129.6 is acceptable.
- **130 The Hat:** Clean. The basket-and-block rigging (130.8) is accurate: a single-sheaved block, the rope rove down through it, and a belaying pin at the rail. That gloss is correct. Ahab trusting Starbuck with the rope (130.9) is intact. The sky-hawk, Tarquin, Tanaquil and the black spot falling into the sea (130.10–14) are complete.
- **131 The Pequod Meets The Delight:** One minor item. 131.9 drops "soon" from "the splash that the corpse soon made". The hedge "may have sprinkled" is kept. The shears, the horse skeleton, the "triply" tempering (rendered "a third time") and "you sail upon their tomb" are all intact.
- **132 The Symphony:** Ahab's speeches in 132.11 and 132.16 are complete and elevated, with only the grammar modernized. I checked every exclamation, repetition and image ("Guinea-coast slavery of solitary command", the marriage-pillow dent, Adam, "magic glass", the windlass and handspike, the Albicore, the Andes hay, the scythes). One minor item: 132.16 "durst not so much as dare" is rendered as "would not so much as dare", which turns a lack of courage into unwillingness and loses Melville's doubled verb. I propose "dared not so much as dare".

## Answers to the batch focus and open questions

1. **132.11 "soil"/"soul":** Confirmed. The candidate reads "fit emblem of the dry nourishment of my soil!", which follows the source. Keep it. Hard rule 3 forbids correcting the text, and adopting the "soul" emendation would be an editorial change.
2. **128.1 "stood up in his boat":** The literal rendering is correct. It is Melville's inconsistency (in 128.4 the captain climbs down his ship's side), and the edition should not resolve it.
3. **130.0 "fain":** "Were forced to hide" is correct. With an infinitive, "fain to" in 1851 usage means "obliged or constrained to", making the best of necessity. It is not the "glad" sense, which is the one in 128.4 ("would then have fain boarded" becomes "would gladly have boarded"), and the repair renders that correctly too.
4. **Lead decision 6:** Confirmed. 128.2 and 131.2 both read "Have you seen the White Whale?" 131.4 follows the same pattern with "Have you killed him?"

## Verdict

Pass. There are no blocking issues. The two non-blocking replacements are in b128-fid.json.
