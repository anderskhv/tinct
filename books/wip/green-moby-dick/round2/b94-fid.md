## Answers to the repair editor's open questions

1. **101.4 "his own live parchment boilers".** Keep the literal rendering. It is the cook's own leathery belly, a pun on the ship's boilers, and should not be interpreted further.
2. **99.8 "Jimimi!"** Keep it as printed. The pun on Gemini survives only in Melville's spelling.
3. **100.17, the added closing quote.** Accept it. The source's missing quote is a typographic lapse before Ahab breaks in. An em dash would also work, but the quote is harmless.
4. **94.12, singular versus plural "assistant's".** I disagree with leaving it. The source's "assistants'" is a plural possessive, and the candidate's singular is ungrammatical after "one of his". Fixed in 94.12.
5. **Pip's "ye" (99.13, 99.15, 99.17, 99.19): CONFIRMED, keep.** It is the second-person plural in a quoted Murray's Grammar paradigm, and Stubb names the grammar book in 99.14. Modernizing it would destroy the conjugation joke and the "we, ye, and they" echo. By contrast, Pip's own "he'll nail ye" and "hoard ye" in 99.21 are correctly modernized to "you", because those are speech, not paradigm.

## Verdict

The batch is **acceptable after one blocking fix (96.6)**. The remaining 15 items are small restorations. The text is otherwise a faithful, complete modern rendering, and the numbers, dates, names and technical sequences are exact.
