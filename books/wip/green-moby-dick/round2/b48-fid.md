## Answers to the repair's open questions

- **48.17 "seethe her" → "Drive her":** this is acceptable. It is a rowing exhortation, and "drive" keeps the sense. "Make her boil, my lads!" would keep the image, but that is optional.
- **48.20 "sided the furthest to windward" → "edged farthest to windward":** this is acceptable. "Kept farthest to windward" would be the more neutral choice if the lead wants less of an interpretive reading.
- **51.2 "piled-up craft" → "piled high with sail":** this is accurate. The ship has just set her topgallants, royals and every studding-sail, so the gloss adds no new claim.
- **51.9 and 51.10 asterisk:** the call-site `*` in 51.9 is correct. The footnote paragraph 51.10 must also open with `*` (item above).
- **Glosses:** these are accurate and brief: trucks (48.23), soundings (48.30), bowline (51.9) and "fullers bleaching cloth" (52.1). I also agree with not glossing "gam" before 53.7.
- **Period language:** I agree with restoring "savage" and "negro" in 48.22, 48.28 and 48.29. This is consistent with lead decision 1.
- **51.5 "funeral-urn prow":** this is acceptable. Plain "urn-like prow" would be closer to the source, but the difference is not worth a change.
