## Questions for the lead / fidelity
1. **32.11** The Latin "penem intrantem feminam mammis lactantem" is left untranslated, while the next Latin phrase is glossed. Was that deliberate, keeping Melville's coy decorum? If not, a gloss would be "(a penis that enters the female, breasts that give milk)".
2. **32.30 vs 32.36–32.37** The list gives "IV. the Thrasher; V. the Killer", but the chapters run IV Killer, V Thrasher. I believe the source has the same order, so I have not flagged it. Please confirm.
3. **32.36** "a sort of Fiji fish": the allusion to the islanders' 1851 reputation for cannibalism is opaque today. A gloss is possible, but it touches period language, so I leave it to the lead.
4. **34.10** "that wild Logan of the woods" (the Mingo leader Logan) is unexplained. It doesn't block the sense, so there's no item.
5. **33.3 / 32.46 / 35.3** "no," is used for "nay" ("no, extorting…"). It's consistent across the edition and I have left it, but when read aloud it can sound like a contradiction.
