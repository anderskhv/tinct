## Questions for the lead

1. **1.10 "the Pythagorean maxim"**: the joke will stop most modern readers. The usual editorial note identifies it as Pythagoras's rule against eating beans. A brief gloss ("the Pythagorean maxim against beans") would restore the joke, but it arguably explains the joke, so I left it as a question rather than an item.
2. **Geographic spellings for the decision-7 sweep** (not flagged as items): 1.11 "AFFGHANISTAN" (inside the mock playbill), 3.18 "Feegees" (landlord's speech), 3.21 "Alleghanian Ridge". Should the playbill and the landlord's dialogue keep them as characterization or quoted-document text?
3. **3.57 "grego, or wraparound, or dreadnaught coat"**: "wraparound" as a noun reads a little oddly. If the source has "wrapall", consider keeping that or using "wrap-all". I have not checked the source.
4. **3.54/3.58 candle vs lamp**: Queequeg enters with a candle, and in 3.58 lights the shavings "from the lamp". This is probably Melville's own inconsistency. Leave it unless the source says otherwise.
