Ishmael's ungrammatical "I does" reproduces the joke of the source's "I _dost_", where Ishmael unconsciously adopts Bildad's form and gets the grammar wrong. The small gloss "unconsciously mimicking him" makes up for the lost italic and is acceptable. This is the only deliberate archaism in the range and should stay.
