## Answers to the repair editor's open questions

1. **64.25 "scougin'":** read it as "scrougin'" (to scrouge = crowd, squeeze, jostle), not "scourging". The main evidence is that Fleece used "scrouge" himself two paragraphs earlier (64.23, "can't get into de scrouge"). The reciprocal "each oder" and the sharks crowding the carcass both fit. The repair's "a-scourgin'" imports a whipping sense the text does not support. Replace it with "a-jostlin'", which is light dialect and matches "crush" in 64.23. This is still a small crux, so the lead may prefer "a-crowdin'".
2. **64.16 "preach to him yourself":** keep "him". It is the source reading, and changing it to "them" would resolve an ambiguity that Melville leaves open.
3. **61.18 stray "!" after "bowsman":** normalizing it to ";" is correct. It is a typographic slip, and the candidate's punctuation is right.

The candidate renders "Esquimaux" as "Eskimos" (65.2) and "Fejee" as "Fijian"/"Fiji" (65.5, 66.4).
