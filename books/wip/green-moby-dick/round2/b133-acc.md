## Verdicts

- **Ch 133: pass after minor fixes.** It reads well. There are local parse problems at 133.7 (stacked "when, while but..." clauses), 133.17 (dense 1851 syntax: "shed off enticings", "quietude but the garment"), 133.29 (a subject with no verb) and 133.32 (a dangling opener, "did crack", "from out ravines"). The footnote at 133.28 is stiff. At 133.1, "sleek" used as a noun reads like a typo, so I proposed "slick".
- **Ch 134: one blocking item.** At 134.10, "guilt and guiltiness" looks garbled. The contrast calls for "guilt and guiltlessness". Please confirm against the source. Other items are minor: "touching" at 134.4 and a participle clause at 134.18.
- **Ch 135: pass after minor fixes.** Ahab's, Starbuck's and Stubb's speeches keep their elevation, and the grammar is modern. The small items are the stray comma in "How, did he get the start?" at 135.4, the unclear subject in "and wet my flesh" at 135.6, "as touching" at 135.10, and "off shoes and jacket to it" at 135.52.
- **Ch 136: pass after one fix.** At 136.2, "the same one who... was dropped astern" dangles. A reader can take it to mean the bowsman instead of the narrator.

## Questions (not proposed as edits)

1. At 135.42, "his great Monadnock hump" is unexplained. Mount Monadnock is a New Hampshire mountain, and "smoky mountain of mist" just before it hints at the meaning. Should it get a gloss ("his great hump, big as Mount Monadnock")? I left it alone.
2. The chapter titles are inconsistent: "The Chase—First Day" but "The Chase.—Third Day", with unspaced dashes. I didn't flag this because the titles must match the source. Please confirm that the source prints them this way.
3. The spelling is "O whale" / "O Parsee" / "O Ahab" in most places, but "oh Ahab" at 135.27 and "oh whale" at 133.17. This is minor, and I didn't flag it.
4. Some readers may stop at "black vomit" (134.37, yellow fever) and at "binnacle-watch" (133.39). Both can be understood from context, so I left them.
