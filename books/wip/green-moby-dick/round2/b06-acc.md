## Questions (not proposed as edits)
1. 15.1 "hung by asses' ears": does this mean the pots' ear-shaped handles? It is opaque to a modern reader, but I am not sure enough of a gloss to propose one.
2. 7.7 "we prefix so significant and unbelieving a word": the word ("late") is never named. Melville leaves it implicit; should it stay unexplained?
3. 7.9 "for stave in my soul Jove himself cannot": this is inverted syntax. I left it as intended rhetoric. The lead may prefer "Jove himself cannot stave in my soul."
