## Questions (no change proposed)

- **76.3 "salamander giants"**: the allusion is to the legendary salamander that lives in fire. A first-time reader may stall here, but I'm not confident enough of a precise gloss to add one. The fidelity reviewer should decide.
- **76.3 "the dread goddess at Lais"**: this is the veiled-statue story (usually placed at Sais). The brief says to keep Melville's names as printed, so I added no gloss, because a gloss would amount to correcting him. Readers will likely not know the story, so this is flagged for the lead.
- **76.2 "lung-celled honeycombs"**: the head's interior is only explained in ch 77 (the junk). In context it reads fine as a forward reference, so no change.
