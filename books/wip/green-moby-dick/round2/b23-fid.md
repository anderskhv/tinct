## Answers to the repair editor's open questions

1. **24.7 "javelin islands"**: I accept "javelin-throwing islands". It is an accurate, minimal gloss (islands whose people wield javelins), and it keeps "javelin". Its companion phrase "succourless" still needs a fix (see item 24.7).
2. **24.21 "Drive down your hat"**: I agree. Jamming the hat on and refusing to uncover before the Czar is the correct sense, and the candidate's "Jam your hat down firmly" is right.
3. **29.5 missing closing quote**: I agree. It is a source typo, and the candidate's `"Enough!" Ahab growled` is correct. "Enough!" for "Avast!" is acceptable.
4. **24.12 "Other"**: keep the printed name (decision 7). In "the words of Other", readers may take it as the common word or "the Other". I propose "the words of a man named Other, the Norwegian whale hunter of those times". This mirrors the edition's own "a man named Bulkington" (23.0), adds no claim and does not normalize the name to Ohthere.

## Verdict

Chapters 23 to 31 pass after these edits: 1 blocking item (28.4, a hedge change plus a compression) and 9 non-blocking items. No other omissions, inventions or softened period terms were found.
