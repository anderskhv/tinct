## Extraction

Speech blocks are paragraphs; uninterrupted chorus speeches remain single paragraphs, including multiple strophes. Verse line breaks are joined to spaces, matching the live files' zero embedded-newline convention. Fourth Episode includes the children's return and Medea's ensuing monologue; Exodos starts with her wait for the messenger. This accommodates the requested seven divisions without discarding the fifth episode or any chorus. Source punctuation and straight apostrophes/quotes are retained. Completeness cross-check and final hashes are recorded in HANDOFF.md.
