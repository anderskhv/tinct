## Defects found

**Ch9 p4 — possible meaning-direction flip; flagging for editorial
verification, treating as blocking until confirmed.** Source: *"Master,
here's the gold you sent me for. What, have you got the picture of old
Adam new apparelled?"* Candidate: *"Master, here's the gold you sent me
for. What — have you got **rid of** the figure of old Adam in his new
clothes?"*

Dromio's line is famously compressed even for Shakespeare glossators, but
the payoff two lines later (Ch9 p6, where Dromio explains "Adam" is the
officer/sergeant who "came behind you, sir, like an evil angel, and bid you
give up your freedom") describes an *encounter with* the arresting figure,
not an escape from him — consistent with the line asking "did you run into
/ get caught by the sergeant," not "did you get away from him." The
candidate's added "got rid of" turns an (ambiguous but source-neutral)
question about whether Antipholus encountered the officer into a specific
claim that he escaped/evaded him — a directional addition not licensed by
the bare source wording. Given (a) the acknowledged difficulty of this pun
even in scholarly editions and (b) that this is the single most
textually-ambiguous line found across the whole play, I'm not certifying
this as a definite reversal — but it is the one paragraph in the play where
the candidate's phrasing makes an interpretive choice the source doesn't
make explicit, and if that choice is wrong it does invert the joke's
logic. **Recommend a second, independent read of Ch9 p4–p6 as a unit before
sign-off — either confirm "got rid of" is defensible against the payoff in
p6, or revise to a phrasing that doesn't commit to a direction (e.g. "have
you run into the figure of old Adam...").**

**Ch11 p154 — clarifying substitution, non-blocking, flagging for
awareness only.** Source: *"There is a fat friend at your master's house...
She now shall be my sister, not my wife."* Candidate: *"...From now on
she'll be my **sister-in-law**, not my wife."* Source uses "sister"
colloquially to mean "sister-in-law" (the kitchen-wench who claimed Dromio
of Syracuse is properly betrothed to his newly-discovered twin brother,
Dromio of Ephesus). The candidate's "sister-in-law" makes the relationship
explicit rather than trusting the period colloquialism — this is a
defensible accessibility clarification, not a fidelity error, since it
preserves the same relationship the source intends. Noting it because it
is a substitution rather than a literal rendering, but it does not require
a fix.

## Verified clean

Proper nouns and running gags carry through consistently: "Porpentine" →
"Porcupine" (Ch7 p17, Ch11 p85), ducats/marks amounts unchanged (200
ducats owed, five hundred ducats rope joke, forty ducats ring, thousand
marks running gag), "respice finem" Latin tag kept and immediately glossed
in-line (Ch10 p24) rather than silently modernized away. The extended
comic set pieces — Dromio of Ephesus's catalogue of beatings (Ch10 p21),
the "Adam"/officer riff (Ch9 p4–10), the Courtesan/"devil" wordplay
including the "God damn me" / "light wench" / "light is an effect of fire"
chain of puns (Ch9 p19) — are all preserved intact with the same logical
chain as source. The climactic recognition scene (Ch11 p94–160) — the
twins' identical claims, the Duke's confusion, Egeon's failure to
recognize his aged voice, the Abbess's reveal, Adriana's "two husbands,"
the final Dromio exchange about seniority — tracks the source exactly:
no actor misattribution, no dropped or flipped negation (e.g. Antipholus
of Ephesus's repeated "I never saw..." denials in p99/p107/p109 all land
on the correct speaker with the correct scope), no altered conditions, no
omitted clauses. Angelo's, the Merchant's, and the Officer's testimony in
the trial-like sequence (p79–90) is not silently altered to resolve the
confusion early — the play's deliberate cross-testimony contradiction is
preserved faithfully.

The one candidate passage with genuinely difficult, quotation-adjacent
material — Antipholus of Ephesus's long recap speech (Ch11 p78, describing
Pinch and the arrest in full) — is fully modernized (no archaic island)
and matches the source's sequence of events point for point against a
careful reread.
