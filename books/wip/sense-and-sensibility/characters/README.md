# Character proposal

This folder proposes book-scoped identities and introductory descriptions. It is not a production `characters.v1.json` bundle and contains no validated UTF-16 mention offsets. Name-evidence coordinates are one-based and identify a literal occurrence, not necessarily the character’s first appearance. Suggested card chapters must be rechecked against the exact disclosure paragraph before integration.

Do not use broad title, pronoun, family, or first-name matching as identity evidence. “Miss Dashwood”, “Mr. Dashwood”, “Mr. Ferrars”, and “Mrs. Ferrars” require scene and time context. Henry and John are different men; Fanny and Henry’s widow are different women; Anne/Nancy and Lucy are different sisters; the two Elizas must remain distinct.

The original-en chapter/paragraph structure is pinned. Completed modern paragraphs map 1:1 to it. On completing modern-en, generate and verify edition-specific mentions against final candidate hashes with existing integration tooling, outside this content assignment. Do not reuse modern-en offsets from original-en. Gate relationship/history cards at the disclosure point and independently check spoilers. No production character files, prior edition references, or cached narration have been changed.
