# Character proposal

Twenty-four book-scoped identities and introductory descriptions; not runtime data. There are no generated UTF-16 mention offsets. All literal evidence matches the pinned original-en coordinates (chapter and paragraph are one-based).

Use the proposed cards only after the stated chapter has been completed. Earlier paragraph-level gates require integration review of every fact in the card, not just its first name occurrence. Fanny’s complete introductory card waits until chapter 3 because it mentions her Ferrars family. Anne is named at 21:31; Nancy is the same woman, first so called at 32:26. Miss Williams may be displayed after chapter 13, but her full Eliza Williams name and family history wait until chapter 31. Chapter 13 paternity claims are Mrs. Jennings’s gossip, not established fact.

“Miss Dashwood”, “Mr. Dashwood”, “Mr. Ferrars”, and “Mrs. Ferrars” need scene and time context. Henry and John are different men; Fanny and Henry’s widow are different women; Anne/Nancy and Lucy are different sisters. The two Elizas are distinct. “Mrs. Brandon” means the elder Eliza in the retrospective chapter 31 account, and Marianne after her marriage in chapter 50. Never generate mentions by a global ambiguous-alias replacement.

Both complete editions have 50 chapters and 1,806 paragraphs, mapped one-to-one. Future integration must generate and validate each edition’s mentions against its final hash; original-en offsets cannot be reused for modern-en. Independent character/spoiler acceptance remains pending. No production character files or cached narration have been changed.
