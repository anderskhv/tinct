# Part G: before → after R3 fixes (for re-verification)

## ch7 ¶16 (text changed)

**before**

> Ethics, however, will require of him that he speak. His heroic courage must then essentially consist in giving up the aesthetic magnanimity — which in casu (in this case), it is true, could hardly be thought to contain any admixture of the vanity that lies in being concealed, since it must surely be clear to him that he is making the girl unhappy all the same. The reality of this heroic courage, however, depends on its having first had its presupposition and then cancelled it. For otherwise one could get heroes enough, especially in our time — our time, which has carried to an unrivalled virtuosity the forgery that achieves the highest by skipping over the middle term.
