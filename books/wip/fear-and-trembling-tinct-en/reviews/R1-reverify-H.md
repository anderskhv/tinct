## 2. New defects in changed text (r0 → current)

Every changed sentence was checked against the Danish. There are no omissions, no additions of substance, no voice or question/exclamation losses, and no anchor problems.

### N1 — ¶25 S4, MEANING SHIFT (hedge introduced), MINOR

- **Danish:** "Forbliver han skjult, men mener ikke kløgtigt …, saa faaer han **vel** Fred, **men** han er tabt for denne Verden."
- **Current English:** "then he **presumably** finds peace, but he is lost to this world."
- **Problem:** here *vel … men* is the concessive construction ("to be sure …, but"), not the conjectural *vel* of ¶23.
  - "presumably" puts doubt on the peace, which the Danish does not doubt.
  - It also weakens the concession–contrast structure, where the peace is granted and the loss is set against it.
  - The r0 wording "no doubt finds peace, but" was actually correct. Finding 23-3 was about ¶23 only, and the applier extended it to ¶25.
- **Fix:** "then he does indeed find peace, but he is lost to this world." Alternatively, "then he finds peace, to be sure, but he is lost to this world."

### Nits (optional)

- **N2 — ¶23 S13, English.**
  - Current: "because it is a contradiction of the universal for it to want to demand itself of one who lacks …"
  - Problem: "of the universal" and "for it" duplicate the subject. "A contradiction of X" can also read as "X's being contradicted". The meaning is right.
  - Smoother: "because it is a contradiction on the universal's part to want to demand itself of one who lacks the conditio sine qua non — the indispensable condition."
- **N3 — n7.34a, lexical echo.**
  - Current: "the supreme ethical expression" for *det høieste ethiske Udtryk*.
  - Problem: it is accurate, but "highest" would keep the root that runs through ¶23 ("higher than the universal") and ¶25 ("the highest").
  - If "highest" re-creates an overlap-screen run, "supreme" may stay.
- **N4 — ¶26 S1, word order.**
  - Current: "since the passion of repentance is not set in movement in it".
  - Problem: the trailing "in it" is awkward.
  - Better: "since in it the passion of repentance is not set in movement". This is ruling-compliant.
- **N5 — ¶26, verb chain.**
  - The epigram ends "Sarah had been deceived before she ever gave herself", and the next sentence begins "No girl has been cheated as Sarah was".
  - Problem: in the Danish, *bedragen* runs through all of these. The break now falls between the epigram and "No girl…" instead of inside the *thi* clause. That is the lesser loss, so it is acceptable as it stands.
- **Minor lexical overlap in ¶25 (no action needed).** "I can conceive of" (*forestille mig*) and "conceived with due reverence" (*opfattet*) share a root in one paragraph. Neither is a fixed term.
