## Screen (minrun 14) after R1

`TOTAL words 3456; in shared 8-word runs 17.6%; in runs>=12 3.2%; in runs>=16 1.0%`

Two runs of 14 or more words remain, both in ¶3, and both are kept:

- **3.3 [16] "it became unreasonable; Abraham had faith. There was one in the world who also had an [expectation]".** This is the *Tiden gik hen … Abraham troede* refrain followed by the *Der var den i Verden, der ogsaa …* formula. It was restored at the reviewer's request (finding 19 and M3). It is a word-for-word rendering of short, plain Danish clauses.
- **3.3 [19] "he too shall not be forgotten. Then he sorrowed, and sorrow did not deceive him, as life had done".** This combines the *skal glemmes* refrain (finding 10), the short sentence *Da sørgede han, og Sorgen bedrog ham ikke, som Livet havde gjort det* and the reviewer's "deceive … as life had done" (finding 23). All of it is forced.

Two other new runs came up and were re-rendered:
- "not wretched enough to have forgotten … therefore" became "not so wretched as to forget …, and for that reason";
- "the wonder of faith is that … and that faith had preserved" became "faith's wonder is this: … and faith had preserved".
