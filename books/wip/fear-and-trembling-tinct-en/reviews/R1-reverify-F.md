## 2. New defects introduced by the edits

I compared every changed sentence in ¶0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 7, 9, 10, 12 and 14, and in n7.15a and n7.15b, with the Danish. There are no omissions, no voice drift and no ungrammatical sentences apart from N-1 below.

**N-1 — MINOR — MEANING SHIFT (¶10, from the F-10.2 re-rendering).**
- Danish: "og bruge al sin Kunst, „der kun er Taarer,“"
- Now: "and to use every art she has, “which is only tears,”"
- *al sin Kunst* is singular and collective: all her art. The quoted clause then says what that art is: only tears. "Every art she has" implies several arts, which conflicts with "which is only tears". A singular "is" after "every art" also reads oddly.
- Fix: "and to use all the art she has, “which is only tears,”". This is still not the reviewer's exact wording, so it should not bring back the screen run the drafter wanted to avoid. Alternative: "and to bring all her art to bear, “which is only tears,”".

**Optional polish (not defects; apply at the drafter's discretion):**
- **P-1 (¶2).** "the interesting — what stands at a crisis, where things hang in the balance. … lives in discrimine rerum — at a crisis, a turning point in affairs". "At a crisis" now appears twice within two sentences, and the gloss anticipates the Latin gloss that follows it. Option: "the interesting — what holds us because things in it hang in the balance". Alternatively, keep the gloss and trim the Latin gloss to "— a turning point in affairs".
- **P-2 (¶2).** "one should not, as sometimes happens, after having oneself loved it pro virili — … — then scorn it" is grammatical but stiff. Option: "one should not, as sometimes happens, first love it oneself pro virili — … — and then scorn it". This keeps *selv*.
- **P-3 (¶3).** *kan mindre beskjæftige*: the modal *kan* is flattened in "has less hold on". Option: "can have less hold on our reflective age". This is marginal, and the current wording is acceptable.

**Checked and passed:**

## 3. Consistency: "reality" for *Virkelighed* (ruling of 2026-09-24: actuality)

`NOTES-F.md` records that the draft followed the older pilot usage. The ruling now requires "actuality" for the concept.

| ¶ | Danish | Current | Required / recommended |
|---|---|---|---|
| 7 | *Den byder at troe paa Virkeligheden* | "believe in reality" | "believe in actuality" |
| 7 | *alle Virkelighedens Trængsler* | "all reality's tribulations" | "all the tribulations of actuality" |
| 7 | *lad Virkeligheden tage det* | "Leave it to reality:" | "Leave it to actuality:" |
| 10 | *saasnart den skal udføres i Virkeligheden* | "carried out in reality" | "carried out in actuality". This is the concept: the aesthetic idea against actuality. |
| 10 | *I Stykket … i Virkeligheden bør der…* | "In reality she ought…" | The ruling allows the idiom to be rendered idiomatically. I recommend "In actuality" so that it echoes the earlier sentence in the same paragraph, where the same Danish word opposes the play and the aesthetic idea. "In real life" is the permitted alternative. "In reality" should go either way, because it collides with *Realitet* = reality. |

¶6 "the rank of real heroes" (*virkelige Helte*) is the adjective and is fine. There are no occurrences in either note. *fatte* / *gribe* are clean: ¶4's *fatte mig kort* is an idiom ("be brief"), and ¶2's *gribe Problemet* is "seize".
