# R2 applied — Part C (ch4 ¶0–11), readability review

- **Review:** `reviews/R1-readability-ch04.md`.
- **Pre-edit draft:** `drafts/history/C-ch4.r2.json`.
- **Revised draft:** `drafts/C-ch4.json`.
- **Scope of edits:** wording only. Each change was re-checked against `source/original-da-final.json`, and none adds content the Danish lacks.

| ¶ | Item | Decision | Old → new / reason |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | "ethical forgetting of the fact that Isaac was the son" | CHANGED | "Or perhaps that speaker had forgotten something that corresponds to the ethical forgetting of the fact that Isaac was the son." → "Or perhaps, just as he had ethically forgotten that Isaac was the son, that speaker had also forgotten something that corresponds to it." The Danish is *glemt Noget, der svarede til den ethiske Forglemmelse, at Isaak var Sønnen*. The reviewer's fix names "faith", but I kept "something": the Danish leaves it unnamed (*Noget*), and the next sentence ("when faith is taken away…") reveals it. |
| 4 | "the faith that makes it hard for him" (referent) | CHANGED | "the faith that makes it hard for him" → "the faith that makes the imitating hard for him". In *den Tro, der gjør ham det svært*, *det* refers back to *eftergjøre* (imitate). |
| 6 | "the terrible thing that love has sanctified…" | CHANGED (wording) / KEPT (idea) | "but if everyone therefore wanted to do the terrible thing that love has sanctified as an immortal exploit" → "but if, on the strength of that, everyone wanted to do the terrible deed that love has sanctified as an immortal exploit". "On the strength of that" ties *derfor* to the passing feeling just mentioned, and "deed" gives *det Forfærdelige* the concreteness of an act. The Danish names no example, so none is supplied. The remaining vagueness is IDEA and belongs to the source. |
| 7 | spiritual-trial sentence logic | CHANGED (partly) | "then surely any thought of sacrificing Isaac would be a spiritual trial — an inner assault on a person." → "then any thought of sacrificing Isaac would, of course, be a spiritual trial — an inner assault on him." "Of course" renders *jo*, and "on him" makes the assault land on the father who lacks Abraham's love. The prescribed first-use gloss is kept. Nothing like "rather than a test" is added, because the Danish does not say it. The contrast is carried by "Only such a man is given such a test" two sentences earlier. |
| 7 | "so that God himself must take Isaac" | CHANGED | "could confess that he lacked courage, so that God himself must take Isaac, if he would have him." → "could confess that he lacked the courage, so that if God wanted Isaac, God would have to take him himself." Danish: *saa Gud selv maatte tage Isaak, hvis han vilde have ham*. |
| 7 | "but not in time" | CHANGED | "— but not in time." → "— though not here, in time." *i Tiden* now cannot be read as "punctually", and I did not substitute "this life", which would lose the word "time". |
| 7 | "judge such a person in this way" | CHANGED | "Would people not, even in the most believing ages, judge such a person in this way?" → "Would not even the most believing ages have passed that same judgment on such a person?" *saaledes* refers back to the judgment just stated (not rejected; blessed, though not in time). |
| 10 | "in both a direct and an inverse sense, incommensurable" | CHANGED (order) / KEPT (idea) | "God's love is, for me, in both a direct and an inverse sense, incommensurable — not measurable by any common standard — with the whole of actuality." → "For me, God's love is incommensurable — not measurable by any common standard — with the whole of actuality, and that in both a direct and an inverse sense." The main claim now comes first. "Direct and inverse" (*baade i direkte og omvendt Forstand*) is not explained, because the Danish does not explain it. That is IDEA. |
| 10 | "married with the left hand" | CHANGED | "to be married with the left hand;" → "to be married with the left hand — in an unequal marriage;" *viet til venstre Haand* is the idiom for a morganatic (unequal) marriage, and the gloss is its dictionary sense. I did not use "beneath his rank", which would say who is lower and so interpret the image. |
| 10 | "For that this is humility I do not deny" | CHANGED | → "For I do not deny, and never shall deny, that this is humility." |
| 10 | "I cannot help looking at it" (minor referent) | CHANGED | → "I cannot help looking at her pictures, although what she paints…". *derpaa* is what the imagination-girl paints (*maler*) in the same sentence. |
| 11 | "the imperfect" pronoun chain | CHANGED | "Is everyone in my time really capable…? Unless I am much mistaken about my time, it tends rather to pride itself on doing what it presumably does not even think me capable of — namely, the imperfect." → "Are all my contemporaries really capable…? Unless I am much mistaken about them, they are inclined rather to take pride in doing what they probably do not even believe me capable of — namely, the imperfect." *min Samtid* (my contemporaries), then *den*, becomes "they". The irony (pride in the imperfect) is left for the reader, as in the Danish. |
| 11 | "for then I would have held back even at the very last minute…" | CHANGED | "for then I would have held back even at the very last minute — without thereby arriving at Mount Moriah too late." → "for if I had, I would have held back even at the very last minute — though without arriving at Mount Moriah too late on that account." *thi da* means "for in that case", that is, had I loved as Abraham did. *holdt igjen* stays "held back", because the Danish does not say what is held back. The apparent tension with punctual arrival is in the source (IDEA). |
| 11 | "in the king's service"; "tragic hero" unannounced | KEPT | *Kongereise* is a journey in the king's service. Capitalizing "King" or pointing to God would be interpretation. The brief says "tragic hero" takes no gloss at this first use, and it is filled in later in the book. |
| 11 | "Abraham's pettiness" (voice note) | KEPT | *Abrahams Smaalighed* is part of the opinion Johannes reports and rejects. "Supposed" is not in the Danish. |
| 8–10 | "movement" before the swimming image | KEPT | IDEA/order; see the brief ("plain; no gloss"). |
| 0–2 | allusions (virgins of Israel, Aladdin, Midas, Themistocles, "toward money I") | KEPT | Ornamental, and the reviewer rated them light. The Danish gives no more. |
