| [19] necessity → death → materialism | CHANGED (wording); the idea is KEPT | Old: "…must necessarily be present, one is saying that no one can experience death before he really dies — which strikes me as crass materialism." → New: "…must necessarily be present **in the movement**, one is **thereby** saying … — **and that** strikes me as crass materialism."
