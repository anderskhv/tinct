| Item | Decision | Old → new / reason |
|---|---|---|
| **[23] SEVERE** *conditio sine qua non* referent | CHANGED | "— the indispensable condition." → "— the indispensable condition for meeting that demand." The Danish sentence names what the condition is *for*: the universal "wants to demand itself" of the sinner. The English now states that link. It does not say what the condition is (innocence, ability and so on), because the Danish does not say. |
| **[23] SEVERE** "strange comedy" link | CHANGED (wording); link otherwise KEPT | "also gave a thought to the possibility that it might occur to a human being to want to act on its teaching" → "also stopped to consider that a human being might take it into his head to want to act on its teaching". *falde ind* is "take it into one's head", which makes the irony about philosophy's unlivable teaching audible. The Danish gives no connective to the sin point, so I added none. The remaining gap is Johannes's own idea-level jump. |
| **[23]** "demonic paradox" | KEPT | This is Johannes's term, and the text sets it against the divine paradox. It is an IDEA-level difficulty. |
| **[25] SEVERE** the garbled "taking the time onto one's conscience … lure out — the dark stirring …" | CHANGED (split into three sentences) | The single long sentence of the previous draft became: "Consider merely the thought of answering in conscience for one's time in this way: of giving conscience time to search out every secret thought in its sleepless tirelessness. Then, unless one makes the movement at every moment by virtue of what is noblest and holiest in a human being, one may discover with anxiety and horror the dark stirring that after all hides in every human life — and, if by nothing else, then by anxiety lure it out. When one lives in company with others, by contrast, one so easily forgets, so easily gets away from it; one is propped up in so many ways and gets the chance to begin afresh." The dropped subject is restored, and the shared object of *opdage* / *fremlokke* ("the dark stirring") now follows "discover" directly. The idiom *tage … paa sin Samvittighed* ("answer for in conscience") replaces the literal "taking the time onto". Every clause is kept, and the resumptive "This thought alone …" sentence is unchanged. The n7.37a anchor, "discover with anxiety and horror", is still valid, and the marker still falls directly after *opdage*, as in the Danish. |
| **[22]** whose self-deception | CHANGED | "the more dazzling the self-deception will be" → "the more dazzling the merman's self-deception will be". Danish *Selvbedraget* has the anaphoric definite article, which points back to the merman's "every self-deception that he can make Agnete happy" two sentences earlier. This is a grammatical resolution. |
| **[23]** "counter-paradox" | KEPT | It is a fixed term (*Modparadoxet*) and an IDEA-level difficulty. The same sentence already defines it: "that the deity will save Agnete". |
| **[24]** "Only after … carry out the universal …" | CHANGED lightly; idea KEPT | "Only after the single individual has been brought to the point of being able to carry out the universal will the analogy to Abraham show itself; and now the paradox repeats itself." → "Only once the single individual has been put in a position to carry out the universal will the analogy to Abraham show itself — and now the paradox repeats itself." (*bragt istand til*). The remaining difficulty is the idea, which Johannes leaves compressed. Adding "restored" or "again" would add explanation. |
| **[25n]** γνῶθι σαυτόν placement | CHANGED (punctuation only) | ", γνῶθι σαυτόν (know yourself), have each" → " — γνῶθι σαυτόν, “know yourself” — have each". In the Danish the maxim stands in apposition to "den græske Anskuelse af Tilværelsen": the maxim *is* how the view is named. So I declined the suggested "Greek maxim", which would drop *Anskuelse af Tilværelsen*. The dashes and quotation marks now mark the phrase as a quoted saying rather than a title. |
| **[26]** "first-priority bond on existence" | CHANGED | "her enormous first-priority bond on existence" → "her enormous prior claim on existence". *Prioritets Obligation* is a secured, first-ranking claim. "prior claim" keeps that legal and financial image in idiomatic English. I declined "first and greatest claim on life" because it drops the image and *Tilværelsen* = existence. |
| **[26]** Tobit smoke detail | CHANGED (wording); content KEPT | "first there had to be the smoking, as the heart and liver of the fish were laid on glowing embers" → "first there had to be the fumigation, with the heart and liver of the fish laid on glowing embers". "fumigation" is an accurate rendering of *ryges*. The Danish does not explain the exorcism, so no explanation is added. |
| **[27]** tense mix "did not turn coward" | CHANGED | "and did not turn coward in the hour of distress" → "and has not turned coward in the hour of distress" |
| **[28]** "or of the explanation" | CHANGED | "or of the explanation" → "or of their explanation". In Danish the definite article in "om Forklaringen" commonly carries the anaphoric or possessive sense. In English, "the explanation" left dangling has no referent. This reverses R1 item 28-5 at the coordinator's request, as a grammatical resolution. |
| **[28]** pity's "strange dialectic" | KEPT | This is an IDEA-level difficulty. The wording is a close, clear rendering of "den fordrer i det ene Øieblik Skyld, i det næste vil den ikke have det". Any gloss would add explanation. |
| **[25]** "someone awakened" (MINOR note) | KEPT | *en Opvakt* is a period religious term. The standard keeps period references without a gloss unless one is essential. |
