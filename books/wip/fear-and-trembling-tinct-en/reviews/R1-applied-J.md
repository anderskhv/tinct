**Book rulings (§C), applied across the part:**
- **fatte** is now "comprehend" (¶58, "I also comprehend that I do not have the courage"; previously "grasp").
- **ængstende** belongs to the anxiety family (¶45).
- **tør** is "dare" (¶47, "dares not comfort"), unchanged.
- **virkelig / virkelige** are the adverb and adjective, not the concept. They stay "really" (¶56) and "actual pain" (¶56).
- **Realitet** and **en Enkelt** do not occur in ¶42–60.
