## Title — PASS

"Var det ethisk forsvarligt af Abraham, at han fortiede sit Forehavende for Sara, for Elieser, for Isaak?" The draft uses the standard's fixed wording from §D. The Danish asyndeton ("for Sara, for Elieser, for Isaak") gains an "and", but that comes from the standard, so it is not the drafter's defect.

## ¶0

**F-0.1 — MAJOR — DISTINCTION BLUR / ADDITION (concealment vs the demonic).**
- Danish: "Hans ethiske Opgave er da den, at vikle sig ud af sin Skjulthed…"
- English: "to work himself free of his concealment — his being hidden, closed up within himself —".
- *Skjulthed* means hiddenness and nothing more. "Closed up within himself" is the vocabulary of *Indesluttethed*, the self-enclosure that Johannes later gives to the demonic. At ch7 ¶28 the demonic man "lukkede sig inde i sig selv" (closed himself up within himself) and "speaks in secret as a demonic nature does".
- Placing that image in the first-use definition of *concealment* merges the general ethical condition of the immediate individual with the demonic before the text has drawn the distinction. Problema III depends on keeping aesthetic concealment, demonic self-enclosure and the paradox apart (¶5: "in their absolute difference").
- The drafter's stated grounding, *vikle sig ud*, is an image of being wrapped or entangled, not of being closed up.
- **Fix:** "to work himself free of his concealment — his state of being hidden — and to become disclosed in the universal." Or, keeping the Danish image: "to disentangle himself from his concealment, his hiddenness, …".

**F-0.2 — MINOR — ADDITION / definition placement.**
- Danish: "som det Almene er det igjen det Aabenbare"
- English: "it is again what is disclosed: what lies open to all."
- The colon gloss works as a second, earlier definition of the disclosure family, but the table allows that definition only at ¶1. "To all" also adds an interpretive universality: the Danish says only *det Aabenbare*, the manifest.
- **Fix:** "as the universal, it is in turn the disclosed." Or "what is disclosed, the manifest." Drop "to all" and leave the definition at ¶1.

## ¶1

**F-1.1 — MINOR — MEANING SHIFT (connective).**
- Danish: "…høiere end det Almene, men det Almene er netop Mediationen."
- English: "…higher than the universal — and the universal is precisely mediation."
- *men* marks the opposition that makes the paradox unmediatable. **Fix:** "— but the universal is precisely mediation."

**F-1.2 — MINOR — ADDITION.**
- Danish: "men den er ikke paa det Rene, naar den vil ansee Abraham…"
- English: "But it is not clear about itself when…"
- *ikke paa det Rene* means "not clear, not in order". "About itself" adds a self-knowledge reading that the Danish does not have.
- **Fix:** "But it is not clear, not consistent, when it wants to…". A simpler option: "But it is confused when…".

## ¶2

**F-2.1 — MINOR — ADDITION (definition wording).**
- Danish: "det Interessante"
- English: "the interesting — what fascinates because something in it hangs in the balance."
- "Hangs in the balance" is supported by *in discrimine rerum* and "Vendepunktets Kategori". "Fascinates" is the drafter's own psychology.
- **Fix:** "the interesting — what holds us because something in it is at a turning point." Or simply: "the interesting — what stands at a crisis, where things hang in the balance."

**F-2.2 — MINOR — Latin gloss inexact.**
- *pro virili (parte)* means "for one's own part, to the best of one's ability", not "with all one's might", which overstates it.
- **Fix:** "pro virili — as best one could —".

**F-2.3 — MINOR — OMISSION.**
- Danish: "efter selv pro virili at have elsket den"
- English: "first love it pro virili"
- *selv* is lost: the target is people who themselves loved it.
- **Fix:** "after having oneself loved it pro virili …, then scorn it".

Everything else in ¶2 passes, including the hedges "I would ask the reader…" and "The reason is said to be", "take … in vain", and the closing question.

## ¶3

## ¶6

**F-6.1 — MINOR — Terminology (*ansvarlig*).**
- Danish: "for hvilken de ogsaa ere Æsthetiken ansvarlige"
- English: "answerable to aesthetics".
- ¶3 and ¶4 render the same formula ("for hvilken han er ansvarlig", "gjør ham ansvarlig") as "responsible", and the standard fixes *Ansvar* as responsibility. The repeated formula should read the same each time.
- **Fix:** "for which they are responsible to aesthetics as well."

**F-6.2 — MINOR — VOICE (echo lost).**
- Danish: "Æsthetiken er imidlertid en høflig og følsom Videnskab"
- English: "courteous".
- In ¶4 Johannes calls himself *høflig* ("I am polite enough"). The ironic echo is lost.
- **Fix:** "a polite and sentimental science".

**F-6.3 — MINOR — ENGLISH.**
- Danish: "det kommer til en Forklaring"
- English: "it comes to an explanation"
- This is unidiomatic. The same problem is in ¶7: "it does not come to an explanation".
- **Fix:** "there is an explanation" / "Ethics has no coincidence, and so there is no explanation".

The other choices pass: "the other" keeps the Danish ambiguity, "filial piety" is acceptable for *Pietets-Hensyn*, and "manager of the public pawnshop" is acceptable.

## ¶7

- English: "believe in reality … trusting the clever calculations … more faithless".
- The Danish plays on *troe* (believe) and *troløse* (faithless), and "trusting" breaks the chain.
- **Fix:** "It warns against believing the clever calculations of the understanding, which are more faithless than the oracles of antiquity."

"Leave it to reality: then it is time…" and "at their own risk and on their own answer" pass.

## ¶12

**F-12.1 — MINOR — MEANING SHIFT.**
- Danish: "han er det rene Menneske"
- English: "is the purely human".
- *det rene Menneske* is "the pure human being", a person rather than an abstraction.
- **Fix:** "he is purely a human being" / "he is the pure human being".

**F-12.2 — MINOR — VOICE / MEANING SHIFT.**
- Danish: "ham kan jeg ogsaa forstaae"
- English: "him too I can understand".
- "Too" suggests Johannes can also understand others. *ogsaa* here is an affirming "indeed", set against Abraham, whom he cannot understand.
- **Fix:** "him I can indeed understand".

**F-12.3 — MINOR — DISTINCTION BLUR (*det Aabenbare*).**
- Danish: "alt hans Foretagende er ogsaa i det Aabenbare"
- English: "out in the open".
- ¶0 rendered *det Aabenbare* as "what is disclosed". The deliberate link between ¶0 (the universal is *det Aabenbare*) and ¶12 (the hero lives in it) is lost.
- **Fix:** "and everything he undertakes is also in what is disclosed, out in the open."

**F-12.4 — MINOR — AMBIGUITY FLATTENED / ENGLISH.**
- Danish: "Guddommens Samvidne med den Enkelte"
- English: "the deity's shared knowing".
- *Samvidne* joins *vidne* (witness) with *sam-* (co-), so it means co-witness or being privy together. "Shared knowing" loses the witness sense and reads awkwardly.
- **Fix:** "silence is also the deity's co-witnessing with the single individual", or "the deity's being privy, as witness, with the single individual".

**F-12.5 — MINOR — MEANING SHIFT (slight).**
- Danish: "et Barn, der bliver et Gudebarn"
- English: "a child of the gods".
- *Gudebarn* is a divine child, one that will be a god. "Child of the gods" reads as parentage.
- **Fix:** "a child who will be divine".

**Process flag (not counted as a defect):** *det dæmoniske* occurs bare here at ¶12, before the table's definition point at ch7 ¶18. The drafter followed the brief's table. §A.5 of the standard, however, says to define at the first occurrence. The coordinator should decide whether the gloss for "the demonic" moves to ¶12. If it does, it must not borrow the self-enclosure wording criticized in F-0.1 for concealment.

### n7.15a

**N-a.1 — MINOR — OMISSION.**
- Danish: "hvorvidt derimod Troens og hele Troeslivet kan blive det"
- English: "whether faith's movements, and the whole life of faith".
- *Troens* is elliptical for "faith's movements and positions", so "positions" is dropped.
- **Fix:** "whether faith's movements and positions, and the whole life of faith, can become so".

**N-a.2 — MINOR — MEANING SHIFT (referent added).**
- Danish: "derfor har han mistvivlet"
- English: "therefore he despaired of it".
- The Danish has no object. The nearest English antecedent of "it" is "this life", which changes the sense.
- **Fix:** "and therefore he despaired". Or, if an object is wanted: "despaired of the matter".

**N-a.3 — MINOR — ENGLISH / logic.**
- Danish: "ikke blot … ikke blot … — men han havde tillige…"
- The split yields "Lessing was not only one of … . He not only had … . He also had …". Two "not only" sentences are left without their "but", and the climax is lost.
- **Fix:** keep one period: "Lessing was not only one of the most comprehensive minds…; he not only had …, … — but he also had a most uncommon gift…".

**N-a.4 — MINOR (optional) — ADDITION.**
- Danish: "Prompthed"
- English: "readiness and exactness"
- This doublet is the drafter's. **Fix:** "a quite rare readiness in his knowledge" or "precision".

The rest of n7.15a passes: the theologia viatorum gloss, "There he stopped; … go further", "autopsy — his own firsthand inspection", and the first-person voice.

## Overall judgment

The draft is faithful, complete and in register. There are no omissions of sentences, hedges, examples or exclamations. The irony (the pawnshop manager, the bald hypocrite, the "higher unity") and Johannes's first person are kept throughout. The footnotes are clean, the Greek and Latin are handled correctly, and the fixed terminology is applied consistently.

One MAJOR defect must be fixed before acceptance: F-0.1. The first-use definition of *concealment* imports the demonic's "closed up within himself", which blurs a distinction the whole Problema depends on.

The 30 MINOR items are mostly connectives (*men*, *paa den anden Side*), term consistency (*ansvarlig*, *det Aabenbare*, *saligere*, *høflig*), a few small additions and omissions, and some English polish. Three of them are optional. With F-0.1 fixed and the connective and term items applied, ¶0–14 are ready to accept.
