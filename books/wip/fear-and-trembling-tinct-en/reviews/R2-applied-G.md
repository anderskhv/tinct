# R2 applied — Part G (ch7 ¶15–21, notes n7.19a, n7.27a): readability review

- **Sources:** `reviews/R1-readability-ch07.md` and the coordinator's list of key items.
- **Pre-edit copy:** `drafts/history/G-ch7.r2.json`.
- **Revised draft:** `drafts/G-ch7.json`.
- **Rule followed:** wording only. Each change was re-checked against `source/original-da-final.json`. No explanation was added that the Danish lacks.

| Item | Decision | Old → new / reason |
|---|---|---|
| [15] "enter into a mystification, by which he annihilates himself in his relation to her" | **CHANGED** | "…enter into a mystification, by which…" → "…enter into a mystification — a deliberate deception — by which he annihilates himself in his relation to her". The Danish *Mystification* means a deliberate hoax or deception, and the gloss gives only that dictionary sense. The reviewer's inference ("so she thinks badly of him") is not in the Danish, so it is not added. "Annihilates himself in his relation to her" renders *tilintetgjør sig selv i sit Forhold til hende* exactly and is kept. |
| [15] "slender lovers" (LIGHT: suggests "flimsy") | **KEPT** | *spinkle Elskere* refers back to part F's "slender heroes" (*spinkle Helte*, ¶7). It should change only if F changes too, to keep one rendering. |
| [15] Axel and Valborg unexplained (LIGHT) | **KEPT** | This is a period reference. §A.9 says no gloss unless essential, and the reviewer found it inferable. |
| [15n] "the idea" | **KEPT** | *Ideen* is a technical term. The difficulty is the idea itself, not the wording. |
| [15n] "Laban (a boor)" hides the Jacob allusion (LIGHT) | **KEPT** | This is the coordinator's ruling from R1. The name "Laban" still carries the biblical echo. |
| [16] "having had its presupposition and having cancelled it" | **CHANGED** (lightly) | → "depends on its having first had its presupposition and then cancelled it". "First … then" only spells out the order that *havt og hævet* already implies. The reviewer's "magnanimous impulse … overcome" names the presupposition and replaces "cancel", which would be interpretation and a departure from the fixed term, so I did not adopt it. |
| [16] "the middle term" | **KEPT** | *det Mellemliggende* is fixed as "middle term" in the terminology table. The difficulty is the idea and the fixed term. |
| [17] "the single individual" used for "anyone" | **KEPT** for all three uses; **CHANGED** the ordinary phrase | Checked against the Danish. All three uses in the augur sentence are *den Enkelte* ("at den Enkelte lexicalsk kan forstaae…, men den Enkelte kan forstaae, at en Augur forkynder den Enkelte Himlens Beslutning"). Under the book ruling, *den Enkelte* = "the single individual", so they stay. The paragraph goes on to set the single individual against the universal. The ordinary *enhver Enkelt* was "Every single person will be able to understand the outcome" → "Any individual will be able to understand the outcome", per the ruling. |
| [17] "an absolute relation to the universal" | **KEPT** | The Danish is exactly "stille sig i et absolut Forhold til det Almene". Johannes contrasts this, deliberately, with "sat i et absolut Forhold til det Absolute". The reviewer's expected "above the universal" would erase that contrast. The difficulty is the idea. |
| [17] "begin where for so many years it has ended: with illusory magnanimity" | **CHANGED** | "…: with illusory magnanimity" → "…: at illusory magnanimity". The Danish *ved* marks the point where aesthetics stops, and "at" makes that a place, parallel to "begin where". |
| [18] "but in absolute faith, but in absolute humility" (LIGHT) | **KEPT** | This is the Danish anaphora *men … men*, restored at the R1 fidelity review's request. It is deliberate, not an error. |
| [18n] tense slips ("then she inflames the merman") | **KEPT** | The Danish itself moves between tenses: *var … holdt … vil … hidser … forsmaaede … vaagner*. The draft follows it. |
| [18n] "lovelier than any woman, and yet beautiful as a saving angel" | **KEPT** | This is exact: "deiligere end nogen Qvinde og dog skjøn som en frelsende Engel". The oddness is in the Danish. |
| [19] "a human pre-existence" | **CHANGED** | "stand for a human pre-existence" → "stand for a previous human existence". The reviewer's "guilty past" is not adopted, because the Danish *Præexistents* does not say "guilty". |
| [20] "the demonic in repentance" | **KEPT** | *Det Dæmoniske i Angeren* is rendered exactly. The difficulty is the idea. |
| [20] "a multiplicity of passions" (LIGHT) | **CHANGED** | "with a multiplicity of passions" → "with a great many passions". This renders *en Mangfoldighed af Lidenskaber*. |
| [21] "the profound contradiction in the demonic" | **KEPT** | *den dybe Modsigelse i det Dæmoniske* is rendered exactly. Johannes leaves the contradiction for the reader to infer from the sentence that follows it. The difficulty is the idea. |
| [21] "the less will Agnete, out of modesty, hide her suffering" (reads backwards) | **CHANGED** | → "the less will Agnete’s modesty lead her to hide her suffering from him". This renders *desto mindre vil Agnete blufærdigt skjule sin Lidelse for ham*. The logic is unchanged: the more cunning his deception, the more openly she will show her suffering. |
