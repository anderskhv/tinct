## Ambiguities and word choices

- ¶0 *som det Almene er det igjen det Aabenbare* — "as the universal, it is again what is disclosed". *igjen* kept as "again" (in turn), not dropped.
- ¶1 *Melleminstantser* — "the intermediate ethical authorities" (legal image of skipping lower courts; echoes pilot ch5 ¶1 "lower court").
- ¶1 "or else there has never been faith, because there has always been faith" — no *netop* in this Danish, so no "precisely" (matches pilot ch6 ¶28).
- ¶2 *fordi den voxede fra Een* — "because it has grown beyond one": kept the Danish's ambiguity (the category outgrew the person / got beyond him) rather than "because one outgrew it".
- ¶2 *tage ... forfængelig* — "take ... in vain" (echo of taking God's name in vain; deliberate).
- ¶2 *Grunden skal være* — "The reason is said to be" (hearsay hedge kept). Final question *Skulde der ... heller ikke være Plads* — "Is there to be no room in the System for such little words either?"
- ¶2 *Concupiscents* — "concupiscence" kept (English word, keeps Johannes's arch tone).
- ¶3 *carambolere* — "carom off each other" (billiards image kept). *Oversigtsmændenes* — "the survey-writers". *stille Fortabelse* — "quiet absorption".
- ¶4 The Danish sentence about the partridge is an anacoluthon (*Jeg er høflig nok til at antage, at Enhver ... ; jeg antager, at Enhver ...*); kept as a broken-off, restarted sentence with a dash. *en halv Snees* (= ten) → "a good ten". *leger Skjul* → "plays hide-and-seek" (keeps the pun on *Skjulthed*). *Nu kommer Humlen* → "Now comes the catch."
- ¶6 *Assistentshusforvalter* — "manager of the public pawnshop" (Copenhagen's Assistenshus was the municipal pawnshop; period reference kept, rendered descriptively rather than by name). *de respektive Makkere i det projecterede Ægteskab* kept literal ("the respective partners in the projected marriage") — the Danish is itself loose about who is meant. *en mærkværdig høiere Eenhed* — "a remarkable higher unity" (Hegelian irony kept).
- ¶7 *Virkeligheden* → "reality" (pilot uses "reality" for *Virkelighed*). *paa eget An- og Tilsvar* → "at their own risk and on their own answer" (the Danish plays on *Ansvar*; "responsibility" is used for *Ansvar* earlier in the same paragraph). *lad Virkeligheden tage det* → "Leave it to reality".
- ¶9 *forsøges* → "be put to the proof" (fixed rendering).
- ¶10 *i hvem den haver Velbehagelighed* — biblical echo (Matt. 3:17) rendered "in whom it is well pleased". *Dog dette veed han sig fri for* — "Of this, however, he knows himself to be free": the referent (making it easier for himself) is left as in the Danish.
- ¶11 no opening dash (SCAN-VERIFICATION F9: it was a speck).
- ¶12 *Samvidne* — "the deity's shared knowing with the single individual" (lit. co-knowledge, being privy together; I avoided "conscience", which is reserved for *Samvittighed*). Reviewer may want a smoother phrase. *Dæmonens Besnærelse* — "the snare of the demon" (Dæmon = demon, not "the demonic").
- ¶13 *holder jeg dem paa Spidsen* — "I keep them on the point" (kept open: at swordpoint / balanced on a tip). *Fortvivlelsens Disciplin* — "the scourge of despair": *Disciplin* here is the penitent's whip (what one "swings"); the secondary sense "discipline" is lost.
- ¶14 *forvirret* (of the bridegroom's face) → "distraught". *værdeligen* (printed archaic form, F5) → "worthily". *Himlen nok var misundelig* → "heaven was surely envious" (*nok* as a supposition, from his point of view).
