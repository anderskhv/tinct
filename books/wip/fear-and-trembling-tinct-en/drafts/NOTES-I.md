## Ambiguities: resolved or kept

- ¶30 "som Individet dog selv ikke har Skyld i": "for which the individual himself, however, bears no guilt". I chose "guilt" over "is not to blame" to keep Skyld = guilt, because ¶37 ("make his doubt into guilt") and the Sarah/Gloucester passage depend on it.
- ¶30 "det Geniale er Forkjærlighedens Udtryk": "what is brilliant in him is the expression of divine favor". "Divine" is carried over from the parallel "den guddommelige Misundelse" in the same sentence. *Forkjærlighed* = partiality or preferential love. A reviewer may prefer "(divine) partiality".
- ¶30 *Sindssvaghed* → "madness", to pair with *dementia*. The literal sense is "mental weakness or derangement".
- ¶30 "Tid ud og Tid ind" → "time and again".
- ¶33 "som da ogsaa dette uden Aandens Tilstand eller i Kraft af Aand": resolved as "the doubting included, which they too manage without being in the condition of spirit and without acting by virtue of spirit". The Danish is compressed. I read *dette* as the doubting, and the whole clause as saying they have no spirit in either sense.
- ¶35 "om det ikke skeer, det faaer han først bag efter at vide": "only afterward does he find out whether that fails to happen", i.e. whether the confusion fails to follow.
- **¶35 and n7.47a *tør*: rendered "may" (permission), not "dare".** In ¶35, "Du tør ikke have Medlidenhed med det Almene" → "you may not have pity on the universal". In the note, "Men tør han tie" → "But may he keep silent?", and "tør han saa tie" → "may he then keep silent?". The ethical question is whether silence is *permitted*, but the Danish carries both senses. Please review. The Danish "Men tør han tie." ends with a full stop; I gave it a question mark.
- ¶38 "Denne Bevægelse er … ligesaavel Ironiens som enhver anden, der har sin Grund i …": "belongs to irony just as much as to any other movement that has its ground in …". The grammar resolves *anden* as "other movement".
- ¶38 "den skal blot vogte sig for Ironien": "it had better simply beware of irony". *den* = "our age".
- **¶39 "at Uforstaaeligheden kunde blive mere desultorisk"**: kept as "more desultory", Johannes's odd word, with no gloss. Please review whether it needs a gloss. It probably means "approached from several sides by leaps, and so made more palpable".
- ¶39 "i Misvisningens Øieblik": *Misvisning* is the compass-needle declination. I rendered it "at the moment their compass needle swings off true", which spells out the nautical image carried by the one Danish word. The literal "at the moment of declination" is opaque. Please review.
- ¶40 "de 3 ethiske Instantser" → "the three ethical authorities". *Instans* is the legal sense ("court, level of authority"). "Courts of appeal" is a possible alternative, and it would echo the pilot's "sent back to a lower court".
- n7.47a "hvem en hemmelig Forstaaelse med Kræfterne i Livet forvisser om hvad Patienten ønsker": kept "the patient" literally. It is a medical metaphor: the ironist knows, like a doctor, what the sufferer secretly wants. Not interpreted further.
- n7.47a "Latterens Fordømmelse" → "the verdict of laughter". "opdrage en frelst ung Slægt" → "bring up a young generation that is saved".
- ¶32 "en forsøgt Mand" → "a man who has been put to the proof", following the fixed rendering of *forsøge*. Its ordinary sense here is "a tried, experienced man".

## Terminology checks

- *Skjulthed/skjult*: "concealed" (¶31); "hides" is used for the verb *skjule* (¶32, ¶38). *Aabenbarelse*: "disclosure" (¶41).
- *Taushed/tie*: "silence" and "keep silent" throughout. "Den æsthetiske Helt var taus" → "was silent".
- *Æsthetiken* → "aesthetics"; *æsthetisk* → "aesthetic". *Ethiken* → "ethics"; *det Ethiske* → "the ethical" (¶40).
- *dømme* → "condemn" where ethics is the subject (¶35, ¶41). Elsewhere it is "judge" (¶30, ¶35).
- *Stolthed* → "pride" (¶33); *Hovmod* → "arrogance" (¶36), to keep the two words distinct.
- *Virkelighed* → "reality" (¶32, ¶33, ¶38), as in the pilot. *Menighed* → "congregation" (¶34, ¶38).
- *Anlæg* → "design" (¶34, n7.47a). Check this against whoever translates ¶26 ("Anlæg i en lignende Stil").
- *sympathetisk Natur* → "sympathetic nature", kept each time, since it is Johannes's repeated formula.
- *Anfægtelse* → "a small spiritual trial" (¶36).

- **Section E (sensitive terms):** "Cumberland's Jew" is the title figure of Richard Cumberland's play *The Jew* (1794). It is kept as a period reference, not a slur. The figure is presented favorably ("even though he does good"). Flagged for the book-level review.
- The Baggesen quotation "blir han udødelig, saa blive vi det Alle" is my own rendering: "if he becomes immortal, then we all will."
- The Bible (¶38, Matt. 6:17–18) is in fresh modern English from the Danish wording: "When you fast, anoint your head and wash your face, so that people will not see that you are fasting."
- "kongelige Obligationer" → "royal bonds" (Danish state bonds). "front margin" is literal (*den forreste Margen*).
- "fiint Sand eller grovt Sand" is literal (the sand for sanding floors).
- "gaae i Slæber" → "shuffle around in slippers".
