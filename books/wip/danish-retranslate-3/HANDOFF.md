# Danish retranslation 3 — Confessions (Augustine)

Content only. Not integrated, not registered, not deployed.

- File: `books/wip/confessions/editions/confessions-modern-da.json` (13 chapters, 462 paragraphs, 1:1 with live modern-en; titles "Bog 1"…"Bog 13").
- Books 10–13 were `[TBD]` placeholders in the live edition. They are now translated from modern-en.
- Books 1–9 (live Danish) were reviewed too: many paragraphs were garbled, so reviewers rewrote them.
- Method: translated by helper agents, then every paragraph reviewed independently against modern-en (462 paragraphs; 334 failed and were replaced by the reviewer's corrected text). Replacements were not reviewed a second time.
- Consistency applied: "himlenes himmel", "himmelhvælvingen", names as in the English (Ambrose, Carthage, Milan).
- Mechanics checked against modern-en: no `[TBD]`, no straight/English quotes or `--`, matching `\n`, `_` and `[n]` counts.

Known open points
- No native-speaker read.
- God's pronouns: Du/Dig/Din are capitalised throughout; Han/Hans for God is mixed in places.
- Bible quotations are plain Danish renderings, not a published Danish Bible text.
- Long sentences mirror the English in places.
