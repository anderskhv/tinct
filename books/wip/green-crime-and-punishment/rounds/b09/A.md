# b09 — Accessibility review (candidate only)

**Coverage:**
- Ch 26: 26.0–26.105 (106 paragraphs)
- Ch 27: 27.0–27.91 (92 paragraphs)
- Ch 28: 28.0–28.39 (40 paragraphs)
- Ch 29: 29.0–29.78 (79 paragraphs)
- Total: 317 paragraphs, all read in order.

**Findings:** 10 proposals, 3 of them blocking.
- Blocking:
  - 27.90: Lebeziatnikov attacks "that hateful, military, Pushkin expression", but this edition's Luzhin never says "horns", so the listener has no idea which expression is meant.
  - 29.32: "You took it!" comes right after "I don't believe it!" and sounds like Katerina Ivanovna accusing Sonia. Proposed: "As if you would take it!"
  - 29.70: an unattributed speech ("I don't want to find a single trace of you in my room!") follows straight on from Luzhin's speech. The speaker is Lebeziatnikov, so the proposal adds his name.
- Non-blocking:
  - reference (3): Porfiry vs Raskolnikov "he" in 26.67; Madame Lippevechsel = Amalia Ivanovna in 27.4; the hands-in-pockets joke at 28.28.
  - syntax (2): the pockets joke at 28.29; Raskolnikov's illogical "and so" in 29.64.
  - typo (1): Amalia's "_gold_" should be "_Geld_" in 28.34.
  - consistency (1): "yellow card" vs "yellow ticket" in 29.32.

**Deliberately not flagged:**
- Amalia Ivanovna's broken Russian and German, e.g. "the young ladies must not novels at night read" and the Karl-from-the-chemist's story.
- Katerina Ivanovna's long, feverish tirades (28.8, 28.10, 29.24).
- Lebeziatnikov's muddled jargon and self-corrections (27.19, 27.21 "and and... devoted...", 29.57).
- Luzhin deliberately getting Sonia's patronymic wrong ("Sofya... Ivanovna").
- Nikolay's fragmented confession and Porfiry's "he-he".
- The tradesman's plain speech.
- Luzhin's stiff legal monologue (29.8).
- Period insults ("trollops", "slut", "Jew" in Luzhin's thoughts), which belong to the characters.
- Typography: dash styles vary between chapters.

**Verdict:** The batch is strong for listening. The prose is clear and well paced, and each scene is easy to follow: the Nikolay confession, the tradesman's explanation, Luzhin and Lebeziatnikov, the funeral dinner and the theft accusation. The three blocking items are short, local fixes: one line that says the opposite of what Katerina means, one speaker with no name, and one allusion whose setup is missing. Once they are fixed, the batch is fit to publish as audio.
