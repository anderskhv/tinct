## Findings
12 proposals: 1 blocking, 11 non-blocking. By category: reference 7, vocabulary 2, anachronism 1, consistency 2. `apply.py check --dry`: 12 applied, 0 rejected.

Notable:
- **19.156 (blocking):** Porfiry says "We had to bother Dmitri too" while Razumihin (Dmitri Prokofitch) is in the room, so a listener will assume he means Razumihin. The proposal adds "the other painter".
- **18.96, 18.100, 18.113:** Raskolnikov's unspoken thoughts come straight after Razumihin's spoken lines with no tag, so a listener hears them as dialogue. Each proposal adds "he thought".
- **19.76:** Razumihin's child-violation challenge has no speaker tag in a three-way exchange.
- **20.16 / 20.17:** The text says "two days before" here, but 19.160 says "three days before". The proposal drops the number.
- **19.100:** Gloss for _vive la guerre éternelle_. **20.56:** Names Napoleon so the list of Toulon, Egypt, Moscow and Vilna makes sense on first hearing.
- Word fixes: "dissembler" becomes "deceiver", "did for" becomes "did away with", and "put people on" (modern slang) becomes "fool people".

## Deliberately not flagged
- Raskolnikov's long, feverish monologues at 19.62 and 20.58 (louse, principle, "trembling creation"), and the fragmented half-asleep stream of images at 20.51.
- Sonia's stammering invitation (18.5, 18.21) and Porfiry's circling "he-he" questions at 19.141 and 19.144.
- Razumihin's long anti-phalanstery tirade (19.74, with "made of rubber") and his speech about the Ivan the Great bell tower.
- Razumihin calling Sonia "Sofya Ivanovna" at 18.68. Raskolnikov corrects it on the spot, so it is intended.
- Lazarus and the New Jerusalem (19.101–19.108). The list of Lycurgus, Solon and Mahomet is left as it is, because the speech says what they have in common.
- The dream at 20.61, where things like the moonlight and the laughing old woman do not fit together. That is deliberate dream logic.

## Verdict
This batch works well as a listening experience. The dialogue is clear and the psychology comes through intact. The real risk for a listener is working out who is speaking. Unmarked inner thoughts that sit right after spoken lines, and one clash with the name Dmitri, are where a listener would get lost. The fixes are small, and apart from them the batch is ready.
