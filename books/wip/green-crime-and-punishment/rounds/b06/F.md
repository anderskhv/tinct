## Considered and rejected

- **Hesitations already kept as dashes** were left as they are, since the device survives. Examples: 18.47 (Pulcheria in the street), 18.104, 18.106 ("Yes, yes—that is, no, not at all!"), 19.94 ("can—not exactly"), 20.47.
- **Trailing sentence-final "...."** was not restored where it only marks a fading voice and the sentence is complete: 18.13, 18.24, 18.39, 18.43, 19.5, 19.92 and 20.68.
- **Wording choices** that are acceptable modern equivalents were left alone:
  - 18.38 "wonderful" for "capital"; 18.111 "put on a performance" for "pull a long face"; 18.87 "Well!" for "Bah!";
  - 19.2 "state" for "Crown"; 19.80 "bluffing" for "humbugging"; 19.26 "kopeck" for "cent";
  - 19.62 "conspiring against me" (for "as thick as thieves... over me"), "wretched act" and "probing me";
  - 19.118 "crucible" for "retort"; 20.7 "thirty degrees of heat" for Reaumur, which is clearer on first listen and does not change the meaning;
  - 20.58 "cannon" for "battery".
- **18.52** "didn't even recognize her": Garnett's "did not know her" means this here, so no change.
- **18.120** "tell everyone about you" for "tell of you" is an acceptable reading.
- **18.103** "On what grounds?" is shortened but keeps the sense.
- **19.161** "mixed up the dates" slightly explains "muddled it", but it is accurate, so it stays.
- **19.62**, the rest of the monologue: the rendering keeps the source's fragmented, self-contradicting rhythm ("Even Zametov is rude. Is Zametov rude?"). Only the "flat/apartment" referent and "the hag" were changed.
- **20.61**, the nightmare: it is faithful apart from three small omissions and certainty drifts, which have been proposed.
- **Names:** no non-source forms were found. The candidate has "Sofya Ivanovna" in 18.68, but that is Razumihin's error in the source, which Raskolnikov corrects. "Mahomet", "Tchebarov", "Svidrigaïlov" and "Dmitri Prokofitch" are all correct.
