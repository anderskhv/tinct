# b11 — Accessibility review (candidate only)

## Coverage
- Ch 32: 32.0–32.69 (70 paragraphs)
- Ch 33: 33.0–33.74 (75 paragraphs)
- Ch 34: 34.0–34.81 (82 paragraphs)
- Ch 35: 35.0–35.37 (38 paragraphs)
- **Total: 265 paragraphs**, all read in order. Source and baseline were not opened.

## Findings
10 proposals: 1 blocking, 9 non-blocking.
- reference: 5 (1 blocking)
- syntax: 2
- typo: 2
- consistency: 1

Notable ones:
- **32.55 (blocking):** Razumihin says "I lost my mind and wronged him! It was his doing — that day under the lamp in the corridor." Here "his doing" reads as though Razumihin still blames Raskolnikov, which contradicts the sentence before it. The proposal rewords it as "It all started that day under the lamp…".
- **32.14:** the mother's phrase is quoted as "_his girl_", and the gloss right after it says "'_your girl_'".
- **32.67:** "Just as a man might endure…" is a fragment with no main clause.
- **35.0:** the misplaced "by warning her in advance" tangles the sentence.
- **Foreign phrases (35.16, 35.22):** "_cher ami_" and "_la nature et la vérité_" are not glossed, while nearby phrases such as _assez causé_, _umsonst_ and _Morgenfrüh_ are. The proposals add short glosses.
- **35.28:** "_O la vertu…_" should be "_Où la vertu…_" (Molière).
- **Allusions:** "Schiller" is glossed at its first use (33.64), and the Wanderers get an inline gloss in 33.19. The translator's note in 33.20 stays, but a listener hears it only as a detached line afterwards.
- **34.67:** a comma is added so "in this one is bound…" is not misheard.

## Deliberately not flagged
- Porfiry's long, circling monologues (33.13–33.24, 33.61, 33.68), including "he-he", self-contradictions and the "psychology cuts both ways" refrain.
- Raskolnikov's feverish free indirect thought in quote marks (32.58–32.61) and the "fresh air" repetitions.
- Razumihin's excited, back-and-forth outbursts (32.12–32.14, 32.43, 32.53, 32.55).
- Svidrigaïlov's long, cynical confessions (35.7, 35.22, 35.26), including the disturbing betrothal and the period prejudice in 35.26. These are content and must be preserved.
- The religious language in 33.22 and 33.56–33.61 (suffering, "Seek and you shall find").
- Probable source-level oddities: 34.3 "that morning" (it is afternoon), "patriarchal quality" (34.19), the tavern being called a "restaurant" (35.34), "He goes to school" (33.19), and the right/left reversal (35.22 vs 35.37).

## Verdict
Strong as a listening experience. The prose is clear and idiomatic, the voices are distinct, and the long monologues stay easy to follow despite their length. The only real comprehension trap is 32.55. The other proposals are small polish items: glossing foreign phrases consistently, one fragment, one tangled clause and two typos.
