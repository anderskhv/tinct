# b08: A (accessibility / listener review, candidate only)

## Coverage
- Ch 24: paragraphs 24.0–24.211, all 212 read in order
- Ch 25: paragraphs 25.0–25.102, all 103 read in order
- **Total: 315 paragraphs**

## Findings
8 proposals: 1 blocking and 7 non-blocking.
- syntax: 2 (1 blocking)
- reference: 6
- vocabulary, anachronism, consistency, typo, markup: 0

Notable:
- **25.36 (blocking):** "don't consider that 'when they overstep all obstacles,' as you put it..." never finishes its clause and sounds garbled. The fix is "don't take that into account when they 'overstep all obstacles,'".
- **24.149:** "I'll become one myself soon! It's contagious!" has no speaker tag, so it can be heard as Sonia speaking. The fix adds "he thought."
- **24.202:** "She's mad and consumptive" comes right after a sentence about Sonia. The fix names Katerina Ivanovna.
- **24.103:** "not unique or even infrequent—but that very uniqueness" sounds self-contradictory when heard.
- **Glosses for foreign terms:** 25.2 _tout court_ and 25.36 _Hofkriegsrath_ (adds "war council").
- **Identifying people:** 25.51 "Dmitri Prokofitch" gets "—Razumihin—", and 25.57 "that fat fellow" becomes "that fat doctor of yours".

Names, italics and forms of address are consistent throughout. There is no anachronistic slang.

## Deliberately not flagged
- The Lazarus reading (24.155–24.187) in King James wording, and Sonia's "She will see God".
- Sonia's broken, repetitive outbursts (24.46, 24.48, 24.56, 24.61), and Raskolnikov's feverish "Three paths" monologue (24.104–24.106).
- 24.209, where Sonia's free indirect thought refers to herself as "her". This is deliberate.
- Porfiry's circling monologues (25.25, 25.32, 25.36): "he-he-he", repetitions, the Sevastopol and Alma analogy, the moth and candle, "cultivated on a certain side", and "c'est de rigueur" (his affected French).
- Raskolnikov's repeated "I won't stand for it!", "You're lying!", "damned Punch", and "priests and deputies". 25.101 immediately glosses "deputies" as witnesses.
- Contradictions that belong to the plot. Porfiry claims Raskolnikov stressed that Razumihin came at his urging, and 25.64 shows that he hadn't.

## Verdict
This batch works very well read aloud. The Sonia chapter is clear and moving, and Porfiry's long speeches are hard only on purpose. Only one sentence (25.36) is genuinely broken. The other fixes are small clarifications for listeners.
