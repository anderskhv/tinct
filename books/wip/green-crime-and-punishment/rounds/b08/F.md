## Considered and rejected

- **The Lazarus passage (24.155–24.187).** The candidate closes each verse with its own quote mark and adds KJV commas and capitals ("Thy brother", "Son of God, Which"). The wording is word-for-word the source's biblical text, and the punctuation matches the KJV. It is left as is, apart from the lost trailing "...." at 24.160, which marks her voice breaking and is restored.
- **25.32 "moth" for "butterfly".** This is idiomatic for "round a candle", and the Russian word covers both. The image is not changed.
- **24.98 "I wasn't bowing to you—I was bowing to all human suffering".** This is a faithful modern rendering of the key line.
- **Dashes in place of ellipses.** Where the candidate uses a dash that still marks a break, I left it. Examples are 24.12 "Are you—going away?", 24.99 "I'm—dishonorable!", 25.39, 25.65 and 25.97. I restored "..." only where the pause itself had disappeared.
- **Word choices.** "Punch" for "punchinello", "rubber ball", "stroke" for "attack of paralysis", "shed" for "barn" and "capital" rendered as "wonderful" are all acceptable modern equivalents.
- **24.66 "that she should not die".** The added "die" is a clarification needed on first listen, not an invention.
- **24.117.** I changed "religious fanatic" back to "maniac" only for consistency with 24.148 and 24.149, which keep the source's word.
