# b13 Accessibility review (candidate only)

## Coverage
- Ch 39: 39.0–39.85 (86 paragraphs)
- Ch 40: 40.0–40.19 (20 paragraphs)
- Ch 41: 41.0–41.29 (30 paragraphs)
- **Total: 136 paragraphs**, all read in order.

## Findings
8 proposals: 1 blocking, 7 non-blocking.
- reference: 4 (39.21, 39.42, 40.3, 40.9)
- syntax: 2 (39.18, 40.11)
- consistency: 1 (39.75)
- vocabulary: 1 (41.20)

Notable:
- **39.42 (blocking):** a translator's footnote ("Dostoevsky appears to have forgotten that it is after sunset...") sits in the middle of the police-station scene as an ordinary paragraph that begins "The translator's note read:". A listener hears it as part of the story. The proposal marks it plainly as "Translator's note:". An editor may prefer to move it out of the reading text entirely.
- **39.75:** "the same peasant who had jostled past on the stairs", but 39.74 says a *porter* pushed past him.
- **40.3:** "whom he had supported almost since he was thirteen" could mean either Raskolnikov or the student.
- **40.9:** "their mother overhearing her talk in her sleep" does not say who talked in her sleep (Dounia).
- **39.18:** inside his own quoted thought, Raskolnikov says "Couldn't *he* stop...". The change of person trips a listener.

## Deliberately not flagged
- Raskolnikov's disjointed monologues in 39.7, 39.10, 39.20 and 39.22 ("Tfoo!", "Foo!", "(Ha-ha-ha! What am I thinking?)", the misspelled "Campany" sign and the letter _a_).
- Ilya Petrovitch's rambling about hats, nihilists, "midwives" and _nihil est_ (39.46–39.56), and the half-remembered fairy-tale line in 39.39.
- The confession line "_It was I killed the old pawnbroker woman..._" (39.83), which is archaic but clear and deliberate.
- The Hay Market bowing, the "drink the cup" imagery, the crosses and Lazarus, and the plague dream (41.16).
- "in prison, _in freedom_" (41.3), a deliberate paradox, plus the Irtish, _drap de dames_, Five Corners and Voznesensky Prospect, all clear from context.

## Verdict
The batch listens very well. The climax, the confession and the epilogue are clear, well paced and correct. The only real obstacle is the stray translator's note at 39.42. The other proposals are small fixes to pronouns and consistency.
