**Coverage:** Ch 21: 21.0–21.129 (130). Ch 22: 22.0–22.109 (110). Ch 23: 23.0–23.58 (59). Total: 299 paragraphs, read in order.

**Findings:** 14 proposals, 2 of them blocking. By category: 6 reference, 3 syntax, 2 consistency, 1 typo, 1 vocabulary, 1 reference/muddle (blocking).
- **Blocking:** 21.68 "Aniska can't make like this" is missing a word ("make one like this"). In 23.17, "borrowing from that man until he offered help" comes right after the talk about Svidrigaïlov, but she means Luzhin, so it is named explicitly.
