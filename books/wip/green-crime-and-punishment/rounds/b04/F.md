### Most important
- **14.113 (invented, blocking).** "and now enough, old woman, your time is up!" becomes the source's "and now enough, madam, leave me in peace!" The candidate had turned his plea to be left alone into gloating. The same paragraph also restores "some power of darkness" in place of "some invisible power".
- **13.76 (hesitation, blocking).** Before the "I was searching..." confession, the source has him screw up his eyes and pause, then restart. The candidate removed both, which made the confession fluent.
- **13.95 (meaning, blocking).** The candidate opened with a duplicated "Here is how I would do it" where the source has "I should do it quite differently".
