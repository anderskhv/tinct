## B. Comprehension read of v2 (`accessibility-read-v2.md`) — wording fixes taken

- **P-I 0, "Taken immediately" misread as "at once".** Now "Taken in his immediacy".
- **P-II 3, "instead of the reverse, that he did not reach it" was hard to parse.** Now "and it is not, the other way round, that he fell short of it".
- **P-II 7, "match each other" suggested agreement.** Now "These two descriptions are linked in ordinary speech as well".
- **P-II 1, "the other" had no clear antecedent.** Now "the second" (see A).

The remaining SEVERE and MODERATE items repeat the v1 items classed in
`resolution-v1-to-v2.md` as belonging to the source. They are:

- the "never existed because it has always existed" formula;
- the order in which the heroes are named;
- the Pythagoras saying;
- "a later one";
- "130 years";
- the Hegel child/man example.

The second reader confirms that the argument can be followed despite them. The
child/man example (P-II 1) is still rated SEVERE by both readers. The sentence
now glosses every term and is grammatical. What remains is that Johannes's
summary of Hegel's example is itself compressed and counter-intuitive, so it
is recorded as a residual difficulty that belongs to the source.
