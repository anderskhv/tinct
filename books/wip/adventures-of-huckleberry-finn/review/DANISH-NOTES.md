# Adventures of Huckleberry Finn (Twain) — Danish edition notes

- The racial term is rendered *neger / negre* (and in compounds, e.g. *negerstjæler*, *negerkvinde*) throughout, as in the glossary. This is the editorial decision to confirm before publication.
- Fixed terms: tante Sally, onkel Silas, frøken Watson, enken Douglas, oberst Grangerford, Hareskår, tow-head, Royal Nonesuch.
- Huck’s and Jim’s dialect is rendered as colloquial Danish with some dropped letters (e.g. *ik’*, *gu’*); it is not a systematic dialect translation.
- Mixed forms: *Bilgevand* for the nickname Bilgewater; *tærte* for pie (unified); *Sallemon* normalised to *Salomon*.
- Chapter 28 contains a stray “v” that is in the English source.

## General

- Distances: English miles are “engelske mil”; feet, yards and inches are kept.
- Coins and units with no Danish equivalent (groat, farthing, shilling, pottle) are kept.
- Speeches that run across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end.
