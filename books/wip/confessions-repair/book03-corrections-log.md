## Majors (all 6 addressed)

- **Para 2 (F10, major)** — Removed invented "though never less than perfect"; restored the dropped comparative ("more incorruptibly pity"). Now: "your pity for them is more incorruptible than ours, yet you are wounded by no sorrow."
- **Para 9 (F24, major)** — Restored "in looking for whom I fail, that I may become strong" sense, replacing the truncated "whom I search for so that I might grow strong." Now: "in looking for whom I fail, so that I may grow strong."
- **Para 10 (F28, major)** — Fixed garbled husks/swine clause. Now: "shut out even from the husks of the pigs I myself was feeding with husks" (coherent prodigal-son allusion: he fed the swine, was barred even from their husks).
- **Para 13 (F31, major)** — Fixed "syllable" → "metrical foot" throughout the verse-meter analogy, restoring the correctness of the "one art, many rule-sets" argument.
- **Para 17 (F35, major)** — Fixed inverted subject: the *morsel/food itself*, not the act of giving, is what the Manichee doctrine treats as a capital crime. Now: "the morsel given to him would seem, to them, as good as condemned to death."
- **Para 20 (F44, major)** — Removed the third-person editorial gloss "— a heretical sect claiming secret spiritual knowledge, which Augustine belonged to for years —". "Manichees" stands alone, already established at paras 5 and 17.

## Systemic fixes

- **Rhetorical questions restored (paras 0, 7, 9, 19)** — F1, F20, F25, F46. Each flattened exclamation/statement changed back to a genuine question per source punctuation.
- **Interpretive connectives removed/softened**:

- **"Subverters" → "Wreckers" (F12, F13, F14, F15)** — Reverted to source's "Subverters" throughout para 5 as a deliberate, non-silent choice (source explicitly names this group; keeping the term avoids an undeclared rename). Fixed "malicious birth" → "inborn malice" (was "appetite," which shifted nature to desire) and closed the question after "Subverters?" per source punctuation.
