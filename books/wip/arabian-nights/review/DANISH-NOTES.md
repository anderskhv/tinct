# The Arabian Nights Entertainments (Lang) — Danish edition notes

## Terms applied

ånd/ånder (genie), åndernes prins/fyrste, vizir/storvizir (definite: viziren/storviziren), kalif, sultan/sultaninde, kalender (pl. kalendere), rokfuglen, Bagdad, Sindbad Sømanden, Haroun al-Raschid, Camaralzaman, Ali Cogia, Den Smukke Perserinde, sekiner (sequins), engelske mil. After review, regexes unified the many spellings reviewers flagged (stor-vizir, store vizir, Storvizirn, Vizirn …).

## Choices a Danish reader may want to revisit

- Address forms vary: the helpers mixed *du / De / I* for the same speaker pairs in places (reviewers harmonised clear clashes inside one speech only). A separate pass could fix the whole book.
- Vocatives: *Herre* (about 43) and *Sire* (about 29) both occur for “Sire”.
- “Three leagues” in one packet became “tre ligaer” by reviewer replacement; other leagues are “mil”.
- Speeches continued across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end; a few reviewer replacements closed a paragraph early.
- Verse is meaning-first and mostly unrhymed.
