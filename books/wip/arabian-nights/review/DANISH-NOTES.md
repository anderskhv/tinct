# The Arabian Nights Entertainments (Lang) — Danish edition notes

## Terms applied

ånd/ånder (genie), åndernes prins/fyrste, vizir/storvizir (definite: viziren/storviziren), kalif, sultan/sultaninde, kalender (pl. kalendere), rokfuglen, Bagdad, Sindbad Søfareren (changed from Sindbad Sømanden on 2026-10-19), Haroun al-Raschid, Camaralzaman, Ali Cogia, Den Smukke Perserinde, sekiner (sequins), engelske mil. After review, regexes unified the many spellings reviewers flagged (stor-vizir, store vizir, Storvizirn, Vizirn …).

## Choices a Danish reader may want to revisit

- “Three leagues” now reads “tre mil” like the other leagues (fixed 2026-10-19).
- Speeches continued across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end; a few reviewer replacements closed a paragraph early.
- Verse is meaning-first and mostly unrhymed.

## Address and naming scheme (2026-10-19 pass, for reading aloud to a child)

- **Everyone says *du* to one person** (*dig, din/dit/dine*) and ***I / jer / jeres*** only to several people. The formal *De / Dem / Deres* and the singular polite *I* are gone from the book (none left mid-sentence; sentence-initial *De* left only where it means “they”).
- **Kings, sultans, the caliph and princes are addressed as *Herre*** (capital H, vocative), with *du*. *Sire* is dropped entirely. *Deres Majestæt / Deres Højhed* as forms of address are dropped too: they would need *De*, so they became *Herre* + *du*. Third-person court references stay (*Hans Majestæt, Hans Højhed*).
- The caliph’s title is ***De Troendes Behersker*** throughout (was mostly *De Troendes Befalingsmand*, a calque).
- Queens and ladies: *frue* (lower case) with *du*; non-royal “sir” stays *herre / min herre* (lower case).
- Names: **Scheherazade** (not Sheherazade; 17 paragraphs, already consistent) and Dinarzade; **Aladdin**; **Sindbad Søfareren** (the established Danish name; all *Sindbad Sømanden* forms, the chapter titles and *›Sømanden‹* changed); **Haroun al-Raschid** in one spelling (the English mixes two); **kaliffen / kaliffens** (Retskrivningsordbogen; *kalifen* forms unified); *den Talende Fugl, det Syngende Træ, det Gyldne Vand* in one capitalisation.
- *Ali Baba og de fyrretyve røvere* is **not in Lang’s Arabian Nights** (it is in his Blue Fairy Book as “The Forty Thieves”), so it is not in this package.
