# Book VIII — drafting notes

61 paragraphs, numbering identical to source. Long's cross-references ((v. 16), (v. 1), (vii. 16), (xi. 12)) dropped; bracketed supplements absorbed.

## Glosses added
- **8.13** "physics (the study of nature)"; Long's "Dialectic" rendered "logic" (no gloss).
- **8.17** "the atoms, that is, chance" — absorbs Long's bracket "[chance]"; needed because "the atoms" alone is opaque to a newcomer.
- **8.56** "indifferent—neither good nor bad for it" — the standard's explained form of "indifferent".

## Source ambiguities (followed Long, logged)
- **8.3** "the ruling principles of these men were the same [or conformable to their pursuits]". Chose Long's bracketed alternative: "their ruling faculties were in keeping with what they pursued" ("the same" alone is unintelligible in English). Alternative: "and their ruling faculties were the same" (i.e. consistent).
- **8.4** "even though thou shouldst burst" → "even if you burst with anger". "with anger" added so the idiom reads; alternative: plain "even if you burst".
- **8.6** "the distribution of them still remains the same" → "the way things are shared out still remains the same". Alternative reading: the allotments are equal.
- **8.8** "leisure [or ability]" → "You are not free to read. But you are free to…" — "free to" carries both time and ability; the repetition is kept through the list.
- **8.17** "correct [that which is the cause]" → "correct the cause" (Long's supplement; the Greek may mean "correct him", the person).
- **8.37** Source text reads "Fergamus"; rendered **Pergamus** (Long's printed spelling, as in the baseline; "F" looks like a transcription error). "would that make them immortal?" kept with Long's ambiguous "them". "What then would those do after these were dead?" → "once these mourners were dead, what would the dead do then?" (those = the dead, these = mourners — Long's reading as I understand it).
- **8.45** "my divine part" rendered with the stable term "the divine spirit within me" (Greek δαίμων). "depressed, expanded, shrinking, affrighted" → "cast down, swelling up, shrinking back, frightened" ("expanded" followed literally; others render it "craving").
- **8.52** "[avoids or] seeks the praise" → "avoids or seeks" — the baseline dropped "avoids"; restored from Long's bracket.
- **8.55** Long's exact structure kept: wickedness harms "only the one person who has the power to be free of it as soon as he chooses".

## Other rendering choices worth checking
- **8.20** "a light" → "a lamp's flame".
- **8.23** "refer it to the gods" → "trace it back to the gods".
- **8.26** "movements of the senses" → "stirrings of the senses" (κινήσεις, not ὁρμή, so not "impulses"); "plausible appearances" → "impressions that seem convincing".
- **8.29** "Wipe out thy imaginations" → "Wipe out the impressions in your mind" (φαντασίαι = standard "impression").
- **8.41** "no other man is used to impede" → "no one else is in the habit of hindering them" (keeps Long's "is used to"; not strengthened to "can"). "abuse" → "insults".
- **8.49** "first appearances" → "what your impressions report at first sight" / "what appears at first sight" — avoids the modern idiom "first impressions" (judging a person quickly).
- **8.32, 8.40, 8.47** Long's dash-marked imagined objections rendered as quoted speech.
- "temperate" (8.1) and "temperance" (8.39) → "self-controlled / self-control"; "manly" → "courageous"; "repent" → "regret" (8.2, 8.10, 8.53).

## Baseline kept (nearly) unchanged
- **8.33, 8.42, 8.59, 8.61** — the baseline was already short, plain and faithful; only "ruling part" → "ruling faculty" and "men" → "people/human beings" where generic.

## Genuinely hard passages
- **8.7** end ("gives to everything… times, substance, cause, activity, and incident" and the whole-vs-whole comparison) — kept close to Long; broken into shorter sentences.
- **8.41** "When it has been made a sphere, it continues a sphere" — kept as Marcus's image without explanation.
- **8.57** the sun's-ray etymology (aktines / apo tou ekteinesthai) — kept Long's Greek words, with "extensions" and "in Greek" so it can be followed aloud; "effusion" vs "extension" rendered "poured away" vs "stretching out".
