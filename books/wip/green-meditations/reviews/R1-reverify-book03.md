## Per paragraph

### 3.0 — CLEAN
The sentence is restructured to "But other powers have already been snuffed out: …". "all this has already gone out" became "have already been snuffed out" (Long: "extinguished"). "whatever else of that kind" became "every other power of that kind". "make use of ourselves" became "make proper use of ourselves". All five members are kept and the logic is unchanged. "Proper" is a slight clarifying addition. It stays within the sense (the next item is "filling up the measure of our duty"), so I accept it. The change resolves the finding: the verb now comes before the list.

### 3.1 — CLEAN
"because they follow" became "because these side effects follow", and "adorn them" became "adorn those things". The pronoun blur is resolved. There is no change in meaning, and "side effects" matches the term used earlier in the same paragraph.

### 3.2 — CLEAN
Changes:
- "other lice" became "lice of another kind". This signals Long's figure without spelling it out.
- "If it is to another life, there is no lack of gods, not even there" became "If you are landing in another life, there is no lack of gods even there". This keeps Long's conditional and the voyage image.
- The comparison became "And the vessel is as far below the part of you that serves it as that servant is above it: for the servant is intelligence and the divine, and the vessel is earth and decay".

The comparison is as circular as Long's own "as much inferior as that which serves it is superior". It is faithful, and it now names both terms explicitly, which removes the blocking one/other mapping problem. The gloss "the body" and the name "Chaldaeans" are retained and recorded (the standard now covers the Chaldaeans).

### 3.3 — CLEAN
Changes:
- "check" became "hold back".
- The second half of the list became "It makes him a fighter …, one whom no passion can overpower, dyed deep with justice, welcoming …". This restores Long's point that the divine spirit is what makes the man all these things, so the fidelity finding is resolved.
- "hold on to the opinion" became "care about the opinion". This fits Long's sense of heeding whose opinion counts.

The "Only rarely …" clause remains a separate sentence. That is acceptable: the fidelity reviewer proposed it only as an option, and no meaning is lost.

### 3.4 — CLEAN

The glosses state only what an oath and testimony do, and they fit the soldier-at-his-post image in the same sentence. There is no added doctrine, and the finding is resolved.

### 3.6 — CLEAN

- "his own intelligence and divine spirit" became "his own intelligence, the divine spirit within him". The standard term is now used in full.

### 3.7 — CLEAN

### 3.8 — CLEAN
"Honor the faculty that forms opinions" became "Revere your power of forming opinions", with "this faculty" changed to "this power" twice. "Revere" restores Long's "Reverence" (the fidelity finding). "Power" separates this faculty from "your ruling faculty", which resolves the two-faculties confusion. "Ruling faculty" is unchanged and keeps its standard rendering. Rejecting the repeated gloss is correct under the standard (the gloss is at II.2).

### 3.10 — DEFECT
Most of the changes are good:
- "the names of the parts it was made from and will break back into" is clear.
- The second item is now coordinated again ("…in life, and always looking at things…"), which fixes the fidelity finding.
- "coincidences and chances of that sort" is fine; Long says "chance", so it is allowed by the standard.

The problem is the new sentence "See, of this thing that is now making an impression on me, what it is, what it is made of, how long it is in its nature to last, and what virtue I need in dealing with it: …".
- (a) The imperative "See" turns what Long gives as further objects of "to see" into a separate command. Long presents them as part of what lifts the mind. This is the same relation change the fidelity reviewer flagged in "It means seeing…", now in another form.
- (b) An imperative addressed to "you" followed at once by "on me" and "I need" jars on first hearing.
- (c) "See, of this thing …, what it is" is unnatural English when read aloud.
- (d) Long's "what each thing is, and of what it is composed" is general, and the change narrows it to "this thing".

Proposed replacement for that sentence:
"It lifts the mind, too, to see what each thing is, what it is made of, how long this thing that is now making an impression on me is in its nature to last, and what virtue I need in dealing with it: gentleness, manly courage, truthfulness, faithfulness, simplicity, contentment and the rest."

### 3.11 — CLEAN
The double "if" period is recast as two imperative sentences followed by "If you do, you will live happily." Long's conditional (sufficiency) is kept by "If you do", and every member is retained. "but keeping" became "and keeping", and "but content" became "content", with no loss of contrast that matters. The finding is resolved. The opening words change, but the section number does not.

### 3.12 — CLEAN
"with the bond … in mind" became "mindful of the bond …". Meaning is identical, and the finding is resolved.

### 3.14 — CLEAN
"how many things" became "how many different things", and "For this" became "For that last kind of seeing". Resolving Long's "this" to "seeing what ought to be done" matches the natural antecedent in Long (seeing / vision), so it is allowed by the standard's rule on resolving "this". "Different" slightly tilts "how many things are signified" toward multiple meanings, but it stays within Long's sense. I accept it.

### 3.15 — DEFECT
"made themselves into women" became "made themselves womanish". This is fine: it resolves the mishearing risk and keeps the period word, as at II.10.

The split sentence "It is also not to defile the divine spirit planted in his breast, …" does not work for a listener. Heard once, "It is also not to …" parses as a negation ("it is also not [the case]" / "this is not part of it"), which inverts the sense of the second half of what is special to the good person. The content is complete; only the phrasing fails the accessibility aim.

Proposed replacement for that sentence:
"It also means not defiling the divine spirit planted in his breast, or disturbing it with a crowd of images, but keeping it at peace, following it obediently as a god, saying nothing contrary to the truth and doing nothing contrary to justice."

## Verdict
Three defects. All are small, and none is a fidelity break in the source sense.
1. **3.5**: AMBIGUITIES.md entry for "[or, practically]" is missing, although the disposition says it was logged. Add the row given above.
2. **3.10**: the imperative "See, of this thing …" sentence recasts Long's coordinated objects of "to see" as a command, and it reads awkwardly. Replace it with the text given above.
3. **3.15**: "It is also not to defile …" is heard as a negation. Replace it with "It also means not defiling …, but keeping it at peace, …".

The other 11 changed paragraphs are VERIFIED CLEAN.
