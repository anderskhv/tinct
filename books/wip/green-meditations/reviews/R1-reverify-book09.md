# Re-verification — Book IX R1 changes
Coverage: every changed id: 9.0, 9.1, 9.6, 9.8, 9.9, 9.10, 9.13, 9.17, 9.23, 9.24, 9.25, 9.35, 9.36, 9.40, 9.41 (15). Each AFTER text was confirmed identical to `candidate.json`. I also checked each accessibility finding the editor rejected, including those in unchanged paragraphs (9.27, 9.28, 9.30, 9.34), for whether it leaves a first-time listener unable to follow the thought.

## Per paragraph

### 9.0 — CLEAN
Changes:
- "are related to" became "are bound up with". This fits Long's "have a relation to" (Greek: akin).
- "He had received from nature…" became "For nature had given him the powers…". This keeps Long's causal "for" and ties the sentence to the unintentional liar.
- "without regard to" became "contrary to what they deserve". This restores Long's opposition to desert and resolves the fidelity finding.
- "things … both of them" became "pairs of things … both things in each pair". The pairs are Long's own list in the next sentence, so nothing is added.
- "set out … on this present ordering" became "moved … to the present ordering". This is Long's "moved from a certain beginning to this ordering".
- "It conceived" became "Providence conceived", which resolves the ambiguous "It".

No omission and no terminology drift ("impulse" and "equally disposed" are kept). The section number and opening are unchanged. The rejected parts of accessibility finding (4) are the gloss on providence (already given at II.3), "without distinction" and "new beings". The doubled "things that come into being … things that come after them" is Long's and is logged. None of this blocks the thought: after the fix, a listener can follow the core claim that these pairs happen alike to everything because of providence's first impulse. The two consecutive sentences opening with "For" mirror Long's "for … for" and are acceptable.

### 9.1 — CLEAN

The referents now match Long's pairing: the air harms animals as animals, and the mind's corruption harms humans as humans. This resolves both the fidelity finding and the BLOCKING accessibility finding. The accessibility finding on "next best voyage" was rejected. The proverb is Long's, it is flagged "as the saying goes", and the sentence spells out what the "next best" option is, so a listener follows it. The number and opening are unchanged.
