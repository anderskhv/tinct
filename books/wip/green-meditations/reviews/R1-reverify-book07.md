## Rejected accessibility findings: listener check

- **7.3 (BLOCKING, rejected): upheld, no defect.** Heard aloud, "the one" is conversation and "the other" is impulse. That gives: in conversation, see what end the words are aimed at; in impulse, watch what is meant. That is a coherent instruction, and it is the pairing Long's own order implies. The listener is not left without a thought. The pairing in the Greek is logged in AMBIGUITIES.
- **7.23 (BLOCKING, rejected): DEFECT (English-level, faithful fix available).** The editor rightly refused the reviewer's analogy. But in the current text, "For if even the awareness of doing wrong is lost…" gives the ear nothing to hang "even" on: "is lost" does not connect with the beauty that "dies out" and is "put out" in the sentence before, so the "For" sounds like a non sequitur. Long's "even" (καί, "also") is the only link, and it can be made audible without adding any premise:
  - Current: "For if even the awareness of doing wrong is lost, what reason is left for living any longer?"
  - Proposed: "For if the awareness of doing wrong dies out too, what reason is left for living any longer?"
  - "too" renders Long's "even", and "dies out" echoes the text's own verb for the beauty. The obscure step itself stays unstated and logged.
- **7.4: upheld.** The gloss is at II.2, where the candidate has "the ruling faculty—the reasoning mind that governs the rest of me". The either/or is intact, and the attachment of the helper clause is Long's own and logged. The sentence can be followed.
- **7.8: upheld.** "if indeed" is Long's conditional, and changing it to "since" would break the rule to keep conditionals.
- **7.9: upheld.** "form and cause", set against "Everything material", can be followed, and the difficulty is real philosophy.
- **7.28 (cause/matter part): upheld.**
- **7.50 (subject/attribution part):** the refusal was right. The remaining audibility problem is reported above under 7.50.
- **7.57: upheld.** The break is in Long's text, and "And remember…" ends the section. No thought is left unfollowable.
- **7.66: upheld.** "For" is Long's word, and the ground it gives is Marcus's.
- **7.67: upheld.** "the limbs of this kneaded lump of matter that has grown around you" identifies the body.
- **7.74 ("or else", "at all"): upheld.** They would tilt Long's either/or.
- **"ruling faculty" cross-cutting note: upheld.** It is glossed at II.2.

## Verdict
Two defects:
1. **7.50:** quotation marks do not resolve the BLOCKING finding for a listener. Replace the first line with `"With food and drink and cunning magic the stream's course is turned aside, to escape from death."`
2. **7.23 (rejected BLOCKING):** "even … is lost" leaves the link unhearable. Use "For if the awareness of doing wrong dies out too, what reason is left for living any longer?"

The other 28 changed paragraphs are CLEAN.
