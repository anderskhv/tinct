## Findings

No blocking findings. Six minor ones:

### 12.0 — MINOR (voice: generic "man")
Current: "then you will be a man worthy of the universe that brought you into being."
Problem: The Greek is ἄνθρωπος, a human being in general, not Marcus as a male. The same word is rendered "a human being" at 11.17 ("begin at last to be a human being while you are still alive", logged in AMBIGUITIES 11.17), and the standard keeps "man" only for Marcus as a man or for a male role. The two closing exhortations of Books XI and XII should match.
Replacement: "then you will be a human being worthy of the universe that brought you into being."

### 9.22 — MINOR (voice: generic "man")
Current: "It is a kind of mutiny—like a man in a public assembly who goes his own way and stands apart from the general agreement."
Problem: This is generic ("one who separates himself"). Everywhere else in VII–XII the standard's "person/someone" rule is applied to the same construction.
Replacement: "It is a kind of mutiny—like someone in a public assembly who goes his own way and stands apart from the general agreement."

### 7.74 — MINOR (terminology drift: ἡγεμονικόν of the universe)
Current: "toward which the ruling power of the universe directs its own impulse"
Problem: This is Marcus's τὸ τοῦ κόσμου ἡγεμονικόν. At 9.21 the same idea is rendered "the ruling faculty of the universe", and the standard says ἡγεμονικόν is always "the ruling faculty". "Ruling power" is the only variant left in VII–XII. The AMBIGUITIES 7.74 row concerns the either/or crux, not this term.
Replacement: "toward which the ruling faculty of the universe directs its own impulse"

### 10.25 — MINOR (terminology drift: ὁρμή)
Current: "then another cause takes it over and makes perception and movement, and in short, life and strength and other things besides."
Problem: Long's "perception and motion" renders αἴσθησιν καὶ ὁρμήν. The standard maps ὁρμή to "impulse", not "movement", and that mapping is already applied where Long says "movement" for ὁρμή: 7.3, 8.6, 8.27, 9.20, 9.30, 11.19, 11.36 and 12.30 (12.30 is logged). Here it was missed. Confirm the Greek reading before editing; if it is κίνησιν, leave the text as it is.
Replacement: "then another cause takes it over and makes perception and impulse, and in short, life and strength and other things besides."

### 10.7 — MINOR (residual opacity)
Current: "or else leave life at once, not in passion, but simply, freely and modestly,"
Problem: Heard aloud, "simply" in this position sounds like "merely/just" ("just leave, freely..."). Long's "with simplicity and freedom and modesty" names the virtue (ἁπλῶς), and "simplicity" is the stable rendering (7.30, 8.50, 10.8, 12.26).
Replacement: "or else leave life at once, not in passion, but with simplicity, freedom and modesty,"

### 8.55 — MINOR (repeated gloss)
Current: "To my own free will, my neighbor's free will is just as indifferent—neither good nor bad for it—as his poor breath and flesh are."
Problem: The standard says "indifferent" appears once, explained, at III.11 (3.10), with "neither good nor bad" as the working rendering after that. The full "indifferent—neither good nor bad" gloss has already been repeated at VI.32 (6.31, outside this packet), and 8.55 repeats it a third time. The per-book review accepted it as "the prescribed gloss" because it could not see the earlier uses. 11.15 correctly uses the bare term. Since the gloss is the stable rendering itself, dropping the word "indifferent" loses nothing.
Replacement: "To my own free will, my neighbor's free will is neither good nor bad—no more than his poor breath and flesh are."

- **12.35, "this great city, the world" and 10.12, "a good divine spirit within, which is to say, happiness":** These are Long's own brackets, absorbed and logged (AMBIGUITIES 12.35, 10.12). Not reopened.
- **7.74, "the nature of the whole" (Long "the nature of the All"):** Kept to avoid "the nature of the universe set out to make the universe". It is recorded in the AMBIGUITIES 7.74 row and read as deliberate.

- **12.13, "an intelligence that governs" (ἡγεμονικόν τινα νοῦν):** An adjectival use inside Marcus's image, faithful and clear, so it is left as is.
