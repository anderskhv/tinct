Proposed replacement: "17. Constantly keep in view the whole of time and the whole of substance, and consider that every individual thing, measured in substance, is a fig seed, and measured in time, a single turn of a hand drill."

## Rejected accessibility findings: can a first-time listener still follow the thought?

Most rejections are sound. Some terms were already glossed in earlier books (ruling faculty II.2, divine spirit within II.13). Some rejections protect Long's imagery or his fragments (10.7 "stay in them", 10.20 "the solemn sky loves", 10.22 the Plato fragment, 10.27 "bonds", 10.37 "persuasion"). Others protect the source's own openness (10.5 atoms/nature, 10.21 "going away", 10.30 the name pairs). In 10.12 the missing link is Long's own, and each sentence is followable. In none of these cases is the thought lost. One rejection does leave the listener unable to follow the thought:

### 10.9 (rejected ACC: "Sarmatians") — DEFECT
The whole point of 10.9 is that the last "hunter" hunts human beings, since Marcus was himself at war with the Sarmatians. That is why the closing "are they not all robbers?" lands. A first-time listener who does not know that Sarmatians are a people hears one more kind of prey after "wild boars, another bears", and the barb is lost. The guide allows "Brief glosses only where a reader cannot follow the thought without them". The name should stay as Long has it, but the listener needs to hear that these are men. That fact is carried by the ethnonym itself, not by historical interpretation, so saying it is not "Sarmatian warriors", which the editor rightly rejected.
Proposed replacement: "10. A spider is proud when it has caught a fly; one man is proud when he has caught a poor hare, another when he has caught a little fish in a net, another when he has caught wild boars, another bears, another men—Sarmatians. If you examine their judgments, are they not all robbers?"

## Verdict
Two defects:
1. **10.16** (changed paragraph): "matter" breaks the rendering of "the whole of substance" used at V.24 and XII.32 and set by the guide. Restore "substance" and keep the "measured in" structure (replacement text above).
2. **10.9** (rejected accessibility finding): the listener cannot tell that "Sarmatians" are human prey, and the point of the section depends on it. Add "men—" before the name (replacement text above).
