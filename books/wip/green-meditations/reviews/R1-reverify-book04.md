### 4.28 — DEFECT
The "Whoever … is X" reordering is faithful, and each member is intact. However, it introduces a new referent problem. In "Whoever withdraws … because he is displeased with what happens, is an abscess on the universe—for the same nature that brings this about brought you about too", the predicate "is an abscess on the universe" now sits between "what happens" and "this". A first-time listener will attach "this" to the abscess or the withdrawal, not to what happens. That makes nature the author of the abscess and loses Long's point: the same nature produces the events you resent and produced you. Long's antecedent is clear ("the things which happen"), so the standard says to resolve it.
Proposed replacement for that sentence: "Whoever withdraws and cuts himself off from the reason of our common nature, because he is displeased with what happens, is an abscess on the universe—for the same nature that brings about what happens brought you about too."

### 4.48 — DEFECT
The first change is good. "call what happened a misfortune, rather than call your remaining free from pain a good fortune" gives Long's antecedents for "that" and "this", and "vexation" = Long. But "Do you, in general, call …" does not resolve the fidelity finding it answers. In modern English "in general" means "usually, as a rule", which admits exceptions. Long's "in all cases" (ὅλως) is universal, and the finding was that this universal force had been lost. As written, the question asks about a habit rather than any case at all.
Proposed replacement for that sentence: "Do you, in any case at all, call something a person's misfortune when it is not a departure from human nature?" (If a closer match to Long's words is preferred: "Do you, in all cases, call something a person's misfortune when it is not a departure from human nature?")

### 4.49 — CLEAN
"think how laboriously that span is lived through: with how much trouble, in the company of what sort of people, and in how feeble a body" keeps Long's "laboriously passed" and all three members. The subject now comes first.

### 4.50 — CLEAN
"run by the short road" and "trouble and warfare" restore Long's τρέχε and στρατεία.

## Verdict
Two defects. Everything else is VERIFIED CLEAN.

1. **4.28**: "brings this about" now follows "is an abscess on the universe", which misattaches "this". Fix: "for the same nature that brings about what happens brought you about too."
2. **4.48**: "in general" weakens Long's universal "in all cases" (the fidelity finding is not resolved). Fix: "Do you, in any case at all, call something a person's misfortune when it is not a departure from human nature?"

None of the other rejected accessibility findings (4.0 ruling faculty, 4.2 providence/alternative/"as a man", 4.13 "a part", 4.20 "from eternity", 4.21 impression, 4.22 "of the universe", 4.31 proportion, 4.32 "same kind") leaves a first-time listener unable to follow the thought. Each is covered by an earlier gloss, by the surrounding sentence, or by Long's own openness.
