“You hurt my heart when you speak like this. I have made up my mind to cut down the Cedar and win a reputation that will last for ever. Now, my friend, I want to give the craftsmen my instructions, so that we can watch them cast our weapons.”
They gave their orders to the craftsmen. The workers prepared the mould (?) and cast enormous axes, celts weighing three talents apiece. They also cast huge swords, each with a hilt weighing two talents. The matching blades weighed thirty manas each. The inlay (?) was gold, thirty manas to a sword. Thus Gilgamish and Enkidu each carried a load of ten talents.
At Erech’s Seven Bolt Gate, the craftsmen heard the news (?) and came together. The people (?) gathered in the streets of broad-marketed Erech, in honour (?) of Gilgamish. The Elders of the city with its broad markets sat before him. Gilgamish addressed them: “Listen to me, Elders of broad-marketed Erech! I am setting out against fierce Humbaba. The Elders of broad-marketed Erech answered Gilgamish: “You are young, Gilgamish, and your bravery raises your confidence too high. You do not fully understand what you are trying to undertake.
