## Edition and extraction

William Muss-Arnolt, “The Gilgamesh Narrative,” in R. F. Harper (ed.), *Assyrian and Babylonian Literature*, New York: D. Appleton and Company, 1901, pp. 324–369. Archive record: https://archive.org/details/assyrianbabylon00harp . Downloaded OCR and full scan URLs are in `source/download-manifest.json`; source files are `assyrianbabylon00harp_djvu.txt` and `assyrianbabylon00harp.pdf`.

The printed selection is itself a scholarly presentation of translated tablet fragments interspersed with editorial summaries and notes. The editions retain the printed translated fragments, including the translator's lacuna brackets and asterisks; editorial narration, footnotes, page references, and colophons are excluded. Tablet VII is represented by its catchword line, which the book identifies as the opening of Tablet VII at the end of Tablet VI. Tablets IV, VII and VIII have sparse surviving translated text in this selection. No material from the unrelated, tentatively assigned alternate recension at the end of p. 367–368 was included. Muss-Arnolt was used as the sole base; no other translator was used to fill gaps.

The printed Tablet I opening on p. 325 was checked against PDF page 441. The final main-recension verses on pp. 366–367 were checked against PDF pages 482–483. The scan-verified passage on printed p. 330 was checked against PDF page 446. Other source passages were extracted from the Archive OCR and proofread against the printed text where inspected. Line breaks were normalized; source wording, spelling, punctuation and lacuna marks are retained. OCR artifacts in the opening were corrected by scan comparison (“He bringeth”).
