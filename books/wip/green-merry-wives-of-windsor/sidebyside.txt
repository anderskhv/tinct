SRC: _Mrs Page._ Why, did you take her in green?
CAN: MISTRESS PAGE. Why, did you take her in green?
--[23.72]--
SRC: _Caius._ Ay, by gar, and ’tis a boy: by gar, I’ll raise all Windsor.    [_Exit._
CAN: CAIUS. Yes, by God, and it is a boy: by God, I'll rouse all Windsor. [Exit.]

CAN: FENTON. You are amazing her: hear the truth of it. You would have married her most shamefully, where there was no proportion held in love. The truth is, she and I, long since contracted, are now so sure that nothing can dissolve us. The offence is holy that she has committed; and this deceit loses the name of cunning, of disobedience, or undutiful title; since by it she avoids and shuns a thousand irreligious cursed hours, which forced marriage would have brought upon her.
--[23.81]--
SRC: _Ford._ Stand not amazed; here is no remedy: In love the heavens themselves do guide the state; Money buys lands, and wives are sold by fate.                      220
CAN: FORD. Don't stand amazed; there is no remedy: in love the heavens themselves do guide the situation; money buys lands, and wives are sold by fate.
