EDITORIAL BRIEF
Vocabulary accessible to a reader around11, without shortening or sanitising the plot. Retain every source paragraph, sentence sequence, clause relationships, dialogue turn, concrete detail, humorous repetition and intentional confusion. Use natural target-language word order where required. Preserve long sentences instead of dividing them merely to improve a readability score. Preserve grim events unchanged. Danish uses natural du forms; formal titles remain where they matter to a joke. Short optional end notes explain old money, religious references and the director’s deliberate word muddles. These notes are outside the source paragraph arrays.

Names: Master Cherry / mester Kirsebær; Talking Cricket / den talende fårekylling; Fire-Eater / Ildslugeren; Fox / Ræven; Cat / Katten; Fairy / Feen; Candlewick / Lysvægen; Land of Toys / Legetøjslandet; Shark / Hajen; Raven / Ravn. Pinocchio, Geppetto, Antonio, Romeo, Medoro, Alidoro and Melampo retained. The source’s own continuity quirks and Pinocchio’s rambling, inaccurate recaps remain; these are not silently corrected to match other chapters.

AUTHORING AND REVIEW
Chapter1 developed as the pilot and retained with review. Chapters2–12 authored by the primary agent;13–22,23–29 and30–36 authored in separate non-overlapping batches by three user-authorised agents. Every translated paragraph and chapter title received a source comparison by an agent other than its author:
1–12: reviewer chapters_13_22.
13–22: reviewer chapters_23_29.
23–29: reviewer chapters_30_36.
30–33: reviewer chapters_23_29.
34–36: reviewer chapters_13_22.
These were AI editorial reviews, not a claim of professional human copy-editing or reader testing with children.

Review repairs included archaic or difficult vocabulary, Danish grammatical cases and natural forms of address, Harlequin’s ambiguous pardon pronoun, idiomatic cat-blaming threat, exact little-toe and fish-species details, proper names, alcohol references, and the Pentecost allusion at the ending. Deliberately nonsensical circus-director speech retained as comedy and marked in a brief end note. Cross-language short-paragraph ratio flags were inspected for omitted meaning; none remain unresolved. Textual similarity to an English baseline was not used: these editions were explicitly commissioned directly from Italian, so such a gate would not validate fidelity. A separately cleared human English Compare edition remains an integration consideration under Tinct’s general book workflow.
