# The Story of the Iliad (Church) — “Iliad for Boys and Girls” — Danish edition notes

- **Greek and Trojan names stay in the English spelling of the source** (Apollo, Helen, Priam, Ulysses, Ithaca, Crete, Hector, Ajax, Aphrodite, Hephæstus). A consistency pass replaced early Danified forms. Troja and Grækenland are Danish.
- Pronunciation notes (e.g. `[1] A-the’-ne.`) are copied exactly, with a plain straight apostrophe.
- Paragraphs that end in the speech-introducing `:—` end in a plain `:` in Danish.
- Mixed: “Ajax den Store / Lille / Mindre” against “Ajax the Less” — not unified.

## General

- Distances: English miles are “engelske mil”; feet, yards and inches are kept.
- Coins and units with no Danish equivalent (groat, farthing, shilling, pottle) are kept.
- Speeches that run across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end.
