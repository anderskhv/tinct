# Danish batch 2 — translation notes

Glossary and conventions used by translators and reviewers (applied to all ten books):

- Quotation: »…« for speech, ›…‹ for quotes inside speech; verse and epigraph line breaks kept; spaced em dash ` — ` (interrupted speech keeps the unspaced dash of the English source).
- Titles: Mr., Mrs., Miss, Sir, Lady, Lord stay English. *Colonel* → *oberst*, *Doctor* → *doktor*, *Master* (a boy) → *unge herre*, *Vicar/curate* → *præst/kapellan*.
- Units: *yards*, *pounds/shillings* kept; English miles → *engelske mil*.
- Names and English place names kept as in the English text (Barton Cottage → *cottagen*, Thrushcross Grange, Wuthering Heights → *Heights*).
- Address: *du* in families and to servants; *De* between strangers and to social superiors; one form per speaker pair.
- Racial term (Huckleberry Finn only): *sort / de sorte* (decision 22 Oct 2026). None of these ten books uses it; the scripted check confirmed zero *neger/negre* in the new files.
- Meditations: fixed terms *det styrende sind*, *fornuft*, *naturen*, *forsynet*, *guderne*; section numbers kept.
- Plays (Medea, Bacchae): speaker labels kept in capitals; chorus *KORET*.

Review findings that recurred, in order of frequency: wrong grammatical gender (*et/en*), calques from English (*det kommer an på*, *tage et blik*), false friends (*bål* for hearth fire, *frøken/fru* for Miss/Mrs., *hytte*), dropped or added hedges (*certainly* → *sikkert*), inconsistent du/De inside one scene, and non-words.
