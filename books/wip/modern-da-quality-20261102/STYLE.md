# Draft modern-da editorial standard

Natural contemporary Danish with fidelity and literary voice. This is a calibration draft, not a claim that every previous sentence is defective.

- Preserve each source claim, argument, image, joke, action, quotation and deliberate ambiguity. No summaries, invented clarification, sanitizing or dropped historical content.
- Edit whole sentences for idiom. Do not use global word replacement as translation.
- Family fiction should read naturally aloud. Preserve fairy-tale cadence and “Der var engang”; avoid unnecessary vocabulary barriers. Flag hys, græmme, thi, ej, hine, skønt and similar terms in context, not as absolute bans. Clear literary vocabulary may be retained deliberately.
- Grimm: Hans/Grete and existing character identities are retained for this pilot. Hush becomes shh when a child quiets another child, but rolig when the speaker reassures an impatient child. Preserve the violence and religious content of the source.
- Adult philosophy retains necessary precision; modern syntax must not replace concepts with vague everyday approximations. The Manual pilot proposes “i vores magt” consistently and requires philosophical review.
- Plays should be speakable. Preserve speaker tags and stage directions. Record address per relationship. Hamlet pilot proposes De when Polonius addresses the Queen; a full-book policy must resolve that consistently.
- Preserve narrative and character voices, including meaningful social differences. Do not flatten everyone into the same casual voice.
- Use hr., fru, frøken in Danish narrative/dialogue where appropriate, reflecting the recorded user preference in open PR #568. Do not merge that policy PR or adopt every unreviewed assertion in it. Preserve exact source quantities; review mile/league terminology with source notes rather than silently converting units.
- »…« outer quotes, ›…‹ inner quotes; consistent punctuation. Preserve emphasis, line breaks and footnote meanings. No unconditional same-count rule can replace a semantic check.
- Work directly from Danish originals for Niels Lyhne and Fear and Trembling. For other works record the intermediary translation; source-language review is needed for ambiguous/high-stakes literary passages.

## Final quality acceptance

Independent full-source comparison, native full-Danish read, independently rechecked corrections, no unresolved major errors, valid structure/mappings, clear source provenance and final exact-text hashes. A pilot is accepted only after a reviewer has evaluated actual passages; passing a mechanical scan is not acceptance.
