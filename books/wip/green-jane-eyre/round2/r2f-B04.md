## Findings

## Considered and rejected
- 37.5: "issue from the grange" became "emerge". Acceptable compression.
- 37.15: "where I had dismissed the chaise" became "left the chaise". The meaning survives, and 37.1 already says the chaise was dismissed.
- 37.46: the fragment "And kissed her, as thus—" is readable in context.
- 37.85: "no holding back my liveliness for his sake" is a close enough gloss of "no repressing of glee and vivacity with him".
- 37.89: "John's wife" became "Mary's voice". Same person.
- 37.207: "I caressed" became "I stroked his face", and "manly cheek" became "cheek". Minor, not worth an edit.
- 37.245: "bronze scrag" became "neck". "Bronze" is dropped, which is minor.
- 38.3, 38.4, 38.6: dialect is normalised. "noan faâl" is correctly rendered "not ugly".
- 38.5: "that sanctum" is dropped. Minor.
- 38.21: "hews down like a giant" became "like a giant felling trees". Acceptable image.
- Dropped italic emphasis (37.32, 37.36, 37.40, 37.220–221, etc.) follows the edition-wide convention.
