## Considered and rejected
- 15.4 "(overlook the barbarism)" is omitted. The apology only makes sense with the French verb _croquant_, and the modern text rightly renders that as "crunching". Legitimate modernization.
- 15.8 "momentarily" and "dilating" are dropped. Very minor, and the image survives.
- 15.25 "harsh to inferiority of every description" becomes "harsh toward anything he deemed inferior". Acceptable.
- 15.38 "you are quenched now" becomes "soaked", so the fire pun is softened. Any fix would be awkward.
- 15.39 "elves" becomes "spirits", and 15.69 "good genii" becomes "guardian spirits". Acceptable.
- 15.61 "very inconsistently so" becomes "very oddly so, since…". The since-clause keeps the logic.
- 16.17 "malignant pranks" becomes "dangerous tricks", and 16.37 "hideous attempt" becomes "murderous attempt". Tone is close enough.
- 16.70 "self-interest" becomes "self-preservation", and the italic _You_ emphasis is lost. The repetition keeps the force.
- 16.72 "oriental eye" becomes "dark eastern eyes". This is a slight gloss, and Mrs. Fairfax's description (black eyes) supports it.
