- 6.2 "my acquaintance of the verandah" → "from the garden": ch. 5 modern calls the verandah a "covered walkway" in the garden, so the reference still points to the right place.
- 6.33 "Like Felix in the Bible": a helpful gloss, not an invention.
- 6.11 "ruddy gloaming" → "warm glow": acceptable modernization.
- 7.9 "this piece of architecture" → "this pillar of a man": keeps the image of a column.
- 7.33 "false front of French curls" → "wig of French curls on her forehead": an accurate gloss.
- 7.34 "rummaging scrutiny" → "inspecting": slightly flat, but the meaning is kept.
- 8.2 "silent as an Indian" → "silent as a statue": a deliberate replacement of a dated ethnic simile. The meaning (motionless silence) is kept. Flagged for the editor's awareness, not proposed.
- 8.0 "not molested by any" → "left alone by everyone else": close enough.
- 8.50 "of state in her mien" folded into "calm dignity": covered.
- 8.52 "my organ of veneration expanding" (phrenology) → "my sense of awe grew": acceptable.
- 8.59 "Barmecide supper" → "imaginary feast": the gloss keeps the sense.
- Minor dropped phrases judged negligible: 6.4 "praise her attention"; 7.12 "from time to time"; 8.1 "started up" → "looked up".
