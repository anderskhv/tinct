## Considered and rejected
- 28.8: "riven chords" is rendered as "severed bonds", and "pinions" as "feathers". The sense is kept, so these are acceptable modernizations.
- 28.60: "_ignis fatuus_" is rendered as "will-o'-the-wisp". This is a correct gloss.
- 28.65: the cat is now "lay a curled black cat", where the source says it "was cushioned". This is a trivial rendering.
- 28.87 and 29.65: Hannah's dialect is normalized, and all of the content is present, including the Henrys, the journeyman needlemaker, the bankruptcy and "threaped".
- 28.108: "This was the climax" is rendered as "last straw". This is acceptable.
- 28.116: "pass before me into the house" is rendered as "come inside". This is a minor loss that doesn't affect the meaning.
- 29.12: the source's "and turn" (in bed) is dropped. This is negligible.
- 29.20/29.21: Jane's gloss "(by which I suppose you mean money)" is dropped, and so is the quoted 'brass' in 29.60. Because 29.20 already turns Hannah's "brass" into "money", the gloss would be redundant. Restoring it would mean putting the dialect word back in 29.20, so I left it.
- 29.33: "Happen ye've been…" is rendered as "I suppose you've been…". This is acceptable.
- 29.104, 29.118, 29.120, 29.121, 29.122: the source italics (_can_, _charity_, _shall_, _I_) are dropped. The modern edition almost never marks emphasis (only about 8 italic spans in the whole edition), so this follows the edition's convention. In 29.118 the distinction behind _charity_ is still spelled out in words.
- 29.105: "as a giant with wine" is kept as "a giant by wine". The allusion to Psalm 78 is still recognizable.
