# Round 1 — B02 (Chapter 34)

**Coverage:** Chapter 34: paragraphs 34.0–34.160 all compared (every SOURCE/MODERN pair read in full).

**Proposals:** 1 total — clarity/recommended: 1 (34.34). No blocking issues.

The chapter is very close to the source, as the batch notes said. It is still modern and readable. The dialogue-heavy stretches were checked line by line. Every qualification and argument in the proposal and refusal (34.100–34.160) is intact. That includes: "not personal, but mental endowments"; "formed for labour, not for love"; "must — shall be"; "not for my pleasure, but for my Sovereign's service"; the Demas and St. Paul allusions; the Rock of Ages; the "half an oblation"/"mutilated sacrifice" argument; "if I may go free"; the age argument (not yet thirty / nineteen); the neophyte/hierophant line; "I scorn your idea of love"; and "worse than infidels". Jane's meditations are also complete: "if life be spared me", "heart, vitals, the entire victim", and "vital after vital". Curly quotes are used throughout.

## Considered and rejected
- 34.13 "beau-ideal of a welcome" → "perfect welcome": acceptable modernization. The meaning is kept.
- 34.25 "sufficed to scare him to estrangement" → "enough to drive him to stay away": the sense is kept.
- 34.36 "moor and moss" → "moor and bog": correct gloss (northern "moss" means bog).
- 34.40 / 34.76 "dissipation": kept from the source. The context ("merry domestic") makes the sense of amusement or diversion clear enough.
- 34.52 "Would not such another ruin you?" → "another such victory": a correct reading of the Pyrrhic allusion.
- 34.54 "fagged away" → "toiled away": fine.
- 34.107 "chiefest of sinners" → "chief of sinners": the allusion (1 Tim. 1:15) is still recognizable.
- 34.111 "proved you… by sundry tests… elicited" → "tested… by various trials… drawn out": fine.
- 34.125 "coadjutor", "put your hand to the plough": left as they are. This is St. John's deliberate register and scriptural allusion.
- 34.142 "neophyte… hierophant": deliberately elevated diction. Left as it is.
- 34.1: the clause order was restructured into two sentences, but the event sequence is unchanged.

## Source-text notes (recorded, not fixed)
- 34.114 "He threw himself down on a swell of heath, and there lay still" is an illustration caption from the Gutenberg edition. It duplicates the end of 34.113. MODERN mirrors it, so alignment is kept.
- 34.134 SOURCE reads "you will serve your heart to God". This is probably a transcription slip for "give" (it echoes Jane's "I will give my heart to God" in 34.131). MODERN correctly uses "give".
