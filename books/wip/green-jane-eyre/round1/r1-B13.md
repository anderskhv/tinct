## Considered and rejected
- 18.13 "deal table" → "plain table", "horn lantern" → "lantern": legitimate modernization.
- 18.31 "one day she might look into the abyss" → "would be free to look": the certainty shifts slightly but the sense is kept.
- 18.52 "old Mother Bunches" → "old fortune-tellers": an acceptable gloss.
- 18.58 "as black as a crock": left as the servant's idiom. It is archaic but readable in context.
- 18.84 "thought it 'le cas'" → "saw fit": the French tag is dropped but the meaning is kept. It is Lady Ingram's affectation, not a French passage from Adèle, Rochester or Céline. Could be restored in a later polish round.
- 18.62 "inconsistent proceeding" → "absurd proceeding": close enough in register.
- 19.22 and 18.27 lose the source italics on "_your_" and "_this_": minor emphasis. Only 18.26 is proposed, because there the italic line is the paragraph's climax.
- 19.68 "beheld his bride happy" → "imagined his bride happy": "looking forward" already frames it as anticipation.
- 19.126 "ministrant spirit" → "guardian spirit": an acceptable modern gloss.
- 19.140/141 "dare censure" → "bear criticism": the sense is kept.
- 19.112 "speaking automaton to enounce its single words" → "speaking machine … its words": acceptable.

## Source-text problems (recorded, not fixed)
