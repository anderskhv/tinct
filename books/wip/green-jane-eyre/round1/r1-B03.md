## Paragraphs specifically asked about
- **36.48 (fire account): passes.** The sequence is correct: the hangings of the room next to her own go first, then she goes down to a lower floor and into the governess's former chamber, kindles the bed, and nobody is in it. The aftermath is complete: the governess ran away two months before, Rochester searched for her and heard nothing, he grew savage and dangerous and wanted to be alone, Mrs. Fairfax was sent to friends with a life annuity, Adèle was put to school, and he broke with the gentry and lived as a hermit. Only trivia are lost ("gin and water" became "gin"). Nothing from 36.47 or 36.49/50 has been pulled in.
- **36.52** (the neighbour paragraph) has real damage: the sequence is reversed (he calls her before climbing), "Bertha!" and the several witnesses are dropped, and an invented line "We shouted for him to come down" is added. A fix is proposed.
- **35.80, 35.83, 35.87, 36.46, 36.50:** 35.83 is fine. The others have small issues only (see proposals).

## Source-text notes
- **36.53 duplicates the end of 36.52** ("The next minute she lay smashed on the pavement", with no final period). This looks like a Gutenberg artifact similar to 38.16/38.17. The modern edition handles it by ending 36.52 on the leap and giving the smash to 36.53. My 36.52 fix keeps that split and does not duplicate the sentence.
