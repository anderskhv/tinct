French handling: in both chapters the edition keeps Adèle's and Sophie's French as it appears in the source, with no gloss. Adèle's long speeches that the source reports as "in French" are already given in English, as the source does. There are two kinds of fix:
- restore missing accents (11.61 "là", 11.123 "êtes", and "Frédéric" in 11.81);
- restore the French in 12.7. There the candidate had swapped Adèle's "Revenez bientôt…" for an English translation, which does not match how the edition handles French everywhere else, chapters 13–14 included. The meaning is clear from context: a goodbye answered with a kiss.

The earlier r1-adele-accent.json pass already covered the "Adele"→"Adèle" fixes. Those paragraphs already read "Adèle" in the candidate text I reviewed.

## Considered and rejected
- 11.2 "prevented by many impediments from returning" → "unable to go back": the core meaning is kept, so this is acceptable.
- 11.15 "judging from…" is moved outside the quotation marks, and "I remember" is dropped: the thought is the same, and the change is minor.
- 11.58 "He intended to have her brought up" → "He wants her raised": the sense is close enough.
- 11.64 "I dare say" → "I'm sure": this is ordinary modernization.
- 11.79 and 11.62: the French is left unglossed. That is the source's approach and the edition's too.
- 11.87 "Tyrian-dyed", "boudoir" and "Parian" are glossed as purple, sitting room and marble: acceptable clarifications.
- 11.95: the italic emphasis on "_I_" is lost, but the sense survives.
- 12.1 "conscientious solicitude" → "genuine concern": this is borderline but acceptable.
- 12.13 "I obeyed him" → "obeyed his silent appeal": a slight expansion, but it is implied by the source.
- 12.54 "no interest in a sense" → "nothing special, really": the hedge is roughly kept.
- 12.56: the moon sentence is a fragment and drops "from behind which she had come". It is readable, and the detail is minor.
- 12.57: the "not lit, only by the bronze lamp" construction is paraphrased acceptably.
- 12.58 "snuffed me" → "sniffed my hand": a trivial specification.
