The main blocking items:
- **14.78 and 14.80:** The source joke is "paving hell with energy" in 14.78. Jane asks "Sir?", and the answer comes in 14.80: "laying down good intentions". The modern text gives the answer away in 14.78, and 14.80 loses the word "good".
- **14.91:** Jane's quoted words "Let it be right" were paraphrased away, but in 14.92 Rochester says "the very words".
- **14.56:** "Most things free-born will submit to anything for a salary" became "people with self-respect will endure anything", which contradicts itself. This goes with the recommended fix at 14.55.
- **14.58:** The modern text adds a clause the source does not have ("when I have so much to answer for myself"). It also loses the self-correction "I started, or rather … was thrust", and drops "half" from "half the blame".

## Considered and rejected
- **13.12:** "Its curtain now drawn" for "dropped". Slightly ambiguous, but it can mean closed. Acceptable.
- **13.39:** "Rather the look of another world" lost "rather". Minor, and the tone is kept.
- **13.84, 13.25, 13.130, 14.49, 14.64, 14.98:** The source italics for emphasis are dropped. These chapters drop italics throughout, and the wording still carries the sense.
- **13.104:** "Surveyed them alternately" became "studied them again". A negligible loss.
- **13.133:** "Combined" became "conspired to manoeuvre". Slightly stronger, but within the sense of "steps … not quite fair".
- **14.1:** "Causes quite disconnected with me" became "unconnected with anything I had done". An acceptable paraphrase.
- **14.10:** "Bursting with repletion" became "bursting with excitement". The sense of being full of things to say is kept.
- **14.15:** "Not without a certain change in their depths sometimes" lost "sometimes". Minor.
- **14.41:** "Superiority" became "advantage". Acceptable. I only fixed "authority" in 14.47 and 14.48, where Jane echoes his word.
- **14.53:** "A dependent … in his dependency" became "her dependency". This personalizes a general statement, but Jane is plainly speaking of herself. Acceptable.
- **14.68:** "Porch of life … mysteries" became "life's entrance … depths". Acceptable.
- **14.86:** "Sententious sage!" became "Wise moralist!". The irony survives.
- **13.3, 13.22, 14.4, 14.6, 14.104, 14.106:** The French is kept untranslated, which is consistent across the chapter.
