Blind reader BL1 (`review/symp-blind-BL1.md`) nevertheless found listening obstacles in some of them. They are recorded here with **optional** micro-edits for Anders to accept or decline. None is applied, and none affects the successor's hashes, gate result or card.

| Para | BL1 finding | Optional micro-edit (proposal only) | Note |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.0 | The listener is never named; "you Phalerian!" is a joke the reader cannot see | none | The unnamed companion is Plato's design. Jowett's footnote explains the Phalerian pun on "bald" (*phalakros*); a gloss would need an added clause |
