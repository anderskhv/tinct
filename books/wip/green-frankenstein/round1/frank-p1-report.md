Blocking (9):
- (1,3) "make a thousand observations": the source means reconciling observations that already exist.
- (1,3) Three pieces of content are dropped: "to the last generation", "those countries … so many months", and "if at all possible".
- (2,5) A dangling modifier makes the girl's father the one who saved the prize money.
- (4,4) The Creature's first appearance: "being" became "figure".
- (4,27) "seemed a small price", plus "transmit" and "foes of our race" dropped.
- (5,6) The condition on the parents' duty is dropped ("according as they fulfilled their duties").
- (5,8) The baby is "placed … as a nurse" (it reads as if the baby were the nurse).
- (6,4) "draw out my gentleness": the source means Elizabeth softens him toward *her* gentleness.
- (6,4) An invented "Perhaps." answers the rhetorical question about Clerval in the opposite sense.

Glosses proposed (one each, at first need): "keeping" (a painters' term), Coleridge's Ancient Mariner and the albatross, syndics, Roncesvalles and the Holy Sepulchre, Cornelius Agrippa, galvanism.

## Considered and rejected
- "St. Petersburgh" → "Petersburg" and "December": I proposed restoring only the Petersburgh spelling. It is not blocking because it sounds the same aloud. The date formats are house style.
- (4,17) "dæmon" → "demon": kept. It is not a neutral substitute, and the reader keeps the sense. Later chapters should use the same rendering throughout.
- (4,11/22/26) Walton calls Victor a "creature", rendered as "person": considered restoring it for the thematic echo. Not proposed, because it is ordinary period usage and changing it would be churn.
- (4,6) "ground sea" → "the sea shift beneath us": loose but serviceable. Not proposed.
- (5,4) "protecting spirit" → "guardian angel": acceptable, and it anticipates the source's own "guardian angel" in (5,7).
- (5,5) "fair exotic" → "delicate flower": a minor loss of the image. Not proposed.
- (5,7) "beyond the frontiers of Italy" while at Lake Como: the source's own oddity is reproduced faithfully. Left as is.
- (6,8) Paracelsus and Albertus Magnus glosses: not needed once Agrippa is glossed.
- (6,10) Philosopher's stone and elixir of life: widely known, so no gloss.
- (6,11) "like an unadept" dropped: a minor loss. The meaning survives in "confused knowledge".
- (6,12) "while the storm lasted" dropped: trivial.
- (3,6) "element" (meaning the sea): clear enough from context.
- (1,4) "such travel books" for "a history of all the voyages made for purposes of discovery": acceptable.
