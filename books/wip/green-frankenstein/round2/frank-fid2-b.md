## Counts
8 findings in total: 1 blocking and 7 non-blocking.

Blocking:
- 15.0 "impossible to touch or see through" (source: "impervious to my touch or sight"). Heard aloud, this says the Creature could not touch the objects at all. The source means they blocked his movement and his sight. Fix: "blocking both my touch and my sight".

Non-blocking:
- 14.3: "morrow" is left archaic in the partly modernized verse.
- 13.5: "persistent feeling" should follow the source's "obscure feeling", which means vague.
- 13.5: "the monster" drops the source's "whom I had created".
- 12.1: "certainty" should follow the source's "imagination of the enormity".
- 15.12: "words" should be "sounds", as in the source; the Creature cannot yet understand speech.
- 15.13: same issue as 15.12.
- 11.27: the source prints "the Juras" but the candidate has "the Jura".

## Verdict
BLOCKING FINDINGS (1).
