# Lincoln: The Gettysburg Address and the Second Inaugural Address — Danish edition notes

- Lincoln’s quotations from the Bible (Psalm 19, Matthew 18, Genesis 3) were translated by the helper from memory and are **not** checked against a Danish Bible edition (Bibelselskabet 1992 or 2020). Check them before publication.
- Terms: Unionen, den Almægtige, syvogfirs (fourscore and seven).

## General

- Distances: English miles are “engelske mil”; feet, yards and inches are kept.
- Coins and units with no Danish equivalent (groat, farthing, shilling, pottle) are kept.
- Speeches that run across paragraphs follow the English: » opens every paragraph, « only at the end.
