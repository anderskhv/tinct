### Non-blocking (stylistic / connotation)

1. **Paragraph 1** — Source: "every man will and may lawfully rely on his own strength and **art**, for caution against all other men." Candidate: "every man will, and lawfully may, rely on his own strength and **cunning** as his guard against everyone else." "Art" in this Hobbesian context means skill/resourcefulness (a neutral capacity), not deceit; "cunning" carries a connotation of craftiness/slyness that mildly moralizes what Hobbes presents as a neutral fact about the state of nature. Suggested fix: "his own strength and skill" or "his own strength and resourcefulness."

2. **Paragraph 5** — Source: "and therefore some man may **perhaps** desire to know, why Man-kind cannot do the same." Candidate: "So someone may **well** want to know why mankind cannot do the same." The source's "perhaps" is a hedge (this is merely a possible objection someone might raise); "well" reads as an endorsement of the question's likelihood/reasonableness rather than a hedge on whether anyone would ask it. Suggested fix: "So someone might well wonder why mankind cannot do the same" (keeps the conditional "might" to preserve the hedge) or simply "someone may perhaps want to know."
