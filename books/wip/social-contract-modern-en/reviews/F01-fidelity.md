# F01 fidelity review: chapters 1 to 6

## Scope
Chapters 1-6, every paragraph read side by side with Cole (ch1: 5, ch2: 10, ch3: 4, ch4: 15, ch5: 3, ch6: 12 = 49 paragraphs; counts match source). Key terms checked against Rousseau's French from my own knowledge only (no other translation consulted). Paragraph boundaries and fragments (ch2 p9, ch4 p14, ch6 p11) are correct: nothing borrowed from neighbors. Latin/French (lèse-majesté) kept; [1]/[2] markers placed as in source. No omissions, reversals or term drift found. No BLOCKING defects.

## Findings
ch4 p7 | MINOR | "real relations" is Cole's rendering of rapports réels (relations between things). It follows "a relation between things", but a general reader may stumble on "real". Suggest an in-sentence gloss. Also "nothing in the state of war supports" is slightly weaker than "by no means deducible from". | Replace "And a state of war cannot grow out of simple personal relations, only out of real relations." with "And a state of war cannot grow out of simple personal relations, only out of real relations, that is, relations between things." and "nothing in the state of war supports this supposed right" with "this supposed right to kill the conquered cannot be derived from the state of war".

ch6 p9 | MINOR | Awkward double "as" for "in our corporate capacity". Source puts the closing underscore before the closing quote (`...whole._"`); candidate puts ” inside the italics (NIT, source artifact, either is fine). | `_“Each of us places his person and all his power in common under the supreme direction of the general will, and, as a body, we receive each member as an indivisible part of the whole.”_`

ch5 p0 | MINOR | "a mere aggregate" adds "mere" and drops "what may be termed"; slight sharpening. Also "as scattered and disunited as before": "as before" is an addition. | Replace "What I see is a mere aggregate, not an association, with no public good and no body politic yet." with "What I see is what may be called an aggregation, not an association; there is as yet neither public good nor body politic." and "is left as scattered and disunited as before" with "remains scattered and without unity".

ch6 p2 | MINOR | "commit them to the group" adds an object not in Cole ("pledge them"). | "How can he pledge them without harming his own interests and neglecting the care he owes to himself?"

ch6 p6 | MINOR | "would soon ask to be his own judge on all": "soon" is added. | "would ask to be so on all" -> "would ask to be his own judge on all".

ch4 p0 | MINOR | "the only basis" adds "only" (Cole: "form the basis of all legitimate authority"); also drops "we must conclude". | "Nobody has any natural authority over other people, and force creates no right. We must conclude, then, that conventions form the basis of all legitimate authority among men."

ch3 p2 | MINOR | Cole's "must I not merely surrender my purse on compulsion; but, even if I could withhold it, am I bound..." is recast as "Am I merely forced to hand over my purse, or...". The "not only...but even if I could keep it" structure is lost, so it reads as an either/or. | "Must I not only hand over my purse under compulsion, but, even if I could keep it back, am I bound in conscience to give it up?"

ch1 p0 | NIT | "As I do," is an odd rendering of "In this inquiry". | "In this inquiry I will always try to join what right permits with what interest requires, so that justice and utility are never split apart."

ch2 p2 | NIT | "the many peoples under him" adds "many" (Cole: "the peoples under him"). | "the peoples under him"

ch2 p7 | NIT | "They come to love their servitude": "come to" is a small addition. | "They love their servitude"

ch4 p12 | NIT | "pointlessly" loses the profit/usefully wordplay of "without profit". | "instead of killing him to no profit, he has killed him usefully."

ch5 p1 | NIT | "it takes public deliberation" for "implies"; "presupposes public deliberation" is closer. | "The giving is itself a civil act, and it presupposes public deliberation."

ch6 p10 | NIT | "a _Power_" with article; Cole has bare "Power" (a defined term). "when precision matters" is looser than "when they are being used with precision". | "and _Power_ when they compare it with others like itself" ... "it is enough to know how to tell them apart when they are used with precision."

## Verdicts on the renderer's flags (all in my chapters)
- ch2 p7 "brutish condition" -> "living as beasts": faithful (Rousseau: leur brutalité). OK.
- ch2 p9 fragment, "profitless infatuation": fragment correctly kept (starts "past abuses", ends at the sentence's end, closing quote and citation intact). "pointless obsession" is acceptable for the d'Argenson folly/infatuation sense. OK.
- ch4 p2 tranquillity -> peace: acceptable (tranquillité civile). Mild risk of confusion with the "state of peace" of p7, but the context (dungeons, Cyclops) keeps it clear. OK, no change required ("calm" would be an optional alternative).
- ch4 p4 "years of discretion" -> "age of reason": correct (l'âge de raison). OK.
- ch4 p7 "real relations": see MINOR above (term kept, gloss suggested).
- ch4 p8 "good polity" -> "sound political order": acceptable for bonne police (good civil order). OK.
- ch4 p11 sense of "no right to kill an enemy except when he cannot make him a slave": rendered correctly ("A man may kill an enemy only when he cannot make him a slave, which means the right to enslave cannot come from the right to kill"), matching the French sense. Whole paragraph faithful, including the vicious-circle question. OK.
- ch4 p12 "killed him usefully": kept; faithful. OK (see NIT on "pointlessly").
- ch5 p0 "mere aggregate": confirmed added "mere"; see MINOR.
- ch6 p9 formula: close to Cole, all elements present; see MINOR for the doubled "as".
- ch6 p10: "insofar as they share in the sovereign power" / "insofar as they are under the laws of the State" preserves the citizen/subject distinction; State/Sovereign/Power definitions intact; "conventional liberty" (ch6 p4) kept as Cole. OK (see NIT on "a Power").
- ch6 p11 fragment: correctly kept as a fragment starting "modern times". OK. All content (Carthaginians, Macedonians, Bodin, d'Alembert, four/five orders) preserved.
