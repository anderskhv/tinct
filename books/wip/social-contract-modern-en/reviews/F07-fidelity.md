# F07 fidelity review: chapters 34-40

## Scope
Read every paragraph pair in ch34 (8), ch35 (4), ch36 (14), ch37 (7), ch38 (7), ch39 (10), ch40 (7) = 57 paragraphs. Paragraph counts match the source in all seven chapters; no merges, splits or borrowing; fragments (ch35 p3, ch36 p12, p13, ch39 p9) stay fragments. Key terms (general will vs will of all, Sovereign, prince/government/magistrates, social compact, sovereignty lowercase) hold. French checked from my own knowledge only where noted.

## Findings
No BLOCKING findings.

ch36 p2 | MINOR | "encroach on private" became "outweigh". Rousseau's "empiètent" is about the one invading the other, not a comparison of weight. | "The better a State’s constitution is, the more public affairs encroach on private ones in the citizens’ minds."

ch36 p3 | MINOR | "these people" after "representatives of the people" reads as "the people" and could mislead; the antecedent is the deputies. | "In some countries men have dared to call these deputies the Third Estate."

ch36 p1 | MINOR | "greedy self-interest of profit" lost "self-interest", and "softness and love of amenities" merged into "a soft love of comfort". | "Personal services turn into money payments because of the bustle of commerce and the arts, the greedy self-interest of profit, and softness and love of comforts."

ch36 p9 | NIT | "Everything that is not in the course of nature" became "Whatever goes against nature", a slight sharpening. | "Whatever lies outside the course of nature has its drawbacks, and civil society most of all."

ch40 p0 | MINOR | "ingenious methods" became "refined methods", which loses the sense of cleverness/cunning that the next clause ("so much art and mystery") relies on. | span: "despising the clever methods of other nations"

ch40 p2 | MINOR | "make great game of" (mock/ridicule) became "have great fun imagining", which changes the act from mocking to inventing. | span: "They make great fun of all the absurdities that a clever rascal or a smooth talker could get the people of Paris or London to believe."

ch40 p4 | MINOR | The "when ... when ... when" subordinate clauses were turned into flat assertions ("Finally, the State stands on the eve of ruin..."), so the conditional structure is weakened. | "Finally, when the State is on the eve of ruin and keeps up only a vain, illusory, formal existence, when the social bond is broken in every heart and the meanest interest brazenly seizes the sacred name of “public good,” the general will falls silent. All men, guided by secret motives, no longer give their views as citizens, any more than if the State had never existed, and unjust decrees aimed solely at private interest are passed under the name of laws."

ch36 p4 | NIT | "slavery overtakes it, and it is nothing" became "it is a slave and nothing". | span: "As soon as they are elected, slavery overtakes it, and it is nothing."

ch36 p13 | NIT | Fragment given an added "when I was"; the source starts "in dealing with". | "in dealing with external relations I arrived at the subject of confederations."

ch36 p12 | NIT | "they did in theirs" adds a past-tense clause not in the source phrasing; harmless.

ch38 p3 | NIT | "a prince or magistrates" is number-mismatched. | span: "become a prince or magistrate"

ch39 p3 | NIT | Added "Yet" before "the prince gains a great advantage"; the source has no contrast marker. Acceptable, optionally drop.

ch40 p5 | NIT | "changing the state of the question" became "change the question". | span: "His fault is to change the state of the question"

ch40 p3 | NIT | "not taken without question" became "accepted only after being challenged", slightly sharper. | span: "even the best advice is not accepted without question"

ch34 p1 | NIT | "appointed for that purpose" shortened to "the appointed magistrates"; the purpose is implied only.

ch35 p1 | NIT | "love ease" became "love comfort"; fine.

## Verdicts on flagged items in my chapters
- ch35 p1 aegis->shield, pusillanimous->fainthearted: faithful, keep.
- ch36 p3 Third Estate: faithful, but fix "these people" (MINOR above).
- ch36 p5 sacrosanct->"sacred": acceptable. "Sacro-saints" is stronger, but the sense is intact; optional "sacrosanct".
- ch36 p8 "flat sound of your languages": acceptable. Cole's "flatness" is rendered clearly; "sound" is a small gloss. French "langues sourdes" (dull, muffled) supports it.
- ch36 p9 "more cowardice than humanity": faithful. Also reads right in the sentence order.
- ch36 p11 "convenient polity"->"convenient organization": faithful, keep.
- ch37 p5 "control execution": faithful ("how the contract is carried out").
- ch39 p3 "untoward possibility"->"unwelcome possibility" and rendering of the concession clause: faithful (French "possibilité fâcheuse"), keep.
- ch39 p6-p7 propositions: faithful. "Is it the Sovereign’s pleasure" and "those who now hold the administration" preserve the sense; "actually in charge" = "now".
- ch40 p2 "the bells": kept as in the source, which is faithful but opaque to a general reader. Leave it; Duc de Beaufort is silently corrected from "Due", fine.
- ch40 p4 split sentence: MINOR above, with replacement.
