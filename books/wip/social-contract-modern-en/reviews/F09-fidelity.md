# F09 fidelity review: chapters 44, 45, 46, 48

## Scope
Read every paragraph pair: ch44 (8), ch45 (13), ch46 (10), ch48 (1) = 32 paragraphs. Key terms checked against the style note (and Rousseau's French from memory where noted). No other translation consulted.

## Findings
ch45 p7 | BLOCKING | "the weakness of the capital secured it against the magistrates who were in its midst" is recast as "The capital was too weak to be a threat, and that protected it from the magistrates in its midst." This invents a claim (the capital as a possible threat) and changes what the weakness does. French: "la faiblesse de la capitale la garantissait contre les magistrats qui étaient dans son sein". | Replace the sentence with: "The weakness of the capital protected it against the magistrates in its midst."

ch45 p2 | MINOR | "This commitment may be carried out in either of two ways, according to the nature of the danger." is rendered "How this is done depends on the kind of danger." The "two ways" is dropped, and the next paragraphs and p4 ("first method / second") rely on it. | Replace last sentence with: "This can be done in either of two ways, depending on the kind of danger."

ch46 p7 | MINOR | "Greece makes no appeal from her judgments" becomes "Greece does not appeal." Left without an object it reads oddly and drops "from her judgments". | Replace last clause with: "Greece makes no appeal from her judgments."

ch46 p8 | NIT | Fragment's first word "have" is dropped ("dealt with at greater length..."). | "have dealt with at greater length in my _Letter to M. d’Alembert_."

ch45 p10 | NIT | "idle" rendered "a dead letter". Slightly stronger/idiomatic than "idle" (French oiseuse = useless). | "...turns into either tyranny or something useless."

ch46 p1 | NIT | "so far from being the arbiter of the people's opinion" becomes "does not rule on the people’s opinion". Acceptable, but the "so far from" emphasis is lost. | "The censorial tribunal is not the arbiter of the people’s opinion; it only declares it, and as soon as the two part ways, its decisions are null and void."

ch45 p9 | NIT | "on a capital point" -> "on a matter of life and death". Defensible (next sentence is about blood of citizens) but narrower than "capital" (French "sur un point capital", crucial, with a pun). | Optional: "on a crucial point".

ch44 p4 | NIT | see Cleomenes flag below; supported, no change needed.

Other paragraphs in 44, 45, 46, 48: no omissions, additions, reversals or term drift found. Key terms hold (general will, Sovereign, prince, government, tribunate, magistrates). Latin/italics/footnote markers [1], [2] placed as in source. Typography fine (curly marks, American spelling).

## Flagged-uncertainty verdicts
- ch44 p4 "Cleomenes" gloss ("his successor Cleomenes avenged his blood"): FAITHFUL. The source paragraph itself names Cleomenes two clauses later ("after Cleomenes Sparta ceased..."), so the in-sentence identification is supported by the passage and is historically right. No change.
- ch45 p3 paraphernalia -> "machinery": FAITHFUL. Also "obstacle to their preservation" became "stands in the way of saving the State". This follows the French sense (guarding against the peril) and the surrounding argument; Cole's "their" is ambiguous. Acceptable.
- ch45 p7 restructure: NOT faithful; see BLOCKING above.
- ch45 p9 "capital point" -> "a matter of life and death": acceptable (NIT above).
- ch45 p10 "tyranny or a dead letter": acceptable but slightly stronger (NIT above).
- ch46 p1 "arbiter" -> "does not rule on": acceptable (NIT above).
- ch46 p6 "no trace of constraint": FAITHFUL. Correctly resolves the antecedent ("it" = constraint, French "il n'en faut aucun vestige").
- ch46 p7 "makes no appeal from her judgments" dropped?: Not dropped, but shortened to "Greece does not appeal," which loses the object; fix above (MINOR).
