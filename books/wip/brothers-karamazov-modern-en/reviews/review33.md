## MINOR

ch41 p34 — MINOR — register / softening — SRC "pray to God for gladness"; CAND "ask God for gladness". "Pray to God" is ordinary modern English, not an archaism, and in Zossima's scriptural exhortation "ask" weakens the religious force. Restore "pray to God".

ch41 p36 — MINOR — addition / hedge added — SRC "Then you will be indifferent to life and even grow to hate it"; CAND "you will be indifferent to life, and you may even come to hate it". "may" is not in SRC and softens a flat statement. Fix proposed.

ch41 p47 — MINOR — omission of hedge — SRC "seeing him that night apparently so cheerful and talkative"; CAND "his friends saw him that night so cheerful and talkative". "apparently" is dropped, so the narrator's implication that the cheerfulness was only apparent is lost. Fix proposed ("seemingly").

## NIT

ch41 p35 — NIT — vocabulary — SRC "You will know that yourself hereafter"; CAND "later". "Hereafter" means the life to come; "later" is vaguer. Same in ch41 p41 ("saved hereafter" / "saved later").

ch41 p38 — NIT — addition — SRC "It shows his time has not yet come"; CAND "It shows only that his time has not yet come". "only" added.

ch41 p41 — NIT — hedge dropped — SRC "the evil-doer might perhaps have been saved"; CAND "might have been saved". "perhaps" dropped.

ch41 p44 — NIT — omission — SRC "they will attain at last, as it were, to a certain semblance"; CAND "they will reach at last a certain likeness". "as it were" dropped.

ch42 p0 — NIT — addition — SRC "All this was done by Father Païssy"; CAND "Father Païssy did all this himself". "himself" is a small intensifier.

ch42 p16 — NIT — imprecision — SRC "that question alone, gradually repeated"; CAND "repeated again and again". The "gradually" sense shifts to frequency.

ch41 p45 — NIT — typography/taste — SRC "God Who calls them"; CAND "God who calls them". Capital "Who" for God lowered; harmless.
