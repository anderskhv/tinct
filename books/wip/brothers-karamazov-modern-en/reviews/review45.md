## MINOR

- ch53 p78 — MINOR — voice/distortion — SRC “It’s not them he’s afraid of—could you be frightened of any one? It’s me he’s afraid of, I thought, only me.” CAND “You’re not afraid of those people—could you be scared of anyone? He’s afraid of me, I thought, only of me.” The “it’s not them … it’s me” contrast is flattened and the person shifts mid-thought (“You’re” then “He’s”). SRC keeps Grushenka’s inner “he” throughout. Also “you little stupid” became “you silly boy” (adds “boy”).
- ch53 p82 — MINOR — reversal risk — SRC “I am drunk! I’m drunk as it is” CAND “Me, drunk! I’m drunk anyway”. “Me, drunk!” reads as an indignant denial (“Me, drunk?”), the opposite of the affirmation.
- ch53 p94 — MINOR — distortion — SRC “You’re a _lajdak_ yourself!” CAND “You’re the _lajdak_!” The retort (“you’re one yourself”) becomes a flat assertion; “yourself” is lost.
- ch54 p34 — MINOR — omission — SRC “it might, perhaps, be of use” CAND “it might be of some use”. Perhotin’s hedge “perhaps” is dropped.
