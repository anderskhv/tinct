## Findings

ch31 p28 — MINOR — distortion — SRC "Besides its being honestly acquired from a ‘sister,’ so highly respected and revered, do you know that now I can look after…" adds a further point; CAND "Never mind that it’s honestly come by, from a ‘sister’ I respect and revere so highly. Do you know, I can now…" dismisses the point instead. Restore the "besides" sense.

ch31 p28 — MINOR — vocabulary — SRC "mamma is capricious and tearful and insane!" CAND "fretful and tearful and out of her mind!" ("capricious" is not "fretful"; the whim is the point). Use "capricious".

ch31 p44 — MINOR — softened irony — SRC "to report on the success of her commission" CAND "to report on how her errand had turned out". The narrator's dry irony about "success" is lost. Use "the success of her errand".

ch31 p12 — NIT — addition — SRC "‘Wisp of tow,’ they shouted" CAND "‘Wisp of tow!’ they shouted" (added exclamation mark).

ch31 p15 — NIT — register — SRC "he came back depressed, terribly depressed" CAND "came home low, terribly low" (a little unidiomatic in American English; "low in spirits" later in the paragraph is fine).

ch31 p15 — NIT — imprecision — SRC "turned sideways to me" CAND "turned away from me".

ch31 p16 — NIT — addition — SRC "Alyosha felt though that he trusted him, and that if there had been some one else … the man would not have spoken so openly" CAND inserts "he thought" and "Still".

ch31 p20 — NIT — sharpened — SRC "seemed to produce a tremendous impression on the captain" CAND "seemed to hit the captain with tremendous force".

ch31 p28 — NIT — sharpened — SRC "she doesn’t like it" CAND "she hates it".
