## Findings

ch88 p22 — MINOR — distortion (concession moved) — SRC "Though Ippolit Kirillovitch was genuinely moved, he wound up his speech with this rhetorical appeal"; CAND "Ippolit Kirillovitch was genuinely moved, though he closed his speech with this rhetorical appeal".
