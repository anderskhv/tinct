## Findings

ch52 p100 — NIT — tone — SRC "Pan Vrublevsky was specially furious"; CAND "was the angrier of the two" (comparative replaces "specially furious").

ch52 p220 — NIT — vocabulary — SRC "You're a trump, Mitya!"; CAND "You're a prince, Mitya!" ("trump" = a splendid fellow; "prince" shifts the image).
