## MINOR

ch12 p16 — MINOR — distortion — SRC “Tapped the ground?” becomes CAND “Banged his forehead?” Same issue as p13: Alyosha is picking up Rakitin’s exact phrase, so it should echo it.

ch13 p20 — MINOR — softened — SRC “The Father Superior could not be von Sohn.” becomes CAND “can hardly be von Sohn.” This adds a hedge Fyodor does not make.
