## Findings

ch39 p13 — MINOR — voice/register — SRC "your words are too obscure" became CAND "your words are too dark to me". Unnatural phrasing and the added "to me" shifts the sense. Fix: "too obscure".

ch39 p14 — MINOR — distortion — SRC "I thought your brotherly face would help him" became CAND "the sight of a brother's face would help him". Garnett's point is the brotherly quality of Alyosha's face, not that a brother's face would help. Fix: "your brotherly face".

ch39 p14 — MINOR — vocabulary — SRC "a look of mortification" became CAND "a look of hurt" (and "mortification" / "your mortification" became "your hurt"). Loses the wounded-pride sense. Fix: keep "mortification".

ch39 p19 — MINOR — distortion — SRC "he never quarreled" became CAND "he never fought with them". Quarrelling is not fighting, so the claim is sharpened. Fix: "he never quarreled with them".

ch39 p19 — MINOR — vocabulary — SRC "never ironical" became CAND "never sneering". The meaning shifts toward contempt. Fix: "never ironic".

ch39 p0 — NIT — omission — SRC "on his pilgrimage to collect alms" became CAND "on a journey to collect alms". "Pilgrimage" is lost.

ch39 p4 — NIT — addition — SRC "he wanted to sob" became CAND "he wanted to sob aloud". "Aloud" is added.

ch39 p16 — NIT — heading — SRC "Notes of the Life of the deceased Priest... taken from his own words" became CAND "Notes on the Life of the late Priest... taken down from his own words". Small changes to a title-like line.

ch39 p34 — NIT — addition — SRC "more and more sweet and joyous and full of love" became CAND "more sweet and joyful and loving than the day before". The comparison is slightly expanded.
