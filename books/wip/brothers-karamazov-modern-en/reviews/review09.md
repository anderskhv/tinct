## MINOR

ch17 p0 — MINOR — DISTORTION / lost repetition — SRC "With me money is an accessory, the overflow of my heart, the framework"; CAND "a trimming, the overflow of my heart, the stage set". "Stage set" is an invented image for "framework". Also SRC "She allowed me, she allowed me much in the dark" (Dmitri's repeated phrasing); CAND "In the dark she let me do a great deal" drops the repetition that style note asks to keep for Dmitri. Fix proposed.

ch17 p12 — MINOR — ADDITION — SRC "I was in fault myself for refusing to treat him with proper respect"; CAND "Partly it was my own fault, since I wouldn't show him proper respect". "Partly" is a hedge not in SRC. Fix proposed.

ch17 p14 — MINOR — NAMES / consistency — CAND "father sent me six thousand roubles" and "money affairs with father" are lowercase, but the same speech in ch17 p0 and throughout ch16 (p21, p22, p28 and others) now capitalizes "Father" where SRC has lowercase "father". The chapters should use one form; proposed fix aligns p14 with the capitalized form used in the neighboring paragraphs.
