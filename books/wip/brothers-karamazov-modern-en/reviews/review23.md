# Fidelity review 23

Batches reviewed: ch36_p000-018 (chapter 36, "V. The Grand Inquisitor")
Paragraphs reviewed: 19 (ch36 p0 to p18)
Counts: BLOCKING 1, MINOR 0, NIT 6

ch36 p14 — BLOCKING — broken quotation / cross-reference — SRC "Thou hast had no lack of admonitions and warnings" became CAND "You had plenty of admonitions and warnings". Alyosha quotes the phrase in p15 (CAND: "what does ‘no lack of admonitions and warnings’ mean?"), so the quoted words no longer appear in the text he is quoting. Restore "You had no lack of admonitions and warnings" (fix proposed).

ch36 p0 — NIT — addition — SRC "Oh, with greater faith, for it is fifteen centuries…" CAND "Oh, with greater faith, in fact, for fifteen centuries…". "in fact" is an added hedge.

ch36 p3 — NIT — verse — SRC "Weary and worn, the Heavenly King / Our mother, Russia, came to bless" CAND "Weary and worn, the King of Heaven came / To bless our mother Russia". The meaning holds, but the line order is rebuilt and the dress/bless rhyme is lost.

ch36 p5 — NIT — tone — SRC "And behold, He deigned to appear" CAND "And so He chose to show Himself". "And so" adds a causal link that SRC lacks, and "behold" is lost.

ch36 p7 — NIT — omission — SRC "cowed into submission and trembling obedience" CAND "cowed into trembling obedience". "submission" is dropped.

ch36 p7 — NIT — vocabulary — SRC "the close, gloomy vaulted prison" CAND "a narrow, gloomy, vaulted cell" (and later "the iron door of the cell"). "close" means airless or confined, and "prison" became "cell".

ch36 p8 — NIT — addition — SRC "dost Thou know what will be to-morrow?" CAND "do You know what tomorrow will bring?". "will bring" is a small gloss.
