# Fidelity review 22

Batches reviewed: ch35_p010-032 (ch35 "IV. Rebellion")
Paragraphs reviewed: 23 (ch35 p10 to p32)
Counts: BLOCKING 2, MINOR 7, NIT 5

## Findings

ch35 p10 — BLOCKING — reversal — causation changed. SRC: "shut her up all night ... in a privy, and because she didn’t ask to be taken up at night ..., they smeared her face". CAND: "They locked her up all night in an outhouse ... because she hadn’t asked to be taken out in the night ... For that they smeared her face". The punishment-for-not-asking now attaches to the locking up, and the smearing becomes a separate "for that".

ch35 p10 — BLOCKING — softened — "filled her mouth with excrement" became "smeared her face with filth and stuffed it into her mouth". "Excrement" is replaced by the vaguer "filth". Restore "excrement".

ch35 p10 — MINOR — distortion — SRC "beat her little aching heart with her tiny fist"; CAND "beat her aching little chest". "Heart" is lost.

ch35 p12 — MINOR — omission — SRC "retiring from the service into a life of leisure"; CAND "retire from the service convinced". "Into a life of leisure" is dropped.

ch35 p12 — NIT — addition — "The servants are summoned" became "The house servants"; "not daring to cry" became "not daring to make a sound".

ch35 p20 — NIT — SRC "Of course, I will" answers "Will you say"; CAND "Of course I am" answers a reworded "Are you going to tell me". Acceptable, slightly stiff.

ch35 p22 — MINOR — hedge dropped — SRC "Some jester will say, perhaps, that the child would have grown up"; CAND "Some jester will say that". "Perhaps" is lost.

ch35 p22 — MINOR — hedge dropped/sharpened — SRC "perhaps it really may happen ... I, too, perhaps, may cry aloud"; CAND "it may really happen ... I too will cry out". "Perhaps" is dropped twice and "may" becomes "will".

ch35 p22 — MINOR — addition — SRC "the oppressor who threw her son to the dogs"; CAND "the oppressor who had her son thrown to the dogs". This adds an agency gloss and is inconsistent with the earlier "the fiend who threw her child".

ch35 p22 — NIT — "I renounce the higher harmony" became "I turn down the higher harmony". This is weaker and less formal than the source.

ch35 p22 — NIT — "those tears are unatoned for" became "were never atoned for". "Never" is added and the tense changed.

ch35 p24 — MINOR — addition/distortion — SRC "One can hardly live in rebellion"; CAND "Nobody can live in rebellion for long". "For long" is added and the force of the claim changed.

ch35 p27 — MINOR — distortion — SRC "is there a being in the whole world who would have the right to forgive"; CAND "is there anyone". This loses the "a being" and "a Being" echo that Alyosha answers.

Other paragraphs (p11, p13 to p21, p23, p25, p26, p28 to p32) were checked and are faithful. In the Thou to You renderings in p22 and p27, "You are just, O Lord, for Your ways are revealed" is correct per the style note.
