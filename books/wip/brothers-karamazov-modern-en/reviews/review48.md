## Findings

ch58 p1 — BLOCKING — sharpened oath — SRC “Well, bother them!” became CAND “Well, to hell with them!” The mild exclamation is made a hard oath. Fix proposed: “Well, forget them!”

ch58 p4 — MINOR — softened — SRC “then I murdered him” became CAND “next I killed him”. Mitya is bitterly echoing the prosecutor’s accusation, so “murdered” should stay (the same word is kept in p87, “The devil murdered father”). Fix proposed.

ch58 p63 — MINOR — sharpened oath — SRC “Oh, hang it!” became CAND “Oh, damn it!” Fix proposed: “Oh, blast it!”

ch58 p100 — MINOR — distortion — SRC “we should come to loggerheads” became CAND “we’d come to blows”. “Blows” implies physical violence; the sense is a clash or deadlock. Fix proposed: “we’d clash”.

ch58 p0 — NIT — addition — SRC “had gone up to the window” became CAND “crept up to the window”. “Crept” adds a manner of movement. SRC “Clearly, precisely, distinctly” became “Clearly and precisely, step by step”.

ch58 p13 — NIT — addition — CAND “so hard that it creaked”; SRC “so that it creaked”.

ch58 p25 — NIT — vocabulary — SRC “foolish idea in your hearts” became CAND “in your heads”.

ch58 p65 — NIT — addition — CAND “and I still jumped down” adds “still”.

ch58 p102 — NIT — omission/addition — SRC “at the same time” is dropped and “at least” is added.

ch58 p136 — NIT — punctuation — SRC “Won’t you search me as I am! Can’t you?” became CAND “...as I am? Can’t you?” The exclamation mark is lost.
