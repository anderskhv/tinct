## MINOR

ch93 p3 — MINOR — scripture altered — SRC "‘What measure ye mete so it shall be meted unto you again,’" became CAND "‘With the measure you use, it shall be measured to you again,’". Style note keeps recognizable KJV wording for scripture. Proposed: "‘With what measure you mete, it shall be measured to you again,’".

ch93 p4 — MINOR — softened / altered quotation — SRC "Fetyukovitch did not so much as reply" became CAND "hardly answered at all" (a hedge SRC lacks). Also the quoted ladies' phrase SRC "crushed for good" became CAND "finished for good", losing the echo of "crush him by mercy" in p3. Fixed both.

ch93 p21 — MINOR — addition — SRC "swing the pestle in the air, and so knocked the old man down" became CAND "knock the old man down by accident". "By accident" is not in SRC.

ch94 p5 — MINOR — distortion — SRC "to abandon me for another with whom he can get on better, like Dmitri" became CAND "to some other woman who suits him better, as Dmitri’s did". "Dmitri’s did" is garbled; Dmitri is the one who left her. Proposed: "for another woman he can get on with better, as Dmitri did."

ch94 p13 — MINOR — sharpened — SRC "It can and ought to be!" became CAND "It is possible and it must happen!". "Ought" is the key word Alyosha repeats in p15 and p19, and "must" is stronger. Proposed: "It can be, and it ought to be!"

ch94 p23 — MINOR — period term — SRC "you will come directly" became CAND "you’re coming soon". Per the style note, "directly" means immediately. Proposed: "you’re coming right away."
