## Findings

ch79 p0 — MINOR — softening / distortion — Suicide note: SRC "I destroy my life of my own will and desire" becomes CAND "I am ending my life". "Destroy" is the note's own verb and is stronger than "ending". Also SRC "Alyosha ran with her to the cottage" becomes CAND "He had hurried back with her", where "back" is unsupported by the source. Fix proposed.

ch79 p27 — MINOR — sharpened / addition — SRC "he was unfair to me, unfair to me about lots of things" becomes CAND "he wronged me, wronged me in lots of ways" ("wronged" is stronger than "unfair"). SRC "with a shudder of offense" becomes CAND "shuddering with injured pride", which adds "pride". Fix proposed.

ch80 p4 — MINOR — softened — SRC "hysterical, intense, almost morbid, curiosity" becomes CAND "feverish, intense, almost unhealthy curiosity". "Hysterical" is the point of the narrator's remark about the ladies and is drained to "feverish". Fix proposed.

ch79 p3 — NIT — addition — SRC "From whom?" becomes CAND "Who told you?" ("told you" is added, though implied).

ch79 p10 — NIT — softened — SRC "For goodness' sake, sit down" becomes CAND "Please, sit down". SRC "a wet towel on your head" becomes CAND "for your forehead".

ch79 p17 — NIT — addition — SRC "I was asleep last time" becomes CAND "The last time I really was asleep" ("really" added).

ch79 p2 — NIT — imprecision — SRC "It's a good thing you came" becomes CAND "I'm glad you came" (slightly more personal warmth).

ch79 p31 — NIT — vocabulary — SRC "what a generous soul" becomes CAND "what a noble soul".

ch79 p36 — NIT — imprecision — SRC "what he had just said" becomes CAND "what Ivan had said a little earlier" (the time reference is loosened).

ch80 p5 — NIT — structure — SRC "except perhaps the lawyers" becomes a separate sentence, "Perhaps the lawyers were the exception". The meaning is intact.
