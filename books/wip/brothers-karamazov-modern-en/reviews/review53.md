## Findings

ch66 p17 — MINOR — register / grammar — SRC "a form of resentful irony against those to whom they daren’t speak the truth, from having been for years humiliated and intimidated by them" vs CAND "a bitter irony aimed at the people they’re afraid to tell the truth, because for years those people have humiliated and cowed them." The CAND is ungrammatical ("afraid to tell the truth" lacks "to them"), and "a form of" and "resentful" were dropped. Replacement proposed in fixes53.json.

ch66 p12 — NIT — vocabulary — SRC "snap up anything without biting it" vs CAND "without chewing"; the sense is the same. Also CAND "Then one day I noticed that for three days running" adds a small connective ("Then one day") not in SRC "Suddenly I noticed for three days in succession". Harmless.
