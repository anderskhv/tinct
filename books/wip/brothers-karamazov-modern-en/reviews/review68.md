## Findings

ch85 p1 — MINOR — softened — SRC "such signs of the times, ominous of an unenviable future" vs CAND "which point to an unenviable future": the word "ominous" (the foreboding force) is lost.

ch85 p17 — MINOR — misread vocabulary — SRC "her scrutinizing eyes" vs CAND "her piercing eyes": scrutinizing (examining, searching) rendered as piercing, a different quality.

ch85 p1 — NIT — softened — SRC "ought to be harassed by them" vs CAND "ought to be troubled by them": slightly weaker.

ch85 p12 — NIT — vocabulary — SRC "withered bosom of their decrepit mother" vs CAND "withered breast": inconsistent with "bosom of their mother earth" two sentences earlier; harmless.

ch85 p14 — NIT — shift — SRC "All this was only introductory, however" vs CAND "At any rate, all this was only an introduction": "however" becomes "at any rate"; negligible.

ch85 p17 — NIT — tone — SRC "alleged that he, he of all men, had despised her" vs CAND "stated that he, of all men": the repeated "he, he" and the force of "alleged" are lost.

ch85 p19 — NIT — omission — SRC "setting apart half that sum, that very day, and sewing it up" vs CAND "could that very day set aside half of it": the second "that very day" emphasis is dropped.

ch85 p21 — NIT — voice — SRC Mitya-style self-talk "let's spend that, too!" vs CAND "Let us spend that, too!": stiffer than the source; a contraction would suit the quoted thought.
