# Tinct — Mission Statement

The written word is among the most important ways we store and transmit knowledge. Yet the way we read has hardly changed in thousands of years. How many books have been abandoned after the first few pages were hard to understand? How many given up on page 42 because you couldn't remember that one character — or didn't know the Russian nickname for Alexei is Alyosha, or that the American Richard goes by Bill? How many great books have been left behind for an empty-calorie podcast or news article?

The digital revolution gave us the Kindle and Audible — two closed ecosystems that digitised the page but didn't reimagine reading. You still read passively. Lose track of characters. Have to buy both the text and audio version, hope they're available in your language, and pray they sync. If you want to discuss what you're reading with AI, you paste a passage into ChatGPT and hope it has the book in its training set.

Tinct changes this. Reading becomes active.

## Read your way

Every book on Tinct is available in multiple editions: the original text, a modern English translation, a simplified version for younger or newer readers, and translations in your language. Switch between them with one click, or read two side by side — the original next to a modern version, paragraph-aligned, the way the No Fear Shakespeare series works but for any book. Audiobook playback syncs automatically to your reading position across devices.

## Discuss with an AI that actually knows the book

Tinct's AI companion has the full text loaded, knows exactly which page you're on, and remembers your reading objectives. Ask it to explain a passage, connect themes across chapters, or help you reflect on what you just read. This isn't a generic chatbot — it's a reading partner built for the book in your hands.

## Never lose context

A living "Cast" section tracks every character and reminds you of their story so far — no spoilers, just what you've read. Intelligent highlights that follow you across editions. Notes you can write freely or pull from chat. Flashcards and spaced repetition to remember what matters.

## A free library of the books that matter most

From launch, Tinct offers a free library of the Western canon — Homer, Joyce, Dostoevsky, Austen, Dante, and more. On Kindle and Audible, the text-plus-audio combination for a single classic can cost $30. On Tinct, every out-of-copyright book is free to read in every edition.

## Everywhere you read

Tinct works on desktop, mobile, and any Android-based e-reader. Your reading position, highlights, and notes sync across all of them. Pick up on your phone where you left off on your laptop, or settle into a chapter on your e-reader knowing everything follows you.

The goal is not to replace the book — it's to make the greatest texts ever written accessible to anyone willing to try, and to make the experience of reading them richer than it has ever been.
