The library

Public library. The books Tinct provides to every reader. We aim to publish only texts that are in the public domain in the United States and in the European Union, including Denmark. For a translation, the translation itself must also be in the public domain. Every book goes through a rights check before we publish it. Tinct's modern editions, introductions and narration are our own work.

Copyright exceptions. Copyright rules differ between countries and we can't rule out every exception. If you believe a text in the public library is still protected anywhere, or that you hold rights in it, write to contact@tinct.app with the title and the reason. We'll restrict the text while we look into it.
