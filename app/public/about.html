Questions

Which AI do you use?

The companion runs on Claude by Anthropic; spoken Talk and the narration voices run on xAI's Grok. Our privacy statement explains exactly what is sent and what is kept.

Is the modern English a real translation?

It is Tinct's own rendering of the whole book in today's English, made with AI from the original, paragraph by paragraph. The original is one tap away, and switching keeps your place.

What happens to my notes and highlights?

They are kept with your account and synced to every device. Reading stays free, so they stay yours with or without Premium.

Can I cancel?

Yes, any time. Premium stays on until the end of the month or year you paid for. The free month needs no card: if you do nothing, nothing is charged.

Does it work offline?

Yes, for reading: every book you open is saved on your device, in the browser and in the Android app. Explanations, Talk and new narration need a connection.
