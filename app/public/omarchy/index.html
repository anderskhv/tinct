tinct

At home in Omarchy

A library at your fingertips.

The complete Tinct experience in its own app window. Read, listen, compare editions, chat and talk with your books. Your account, credits, highlights and reading places work as usual.

Install inside Omarchy

Open a terminal in Omarchy and run:

curl -fsSL https://tinct.app/omarchy/install.sh -o /tmp/tinct-install.sh
bash /tmp/tinct-install.sh

Open the Omarchy launcher and type Tinct. When the browser asks, allow the local network connection so Tinct can follow your desktop colours. The theme connection only reads the Omarchy palette on your computer.

Using Try Omarchy on a Mac? Run the installer inside the Linux desktop. Enable microphone access in Try Omarchy and in the browser when you want to use Talk.

Your colours, or a little Persia

Press ? or Ctrl K for commands and appearance. Follow Omarchy picks up desktop theme changes automatically. Prince of Persia pairs midnight blue and gold with sandstone reading pages. Choose Tinct to return to your usual appearance.

Letter shortcuts pause while you type or select text. You can turn them off in the command menu. Ctrl K still opens commands from a text field. Arrow keys, Page Up, Page Down and Space retain the reader’s usual navigation.

Keep it simple

The app uses Omarchy’s Chromium browser and updates with Tinct. It needs an internet connection and your normal Tinct account for account features. Running the installer again updates the small theme connection.

If colours stop following, start Tinct from the launcher, check the browser’s local network permission, and select Follow Omarchy again.

To remove the launcher and local theme connection:

bash ~/.local/share/tinct-omarchy/install.sh --uninstall

This leaves your browser data, books and account untouched.
