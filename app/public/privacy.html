Overview

Tinct is a reading platform for classic literature, available at tinct.app and as an Android application. It is an independent product operated from Copenhagen, Denmark. In this policy, "Tinct", "we" and "our" refer to the service and its operator; "you" refers to a reader of the service.

Tinct collects the data needed to run the service: to keep your place in a book, to synchronise your reading across devices, to answer your questions about a text, to read books aloud, to process payments, and to understand whether the product is useful. Tinct does not sell your data, does not share it with third parties for advertising, and does not run advertising.

What we collect

Account data

If you create an account, your email address and authentication credentials are stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider. Passwords are hashed by Supabase and are never stored in plain text. If you sign in with Google, Google provides us with your email address and name. When you create an account we also record whether you agreed to receive occasional email (see below), and which link or campaign brought you to Tinct, if any.

Reading data

Your reading position, preferences, highlights, notes, reading history, chat history and other reading-state data are stored so that the service can keep your place and synchronise it across your devices. If you are signed in, this data is associated with your account and stored with Supabase and Cloudflare. If you are not signed in, it is kept in your browser, except where a feature needs our servers: questions you ask the AI companion, chapter recaps and notes, and narration are processed on our servers and by the providers below even when you are not signed in.

Voice

Talk, the spoken conversation feature, uses your microphone only while a conversation is open, and only if you allow it. Your voice is streamed directly from your device to xAI (see below) to produce the spoken reply; Tinct does not store the audio. A written transcript of the conversation is saved to your chat history like a typed chat. Dictation uses your browser's own speech recognition, which in some browsers (for example Google Chrome) is processed by the browser maker. The Android app asks for microphone permission for these features only.

Issue reports

When you report an error in a text, we store the selected passage, your comment, where it appears in the book and, if you are signed in, your user ID. The report is reviewed automatically by Anthropic's Claude to suggest a correction, and a notice of it is emailed to us. If you are signed in, we may email you when your report has been reviewed.

Payment data

Payments are processed by Stripe. Tinct does not store card numbers. We give Stripe your email address, your account identifier and, if known, the link or campaign that brought you to Tinct, and Stripe provides us with a customer identifier and transaction records for your subscription.

Product analytics

Tinct records first-party usage events to understand whether the product is useful and where readers get stuck. These events include the page viewed, book and chapter identifiers, time spent reading, your browser's user agent (browser, operating system and device type), the referring page, the link or campaign that brought you (including campaign and ad-click parameters, if present in the link), feature use such as chat, audio and checkout, and timestamps. Events carry a random identifier for your device. It stays the same until you sign out, which gives a fresh one; if you create an account, events from that device before sign-up become linked to your account.

When you are signed in, events are associated with your user ID and account email, so that we can see how individual readers use Tinct and help them during the beta period. We also record, per reader, how much each AI and audio feature is used and what it costs us (for example tokens, seconds of audio, model and provider), without the content of your messages; for readers who are not signed in, this is recorded under a code derived from your IP address rather than the address itself.

What we do not collect

Tinct does not collect your precise location, contacts, photos, or camera. We do not record your microphone outside an open Talk conversation.

How we use it

  • To run the service: account data, reading data and browser storage are used to sign you in, keep your place, and synchronise your reading across devices.
  • To answer questions about a text: when you use the AI reading features, the relevant passage, your messages and, where it helps the answer, your recent reading history are sent to the AI provider that powers that feature (see below).
  • To read books aloud: book text is sent to our speech provider to produce narration. No personal data is included.
  • To bill subscriptions: payment data is used to manage your Premium subscription and to issue receipts.
  • To help readers and improve the product: analytics and issue reports are used to fix errors, to understand how features are used, and to see how individual signed-in readers are getting on so that we can help them. Account-level analytics and the database are accessible only to the site administrator.
  • To email you, if you agreed: when you create an account you can choose, unticked by default, to receive occasional email about your reading and Tinct. We email only readers who agreed. You can withdraw at any time by replying to such an email or writing to contact@tinct.app. Account and service emails, such as password resets and issue-report replies, are sent regardless.

Tinct does not use your content, messages or reading data to train AI models.

Cookies and local storage

Tinct uses first-party browser storage to make the service work. This includes Supabase authentication storage, reading progress, preferences, offline and cache data, the device identifier described above, and small flags recording that an in-app notice has been dismissed. After sign-in, Tinct sets a first-party tinct_auth cookie, kept for up to a year, so that the homepage can route signed-in readers directly into the application.

Tinct stores attribution information, such as campaign parameters and the referring site, in browser storage to understand which links bring readers to the service. Signing out removes your reading data and the device identifier from the browser.

Tinct does not use tracking cookies or third-party advertising cookies, and loads no third-party advertising or analytics scripts.

Third-party services

Tinct relies on the following providers to operate the service. Each processes data only as needed to provide its function.

  • Supabase: authentication and database (PostgreSQL), hosted in the European Union (Ireland). Supabase also sends account emails, such as sign-up confirmations and password resets. Row-level security limits each reader to their own data.
  • Cloudflare: application hosting (Workers), storage of synchronised chat history and reading positions, and file storage (R2) for audio, on a global network.
  • Anthropic: the AI companion, recaps, chapter notes and the review of issue reports are powered by Anthropic's Claude. Your messages and the relevant text are sent to Anthropic's API. Anthropic does not use API data to train its models. See Anthropic's privacy policy.
  • xAI: Talk (spoken conversation) is powered by xAI's Grok. While a conversation is open, your voice, the relevant text and your recent conversation are sent to xAI. xAI also produces the narration voices, from book text only. See xAI's privacy policy.
  • OpenAI: when you ask the companion to check sources, your recent questions are sent to OpenAI to run a web search. OpenAI is asked not to store them. See OpenAI's privacy policy.
  • Stripe: payment processing for Premium subscriptions.
  • Brevo: sending Tinct's own email: issue-report notices and replies, and the occasional emails you agreed to.
  • Google: optional sign-in. If you sign in with Google, Google provides your email address and name.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Union, including in the United States. All connections to Tinct and to these providers are encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Data retention and deletion

Account and reading data are kept for as long as your account exists. Analytics events and usage records are kept for 24 months and then deleted automatically. Data stored in your browser remains on your device until you sign out or clear your browser storage.

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by writing to contact@tinct.app. Deletion is completed within 30 days of the request and covers your account, reading data, chat history, analytics and usage records, issue reports and your Stripe customer record. Copies that providers keep for their own legal or security purposes are governed by their policies.

Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of all data Tinct holds about you.
  • Deletion: request that your account and all associated data be deleted.
  • Portability: request your data in a portable format.
  • Correction: request that inaccurate data be corrected.
  • Withdrawal of consent: for optional processing such as email, withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, write to contact@tinct.app. You may also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes significantly, Tinct will notify you at the email address on your account. Minor wording changes are not notified; the date at the top of this page records the most recent revision.

Contact

Questions about this policy or about your data: contact@tinct.app
