The Adventures of Pinocchio

Carlo Collodi

A runaway wooden puppet lies, boasts and trusts every swindler on the road home to his father.

Chapters
  1. Chapter 1How Master Cherry the carpenter found a piece of wood that cried and laughed like a child.
  2. Chapter 2Master Cherry gives the piece of wood to his friend Geppetto, who takes it to make himself a wonderful puppet that can dance, fence with a sword, and turn somersaults.
  3. Chapter 3Back home, Geppetto starts making the puppet at once and names him Pinocchio. The puppet’s first naughty tricks.
  4. Chapter 4The story of Pinocchio and the Talking Cricket, which shows how naughty children hate being corrected by those who know more than they do.
  5. Chapter 5Pinocchio is hungry and finds an egg to make an omelette; but just at the best moment, the omelette flies out of the window.
  6. Chapter 6Pinocchio falls asleep with his feet on the pot of coals, and next morning wakes up with his feet burned away.
  7. Chapter 7Geppetto comes home and gives the puppet the breakfast the poor man had brought for himself.
  8. Chapter 8Geppetto makes Pinocchio new feet and sells his own coat to buy him an ABC book.
  9. Chapter 9Pinocchio sells his ABC book to go and see the puppet theatre.
  10. Chapter 10The puppets recognise their brother Pinocchio and give him a wonderful welcome; but just at the best moment the puppet master Fire-Eater appears, and Pinocchio is in danger of a terrible end.
  11. Chapter 11Fire-Eater sneezes and forgives Pinocchio, who then saves his friend Harlequin from death.
  12. Chapter 12The puppet master Fire-Eater gives Pinocchio five gold coins to take to his dad Geppetto; but instead Pinocchio lets the Fox and the Cat trick him and goes off with them.
  13. Chapter 13The Red Crayfish Inn.
  14. Chapter 14Having ignored the Talking Cricket’s good advice, Pinocchio runs into the murderers.
  15. Chapter 15The murderers chase Pinocchio; and after catching him, they hang him from a branch of the Great Oak.
  16. Chapter 16The beautiful blue-haired Girl has the puppet brought in: she puts him to bed and calls three doctors to find out whether he is alive or dead.
  17. Chapter 17Pinocchio eats the sugar but will not take his medicine; when he sees the gravediggers coming to take him away, he takes it. Then he tells a lie, and his nose grows as punishment.
  18. Chapter 18Pinocchio meets the Fox and the Cat again and goes with them to plant the four coins in the Field of Miracles.
  19. Chapter 19Pinocchio is robbed of his gold coins, and as punishment gets four months in prison.
  20. Chapter 20Freed from prison, he sets off for the Fairy’s house; but along the way he meets a horrible snake, and then gets caught in a trap.
  21. Chapter 21Pinocchio is caught by a farmer, who forces him to act as watchdog for a henhouse.
  22. Chapter 22Pinocchio discovers the thieves and is set free as a reward for being faithful.
  23. Chapter 23Pinocchio mourns the death of the lovely Girl with the blue hair: then he meets a Pigeon who carries him to the seashore, where he jumps into the water to help his father Geppetto.
  24. Chapter 24Pinocchio reaches the island of the Busy Bees and finds the Fairy again.
  25. Chapter 25Pinocchio promises the Fairy to be good and to study, because he is tired of being a puppet and wants to become a good boy.
  26. Chapter 26Pinocchio goes to the seashore with his schoolmates to see the terrible Shark.
  27. Chapter 27A great fight breaks out between Pinocchio and his schoolmates: one of them is hurt, and Pinocchio is arrested by the police.
  28. Chapter 28Pinocchio is in danger of being fried in a pan like a fish.
  29. Chapter 29He returns to the Fairy’s house, and she promises that the next day he will no longer be a puppet but will become a boy. A grand breakfast of milky coffee is planned to celebrate this great event.
  30. Chapter 30Instead of becoming a boy, Pinocchio secretly leaves with his friend Candlewick for the Land of Toys.
  31. Chapter 31After five months of easy living, Pinocchio is amazed to find a fine pair of donkey ears growing, and becomes a little donkey, tail and all.
  32. Chapter 32Pinocchio grows donkey ears, then becomes a real little donkey and starts to bray.
  33. Chapter 33Now a real little donkey, he is taken to be sold, and the Director of a company of clowns buys him to teach him to dance and jump through hoops: but one evening he becomes lame, and another man buys him to make a drum from his skin.
  34. Chapter 34Thrown into the sea, Pinocchio is eaten by fish and becomes a puppet again: but as he swims to safety, he is swallowed by the terrible Shark.
  35. Chapter 35Inside the Shark, Pinocchio finds… who does he find? Read this chapter and you will know.
  36. Chapter 36At last Pinocchio stops being a puppet and becomes a boy.
