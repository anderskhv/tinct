Book 1

Modern English

Every state is a kind of community, and every community is established with a view to some good — for human beings always act in order to obtain what they take to be good. But if all communities aim at some good, then the state, or political community, which is the highest of all and embraces all the rest, aims at the highest good, and to a greater degree than any other.

Jowett (1885)

Every state is a community of some kind, and every community is established with a view to some good; for mankind always act in order to obtain that which they think good. But, if all communities aim at some good, the state or political community, which is the highest of all, and which embraces all the rest, aims, and in a greater degree than any other, at the highest good.
