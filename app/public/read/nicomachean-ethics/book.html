Book 1 — The Good for Man

Modern English

Every art and every systematic branch of knowledge, along with every action and deliberate choice, seems to aim at some good. This is why people often describe the highest good as "that at which all things aim."

Chase (1847) / Ross tradition

Every art, and every science reduced to a teachable form, and in like manner every action and moral choice, aims, it is thought, at some good: for which reason a common and by no means a bad description of the Chief Good is, “that which all things aim at.”

How this edition was made. Tinct's modern English is drafted with AI from D.P. Chase's public-domain translation (1847), then checked by a separate AI review against that translation. The original is one tap away, and if you spot a mistake, tell us.
