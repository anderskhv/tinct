Revelation — chapter by chapter

All 22 chapters — from the inaugural vision on Patmos to the closing benediction.

Revelation moves through a series of visions structured around sevens: seven churches, seven seals, seven trumpets, seven bowls. Read the whole thing through once before puzzling over the parts. The cumulative effect — not the individual symbol — is what the writer is after. Chapters 2–3 are the seven letters. Chapters 4–5 are the throne and the Lamb. Chapters 6–11 are the seals and the trumpets. Chapter 12 is the structural centre. Chapters 17–18 are Babylon's fall. Chapters 21–22 are the New Jerusalem.

Letters to the seven churches · 1–3

The inaugural vision. Seven tailored letters with praise, rebuke, and promise.

Revelation 1

Revelation 1

The risen Christ appears to John on Patmos in overwhelming glory — blazing eyes, feet like burnished bronze, a voice like many waters — and commissions the entire apocalypse.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb
Revelation 2

Revelation 2

Christ addresses Ephesus, Smyrna, Pergamum, and Thyatira — four distinct communities with four distinct situations, receiving precise praise, rebuke, and promise.

Appears: John of Patmos
Revelation 3

Revelation 3

Three more church letters: Sardis is called alive but dead, Philadelphia is given an open door, and lukewarm Laodicea hears the most memorable line — Christ knocking at a door.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb

The throne and the seals · 4–7

The heavenly throne room, the slain Lamb, and the opening of the six seals.

Revelation 4

Revelation 4

A door opens in heaven. John enters and sees the throne of God — jasper and carnelian, surrounded by twenty-four elders and four living creatures singing without ceasing.

Appears: John of Patmos
Revelation 5

Revelation 5

When no one can open the sealed scroll, John is told the lion of Judah has conquered — but what he sees is a slain lamb, and heaven erupts in worship.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb
Revelation 6

Revelation 6

The first six seals release the four horsemen — conquest, war, famine, death — then the martyrs' cry under the altar, and finally total cosmic collapse at the sixth seal.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb
Revelation 7

Revelation 7

An interlude between seals: 144,000 Israelites sealed on their foreheads, then a numberless multitude from all nations before the throne — the double portrait of the redeemed.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb

The trumpets · 8–11

The seventh seal, four trumpets, the abyss opened, and the two witnesses.

Revelation 8

Revelation 8

The seventh seal opens with half an hour of silence. Then four trumpets wound a third of the earth, sea, rivers, and sky — ecological judgment on a cosmic scale.

Appears: John of Patmos
Revelation 9

Revelation 9

Locusts from the abyss torment the unsealed for five months, then a two-hundred-million-strong cavalry kills a third of humanity — and still the survivors do not repent.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Dragon
Revelation 10

Revelation 10

A giant angel straddles sea and land; seven thunders speak but are sealed up; John eats a sweet-then-bitter scroll and is recommissioned to prophesy to the nations.

Appears: John of Patmos
Revelation 11

Revelation 11

Two witnesses prophesy and die, lie in the street three days, then rise and ascend to heaven — and the seventh trumpet declares the eternal kingdom has arrived.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Beast

The dragon and the beasts · 12–14

The structural centre. Woman, dragon, two beasts, the 144,000 on Zion.

Revelation 12

Revelation 12

The cosmic backstory of the book: the pregnant woman, the great red dragon, the birth of the male child, and the war in heaven that casts Satan to earth.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Dragon · The Lamb
Revelation 13

Revelation 13

The beast from the sea rules with the dragon's authority and blasphemes God for forty-two months; the beast from the land enforces its worship and institutes the mark.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Beast · The Dragon
Revelation 14

Revelation 14

The 144,000 worship on Zion; three angels declare judgment and Babylon's fall; then the earth is reaped — grain and grapes — and blood flows from the winepress for 1,600 stadia.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb · The Beast · The Whore of Babylon

The bowls · 15–16

The song of Moses, then seven bowls of wrath poured without pause.

Revelation 15

Revelation 15

The conquered-of-the-beast sing the song of Moses on a sea of glass and fire; the heavenly temple opens; seven angels emerge with the seven bowls of final wrath.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Beast
Revelation 16

Revelation 16

Seven bowls poured without pause: sores, bloody sea, bloody rivers, scorching sun, darkness, the Euphrates dried, and finally the greatest earthquake in human history.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Beast · The Dragon · The Whore of Babylon

Babylon's fall · 17–19

The whore on the scarlet beast, the great lament, the rider on the white horse.

Revelation 17

Revelation 17

An angel decodes the vision: the woman on seven hills is Babylon-Rome, her scarlet beast has seven kings and ten more to come, and the Lamb will conquer them all.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Whore of Babylon · The Beast · The Lamb
Revelation 18

Revelation 18

The fall of Babylon is sung as a dirge: kings, merchants, and sailors weep over the empire's ruin; heaven is told to rejoice; a millstone plunges into the sea.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Whore of Babylon
Revelation 19

Revelation 19

Hallelujah resounds in heaven over Babylon's fall; the Lamb's marriage supper is announced; then the rider on the white horse, Word of God, rides to his final victory.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb · The Beast · The Bride / New Jerusalem

The end · 20–22

The millennium, the final judgment, the New Jerusalem, the closing benediction.

Revelation 20

Revelation 20

Satan is bound for a thousand years; martyrs reign with Christ; then Satan is loosed, defeated, and cast into the lake of fire alongside Death and Hades at the great white throne.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Dragon · The Beast
Revelation 21

Revelation 21

A new heaven and earth; the New Jerusalem descends as a bride; God tabernacles with humanity; every tear is wiped away; the city is a perfect cube with no temple and no sun.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb · The Bride / New Jerusalem
Revelation 22

Revelation 22

The river of life flows from the throne; the tree of life heals the nations; the servants see God's face; and the book ends with the oldest Christian longing — come, Lord Jesus.

Appears: John of Patmos · The Lamb · The Bride / New Jerusalem
