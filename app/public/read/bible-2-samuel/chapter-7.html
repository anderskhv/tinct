2 Samuel 7

David proposes to build God a house; God proposes to build David one — and the word that turns on that pun carries the weight of two religions.

Summary

David is in his house of cedar and rests from his enemies round about. He says to Nathan the prophet that he is living in cedar while the ark of God dwells within curtains. Nathan, without consulting God, tells David to do all that is in his heart. But that night the word of the Lord comes to Nathan and tells him to correct his earlier answer. The Lord has not dwelt in a house since the day Israel came out of Egypt, has moved from tent to tent, and has never spoken a word to any of the judges he appointed asking why they had not built him a cedar house. He has taken David from the sheepfold, from following the sheep, to be ruler over his people Israel; has been with him wherever he went; has cut off all his enemies; has made him a great name. He will appoint a place for his people Israel and plant them so they are disturbed no more; neither will the wicked afflict them any more as they have done from the time of the judges.

The oracle turns: God tells David that he will make David a house. When David's days are fulfilled and he sleeps with his fathers, God will set up his seed after him, who will come from David's own bowels, and will establish his kingdom. That son shall build a house for God's name, and God will establish the throne of his kingdom forever. 'I will be his father, and he shall be my son. If he commit iniquity, I will chasten him with the rod of men and with the stripes of the children of men: but my mercy shall not depart away from him.' David's house, kingdom, and throne will be established forever before God. Nathan tells David all these words. David goes in, sits before the Lord, and prays: who is he, and what is his house, that God has brought him here? And yet, as if this were a small thing, God has also spoken of his servant's house for a great while to come. He asks God to do as he has promised, so that his name may be magnified forever.
