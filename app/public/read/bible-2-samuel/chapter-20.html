2 Samuel 20

Before David has returned fully to Jerusalem, another man blows a trumpet and declares a new rebellion.

Summary

There happens to be there a man of Belial, Sheba the son of Bichri a Benjaminite. He blows a trumpet and says: we have no part in David, neither have we inheritance in the son of Jesse; every man to his tents, O Israel. All the men of Israel go up from after David following Sheba; but the men of Judah cleave unto their king. David comes to his house at Jerusalem and takes the ten concubines he had left to keep the house and puts them in ward, feeding them but not going in unto them. They live in widowhood. He says to Amasa: assemble me the men of Judah within three days, and be thou here present. Amasa goes but tarries longer than the appointed time. David says to Abishai: now shall Sheba do us more harm than did Absalom; take thy lord's servants and pursue after him. Joab's men, the Cherethites and Pelethites, go out after Abishai and pursue Sheba. They come to the great stone of Gibeon. Amasa comes there also. Joab is girded with a sword in his hand; it falls out as he approaches Amasa. He takes Amasa by the beard with his right hand to kiss him and drives the sword into his belly, so that his bowels pour out to the ground. He dies.

Joab leads the pursuit north. One of Joab's men cries as they pass a certain man standing by the slain Amasa: he that favoureth Joab and he that is for David, let him follow Joab. He moves the body off the road and throws a garment over it; all the people go on after Joab to pursue Sheba. They come to Abel Beth-maacah and besiege the city, raising a ramp against it. A wise woman calls out from the wall: hear, hear! She says to Joab: we are peaceable and faithful in Israel; seek you to destroy a mother in Israel? Joab answers that he seeks only Sheba the son of Bichri who has lifted up his hand against the king David; deliver him, and he will depart from the city. The woman says: his head shall be thrown to thee over the wall. She speaks to all the people in her wisdom; they cut off Sheba's head and throw it to Joab. He blows the trumpet; the men scatter from the city. Joab returns to Jerusalem. The chapter closes with the restored administrative list: Joab over the host, Benaiah over the Cherethites and Pelethites, Adoram over the tribute, Jehoshaphat the recorder, Sheva the scribe, Zadok and Abiathar the priests, Ira the Jairite a chief ruler about David.
