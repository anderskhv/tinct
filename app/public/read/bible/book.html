There was evening, and there was morning — the first day.

⁶ God said, "Let there be an expanse in the middle of the waters to separate water from water." ⁷ So God made the expanse and separated the water under the expanse from the water above it. And it was so. ⁸ God called the expanse "Sky." There was evening, and there was morning — the second day. ⁹ God said, "Let the water under the sky be gathered to one place and let dry ground appear." And it was so. ¹⁰ God called the dry ground "Land," and the gathered waters he called "Seas." And God saw that it was good.

¹⁶ God made two great lights — the greater light to govern the day and the lesser light to govern the night.
