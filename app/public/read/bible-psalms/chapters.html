Psalms — chapter by chapter

All 150 psalms, arranged in the Psalter's five ancient books.

The Psalter is not a narrative. It is an anthology — the same speakers, the same God, the same range of address, but the occasions are particular and varied. The five books within it each end with a doxology. Book 1 (Psalms 1–41) contains most of the Davidic laments. Book 2 (42–72) closes with the note "the prayers of David are ended." Book 3 (73–89) is dominated by Asaph and the Korahites. Book 4 (90–106) opens with Moses's psalm and contains the great enthronement psalms. Book 5 (107–150) closes with the Songs of Ascents, the Hallel psalms, and the five final doxologies of pure praise.
