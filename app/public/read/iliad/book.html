Modern English

Agamemnon and Achilles quarrel—Achilles withdraws from the war and sends his mother Thetis to ask Zeus to help the Trojans—Zeus and Hera have a scene on Olympus.

Butler (1898)

The quarrel between Agamemnon and Achilles—Achilles withdraws from the war, and sends his mother Thetis to ask Jove to help the Trojans—Scene between Jove and Juno on Olympus.
