Treasure Island — chapter by chapter

All 34 chapters — from the old sea-dog at the Admiral Benbow to the gold loaded aboard the Hispaniola.

Treasure Island divides into six parts of unequal length. Part One (Ch. 1–6) is the Admiral Benbow and the treasure map. Part Two (Ch. 7–12) is Bristol, the ship, and the discovery of the mutiny. Part Three (Ch. 13–20) is the first days on the island — the stockade, the first battle, Ben Gunn. Part Four (Ch. 21–24) is the stockade attack and Jim's sea adventure. Part Five (Ch. 25–30) is the island's second half — Jim aboard the Hispaniola alone, then Silver's camp. Part Six (Ch. 31–34) is the treasure hunt and the ending. The pace accelerates in the final third: Stevenson designed it to move.

Part One · The Admiral Benbow

A sailor arrives, a chest is opened, a boy finds a map.

Chapter 1

The Old Sea-dog at the Admiral Benbow

A scarred, heavy sailor arrives at the Admiral Benbow and stays — watching the road, drinking rum, singing "Fifteen men on the dead man's chest," and paying Jim a silver fourpenny to watch for a seafaring man with one leg. He terrorizes the inn for months.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Billy Bones
Chapter 2

Black Dog Appears and Disappears

A pale ex-pirate named Black Dog visits the Admiral Benbow looking for Billy Bones. They argue behind closed doors; Black Dog flees with a wound; Bones collapses in the road from a stroke. Dr. Livesey warns him off rum. Bones ignores the warning.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Billy Bones · Dr. Livesey
Chapter 3

The Black Spot

The blind man Pew arrives, forces Jim to guide him, slips the black spot into Billy Bones's hand. Bones has six hours to produce "the thing." He tells Jim it's a map — then dies of heart failure on the floor.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Billy Bones
Chapter 4

The Sea-chest

Jim and his mother open the sea-chest by candlelight, take what Bones owed them in coin and the sealed packet from the bottom. They hear Pew's stick on the road and run just before the pirates arrive.

Appears: Jim Hawkins
Chapter 5

The Last of the Blind Man

Revenue riders scatter the pirates. Pew is abandoned by his men and killed by a horse on the road. Jim gives the sealed packet to Mr. Dance, who takes Jim to the squire and Dr. Livesey.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Dr. Livesey
Chapter 6

The Captain's Papers

Livesey opens the packet: inside is Flint's treasure map, with three red crosses and a note marking the main hoard. The squire immediately plans the expedition and promises secrecy he will demonstrably not keep.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Dr. Livesey · Squire Trelawney

Part Two · Bristol and the Hispaniola

The expedition assembled, the crew hired, the mutiny discovered.

Chapter 7

I Go to Bristol

The squire's letter arrives announcing the Hispaniola is ready. Jim travels to Bristol. The squire has hired the crew with the "invaluable help" of Silver — though the squire doesn't present it in those terms.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Squire Trelawney
Chapter 8

At the Sign of the Spy-glass

Jim meets Long John Silver — charming, one-legged, instantly warm. Then he spots Black Dog from the Admiral Benbow sitting in the corner. Silver chases Black Dog out and explains him away so fluently that Jim's suspicions disappear.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver
Chapter 9

Powder and Arms

Captain Smollett boards the Hispaniola already angry: the ship's destination is common knowledge among the crew, who were not his choice. He demands the arms and powder be secured under the cabin. The squire is furious. Livesey is quietly impressed.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Captain Smollett · Squire Trelawney · Dr. Livesey · Long John Silver
Chapter 10

The Voyage

The Hispaniola is at sea. Silver is the voyage's social center — managing the crew, cooking, telling stories. Jim knows something is wrong but cannot hold onto the knowledge in Silver's presence. The useless mate disappears overboard.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver · Captain Smollett
Chapter 11

What I Heard in the Apple-Barrel

Jim hides in an apple barrel and hears Silver explain the full plan to two crew members: kill the honest party after the treasure is loaded, take the gold, sail home free. Silver is Flint's old quartermaster. This is his last operation.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver · Israel Hands
Chapter 12

Council of War

Jim tells the captain and the squire everything heard in the apple barrel. They count loyal men: seven against nineteen. The captain's plan is to let the pirates go ashore and act from there.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Captain Smollett · Dr. Livesey · Squire Trelawney

Part Three · The Island

The stockade, Ben Gunn, the first battle.

Chapter 13

How I Began My Shore Adventure

Jim jumps into a pirate boat on impulse and runs into the jungle when they beach. He explores alone. Silver, nearby, murders a loyal sailor who won't join the mutiny — the first death on the island.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver
Chapter 14

The First Blow

Jim crosses the island interior alone. A scream from the shore — another loyal sailor killed. He presses on. Running back to the ship is not possible; running forward is all that is left.

Appears: Jim Hawkins
Chapter 15

The Man of the Island

A wild sun-blackened man in goatskins — Ben Gunn — cuts Jim off in the trees and falls on his knees. He has been on the island three years, marooned by Captain Flint. He wants cheese and says he has been doing something useful with his time.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Ben Gunn
Chapter 16

Narrative Continued by the Doctor: How the Ship Was Abandoned

Dr. Livesey's narration. The shots from shore confirm murders. The honest party abandons the Hispaniola and rows to the island with arms and supplies to make for the stockade.

Appears: Dr. Livesey · Captain Smollett · Squire Trelawney
Chapter 17

Narrative Continued by the Doctor: The Jolly-boat's Last Trip

The jolly-boat reaches shore under cannon fire. The honest party gains the stockade. The doctor notes Jim's absence without comment.

Appears: Dr. Livesey · Captain Smollett · Squire Trelawney
Chapter 18

Narrative Continued by the Doctor: End of the First Day's Fighting

The pirates attack the stockade. One of the honest party is killed. The stockade holds. The doctor's narration ends here and Jim's voice returns in Chapter 19.

Appears: Dr. Livesey · Captain Smollett
Chapter 19

Narrative Resumed by Jim Hawkins: The Garrison in the Stockade

Jim reaches the stockade and is reunited with the honest party. He tells them about Ben Gunn. Captain Smollett, wounded, makes a note of his good service and then gives him watch duty.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Captain Smollett · Dr. Livesey · Squire Trelawney · Ben Gunn
Chapter 20

Silver's Embassy

Silver approaches under a white flag and offers terms: chart for safety. Captain Smollett refuses everything. Silver is polite about the refusal and leaves.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver · Captain Smollett
Chapter 21

The Attack

The pirates attack in force. Three on the honest side are killed; five pirates are killed. The stockade holds. Smollett is wounded but the position is secure.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Captain Smollett · Dr. Livesey · Squire Trelawney

Part Four · Jim's Sea Adventure

Jim cuts the Hispaniola adrift, fights Israel Hands, walks into Silver's camp.

Chapter 22

How I Began My Sea Adventure

Jim slips away from the stockade without permission. He finds Ben Gunn's coracle and plans to cut the Hispaniola adrift so the pirates cannot use her.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Ben Gunn
Chapter 23

The Ebb-tide Runs

The coracle proves unsteerable. Jim reaches the Hispaniola by tide-drift alone, cuts the anchor cable, and clings to the drifting ship in the darkness.

Appears: Jim Hawkins
Chapter 24

The Cruise of the Coracle

Jim wakes adrift off the southwest tip of the island in the coracle. He drifts all morning. Eventually wind changes and he paddles desperately to shore. He can see the Hispaniola, also drifting and in trouble.

Appears: Jim Hawkins
Chapter 25

I Strike the Jolly Roger

Jim boards the Hispaniola and finds Hands wounded and the other man dead. He hauls down the Jolly Roger. He and Hands negotiate: Jim will sail if Hands guides him.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Israel Hands
Chapter 26

Israel Hands

Hands has been crawling toward a hidden knife all morning. He charges Jim up the rigging. Jim shoots him at the crosstrees; Hands falls into the sea. The dirk pins Jim to the mast.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Israel Hands
Chapter 27

"Pieces of Eight"

Jim reaches the stockade at night and climbs in. A voice speaks in the dark. It is Silver's parrot. The pirates have taken the stockade. Jim is Silver's prisoner.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver
Chapter 28

In the Enemy's Camp

Jim is Silver's prisoner in the stockade. Silver protects him from the crew and explains his situation candidly: between the honest party's deal and the crew's impatience. Jim tells him everything about the sea adventure.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver

Part Five · Silver's Parole

The doctor's deal, the black spot, Jim on parole with Silver.

Chapter 29

The Black Spot Again

The crew delivers the black spot to Silver — four charges of dereliction. Silver refutes each one, produces the chart, and is reinstated by vote. Jim watches Silver manage his crew and cannot quite disapprove.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver
Chapter 30

On Parole

Dr. Livesey treats the sick pirates. Silver gives him a private word with Jim. Livesey tells Jim Ben Gunn's information has changed everything — and to stay with Silver.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver · Dr. Livesey
Chapter 31

The Treasure-hunt--Flint's Pointer

Silver leads the treasure hunt. They find a skeleton pointing northwest — Flint's signpost. The pirates are frightened. Silver ignores the fear and keeps moving toward the cache.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver
Chapter 32

The Treasure-hunt--The Voice Among the Trees

A voice from the trees sings Flint's song and names the pirates' dead. The pirates are frozen with fear. Silver identifies it as a trick and marches on. We know it is Ben Gunn.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver · Ben Gunn

Part Six · The End

The treasure hunt, the reversal, the gold, Silver's disappearance.

Chapter 33

The Fall of a Chieftain

The treasure cache is empty. The crew turns on Silver. The honest party arrives from the trees and shoots three of the most dangerous pirates. Silver surrenders.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver · Dr. Livesey · Squire Trelawney · Ben Gunn
Chapter 34

And Last

The gold is loaded. Silver escapes in a Spanish American port with a portion of the treasure. Jim, home in England, has nightmares and will not go back to sea. Silver he does not expect to see again — and, in some part of himself, is glad of it.

Appears: Jim Hawkins · Long John Silver · Dr. Livesey · Squire Trelawney · Ben Gunn
