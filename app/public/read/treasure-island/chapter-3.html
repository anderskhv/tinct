Summary

A few days after Jim's father's death, a blind man arrives at the Admiral Benbow. He is dressed as a beggar, feels his way with a tapping stick, and speaks politely. He asks Jim to guide him to the captain. Jim, frightened and unable to explain why he is frightened, does so. The blind man moves with such precision that Jim understands he was not always blind, or not always as helpless as he appears.

The blind man — Pew, though Jim doesn't know his name — grabs Bones's wrist and presses something into his palm with sudden force. "There is your black spot," he says. Bones opens his hand: a circle of black on one side, and on the other, written in a rough hand: "You have till ten tonight." It is six o'clock. Bones has four hours. He tells Jim, urgently, that he must call the captain of the squire's vessels and get help, and that the thing they want is "a chest" — specifically "the map."

Bones tries to rise from his bed. He does not make it. He falls across the floor and is dead before Jim can help him. The black spot has killed him as surely as if it had been a bullet, though the immediate cause is his heart — the second stroke Dr. Livesey predicted. Jim is now alone in the inn with the body of a dead pirate, a deadline in four hours, and the knowledge that other pirates are coming for something that is somewhere on the premises.
