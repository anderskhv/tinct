Book One — The Ingmarssons

Modern English

A young farmer was plowing his field one summer morning. The sun shone, the grass sparkled with dew, and the air was so light and bracing that words could not describe it. The horses were frisky from the morning air and pulled the plow along as if in play. They moved at a pace quite different from their usual gait. The man had to practically run to keep up with them.

Howard (1915)

A young farmer was plowing his field one summer morning. The sun shone, the grass sparkled with dew, and the air was so light and bracing that no words can describe it. The horses were frisky from the morning air, and pulled the plow along as if in play. They were going at a pace quite different from their usual gait; the man had fairly to run to keep up with them.
