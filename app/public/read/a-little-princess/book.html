Sara

Modern English

One dark winter day, a little girl with an unusual face was riding slowly along London's broad streets in a cab beside her father. Yellow fog lay so thick and heavy over the city that the street lamps had been lit, and gaslight shone from the shop windows just as it did at night.

Original (1905)

Once on a dark winter's day, when the yellow fog hung so thick and heavy in the streets of London that the lamps were lighted and the shop windows blazed with gas as they do at night, an odd-looking little girl sat in a cab with her father and was driven rather slowly through the big thoroughfares.
