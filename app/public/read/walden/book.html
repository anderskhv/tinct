Economy

Modern English

When I wrote these pages, or most of them, I lived by myself in the woods, a mile from my nearest neighbor, in a house I had built with my own hands beside Walden Pond in Concord, Massachusetts. I supported myself entirely by manual labor. I stayed there for two years and two months. For now, I am once again a temporary resident of civilized life.

Thoreau (1854)

When I wrote the following pages, or rather the bulk of them, I lived alone, in the woods, a mile from any neighbor, in a house which I had built myself, on the shore of Walden Pond, in Concord, Massachusetts, and earned my living by the labor of my hands only. I lived there two years and two months. At present I am a sojourner in civilized life again.
