Dedication

Modern English

You've come again, you hovering shapes—I see you now, just as you first appeared to my clouded eyes back then. Should I try, this time, to catch and hold you fast? Does that old illusion still have such a grip on my heart? You're crowding closer now. Then let your reign be granted—sway me out of your misty, shadowy realm! My chest is trembling with a youthful passion, stirred by the magic breezes that rise around your approach.

Bayard Taylor (1870)

Again ye come, ye hovering Forms! I find ye, As early to my clouded sight ye shone! Shall I attempt, this once, to seize and bind ye? Still o’er my heart is that illusion thrown? Ye crowd more near! Then, be the reign assigned ye, And sway me from your misty, shadowy zone! My bosom thrills, with youthful passion shaken, From magic airs that round your march awaken.
