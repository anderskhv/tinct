Cathedral of 28

Cathedral

Gretchen at mass. The Evil Spirit whispers behind her as the choir sings the Dies Irae. She faints.

Summary

A cathedral, a solemn Mass. Gretchen has come in — perhaps for a requiem, perhaps simply because she goes to church, as she always has. The choir is singing the Dies Irae, the Latin hymn of divine judgment: day of wrath, that day shall dissolve the world in ashes. The organ sounds.

The Evil Spirit is standing behind her, unseen. It speaks into her ear: how different it was, Gretchen, when you stepped here to the altar as an innocent, stammering prayers from your worn little prayer-book. Half child's-play, half God in your heart. Where is your head now? In your heart, what crime? Do you pray for the soul of your mother? She has slept into long, long torture from the drops you gave her. Whose blood is on your doorstep? The choir sings: judgment, revelation, nothing left unavenged.

Gretchen tries to respond to the hymn but the words collapse. The walls press in. The pillars hold her fast. The vault presses down. She needs air. The Evil Spirit continues: wrath seizes you, the trumpet sounds, the graves tremble. Gretchen is on the verge of fainting. A neighbor asks if she is unwell. She falls.
