Tablet I

Modern English

Let the one who has tested the heart of all things teach the nation. Let the one who holds all knowledge school all the people; let him pass on his wisdom so that they share it together. Gilgamesh was that master of wisdom, who knew all things. He was the one who uncovered what was hidden. He handed down the tradition of the days before the Flood. He went on a far journey, came back weary and worn by his labour, and carved the whole of his hardship on a tablet of stone.

Thompson (1928)
