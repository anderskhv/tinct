Hosea — chapter by chapter

All 14 chapters — from the marriage command to the closing call to return.

Hosea divides naturally into three movements: the marriage allegory (chapters 1–3), the great indictment (chapters 4–10), and the turn toward love and restoration (chapters 11–14). Chapter 11 is the book's astonishing centre. Chapter 14 closes the book by addressing the reader directly.

The Marriage Allegory · Chapters 1–3

The marriage to Gomer, the three named children, the buying-back.

Hosea 1

The Marriage as Living Parable

A prophet marries and names his children after disaster — three names that become, by the chapter's end, the vocabulary of reversal.

Appears: Hosea · Gomer, daughter of Diblaim · The Three Children · Yahweh
Hosea 2

Allure Her Into the Wilderness

A lawsuit over misdirected gifts becomes a wilderness courtship — the covenant stripped away and then renewed under new names.

Appears: Hosea · Gomer, daughter of Diblaim · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
Hosea 3

Buy Her Back for Fifteen Pieces of Silver

A five-verse purchase of a wife at market price carries the whole book's theology of love that costs the lover rather than the beloved.

Appears: Hosea · Gomer, daughter of Diblaim · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh

The Indictment · Chapters 4–10

No knowledge of God, no truth, no mercy — priests, princes, and kings under judgment.

Hosea 4

No Knowledge of God in the Land

The priests who grew fat on the people's sin become the face of a failed covenant in which the knowing of God has simply stopped.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh · The Prophets as Class
Hosea 5

Ephraim Shall Be Desolate

From priests to princes: the indictment spreads through every layer of Israelite leadership, and the LORD prepares to withdraw like a lion to its lair.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
Hosea 6

I Desire Mercy, Not Sacrifice

Israel's liturgically correct repentance speech is answered with a question: your love is like the morning dew — what am I supposed to do with that?

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
Hosea 7

Ephraim Is a Half-Baked Cake

Three vivid images — the half-baked cake, the silly dove, the treacherous bow — map a kingdom that has lost the self-knowledge to diagnose its own ruin.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
Hosea 8

They Sow the Wind

Altars built for sin, calves crafted by human hands, and foreign lovers hired for silver — Israel has sown the wind and will reap the whirlwind.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
Hosea 9

The Prophet Called a Fool

Feast turned to famine, prophet named fool: the chapter locates the watchman inside the ruin he is watching and finds he is not exempt from it.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh · The Prophets as Class
Hosea 10

Israel Is a Luxuriant Vine

A vine that grew prosperous and spent its fruit on more altars — the chapter traces how religious infrastructure multiplies precisely when covenant loyalty collapses.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh

Love and Return · Chapters 11–14

How can I give you up, Ephraim? The parent who will not let go, and the closing call.

Hosea 11

How Can I Give You Up, Ephraim?

The parent who taught Ephraim to walk cannot give him up — and says so in the middle of the judgment, without resolving the tension between them.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
Hosea 12

Jacob Fled to the Field of Aram

Jacob wrestled with God at Bethel and prevailed — and Bethel is now Beth-aven, the house of vanity, and the wrestling has become mere commerce with Assyria.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh · The Prophets as Class
Hosea 13

I Will Be Their King: Where Now Is Your King?

The darkest chapter in Hosea sweeps through images of drought, beasts, and collapsed kingship before challenging Death itself — in a verse that will echo in Paul's letters.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
Hosea 14

Return, O Israel, to the LORD

Words offered as sacrifice, dew as the divine presence, a vine restored — and then the book hands itself directly to the reader and asks which kind of walker they are.

Appears: Hosea · Israel / Ephraim · Yahweh
