Ezekiel — chapter by chapter

All 48 chapters, from the chariot to the city named "The LORD Is There"

Ezekiel divides into three movements. Chapters 1–24 are oracles of judgement against Jerusalem and Judah, delivered before the city's fall. Chapters 25–32 are oracles against the foreign nations — Ammon, Moab, Edom, Philistia, Tyre, Sidon, Egypt. Chapters 33–48 are oracles of restoration: the watchman recommissioned, the dry bones, the war of Gog, and the long visionary tour of the new temple and the river of life.

Ezekiel 28

Ezekiel 30

Ezekiel 30

Lament over Egypt as a Day of the Lord oracle: the cities desolated from north to south, and the arms of Pharaoh broken by Nebuchadnezzar's sword.

Appears: Ezekiel

Ezekiel 31

An allegory of the cedar of Lebanon: Pharaoh is invited to see what happened to Assyria, that great tree felled and cast into Sheol, and to understand his own coming fall.

Appears: Ezekiel
Ezekiel 32

Ezekiel 32

Two laments close the Egyptian oracles: Pharaoh brought down from young lion to dragon to the pit, and the underworld vision where Egypt lies with Assyria, Elam, and all the fallen empires.
