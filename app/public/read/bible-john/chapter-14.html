John 14

I am the way, and the truth, and the life — and the promise of the Holy Spirit as another Paraclete, a helper who will come when Jesus is gone.

Summary

Let not your hearts be troubled; believe in God, believe also in me. In my Father's house are many rooms. If it were not so, would I have told you that I go to prepare a place for you? And if I go and prepare a place for you, I will come again and will take you to myself, that where I am you may be also. Thomas interrupts: Lord, we do not know where you are going. How can we know the way? Jesus answers I am the way, and the truth, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through me. If you had known me, you would have known my Father also. From now on you do know him and have seen him. Philip asks to be shown the Father; Jesus says have I been with you so long and you still do not know me? Whoever has seen me has seen the Father. He is in the Father and the Father is in him; the words he says are not his own.

Jesus promises that whatever the disciples ask in his name he will do, so the Father may be glorified in the Son. If you love me, keep my commandments. He will ask the Father, who will give them another Helper — the paraklētos, the Spirit of truth — to be with them forever. The world cannot receive him because it neither sees nor knows him; the disciples know him because he dwells with them and will be in them. Jesus will come back to them; they will see him; they will live because he lives. On that day they will know he is in the Father and they are in him and he in them. He who has his commandments and keeps them is the one who loves him; the Father will love that person, and Jesus will love him and manifest himself to him. Judas (not Iscariot) asks how he will manifest himself to them and not to the world; the answer is the commandment of love and the promise of the Spirit. Peace I leave with you; my peace I give to you; not as the world gives do I give to you. Let not your hearts be troubled or afraid. You heard me say I am going away and coming to you. If you loved me, you would have rejoiced, because I am going to the Father, for the Father is greater than I.
