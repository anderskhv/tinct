Judges 18 of 21

Judges 18

The tribe of Dan, unable to secure their territory in the lowlands, sends spies north, steals Micah's idols and Levite, and takes the city of Laish by force.

Summary

In those days there was no king in Israel. The tribe of Dan is seeking an inheritance to live in, for until that day no inheritance had fallen to them among the tribes of Israel. The Danites send five valiant men from their clans to spy out the land and explore it. They pass through the hill country of Ephraim to the house of Micah and stay there. They recognize the voice of the young Levite. They ask him: who brought you here? And is your being here good for us? He tells them about his arrangement with Micah. They ask him to inquire of God whether their journey will succeed. The priest says: go in peace; the journey you are on is before the Lord. The five spies go on to Laish, which is a city whose people live in security, after the manner of the Sidonians, quiet and unsuspecting, with no one in the land to abase them or to dispossess them. They return and report: arise, and let us go up against them. For we have seen the land, and it is very good. And will you do nothing?

Six hundred men armed for war set out from the clans of Dan. They camp at Kiriath-jearim and go up into the hill country of Ephraim, coming to the house of Micah. The five spies tell their companions about the shrine. They turn aside, enter the house of the young Levite, and take the carved image, the ephod, the household gods, and the metal image. The Levite says: what are you doing? They say: come with us and be our father and priest. Is it better for you to be priest to the house of one man, or to be priest to a tribe and clan in Israel? The Levite's heart is glad; he takes the ephod, the household gods, and the carved image and goes with them. When Micah and his neighbours pursue and complain, the Danites say: do not let your voice be heard among us, or else angry fellows will attack you. Micah turns and goes home. The Danites take Laish, strike it with the sword, and burn it. They rebuild it and call it Dan, after the name of their ancestor. They set up Micah's carved image; Jonathan the son of Gershom, son of Moses, and his sons are priests to the tribe of Dan until the day of the captivity of the land.
