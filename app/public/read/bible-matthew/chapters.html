Matthew — chapter by chapter

All 28 chapters — from the genealogy of Abraham to the Great Commission on the mountain in Galilee.

Matthew is structured around five great discourses and the narrative that connects them. Chapters 1–2 are the infancy narratives. Chapters 3–9 are the Galilean ministry, culminating in the Sermon on the Mount (5–7). Chapters 10–12 are the missionary discourse and the first controversies. Chapter 13 is the parables of the kingdom. Chapters 14–17 are the Galilean withdrawal and the feeding miracles. Chapter 16 is Peter’s confession and the rock saying. Chapters 18–20 are the community discourse and the journey to Jerusalem. Chapters 21–25 are Jerusalem: the entry, the temple, the seven woes (23), and the eschatological discourse (24–25). Chapters 26–27 are the Passion. Chapter 28 is the resurrection and the Great Commission.
