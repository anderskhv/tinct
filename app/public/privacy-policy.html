Privacy Policy

Tinct — tinct.app
Last updated: March 25, 2026

Tinct is a small, independent tool built by a single person in Copenhagen, Denmark. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and what rights you have. It's written to be read, not to be scrolled past.

What we store

  • Your email address — to create your account and let you sign in.
  • Your content — highlights, notes, reading progress, and conversations with the AI reading companion.
  • Usage data — which pages you visit and which features you use. This helps us improve the product.

What we don't do

  • We never sell your data to anyone.
  • We never share your personal information with third parties.
  • We never use your content to train AI models. Neither does Anthropic — see below.
  • We don't run ads.

Who can see your data

Only the founder has access to the database, for operational purposes — fixing bugs, understanding how features are used, and keeping the service running.

AI features and your data

AI features are powered by Anthropic's Claude. When you use them, your content and messages are sent to Anthropic's API for processing.

Anthropic does not use API data to train their models. They retain inputs and outputs for up to 30 days for trust and safety purposes, then delete them. See Anthropic's privacy policy for details.

How we protect your data

Your data is stored on Supabase (hosted in the EU) with row-level security — meaning each user can only access their own data. All connections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

Your rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access — ask for a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Delete — ask us to delete your account and all associated data.
  • Export — ask for your data in a portable format.
  • Correct — ask us to fix any inaccurate data.
  • Withdraw consent — for optional things like email contact, you can change your mind at any time.

To exercise any of these rights, email contact@tinct.app.

Email contact

If you opted in during signup, the founder may occasionally reach out with a personal email — not marketing, not newsletters, just a human checking in. You can opt out at any time by replying to any such email or by emailing contact@tinct.app.

Cookies

We don't use cookies. Your session is stored in your browser's local storage. We don't use tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we'll let you know via the email address on your account. Minor wording changes won't be notified.

Questions? Email contact@tinct.app.
