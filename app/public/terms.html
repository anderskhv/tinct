1. The service

If a change materially reduces what you've paid for, we'll tell you in advance and you may cancel.

2. Who can use Tinct

Accounts: you need to be at least 13 to create an account. If you're under 18, a parent or guardian must agree to these terms with you.

3. Subscriptions, AI use and payment

Subscriptions are billed in advance through Stripe and renew automatically until you cancel.

Premium includes a monthly amount of AI use, shown as a bar in the app. It resets on your billing date and doesn't roll over. When it runs out, your account works like a standard account until the next billing date: you can still read and listen, and AI chat, Talk and recaps pause. You can buy a top-up to keep using them; top-ups carry over while your subscription is active.

Top-ups have no cash value and can't be transferred or refunded, except where the law requires it.

We'll give at least 30 days' notice by email before a price change.

EU right of withdrawal: you normally have 14 days to withdraw. When you start using Premium or a top-up, you ask us to begin providing it straight away. You lose the right of withdrawal for content that has been fully delivered, and a withdrawal from a service already in progress may be reduced in proportion to what you've used.
