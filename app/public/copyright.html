Tinct copyright

The books

Every book in the Tinct library is in the public domain in the European Union, or is a new translation made by Tinct from a public-domain original. Our Credits page says where the original texts come from.

Where you are matters

Copyright terms differ between countries. Where a book is still protected, Tinct doesn't offer it there. The Master and Margarita, for example, isn't available in the United States or Russia.

Tinct's own work

Modern editions, translations, introductions, narration, design and software belong to Tinct.

Your own books

Files you upload stay private to your account. Tinct never publishes, shares or offers them for download, and other readers can't see them. You confirm you have the right to use what you upload (Terms).

Report a problem

If you believe something on Tinct infringes your rights, write to contact@tinct.app with the work, where it appears, and your contact details. A person reviews every report, and we remove material that infringes.
