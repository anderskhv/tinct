One Tinct, every screen

These tokens and components are lifted from what production already does best: the library's dark room and its pills, the reader's papers and v2 sheets. A new screen links this file and uses tui- classes instead of writing its own buttons, dialogs or colours.

Link: <link rel="stylesheet" href="/lab/tinct-ui.css?v=20261008ds">. Theme: data-tui-theme="light|book|dark|eink" on <html> or on any element. Rules for agents: docs/design-system.md.

Tokens

Colour

Four themes, the same token names. Dark is the library's green-black room; light and book are the reader's papers; e-ink is pure black on white with no shadows or motion. The deep teal accent is for links, switches and focus on paper, never for a large fill.

Usage

Rules

Fonts per role

  • Playfair Display: page, dialog and sheet titles. Nothing else.
  • EB Garamond: book text, book titles, list titles, dialog body.
  • System UI: every button, chip, input, meta line and shelf head.
  • IBM Plex Mono: figures (percent, chapter, speed, time, counts).

Buttons

  • All buttons are pills; no square or 5px corners.
  • One primary per surface; Cancel is secondary, to its left.
  • Sentence case, no tracking, never mono, never capitals.
  • 44px tall or a 44px tap area.

Overlays

  • A decision: tui-dialog. Settings or a list: tui-sheet.
  • A dialog over the library carries data-tui-theme="dark".
  • Escape and the scrim close; focus returns to the opener.

Colour

  • Use tokens, never hex, in new CSS.
  • Teal accent for links, switches and focus only.
  • E-ink: no shadows, no motion, no blur, borders instead.
