# Author-flap image handoff

29 September 2026. **63 downloaded and visually checked images cover all 101 registered books.** Every named author has a historical portrait or a clearly labelled traditional depiction. Five anonymous or collective entries use manuscript, tablet or early printed-book images. Marx and Engels have separate credited portraits; The Federalist Papers has portraits of Hamilton, Madison and Jay.

## Integration

Use `author-images.json`: `books` maps exact catalogue IDs to one or more image IDs; `images` supplies local asset paths, source and licence links, creator credits, captions, alt text and file hashes. Serve the assets locally. The existing six author portraits are represented using the same source files; match by author and reuse them where appropriate.

Display the depiction label where an image is traditional, posthumous or disputed. A Bible or manuscript illustration must be labelled as a collection image, never as its author's portrait. For coauthored works, present both/all named portraits together with their names rather than silently choosing one author.

Retain the credit line, clickable source and exact licence in an accessible image-credit view. For CC BY-SA images, retain the specified licence and apply it to any image adaptations; record crops or other changes. The image licence does not automatically apply to unrelated site code or prose. CC0 and public-domain images are also credited for provenance. No noncommercial-only or no-derivatives licence is used. Licence statements and original metadata are preserved under `evidence/`.

Policy source: [Wikimedia Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia). Rights were assessed per file, not inferred from the age of the depicted person or the site's name. Source declarations are preserved; this is a documented reuse assessment, not a claim of a single copyright rule worldwide.

The contact sheets are inspection aids only. Use files under `assets/` in the product. No live application files have been changed here.
