Howard Pyle (1853–1911) wrote and illustrated this retelling of the Arthurian legend, published in 1903. It follows Arthur from the sword in the stone to the founding of the Round Table, then tells the stories of Merlin, Sir Pellias and Sir Gawaine.

Pyle drew on Malory and the older romances and told them in his own old-fashioned voice. This edition keeps the story, names and episodes, and renders the language in clear modern English.
