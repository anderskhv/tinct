Frances Hodgson Burnett (1849–1924) published The Secret Garden in 1911. Mary Lennox, a sour and neglected child, is sent from India to her uncle’s great house on the Yorkshire moor. There she finds a garden that has been locked for ten years, a servant boy who understands animals, and a hidden cousin who believes he is dying.

The book follows the garden’s return to life and the children’s with it. This edition keeps Burnett’s story and voice in clear modern English.
