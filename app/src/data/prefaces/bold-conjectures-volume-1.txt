Bold Conjectures, Volume I collects transcripts of fourteen interviews with the Oxford physicist and philosopher David Deutsch, recorded between 2012 and 2024 and edited by Logan Chipkin. The interviewers include Robert Lawrence Kuhn of Closer To Truth, the economist Tyler Cowen, the physicist Sean Carroll, the investor Naval Ravikant, the science journalist John Horgan and the philosopher Peter Boghossian, along with podcasters and students of Deutsch's work.

Each transcript opens with a table of episode details, usually with a short list of the main ideas and a list of topics, and then gives the conversation with timestamps. The subjects range across Deutsch's two books, The Fabric of Reality and The Beginning of Infinity: the many-worlds interpretation of quantum theory, constructor theory, Karl Popper's theory of knowledge, explanation and truth, artificial general intelligence, free will, optimism, education and Taking Children Seriously, and politics.

The interviews run roughly in date order, so the volume shows Deutsch's positions being tested against very different interlocutors. Chipkin's preface warns that these are transcriptions of unscripted speech and that some of Deutsch's views, for instance on free will, time travel and constructor theory, have since changed. Read the chapters in any order, as a companion to Deutsch's books or as an introduction to them.
