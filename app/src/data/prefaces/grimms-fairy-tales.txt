Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm gathered these tales from oral sources and published them in German in 1812 and 1814. This English text is based on the translations of Edgar Taylor and Marian Edwardes and presents 65 tales as separate reading units, from the golden apples of the opening story to Snow-White and Rose-Red at the close.

The stories are short, direct and repeated in patterns. A king loses golden apples, a poor man trades a lump of gold for a horse, a fisherman’s wife keeps asking for more. Things come in threes, rhymes recur word for word, and helpers appear to those who treat them well. Some tales are comic, some are cruel, and a few end without a clear moral. The narrator rarely explains.

You can read the tales in any order or straight through. Familiar titles such as Hansel and Gretel, Rapunzel, Little Red-Cap and Briar Rose sit beside less familiar ones, including Cat and Mouse in Partnership and The Juniper-Tree. In this collection the story known as Snow White appears under the title Snowdrop.
