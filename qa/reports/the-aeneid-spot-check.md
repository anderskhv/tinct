## Issues Found
1. **Ch 12 p41 — Heavy compression**: The arming scene loses significant detail. Acceptable for prose retelling but notable.
2. **Ch 12 p41 — "din onkel Hektors eksempel"**: Hector is described as "uncle" which is an editorial interpretation, not in the Dryden verse. Minor but worth noting as editorial insertion.
3. **Ch 6 p17 — Cross-paragraph merging**: The DA paragraph incorporates dialogue from subsequent EN paragraphs. Alignment holds at paragraph count level but content boundaries shift slightly.

## Verdict: PASS
The Aeneid Danish translation is strong. Accuracy and fluency are consistently high. Literary quality dips in summary paragraphs and heavily compressed action scenes but is excellent in emotionally charged passages. No translationese issues detected. No false cognates, no dropped verb prefixes, no English word order artifacts.
