### 1. [Chapter 5, Paragraph 223] (reason: watchlist — "realisere")
- **Original (EN):** "Very true, he replied; and I agree with you that in the best-ordered State there is the nearest approach to this common feeling which you describe."
- **Danish:** "Helt sandt, sagde han. Og jeg er enig i, at den bedst ordnede stat kommer taettest paa at realisere denne faelles folelse."
- **Issue:** "realisere denne faelles folelse" for "the nearest approach to this common feeling." The English says "nearest approach to" — a state coming close to achieving something. "Realisere" in Danish means "become aware of," not "achieve/bring about." In a philosophical dialogue about ideal states, precision matters.
- **Suggestion:** "komme taettest paa at virkeliggore denne faelles folelse" or "komme naermest denne faelles folelse" (closer to the original's "nearest approach").

### 2. [Chapter 5, Paragraph 414] (reason: watchlist — "realiseres")
- **Original (EN):** "I want to know whether ideals are ever fully realized in language? Does not the word express more than the fact, and must not the actual, whatever a man may think, always, in the nature of things, fall short of the truth?"
- **Danish:** "Kan idealer nogensinde realiseres perfekt i praksis? Kommer det faktiske ikke altid til kort i forhold til idealet? Hvad folk end maatte mene, ligger det ikke i tingenes natur? Er du enig?"
- **Issue:** "realiseres perfekt i praksis" for "fully realized in language." Two problems: (a) "realiseres" again means "become aware of" in Danish, not "made real"; (b) the Danish says "i praksis" (in practice) but the English says "in language" — a meaning shift. Socrates is asking about the gap between words and reality.
- **Suggestion:** "Kan idealer nogensinde fuldt ud virkeliggores i sproget?" — restores both the correct verb and "in language."

### 3. [Chapter 6, Paragraph 205] (reason: watchlist — "realiseret")
