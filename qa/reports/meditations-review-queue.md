### 2. [Chapter 10, Paragraph 13] (reason: "aktuelle" — potential false cognate)
- **Original (EN):** "What use is there of suspicion at all? or, why should thoughts of mistrust, and suspicion concerning that which is future, trouble thy mind at all? What now is to be done, if thou mayest search and inquiry into that, what needs thou care for more?"
- **Danish:** "Hvad behov er der for mistænksomhed? Hvorfor pine dig selv med hypotetiske fremtider? Hvis handling er nødvendig, undersøg den aktuelle situation."
- **Issue:** "den aktuelle situation" — in Danish, "aktuel" primarily means "current/present" (which is the intended meaning here), but it can also mean "relevant/topical". The word is correct in this context (the original says "what now is to be done"), but it reads slightly modern/corporate. Marcus Aurelius would more naturally say "den foreliggende situation" or "det, der ligger for" in elevated Danish.
- **Suggestion:** "den foreliggende situation" (more philosophical register) or keep "aktuelle" if the modern tone is intentional.
