# Spot-Check: Paradise Lost
Date: 2026-04-08
Editions checked: modern-da (against original-en)
Focus: Verse-to-prose compression quality

## Summary
- Modern DA: avg 3.8/5 across dimensions — **NEEDS REVIEW**
- Paragraph alignment: **MISALIGNED in chapters 1 and 6** (critical finding)
- Prose quality is generally good but alignment issues undermine split-pane reading

## Critical Finding: Paragraph Misalignment

The modern-da edition has paragraph-level content drift relative to the original-en in at least two chapters:

**Chapter 1, p35-37**: EN p35 describes the barbarian multitudes from the North; DA p35 describes Satan's footsteps on the burning lake (EN ~p28 content). The DA text has shifted forward by approximately 7 paragraphs. This means the verse-to-prose conversion redistributed content across paragraph boundaries differently.

**Chapter 6, p48**: EN p48 is Satan rallying troops ("Arm, Warriors, arm for fight"); DA p48 is God sending the Son in his chariot ("Ga da, du Maegtigste, i din Faders kraft!"). These are entirely different scenes — one is the rebel forces preparing, the other is the divine counterattack. The DA appears to be ~10-15 paragraphs ahead of the EN at this point.

**Chapter 12, p39**: Correctly aligned — both discuss the imperfection of the law and the transition to grace.

**Impact**: Split-pane reading (original left, Danish right) will show mismatched content in chapters 1 and 6. This defeats the purpose of the aligned edition system.

## Issues Found

### Critical
1. **Paragraph misalignment in chapters 1 and 6**: The modern-da edition has content drift where prose paragraphs don't correspond to the same original-en paragraphs. This breaks split-pane reading. The verse-to-prose conversion appears to have consolidated or redistributed verse paragraphs differently, causing the DA content to shift relative to the EN.

### Minor
2. **Ch 6 p1 — Lost imagery**: The cave of light and darkness ("Grateful vicissitude") is replaced with narrator framing. Acceptable compression but a literary loss.
3. **Ch 12 p39 — "midlertidig foranstaltning"**: Slightly bureaucratic register for theological poetry. Minor.

## Verdict: NEEDS REVIEW
The Danish translation quality is excellent where it aligns — fluent, accurate, and often literary. However, the paragraph misalignment in at least 2 of 12 chapters is a critical structural defect that breaks the aligned edition system. **The modern-da edition needs a re-alignment pass for chapters 1 and 6 at minimum, and ideally all 12 chapters should be verified.**

Recommended fix: Re-generate the modern-da edition with strict paragraph-by-paragraph correspondence to the original-en, ensuring each DA paragraph translates exactly its corresponding EN paragraph (even if this means some DA paragraphs are very short or very long).
