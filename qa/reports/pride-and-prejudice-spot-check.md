# Spot-Check: Pride and Prejudice
Date: 2026-04-08
Editions checked: original-en, modern-da
Chapters sampled: 1, 30, 61 (first, middle, last paragraph each = 9 samples)

## Overall Verdict: PASS

**Average scores:** Accuracy 4.6 | Fluency 4.3 | Literary 4.2 | Translationese 4.1
**Lowest dimension:** Translationese (4.1) — a few spots where English syntax leaks through.
**No sample scored below 3 on any dimension.**

The Danish translation is strong overall. It preserves Austen's irony and wit well, handles the long nested sentences competently, and reads as natural Danish prose rather than machine-translated text. A few minor issues are flagged below but nothing that would block launch.

## Austen-Specific Assessment

- **Irony preservation:** Strong. The famous opening, Mrs. Bennet's characterization, and Lydia's oblivious letter all land with the right bite.
- **Social register differences:** Good. Mrs. Bennet's breathless speech, Lydia's casual mercenary tone, and the narrator's wry distance are all distinguishable.
- **Wit:** The devastating one-liners translate well ("permanent tåbelig", "hendes trøst var sladder og besøg").
- **Period register:** Mostly appropriate. One anachronism noted: "droppet" (Ch 30, P13) is too modern/colloquial for the narrator's voice.

## Issues to Consider (Non-Blocking)

1. **Ch 30, P7:** "Inden for synsvidde af" is a translationese calque of "within view of" — consider rephrasing to more natural Danish.
2. **Ch 30, P13:** "Emnet blev droppet" — register mismatch. "Droppet" is too casual for Austen's narrator.
3. **Ch 1, P18:** "Design" translated as "Hans grund" loses the scheming connotation. "Hensigt" would be more precise.
4. **Ch 1, P34:** "Upålidelig temperament" is slightly clinical — "svingende humør" would be more idiomatic.

## Verdict: PASS

The translation is launch-ready. Average 4.42/5 across all dimensions, no score below 3. Austen's distinctive ironic voice and character differentiation are well preserved in Danish. The four non-blocking issues above could be addressed in a quality pass but do not warrant holding the release.
