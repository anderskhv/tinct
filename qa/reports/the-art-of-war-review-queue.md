## Flagged Items

### 1. [Chapter 1, Paragraph 1] (reason: dropped meaning — "the art of")
- **Original (EN):** "Sun Tzu said: The art of war is of vital importance to the State."
- **Danish:** "Sun Tzu sagde: Krig er af afgorende betydning for staten."
- **Issue:** "Krig er af afgorende betydning" drops "the art of" from "The art of war." This is the opening line of the entire book. The title concept — that war is an *art*, a discipline requiring study and mastery — is the foundational premise. Dropping it reduces the sentence to a truism ("war is important") and loses the philosophical claim that war is a *craft*.
- **Suggestion:** "Krigskunsten er af afgorende betydning for staten." — "Krigskunsten" (the art of war) is the standard Danish translation of the concept and preserves the opening's full meaning.
